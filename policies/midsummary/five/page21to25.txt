Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan covers Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It offers comprehensive medical coverage including prior authorization, telehealth, urgent care, and copays/coinsurance for medically necessary services. Key details include a deductible, copays, and coinsurance. The plan specifies limitations on certain health information, notably regarding confidential data and requires written consent for all disclosures. Hometown Health maintains former member data for legal retention, subject to authorized personnel.  The plan covers medically necessary services, but excludes certain uses and disclosures. It mandates a specific schedule of benefits outlining costs and coverage.
