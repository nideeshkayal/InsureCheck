The Hometown Health 2018 Small Group PPO Plan covers Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties for comprehensive coverage, emphasizing medically necessary services requiring prior authorization.  The plan includes limitations on costs, excluding non-preferred provider charges, and co-insurance. It covers illnesses, injuries, and symptoms, excluding cost-effective interventions. Criminal activity is covered, and Custodial Care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring ongoing medical personnel.  Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-effective interventions, and prescription drug costs exceeding allowed amounts. The plan establishes HDHP requirements including pre-tax HSA contributions, and Network limitations requiring services from Hometown Health and OneHealth providers at the Preferred Provider In-Network rate.
