Hometown Health’s Provider Relationship Plan provides comprehensive coverage for independent contractors, excluding prescriptions, investigation drugs, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services. It mandates pregnancy notification, concurrent medical record review, retrospective authorization requests, and second opinions for diagnosis uncertainty. Eligibility requires US citizenship, residency, employment, and payment. Dependents may enroll with employer eligibility. Coverage begins when a spouse/domestic partner becomes eligible. The plan emphasizes medical care for injury and sickness, with cost-sharing applicable to other illnesses. Legal ward eligibility applies to disabled children (26+), requiring specific criteria. The plan’s primary objective is to control medical decision-making for Hometown Health's independence.
