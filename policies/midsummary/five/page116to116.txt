Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO plan offers expedited external review for adverse determinations within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review for conditions jeopardizing health or function. Authorization is required for all claims. This plan is governed by the EOC, Group Subscription Agreement, and Schedule of Benefits, constituting the entire contract.  Changes to this plan must be endorsed by an authorized officer. Payments for overpayments must be returned to Hometown Health. The plan includes automatic modification to comply with federal and Nevada law.  Key coverage details include specific limits and conditions.  Important exclusions and restrictions are defined, and the plan’s terminology is crucial for understanding the obligations. This plan is designed for members legally capable of contracting.
