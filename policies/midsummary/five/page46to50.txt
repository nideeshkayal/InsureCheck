This policy provides comprehensive coverage for medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals experiencing acute care needs, with limitations based on eligibility and provider restrictions. Coverage is based on Durable Medical Equipment (DME) cost, following national standards.  Key exclusions include artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, and inpatient hospital care. Hearing aids are covered for repair/replacement annually, requiring approval from providers.  Home health care is limited to skilled nursing and therapies, excluding Bluetooth and GPS. Hospice services cover six months or less life expectancy, with limited mental health benefits. Kidney dialysis is not covered except in Medicaid, and lab/diagnostic services include x-rays, fluoroscopy, and imaging. Prior Authorization is required for certain medications, contingent upon physician review and FDA approval, and reimbursement may be lower than standard.  Hometown Health maintains the right to review requests and may deny reimbursement for unused policy. Member responsibility includes deductible, copays, and coinsurance.
