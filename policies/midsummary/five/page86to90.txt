Hometown Health’s COBRA continuation plan provides coverage for terminated or reduced-work employees, triggered by qualifying events like divorce, dependent coverage, or notification. It’s crucial to contact Hometown Health or your employer to initiate the process, subject to stringent notification requirements (60 days from notification or 60 days from end) and potential plan administrator non-obligation. COBRA continuation covers individuals impacted by termination or reduced hours, including potential Medicare eligibility.  It’s dependent on meeting eligibility criteria, including being covered by another plan or eligibility for Medicare, and payment delays. USERRA offers continued coverage for employees on leave with or without pay, mirroring COBRA benefits, with 102% employer premium.  The policy terminates on a 12-week period, with a total of 12 weeks of benefits, contingent on meeting the defined conditions outlined in the Employer Group’s policy.
