Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan offers limited coverage. It excludes prescriptions for conditions like impotence, investigation drugs, and medical treatment. The formulary restricts rebate programs for pediatric vision coverage, limiting lens/contact replacements and contact lens insurance. Artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services are specifically excluded. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices are not covered.

Out-of-network services exceeding allowed amounts are not covered. Prior authorization is required for certain services like cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation, chemotherapy, and infusion therapy, determined by the Utilization Management Committee. Notifications are needed at least five business days before elective inpatient/outpatient surgery. Provider notification is required for pregnancy for benefit management.  The plan requires coverage outlined in the policy, and costs exceeding allowed amounts for inpatient/outpatient surgery are not covered.
