Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, focusing on eye exams, lens options, and contact lenses. The plan excludes reproductive technologies, including surrogacy and embryo transfer, unless specifically covered. It also excludes drugs dispensed by non-participating pharmacies and prescription medications not acting within their license. Crucially, it excludes artificial lenses, contact lens modifications, and conditions like impotence.  The policy excludes services related to investigation drugs and medical treatments.  Reimbursement rates for participating pharmacies do not include rebates for pediatric vision coverage. Members must present ID for refills and failure to do so results in non-reimbursement. The plan’s eligibility period is 19 years, and it covers annual eye exams and lens options.  The policy’s exclusion policy aims to limit member benefits compared to utilization of the plan.
