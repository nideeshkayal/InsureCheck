Hometown Health’s Policy provides comprehensive outpatient prescription drug coverage for members, emphasizing responsible cost management. Coverage includes generic, preferred brand, specialty pharmaceuticals, and diabetic supplies, with reimbursement subject to deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Specialty pharmaceuticals require a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription and limited supply.  Coverage is for outpatient prescriptions, with limited mail order options and potential for changes.  Hormone Replacement Therapy requires FDA approval or state law, and topical ophthalmic products have limitations.  The policy offers a formulary alternative for Hormone Replacement Therapy, with a maximum cost of $100.  Coverage for pediatric vision includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lenses, with specific requirements.  Eligibility for pediatric vision includes a defined age of 19, and coverage extends to diabetic supplies medically necessary. Contact Hometown Health at 775-982-3232 for questions and policy details.
