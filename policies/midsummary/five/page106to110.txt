H.O.METOWN HEALTH's 2018 Small Group PPO plan provides comprehensive coverage for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review for Urgent Care claims. The policy mandates a 48-hour notification period and a potential reduction or termination of coverage upon disagreement with the denial. Expedited External Review is permitted for conditions jeopardizing life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, requiring a 30-day notification. Compliance with U.S. regulations is crucial, with the IRO (Independent Review Organization) reviewing claims and providing final determinations within 15 days.  The plan includes expedited appeal options for Urgent Care denials, subject to a 15-day delay.  The OCHA (Office of Consumer Health Assistance) offers toll-free support at 888-333-1597.  Medical records are available for external review, with the IRO reviewing claims anew and allowing for the right to appeal any denial. The plan’s core is timely benefit review, with specific guidelines for Urgent Care claims and potential for contract termination.
