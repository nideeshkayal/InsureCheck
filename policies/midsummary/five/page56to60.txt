Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, including annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lens services. Coverage is limited to participating pharmacies for specialized pharmaceutical prescriptions, focusing on Hormone Replacement Therapy with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. The plan excludes complications resulting from procedures, injury, or loss related to a riot or criminal act, and cosmetic surgery. It also excludes psychological factors, medical problems impacting functionality, and treatments for natural remedies.  Key exclusions include medications related to erectile function, sensitive areas, and conditions affecting body appearance. The plan’s maximum prescription drug cost is $100. The plan’s eligibility period is 19th birthday.  Contact Hometown Health at 555-123-4567 for further details.
