Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. The plan covers medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases, excluding cost-effective interventions, prescription drug costs exceeding limits, and non-preferred providers. Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-sharing, and criminal activity is covered. Custodial care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring continued medical personnel.  Coverage includes telehealth and Urgent Care Center services, with In-Network Copayments and Coinsurance for Out-of-Network providers.  The policy prioritizes the Member’s Out-of-Pocket Maximum.  The Schedule of Benefits details costs, restrictions, and rights emphasizing cost-effectiveness, while managing Prescription Drug benefits through a Pharmacy Benefit Manager.  Contact Hometown Health for detailed information and coverage details.
