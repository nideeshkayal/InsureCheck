Hometown Health Insurance provides comprehensive genetic counseling coverage for inherited disorders, birth defects, and chromosome abnormalities, with limitations to one lifetime for each condition. Coverage includes mastectomies for breast cancer survivors and reconstructive surgery for breast cancer. Medical pharmacy benefits apply to specialized pharmaceuticals like injectables and oral medications, with exceptions for dosage issues. Mental Health Services are covered with limits defined in the Schedule of Benefits and newborn/maternity care with transportation costs.

The policy excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy. Prior authorization is required for certain mental health services. Gynecological care requires a 48-hour stay for vaginal deliveries and a 96-hour stay for Cesarean sections, requiring a referral. Dental services are also covered with a 48-hour stay for jawbone repair and congenital defects.  Coverage includes emergency jawbone repair, and treatment for congenital defects.
