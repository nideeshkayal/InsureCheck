Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan covers Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties, providing comprehensive medical coverage for a wide range of services. The plan offers specialized medical support for individuals with ASD, including ASD screening, diagnosis, treatment with licensed behavioral interventionists, and autism behavior support. Coverage includes allergy testing, therapy limits (600 hours for habilitation, 600 hours for rehabilitation), and copays, deductibles, and coinsurance. Prior authorization is required for ASD treatment.

Significant exclusions include FDA-approved medications and devices, extraneous expenses related to clinical trials, and limited coverage for diabetes management (infusion pumps, medication, equipment) and family planning services. Genetic counseling is now offered for inherited conditions, birth defects, and genome-related effects, with a limit of one lifetime testing per member. Coverage is capped at $600 for routine health services and excludes medication expenses. Contact Hometown Health for more details and to obtain your plan details.
