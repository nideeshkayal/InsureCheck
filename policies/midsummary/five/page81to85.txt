Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan offers monthly premium coverage for eligible individuals triggered by birth, adoption, or placement.  Premiums are regulated by Nevada Division of Insurance, requiring documented creditable coverage and eligibility based on birth, adoption, marriage, residency, and Social Security. Payments are primarily through payroll deductions, with employer-specific payment methods.  Coverage is contingent on specific requirements including notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, and timely documentation. Crucially, termination occurs upon death, dependents’ coverage ends, or a request to terminate, potentially resulting in retroactive termination.  Hometown Health maintains meticulous record-keeping, with termination triggering a Certificate of Creditable Coverage.  The plan offers eligibility requirements, including fraud prevention, and provides refunds within 30 days of termination, subject to medical cost reimbursement, excluding services after termination.  Hometown Health is not responsible for benefits received after termination and offers exclusions for services after termination.
