The H.O.METOWN HEALTH Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive insurance coverage for medical expenses resulting from third-party negligence impacting policyholders. The policy grants underwriting rights to those liable, establishing a right to reimbursement for damages – including personal injury, property loss, and medical costs – upon receiving compensation. It establishes a subrogation right, assigning the member’s recovery to the third party, ensuring full reimbursement.

Key exclusions include liability beyond the policy’s scope, such as direct negligence from the member themselves, and specific provisions related to benefits and claims, including the avoidance of the “Make-Whole Doctrine.” The policy mandates a separate account for funds and defines reimbursement based on third-party payment. Crucially, the policy requires Member cooperation with claims and provides reporting requirements. The policy’s termination is possible if information isn’t provided, ensuring rights for heirs and estates remain intact.
