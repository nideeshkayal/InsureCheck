H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers a Group PPO Plan for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review for Medicare-eligible individuals. Coverage is based on age and employment status, with key limits and conditions outlined in the policy. The plan includes Expedited Appeal for Urgent Care denials, requiring notification and a response period for claims.  Significant exclusions include organizations, potential for recovery exceeding benefit amounts, and adverse benefit determinations impacting coverage.  The policy is primarily for the first 30 months and then secondary to Medicare.  Relevant terminology includes “benefit determination,” “recission of coverage,” “urgent care claims,” and “concurrent care decisions.”  Notification of benefit determination is delivered within 48 hours of receipt, and extension is possible for unforeseen circumstances with 15-day notification.  The policy emphasizes physician judgment for Urgent Care, requiring thorough review before coverage is extended.  Customer service is available to handle informal appeals within 60 days of adverse determination.
