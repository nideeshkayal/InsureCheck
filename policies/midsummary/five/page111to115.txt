Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review. This plan covers urgent care, with expedited external reviews for adverse determinations related to this service, requiring notification and potential appeals. Independent third-party reviews of claims are conducted anew, guaranteeing appeals.  Members can submit requests for external review within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review period for conditions jeopardizing health or function. Authorization is required for all claims. The EOC supersedes all previous agreements, emphasizing representation rather than guarantees. Clerical errors will not invalidate coverage, but will result in adjustments to premiums.  The plan includes automatic modification to comply with federal and Nevada law. Payment for overpayments must be returned to Hometown Health.
