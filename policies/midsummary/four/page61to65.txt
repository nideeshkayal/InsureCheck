Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage for 50-77 year-old members with psychiatric hospital stays, including inpatient care, outpatient mental health, and nutritional therapy. Coverage extends to 28 meals after discharge, contingent on provider confirmation.  Critical coverage includes specialized mental health care, requiring prior authorization from the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services, including dietary changes and physical activity guidance, are provided.  Prior authorization is mandated for specialist services, particularly mental health, and delegated to the provider.  IV immune globulin and other drugs for home dialysis are covered.  Nursing facility care, including meals and therapy, is included.  Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services are covered, including x-rays and supplies.  Coverage includes limitations on certain therapies and treatments. Provider verification is essential. Please contact Aetna BetterHealth.com/new-jersey-hmosnp/providers/prior-authorization.html for complete details.
