Medicare Part D offers coverage for specialized medical services, including emergency care, with restrictions on direct billing. Members have rights regarding medical record access, consent for PHI sharing, and treatment decisions. Network providers cannot bill for these services, and balance billing or unapproved care is prohibited. Members are responsible for all costs, including unapproved services, and must communicate with Member Services.  Understanding your health conditions, medications, and support systems is crucial.  Prioritize providing accurate information to your Care Manager regarding changes to your health, medications, living arrangements, and support systems.  Respect other members’ rights and expectations are essential.
