Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+. Coverage includes prescription drug benefits with limitations on dosage and usage, and network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME suppliers. Members must promptly report any changes to their membership record. Key restrictions include zero monthly premiums and no premium. The policy covers behavioral health and mental health services, with prescription drug costs. Important resources include Member Services (1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711)) and Aetna Medicare.com/NJDSNP, SHIP, QIO, Medicare, NJ FamilyCare (Medicaid), Office of the Insurance Ombudsman, and the New Jersey Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman.
