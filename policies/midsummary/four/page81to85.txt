The Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements significantly broaden coverage for individuals with diabetes, increasing medication adherence support through physician-driven prescriptions, specialized pharmacy access, and updated hospital stay requirements. Key changes include limitations on non-covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures, restricting coverage to drug lists and physician-driven prescriptions. The MDPP now provides enhanced coverage for chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. Coverage extends to medication adherence, including doctor-written prescriptions and specialized pharmacy access. Importantly, the MDPP covers emergency medications, as well as new rules regarding drug lists and surgical procedures.
