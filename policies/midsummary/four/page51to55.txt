This plan, Aetna Premier Plus Plus, provides comprehensive routine medical coverage for individuals across multiple health needs. It covers annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic care. Key coverage includes a $305 monthly Extra Benefits Card for transportation, utilities, personal care, and rent/mortgage assistance. Notably, the plan includes HIV screening up to three tests per pregnancy for women at risk, prior authorization for home health services, and coverage for fertility services, with specific conditions for ongoing benefits. Importantly, coverage excludes lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent/mortgage, and the plan’s benefits continue on the next page.
