Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals, focusing on specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. Key features include prescription drug dosage limits impacting costs, and coverage for behavioral health and mental health services with limited usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, DME, and other healthcare professionals, and includes transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care. The policy covers health care services, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), durable medical equipment (DME), and payment for kidney dialysis when outside the network’s service area. Out-of-network care requires prior authorization for emergency or urgently needed services. Coverage details include limitations on Medicare benefits for certain services and the requirement for prior authorization.
