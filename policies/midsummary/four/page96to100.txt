Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices, up to 90 days for long-term care facility stays. Coverage includes emergency medications and experimental treatments, subject to quantity limits and provider evaluations. The policy mandates provider and pharmacy directory listings for essential network pharmacies and provider assessments.  Key exclusions include potential out-of-network costs and limited supply options (up to 30 days).  The EOB details for drug usage, costs, and price changes are crucial.  Vaccinations are covered.  Exception requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage impact deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services is required to identify errors and provide support.  Visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for full details.
