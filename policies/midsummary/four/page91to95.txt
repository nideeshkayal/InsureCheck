The Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP provides comprehensive Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, covering chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Coverage extends beyond 90 days for long-term care facilities, requiring supply run notifications and provider evaluation. Emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures are subject to quantity limits and provider assessment. Temporary supply options are available up to 30 days for medication costs, impacting deductibles and copays. The Drug List dictates covered medications, limits, and provider oversight.  Eligibility for NJ FamilyCare results in “Extra Help” for Medicare Part D prescription drug costs, affecting premiums, deductibles, and copays. Network pharmacies are critical for drug access, and provider assessments are mandatory. Consult the provider and pharmacy directories for network pharmacies.
