Medicare membership termination within the Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) plan requires 60-day denial of membership, followed by enrollment in a new plan or Original Medicare. Automatic disenrollment occurs upon Medicare Part A and B coverage commencement. Key considerations include Medicare Part A and B coverage, Medicaid eligibility, and disenrollment triggers – including missing information during enrollment and unauthorized use. Medicare offers assistance at 1-800 MEDICARE (TTY: 711) or Aetna Better Health of New Jersey (1-844-362-0934).  Members must contact these resources for eligibility and disenrollment procedures, including filing complaints.
