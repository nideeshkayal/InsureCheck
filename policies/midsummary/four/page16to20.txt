Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for 19+ individuals, offering prescription drug benefits with limited dosage/usage. The policy covers behavioral health and mental health services with zero premiums and no premium. Coverage is dependent on provider participation, with network providers including doctors, nurses, and DME.  A Care Manager provides specialized health care support, and SHIP offers counseling and Medicare changes assistance.  The policy includes appeals for coverage disputes, and the call is available 24/7 at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711).  The website, AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP, provides resources for Medicare recipients including cost reduction strategies.  Important resources include the Office of the Insurance Ombudsman and the New Jersey Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman, addressing potential service and billing issues.
