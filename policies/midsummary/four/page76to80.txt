The Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare beneficiaries with diabetes, focusing on physician services, prosthetics, pulmonary rehab, and drug administration. Coverage includes vision care (eye exams, glasses/contact lenses) and glaucoma screening.  The MDPP offers supervised exercise therapy (SET) training, transportation, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs) for terminal prognoses, aligning with hospice care. Urgent care is covered.  Significant exclusions include experimental medical treatments, surgical treatment for morbid obesity, and items not considered “reasonable and medically necessary.”  The MDPP also offers wigs for chemotherapy hair loss, a new benefit for up to $400 annually, and includes chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for diabetic foot disease.  Veterans receive VA-specific coverage as well.
