The Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid recipients, focusing on medical, dental, and surgical expenses. Coverage includes emergency care with transfer approval, behavioral health, prescription drugs, and DME. Key limitations include defined coverage amounts, dates, and eligibility requirements, with out-of-network care permitted under specific conditions involving a network provider.  The policy necessitates medical records from emergency providers for claims processing and reimbursement.  Coverage extends to MLTSS, including home and community-based services for members with nursing facility needs, and transportation (MAVs & ambulance for non-emergency).  Eligibility requirements and mandatory medical record requirements are present.  Member Services offers appeal rights and benefits details, while payment for clinical research studies is non-refundable.  Contact details for Member Services are provided.
