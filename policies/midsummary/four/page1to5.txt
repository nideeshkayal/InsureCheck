Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage through December 31, 2024, extending prescription drug costs while excluding certain services. The plan covers behavioral health, mental health, and substance use disorder treatment, including Medicare and NJ FamilyCare prescription drug costs. Key terms include “dual eligible special needs plan” and “member services” support.  Prescription drug costs are covered, but specific services are excluded.  Enrollment depends on contract renewal and is subject to change.  Provider referrals are not required for in-network providers, and mail order prescriptions are available through the network mail order delivery program.  Members are required to use in-network providers and DME suppliers.  The plan provides free interpreter services for language support, and call 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for assistance.
