The insurance policy outlines a rigorous process for appealing Medicare Part B prescription drug decisions, prioritizing timely review. Authorization is required within 72 hours of an appeal, or external appeal, and must be for a Level 2 appeal. The Insurance Company must authorize/provide medical care within 72 hours.  Coverage amounts and limits apply; key exclusions include assisted living, caregiver training, and chore services.  The IRO review can take up to 14 days, with a Level 2 appeal potentially leading to a Level 3 appeal.  Important:  The policy clarifies that the benefits continue during the review, and the Fair Hearing process is available for eligible cases.  Requesting the External Appeal Application form within 60 days is essential for Level 1 appeals.  Refer to the New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance for assistance.
