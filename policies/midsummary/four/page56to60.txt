Aetna Premier Plus Plus provides comprehensive medical coverage for routine medical services including annual physicals, bone mass measurements, breast cancer screenings, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screenings, and orthodontic care. The plan covers up to three HIV screening tests per pregnancy, including home health services requiring prior authorization and hospice care (eligible for Original Medicare). Coverage includes drug therapy and spiritual counseling, continuing the benefits on the next page.  Crucially, it excludes lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent payments. Prior authorization is needed for inpatient services, particularly psychiatric hospitals, acute hospitalization, and short-term care facilities.  The plan offers lung cancer screening every 12 months for members aged 50-77 with a counseling visit.
