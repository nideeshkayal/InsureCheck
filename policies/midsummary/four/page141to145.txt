The Medicare Part D coverage review process begins with a QIO review within 14 calendar days following a denial. If approved, the QIO pays for hospital services until medically necessary, maintaining the original discharge date.  A Level 2 Alternate Appeal is possible if disagreement exists with the initial denial, requiring a review of the case.  Crucially, the QIO provides a detailed explanation of the denial within one day.  Key information includes coverage amounts, restrictions like medication exclusions, and policy details.  The QIO’s primary responsibility is to ensure appropriate medical services are provided, and the original discharge date remains in effect.
