Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+ within Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid contracts, focusing on behavioral health and mental health treatment. Coverage includes prescription drug costs for Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. The plan offers eligibility requirements including contract renewal status, Member ID card completion, Provider & Pharmacy Directory enrollment, and DME supplier inclusion. Key exclusions include out-of-network care, coverage for specific services, and limitations on the Health Risk Assessment (HRA) process. Costs are zero, and the plan includes no premium. Members must follow all rules outlined in the Evidence of Coverage document. Access to the Evidence of Coverage is available via phone or website.
