Medicare Part D drug coverage decisions prioritize assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals. Significant exclusions apply to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs, requiring prior authorization (PA) for certain medications. Appeals are available after denial, with expedited coverage (24-hour response, 24-hour complaint) and fast appeal (60 days after initial letter) options. Expedited redetermination is also expedited. If denied, an explanation letter is sent. Payments must be made within 30 days, and the Independent Review Organization (IRO) reviews requests, requiring payment within 30 calendar days. Contact the QIO for crucial medication appropriateness before discharge.
