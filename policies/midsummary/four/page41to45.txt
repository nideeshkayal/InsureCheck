The Aetna Medicare NJDSNP plan offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage for a 24-month period, contingent on medical necessity. Coverage includes annual routine physical benefits, prioritizing bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening (mammograms), cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction visits, and blood tests for cervical and vaginal cancer. It has coverage limits and restrictions, including medical record requirements and potential for out-of-network charges. Prior authorization is required for non-emergency transportation by fixed-wing aircraft. The plan extends coverage for colorectal cancer screening (no minimum age limit), flexible sigmoidoscopy, and cervical and vaginal cancer screening (Pap tests and pelvic exams). It includes Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services requiring prior authorization, and covers Chiropractic services.
