Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals, including specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. Key coverage areas are health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). Prior authorization is required for specialist services, especially kidney dialysis, necessitating member notification changes to the PCP.  The plan offers coverage for home and community-based services for members with nursing facility needs, including assisted living, therapy, and transportation, managed by the provider transition process. Out-of-network specialists are permitted for up to four months after termination. Quality of care complaints are addressed through provider transition, ensuring uninterrupted access to qualified providers. Network providers can leave, requiring notification to maintain uninterrupted access to qualified providers.  Coverage levels are defined by the plan, including limitations on out-of-network care and specific conditions.
