The IURO Appeal policy outlines comprehensive Medicare Part D drug coverage decisions, prioritizing evidence of coverage for assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals. Key exclusions apply to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs, requiring prior authorization (PA) for formulary exceptions.  The policy mandates submission of a Fair Hearing application form within 60 days of a decision letter and expedited decisions (72 hours) upon receipt of a completed application.  Requests for coverage decisions and exceptions require a physician's detailed medical justification, with deadlines for fast decisions (24 hours) and complaints within 24 hours of denial.  The policy also covers payment obligations and service stops, and details the appeals process including potential for additional rights if a Level 2 appeal is denied.
