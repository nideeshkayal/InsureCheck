Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP provides comprehensive Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices up to 90 days. Coverage includes emergency medications, experimental treatments, quantity limits, provider evaluations, temporary supply options, and EOB details. Key conditions include Exception Requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage, impacting deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs. Vaccinations are covered, with Medicare Part D vaccines free. Members must contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) to report errors and visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for further details. Policy dictates that network providers must bill for covered services and drugs after enrollment, and Aetna directly handles provider payments. Providers are prohibited from billing for services not covered, and a 12-month review period applies to Part D prescriptions.
