Aetna Premier Plus Plus Coverage offers comprehensive medical benefits for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries and individuals undergoing therapy & nursing.  Coverage includes physician services (doctor's office, limited services), prosthetics (breast prostheses, repairs), pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors). Prior authorization is required for surgery, partial hospitalization, and prostate cancer screening.  Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) screening & counseling are covered, including chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, and hepatitis B.  A primary care provider session, SNF care, and drug administration (blood clotting factors) are required. Two quit attempts are allowed within a 12-month period, and OTC smoking cessation products are covered.  Qualified personnel for SET sessions are required through Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) – 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.  Coverage limits apply, including limited services.
