Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage involves a process with escalating levels of review and expedited action. Coverage decisions are handled by Member Services or Aetna Assure Premier Plus, with a 24-hour fast appeal option available for initial review. Standard coverage decisions are 14 days, while Medicare Part D coverage is 72 hours.  Expedited coverage is required for missing records or review, and requires a doctor's authorization. Appeals are initiated via a Level 1 appeal process, requiring contact with Aetna Assure Premier Plus.  A 44-day review period may occur for late requests, potentially leading to dismissal.  Key definitions include exclusions for missing records, special rules for home health, skilled nursing, and CORF, and the requirement to consult with Member Services for assistance.
