The Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. Coverage now includes emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures. Key information includes the Drug List as the primary source for drug identification, quantity limits, and provider assessment.  Providers must evaluate diagnoses and prescribe necessary tests before coverage is granted. A temporary supply of up to 30 days is available for initial coverage.  The MDPP includes a one-time prescription override for supply runs, potential refills, and exceptions to the Drug List.  Understanding the Drug List, quantity limits, and provider assessment is vital for full coverage.
