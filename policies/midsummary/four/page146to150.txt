Medicare Part D coverage review involves a tiered appeals process, beginning with an initial decision by the IRO. Subsequent steps involve Level 1 or Level 2 appeals, triggering a Level 3 appeal. The ALJ and Attorney Adjudicator handle Level 3 decisions, continuing the process if the initial decision is not upheld.  A key element is the denial process, requiring a Case Review if disagreement exists with the initial decision.
