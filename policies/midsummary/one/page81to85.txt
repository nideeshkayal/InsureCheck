Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage is structured in five tiers, each with varying copays and cost-sharing. Tier 1 and Tier 2 drugs have copays up to $30, while Tier 3 and 4 drugs have up to $30. The plan prioritizes preferred retail pharmacies to minimize costs and limits out-of-network access.  Coverage for vaccines is tiered, with some requiring fees for administration, and reimbursement varies based on the vaccine. The Catastrophic Coverage Stage offers zero out-of-pocket costs for covered drugs, focusing on cost savings through preferred retail pharmacies and limitations on out-of-network access.  Detailed information regarding vaccine coverage, including cost components and reimbursement policies, is crucial, and users should consult the Drug List for specific details.
