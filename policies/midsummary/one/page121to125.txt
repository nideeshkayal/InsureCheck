Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers two termination pathways: annual enrollment and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment.  Termination triggers upon new Medicare plan enrollment, typically January 1st, and is mandated by Cigna Healthcare for relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change.  Members must continue using their current plan’s network providers and adhere to specific network rules.  Termination involves adjusting healthcare coverage, potentially switching to Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage without a separate prescription drug plan.  Crucially, members must contact Customer Service for detailed termination policies, including reimbursement responsibilities, including third-party payment prioritization.  Violations can trigger an Inspector General investigation and potential disenrollment, requiring immediate reporting to the Compliance and Ethics Hotline.  The plan’s termination process is governed by the Social Security Act, CMS regulations, subrogation rights, and lien requirements, ensuring compliance with federal laws and Medicare Secondary Payer rules.
