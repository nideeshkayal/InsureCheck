Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must verify coverage and limits, crucial for understanding potential benefits. HCBS (Health Care Business Services) eligibility is required, necessitating state notice, approved HCBS plan authorization, and documentation. Key requirements include proof of state residency, HIV status, low income, and uninsured/under-insured status.
