Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members require utilization of provider networks for medical care, adhering to detailed eligibility requirements for coverage. Key provisions include medically necessary services aligned with Medicare standards, with specific exclusions for out-of-network care requiring prior authorization and cost-sharing. The plan mandates provider network access for routine physical exams, X-rays, and specialized treatments.  Changes to provider networks are permitted, and members are obligated to verify new assignments with Customer Service.  Prior authorization is required for out-of-network services.  Emergency care is covered, with physician assessment determining stability; follow-up care is required.  Refer to the Provider and Pharmacy Directory for location and provider information.
