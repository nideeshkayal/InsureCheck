Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers coverage decisions for medical services, requiring a formal process for determining benefits and payment amounts. Initial coverage decisions assess medical needs and determine if services are covered under the plan. Appeals are initiated for incomplete requests or disagreements. Level 1 appeals handle initial reviews, while Level 2 appeals are triggered for incomplete requests. Critical information includes coverage amounts, limits, exclusions (e.g., cosmetic treatments), and the need for Customer Service for assistance.  Key definitions include “fast coverage decisions” and “coverage decisions” for Part B prescription drugs, requiring specific conditions. The plan utilizes a step-by-step process: requesting coverage, considering fast vs. standard decisions, responding to requests, and reviewing timelines for Part B and other medical services.
