Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) employs a grievance process for coverage disputes, initiated via phone or written correspondence. Initial complaints target quality of care, waiting times, or customer service. Critically, a denial of fast coverage decisions triggers an automatic “fast complaint,” requiring expedited resolution.  The process allows for complaints regarding quality of care and provides options to submit to the Quality Improvement Organization.  Members can voluntarily terminate their membership during annual enrollment (October 15 – December 7) or switch to another Medicare Advantage plan during Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment (January 1 – March 31). Membership ends upon the commencement of new coverage plans, typically January 1st.
