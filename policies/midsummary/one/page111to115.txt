The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for appeals, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions.  Coverage is determined by approval, with the IRE crucial for drug requests, establishing deadlines for appeals and standard reviews.  The Notice – a document detailing coverage requirements – requires signing and doesn't signify agreement to discharge dates. The QIO assesses medical appropriateness and hospital discharge dates, verifying Medicare service.  If no medical justification is found, coverage ends at noon on the day after the appeal.  Level 1 appeals are mandatory for denial decisions, requiring detailed discharge date explanations.  A fast-track appeal is available for specific services, such as Home health, skilled nursing, and CORF, for three services.  A Level 2 appeal is permitted for denied decisions, requiring further review.  The QIO will provide a Detailed Explanation of Non-Coverage if a denial is upheld, and a Level 3 appeal if the initial denial is reversed.
