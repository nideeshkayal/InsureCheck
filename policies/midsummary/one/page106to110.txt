Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans utilize the independent review organization (IRE) for drug requests, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions. The plan’s 72-hour review process follows physician statements, focusing on formulary exceptions for not-on-list drugs.  Coverage is determined through the IRE’s approval, with coverage amounts based on the review’s findings.  Denials trigger letters outlining the decision, allowing a Level 3 appeal if insufficient coverage is found.  A crucial step is immediately notifying the IRE of any denial, adhering to a 72-hour deadline for appeals.  The IRE’s decision impacts coverage amounts and allows for a Level 3 appeal if needed.  The document emphasizes the importance of reviewing the written notice following an appeal, detailing rights to quality of care and reporting issues.
