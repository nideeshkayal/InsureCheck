Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D provides $0/day copays for the first 20 days of coverage, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Medications are tiered, increasing cost with higher tiers, and are subject to Drug List verification. Temporary supplies are available for emergencies, with a 30-day supply for prescription writing. Changes to the Drug List trigger immediate updates, including additions and removals of drugs and cost-sharing tiers. Coverage is tiered, with adjustments to drug lists based on Medicare requirements. Prior authorization is required for certain conditions, and the plan may adjust drug lists.  Expedited reviews ensure compliance, and critical considerations include eligibility, exclusions, Catastrophic Coverage Stage, Medicare-certified hospice patients, and potential Medicaid coverage. Contact customer service for refills, reimbursement, and Medicaid assistance.
