Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage centers on managing medication expenses through a structured system. The Enhanced Drug Coverage option provides quantity limitations and opioid pain medication coverage, while a Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program assists members in safely utilizing medications, particularly for cancer-related pain or hospice care.  Payments are determined by the Drug List, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance based on drug utilization.  Out-of-pocket costs are excluded for drugs outside the United States and not covered by the plan.  The Real-Time Benefit Tool provides estimated costs, requiring Chapter 5 for detailed rules and exclusions.  The plan tracks both “Out-of-Pocket Costs” and “Total Drug Costs” to ensure compliance with Medicare’s requirements, and the process includes receiving an EOB report detailing payment stages.
