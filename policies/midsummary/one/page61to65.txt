Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits with $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Transportation services are available at $10 copays for ambulatory customers, and vision coverage includes a separate cost share with the PCP/Specialist and Medicare-covered vision benefits. Routine eye exams are covered annually with a $0 copayment. Eyewear allowance is applied to retail value, and there are limitations on the number of covered services.  Cosmetic surgery and experimental medical procedures are not covered. The plan excludes specialized medical and surgical procedures, full-time nursing care in the home, and non-routine dental care.  The policy limits cost sharing, with exclusions for services considered “reasonable and necessary” under Original Medicare – this definition is narrowed to focus on current medical and surgical procedures, rather than specialized treatments.  Coverage terms are outlined in the plan document, with contact information available on the policy card.
