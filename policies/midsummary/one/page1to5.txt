Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical coverage through a network of providers, with monthly premiums and utilization of network providers. Coverage includes medical services, prescriptions, and potential copays/coinsurance, with defined costs based on service. The plan covers Medicare, State Health Insurance Assistance Program, Quality Improvement Organization, and Social Security.  Drug payments are a key focus, including potential cost increases due to drug safety measures, requiring careful monitoring.  Eligibility includes group insurance and employer-sponsored coverage, emphasizing the importance of reviewing your membership and ending it properly.  Key definitions and terms are included, focusing on plan benefits, exclusions, and termination procedures.
