Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement policy prioritizes timely access to covered services and drugs for members. It mandates adherence to established guidelines for billing and reimbursement, focusing on PCPs within the network. Critical details include coverage amounts, limitations (e.g., specific drug restrictions), and the right to refuse treatment, including terminating medication. Advance Directives are vital for authorizing medical decisions, and patients can request copies of these documents.  The policy covers medications, medical procedures, and devices, while defining provider qualifications and network agreements.  It emphasizes patient rights regarding refusal and termination, requiring clear communication to physicians. Compliance is key to avoiding denial, and the policy includes a grievance process for unresolved issues.  Members are responsible for premiums, share of cost for Medicare Part B, and must continue paying a premium for Medicare Part B.  It also addresses coordinated benefits with other insurance plans, requiring communication with the Medicare provider.
