Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits for Medicare patients, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics. Prior authorization is required for medical nutrition therapy, particularly for diabetes, renal disease, and after kidney transplant, ensuring patient care.  The plan covers preventive obesity screening and therapy, offering counseling and coordination with primary care.  Outpatient mental health care is provided through a psychiatrist or doctor, including psychotherapy, telehealth, and nurse practitioner services.  Telehealth services are available through a smartphone, telephone, or computer, utilizing a network provider with copays ranging from $0 to $350, with $0 copays for Primary Care and Specialist services. Coverage extends to rural areas and critical access hospitals, with exceptions based on patient circumstances.  The plan includes Medicare-covered telehealth services, with $0 or $350 copays depending on the setting.
