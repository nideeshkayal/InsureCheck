Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) requires patients to contribute a portion of their medical expenses, including medications and potentially recouping costs beyond standard reimbursement rates. Vaccines necessitate full coverage, while others have reduced reimbursement based on coinsurance or copayments. Requests for reimbursement trigger a 12-month timeframe for Part C and Part D claims, requiring timely submission and a medical assessment. Cigna evaluates requests based on medical appropriateness and cost, considering cash pricing versus negotiated rates. Denial results in a written explanation and an appeal process, with potential for denial due to non-compliance with policy guidelines.
