Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive cardiovascular disease coverage, with $125 copay, $50,000 max/year, and $0 copay. Coverage includes blood, physician services, and home health, with a 462025 Evidence of Coverage chapter focusing on transplant care.  Key terms include annual restarts, meals for ESRD customers, and prior authorization for medical and social services.  Crucially, the plan requires formal inpatient orders and prior authorization for medical nutrition therapy, especially for individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant, and limits inpatient mental health care to 190 days. Medicare Part B coverage exists for medications and medical nutrition therapy, with prior authorization required for certain procedures.  The plan also covers drug coverage through Original Medicare, excluding hospice, emergency services, and chemotherapy/radiation drugs. Medicare Part D covers some antigens.
