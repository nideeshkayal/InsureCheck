Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are covered under this Medicare HMO plan, with drug payments determined by cost-sharing. Eligibility is based on U.S. citizenship or lawful presence within the designated service area and residency. Crucially, members must have both Part A and Part B coverage to remain eligible. The Evidence of Coverage document outlines contract terms, formulary changes, and potential riders – all impacting costs and coverage. A vital aspect is the Plan Premium, which is not required but is essential for continued coverage.  Failure to have creditable prescription drug coverage results in a penalty – up to 14% of unfulfilled coverage – based on months without coverage. The Provider and Pharmacy Directory assists with network providers, ensuring access to medically necessary services.  Medicare annually updates the contract, impacting coverage, and members must stay informed of changes.
