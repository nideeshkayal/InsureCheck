Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks for medical care, participating in clinical research studies involving significant cost-sharing with Original Medicare. Coverage begins after leaving the plan and restarts upon returning to Original Medicare, irrespective of continued enrollment with the same provider. Initial rent is 36 months, followed by 24 months of DME and oxygen payments, with copays for oxygen.  Ownership rules apply to certain DME items, requiring new payments after leaving the plan.  Medicare covers medically necessary services like hospital stays and surgical procedures after the study's cost is covered.  Leaving the plan results in a return of DME, requiring a new cycle of payments.  A key exclusion is DME ownership rules, with returns required after the initial rent period, even after continued enrollment. Medicaid or QMB eligibility is required for provider proof.
