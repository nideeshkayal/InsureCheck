Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits for Medicare-covered members, prioritizing enhanced care for individuals experiencing chronic conditions. Key coverage includes laboratory tests, X-rays, physician services, and expanded dialysis treatment options, with a focus on kidney disease education and outpatient dialysis. Prior authorization is required for oral cancer drugs ($0 or $350 copay) and dialysis, requiring a $0/day copay for the first 20 days and $214 for the next 20.  The plan expands coverage for cataract removal/surgery and screening for alcohol misuse, including pregnancy.  Significant changes include enhanced kidney disease coverage, including home dialysis, outpatient dialysis, and self-dialysis training, with a $0/day copay for the first 20 days and $214 for the next 20.  Transportation benefits are available at a $10/visit, and urgent medical services are covered worldwide with a $0 copayment.  The plan also incorporates enhanced medical benefits for individuals experiencing chronic conditions, including increased coverage for dialysis and home dialysis equipment, and expanded medical benefits related to kidney disease.
