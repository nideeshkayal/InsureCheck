Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D prescription drug coverage provides $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. This plan covers all Part D drugs on the Drug List, with restrictions including network pharmacy usage, specialized services, and cost-sharing tiers.  The Drug List is crucial for determining which medications are covered and their specific conditions.  The plan offers long-term supply options through retail pharmacies with preferred cost-sharing, and generic and biosimilar alternatives are utilized.  Significant exclusions include out-of-network pharmacy access and specialized pharmacy services.  To ensure coverage, users must consult the Drug List, contact Customer Service for assistance, and understand the tiered cost-sharing system for each drug.  The Drug List is regularly updated, emphasizing the importance of reviewing it for changes in covered medications.
