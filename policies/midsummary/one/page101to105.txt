Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans provide coverage decisions for medical services, subject to a 72-hour appeal window after an appeal is initiated. The plan responds within 14 calendar days for Part B prescriptions or 72 hours for other medical services.  Key details include coverage amounts, exclusions (e.g., certain drugs not on the plan’s formulary), and reimbursement processes. Requests involve a coverage decision check and adherence to rules, with payment within 30 days of the request. The Independent Review Organization (IRE) reviews appeals up to 14 days.  The IRE’s decision determines coverage or denial, authorizing or providing services within 72 hours.  Part D prescription drug decisions and appeals are crucial.  The policy defines coverage determination, including exceptions for drug requests (covering non-formulary drugs).  Request an exception if a drug isn’t covered, and the exception types are including altering coverage, cost-sharing, or alternative drugs.
