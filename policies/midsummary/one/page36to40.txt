Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits covering 36 months initial, with subsequent 24 months of DME and oxygen, up to $5,800 annually for Medicare Part A and B. Key coverage includes medical necessity, MA coordinated care plan requirements (90-day transition), and provider network rules.  Exclusions include durable medical equipment ownership and balance billing.  A $15 copayment applies for virtual therapy, with potential for $15 copay for acupuncture. Medicare-covered cardiovascular disease risk reduction visits ($10 copay) are also covered, with prior authorization required for non-emergency services. The plan includes a one-time screening ultrasound (12 visits in 90 days, $15 copay) and provides coverage for chiropractic services with a $20 copay.  Chiropractic services, Medicare-covered virtual therapy ($15 copay), and cardiac rehab programs are also included.  Policy numbers and dates are available upon request.
