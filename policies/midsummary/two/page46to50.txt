SHSC Ind SC PY25 is a medical insurance policy for South Carolina residents, prioritizing dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Prior authorization is required for contact lens, pain management, and personal care services, significantly impacting out-of-pocket maximums. The policy offers coverage for anesthesia for surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) but limits it for out-of-network care.  Significant exclusions include air ambulance transportation, limiting billing to in-network costs, and billing limitations apply to in-network services. Mental health services are covered in inpatient or intermediate care settings, including detoxification, and substance use services are covered through inpatient or intermediate care. Policy details include deductibles, coinsurance, and copays, with a $3,000 annual out-of-pocket maximum excluding premiums. The policy covers primary and specialty care, including physician assistants and nurse practitioners, with prior authorization needed for certain services.  It also offers coverage for pediatric vision services and pregnancy services, with specific requirements for pediatric care.
