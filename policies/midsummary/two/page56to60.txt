SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents across various medical services. The policy covers outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening, with associated costs and limits. Key exclusions include medications not covered, 72-hour expedited review, and out-of-network expenses. Speciality visits, urgent care, and transplant services are covered when directed by a physician. The policy excludes care for conditions like learning disabilities, behavioral problems, and experimental treatments, and does not cover cannabis, vitamins, or services not affiliated with the network. The policy number is 123456.
