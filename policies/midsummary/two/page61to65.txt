SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents for outpatient care, surgical procedures (including colorectal cancer screenings and mammograms/OBGYN), Pap tests, newborn hearing screening, and nutritional counseling. The policy covers pediatric care, including learning disabilities, behavioral problems, accidental dental services, and ovarian cancer screening. It excludes transplantation, animal tissue, high-dose chemotherapy, donor tissue procurement beyond intended recipients, jaw joint problems, and surrogate parenting. Coverage limits are established and the policy details termination and eligibility. The document emphasizes limitations on services related to experimental treatments, cosmetic procedures, and potential unforeseen complications. Refer to the Eligibility and Termination section for full details.
