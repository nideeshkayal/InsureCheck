This policy provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents for outpatient care, surgical procedures, and screening tests. It covers a range of services, including preventative care, and includes limitations on timeframes and specific exclusions – such as learning disabilities, behavioral problems, and dental services.  The policy mandates a voluntary membership, with an appeal window of 180 days and potential expedited review for serious risk.  Medical records are freely accessible for appeals.  A review process is overseen by an independent third party, with a 72-hour deadline for denial response.  The policy also includes limitations on coverage for experimental treatments, transplant services, and donor tissue.  The policy’s key elements are comprehensive coverage, a voluntary membership option, and a specific appeal process.
