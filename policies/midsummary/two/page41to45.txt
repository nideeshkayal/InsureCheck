SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, prioritizing dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Key provisions include prior authorization requirements for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care, significantly impacting out-of-pocket maximums. Surgical procedures – blepharoplasty, mastectomy, and mastopexy – necessitate prior authorization, and behavioral health requires prior authorization, excluding emergency care. Retrospective reviews are performed by the provider. Cost-sharing encompasses deductibles, coinsurance, and copays, with a maximum out-of-pocket expense. Out-of-network air ambulance transports are limited to in-network costs.  The policy addresses ASD coverage, including assessment, screening, diagnosis, and treatment for individuals under 16 diagnosed before age 16. Services include behavior training, evaluation, habilitative services, pharmacy services, and psychiatric care.  Prescription drug coverage includes medical necessary services when provided in a medical office setting.
