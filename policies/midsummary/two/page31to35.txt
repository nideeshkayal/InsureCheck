SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Coverage begins with prior authorization for medically necessary services, including specialist consultations, CPT approval, and continuity/transition management. Key restrictions include coverage limits of 60 days, out-of-network providers are not required to pay premiums, and eligibility includes life-threatening pregnancy risks. Prior authorization is required for medically necessary services like chemotherapy, chiropractic, cochlear implantation, and DME.  It extends to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days, with enhanced prior authorization demands for home health care visits (6), home infusion services, hospice inpatient services, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services covering acute care.
