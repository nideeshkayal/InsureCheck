SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive South Carolina resident insurance coverage, prioritizing relocation, premium compliance, and dependent child protection. The policy covers health services with defined limits and exclusions, including accident/injury, adverse benefit determination, and cost-sharing. Key coverage includes emergency medical care, mandated annual approval, and 31-day notice of premium increases.  Eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations for transplant programs annually, focusing on strict criteria.  The policy also mandates specific dollar amounts for co-payments and details cost-sharing. Emergency medical care is defined as hospital-based treatment for life-threatening conditions, and the Exchange/Marketplace is managed by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services.
