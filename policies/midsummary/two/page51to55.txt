SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical insurance for South Carolina residents, covering outpatient care including surgery and supplies. Key coverage includes Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN (baseline for females), newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.  The policy offers coverage for radiation oncology treatment, facility charges, and supplies.  It utilizes a closed formulary, with exclusions for medications not covered, and expedited review for 72 hours. Costs are not required for in-network services.  Expedite review is available for medical needs, adhering to state and HRSA guidelines, and including current preventive care guidelines.  Refer to the Health Insurance Marketplace for complete preventive service requirements.
