SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay, and dependent child age. The policy covers emergency medical care, including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing. Eligibility is based on Center of Excellence evaluations, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria. Crucially, the policy defines Health Care Professional/Provider, Health Care Services, Health Plan/Issuer, and Home Health Aide, essential components of coverage. The policy includes a Facility Definition – institutions providing health services. A Final Internal Adverse Determination (Final Determination) signifies an upheld adverse benefit determination, completing the internal appeal process.  Member eligibility is determined by enrollment and premium payment, with network agreements and out-of-network exclusion. The policy offers coverage amounts, limits, and conditions, including specific requirements for Center of Excellence evaluations.
