SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, focusing on relocation, premium failure, and dependent child needs. The policy covers critical benefits including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, cost-sharing, and eligibility based on Center of Excellence evaluations. Key exclusions include cost-sharing beyond defined deductibles and co-insurance, specific drug limitations, and limitations on quantity of services. The policy defines “Qualified Health Plan” as essential benefits, cost-sharing, and compliance with the Health Insurance Marketplace. It includes coverage for serious conditions (cancer, myocardial infarction, pregnancy), prosthetics and orthotics, and SNF care. The Schedule of Benefits details services, copays, and maximums, and it replaces the previous schedule upon effective date, including sexual dysfunction coverage.  Subscribers are defined as properly enrolled individuals, excluding dependents.  The policy’s focus is on essential medical services, covering significant conditions, and ensuring compliance through rigorous requirements, setting eligibility and prioritizing essential benefits.
