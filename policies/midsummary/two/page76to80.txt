First Choice Next’s reimbursement policy governs out-of-network provider claims, with a mandatory five-day prior authorization deadline for most services. Coverage is contingent on proper medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status, as outlined in the Act.  The policy includes a $40 initial payment and a $20 subsequent payment, with premium deductions applied.  Significant exclusions exist – coverage is not provided for certain experimental/investigational services or if the provider isn’t deemed medically necessary.  The policy mandates complete claim forms, proof of loss, and a written statement of loss, with a one-year retention period for losses.
