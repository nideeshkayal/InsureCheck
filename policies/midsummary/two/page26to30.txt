SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependents qualifying for incapacitation due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Enrollment is required within 31 days of birth, with coverage including medical costs, including incapacitation, continuation, and dependent support.  Crucially, the policy includes exclusions for specific conditions and limits on duration.  Renewal is conditional on acceptance by First Choice Next or its agent, and failure to authorize results in a conditional receipt.  Coverage extends for a child in a postsecondary educational institution during a leave of absence, with reimbursement for medical costs.  Premiums are paid before the grace period to reinstate coverage, with renewals contingent on provider authorization, subject to premium limits of 60 days.  The policy also addresses potential provider departures, allowing for in-network cost-sharing for up to 90 days.  The insurer adheres to all applicable regulations, including the No Surprises Act.
