First Choice Next offers a $40/month, $0 deductible policy for out-of-network providers, covering standard health plans with lifetime and annual dollar limits.  Crucially, this coverage is deducted from premium payments and is *not* coordinated with other insurance plans. The policy includes a HIPAA Privacy Notice, which is not covered by other plans.  The insured can change beneficiary at any time via written notice, but beneficiary designation is not irrevocable. The policy explicitly excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions.  Medical assistance related to liability claims is not covered.  This policy is issued by First Choice Next and is subject to IRS tax identification.
