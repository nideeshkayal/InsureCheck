South Carolina law mandates independent review of First Choice Next adverse benefit determinations under the Public Health Service Act. This review prioritizes medical necessity, suitability, effectiveness, and experimental/investigational status, requiring internal appeal completion before expedited external review. Expedited reviews are permitted for retrospective adverse benefit determinations, subject to specific conditions, including urgent medical needs. An IRO is assigned after a request, with a five-day decision and communication to the requestor. Prior authorization is required for out-of-network coverage unless stipulated. Failure to file a claim within 180 days results in claim invalidation, and a disability notification within six months of loss is critical.  Consumer complaints can be filed via consumers@doi.sc.gov, and a complaint form is available at https://doi.sc.gov/consumers. Contact the DOI Consumer Services Division at 800-867-2177 for more information.
