First Choice Next offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, focusing on mandatory prior authorization and expedited review. Coverage is $40/month and $20 initial, with $0 deductible. Premiums are deducted, impacting payment. Crucially, health information is protected by HIPAA, with a Privacy Notice available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx.  The policy does not coordinate benefits with other coverage. Subrogation applies when a health insurer has liability for medical assistance. First Choice Next excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions, and does not coordinate benefits with other plans. Lifetime and annual dollar limits apply, covering preventative services.
