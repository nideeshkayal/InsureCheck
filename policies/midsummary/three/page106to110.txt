The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program mandates comprehensive Part D drug cost tracking for members, requiring annual review and proactive adjustment to ensure compliance with the new Catastrophic Coverage Stage.  This stage includes exclusions for drugs outside the U.S., territories, or not covered by the plan, and drugs at out-of-network pharmacies not meeting plan requirements, including payments for drugs exceeding $35 per insulin product regardless of tier.  The program utilizes five cost-sharing tiers – Non-Preferred (specialty), Preferred Generic, and various branded tiers – with a maximum monthly supply cost of $35, determined by the drug's cost and location.  Key information includes the requirement to report all Part D drug costs, with a maximum monthly supply cost of $35 per insulin product.  The plan also covers Part D vaccines, with a maximum cost of $35 per month regardless of tier, and emphasizes the importance of reviewing the Drug List for accurate cost assessment.
