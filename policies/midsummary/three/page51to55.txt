The Medicare Part A and B plan provides coverage for $10,000 annually for chronic low back pain treatment, including ambulance services ($255/per-way for Medicare-covered ground and $20% coinsurance for air ambulance) and eight sessions of improvement. Coverage is for 12 weeks or longer, with no systemic causes, surgery, or pregnancy.  The plan covers annual wellness visits, including lab services ($0 copay) and annual physical exams ($0).  There is no insurance, copayment, or deductible for osteoporosis risk.  Breast cancer screening is covered for women aged 35 and 39, and for women 40 and older with mammograms every 24 months.  Colonoscopy is covered for high-risk individuals, with a minimum limit of 120 months.  Specific exclusions include potential for complications from the condition; reimbursement is dependent on the medical necessity of the treatment. Refer to the Medical Benefits Chart for complete details.
