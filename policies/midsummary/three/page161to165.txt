Jefferson Health Plans prioritize swift resolution of grievances through expedited procedures for critical decisions, including coverage, appeals, and drug requests. Expedited processes are available up to 14 days, subject to justification.  If a complaint necessitates extra time, up to 14 additional days can be added, up to a total of 44 days.  For quality of care complaints, two options exist: direct complaint to the Quality Improvement Organization or a formal appeal to the Appeals Unit.  Detailed information on coverage, exclusions, and terminology is found in Chapter 2.  The plan also outlines the process for addressing grievances, including the initial complaint file, investigation by the Appeals Specialist (within 24 hours), and response outlining denials or reasons for denial.
