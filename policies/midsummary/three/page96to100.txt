Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides comprehensive Medicare Part D drug coverage, extending to brand-name, generic, and biological medications. Coverage includes 30-day supply limits, except for routine chiropractic care, with a 90-day supply for new members and an emergency supply for long-term care. Annual Drug List updates necessitate immediate attention, with exclusions for non-Medicare-covered drugs, fertility treatments, cosmetics, and sexual dysfunction. “ExtraHelp” programs are now excluded for drugs not typically covered, requiring proactive review. Medicare-certified hospice patients require separate notification for prescription refills. Policy numbers and contact information are available at JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare.  The Drug List is updated annually with new generic and biosimilar additions, impacting cost-sharing and utilization.  Crucially, coverage is subject to withdrawal, replacement, or other changes, requiring immediate action.
