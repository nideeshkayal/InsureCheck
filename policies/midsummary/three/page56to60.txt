Medicare Part A and B covers chronic low back pain with annual out-of-pocket expenses up to $10,000, including ambulance and air ambulance services, eight sessions of improvement, and wellness visits (free, 12-month). Diabetes screening is available for eligible individuals 35-39 with a minimum of 120 months, including annual physical exams ($0) and annual wellness visits (free, 12-month).  Coverage includes DME (wheelchairs, crutches, etc.) with $0 costs, diabetic monitoring supplies ($0), and emergency room visits ($100) contingent on medical conditions. Prior authorization is required for supplemental services like bed orders, IV infusions, and DME, with out-of-network costs mirroring in-network rates.  Medicare covers hearing exams at $35, while specialist visits can exceed $1,000.  HIV screening is $0 copay for every 12-month screen for women during pregnancy.  Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers coverage for DME, bed orders, and IV infusion pumps with 36-month enrollment, ensuring comprehensive benefits for Medicare recipients.
