The chapter outlines the Medicare Level 3 appeal process following a Level 2 decision. An initial Level 2 appeal triggers a Level 3 process, requiring patient filing within 24 hours. This involves three independent review organizations: the Independent Review Entity (IRE) and Medicare, overseen by the QIO. The IRE assesses medical appropriateness, while Medicare oversees the IRE’s review. If the IRE denies, the patient remains liable for full cost. If approved, reimbursement continues until the designated care period concludes. The process emphasizes timely action and coverage continuation deadlines.  Key details include the coverage duration, potential for appeals, and necessary notification, requiring patient contact information for the Quality Improvement Organization.
