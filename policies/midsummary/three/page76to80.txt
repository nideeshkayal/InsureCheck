Medicare supplemental coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, and psychiatric services. Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring a 20% coinsurance. Key details include prosthetic and orthotic device coverage, including repair and replacement, COPD members receive $0 copay for pulmonary rehabilitation, and telehealth services are available at $0 copay for 24/7 access via Teladoc. Medicare covers six kidney disease education services per lifetime at $0 copay, and outpatient dialysis treatments, including dialysistreatments temporarily unavailable, are covered with 20% coinsurance. Alcohol misuse screening is offered, with four counseling sessions per year for positive results. Prior authorization is required for certain facilities. The policy covers blood-including storage and administration services beginning with the first pint used, and all other components of blood are covered beginning with the first pint used.
