Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers no Part D drug costs under the Catastrophic Coverage Stage. Coverage begins with a $35 monthly supply cost for each drug, tiered based on slower-priced cases and maximum supply. Mandatory Part D vaccine coverage is $35 per supply, with vaccine cost and administrative charges factored in.  Vaccination administration is crucial and reimbursement is typically full, with reduced amounts for other vaccinations.  The Catastrophic Coverage Stage ends with no Part D costs, requiring full coverage until the end of the year.  Refer to page 111 for details. Contact [Insurance Provider Contact Info] for questions.
