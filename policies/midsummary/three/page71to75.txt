Medicare Supplemental Coverage offers comprehensive medical benefits, ranging from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with coverage levels adjusted based on Medicare-covered stays. Key services include outpatient hospital observation ($375/stay), diagnostic tests/therapies ($275/visit), and blood testing ($275/pint). Prior authorization is required for specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services. Out-of-network services incur a 20% coinsurance. Medicare-qualified mental healthcare includes psychiatrist, clinicalpsychologist, clinicalsocialworker, nurse practitioner, physicianassistant, and licensed professional counselor, with a $35 copay. Rehabilitation services encompass physicaltherapy, occupationaltherapy, speech-language therapy, and independent therapist offices. Medicare-covered ambulatory surgical center services cost $245. Telehealth services for acute illnesses or stroke are permitted within 6 months of an in-person visit. Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex is included.
