The JeffersonHealthPlans Flex (PPO) coverage plan requires immediate action.  Users must contact the Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit at 866-901-7096 to file a complaint regarding medical care.  A call to the Pharmacy Department (866-371-3239) is needed to address prescription drug coverage issues, including potential reimbursement obligations as outlined in Chapter 7.  The Medicare Complaint Form (www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx) is essential for submitting complaints, requiring review of coverage details and potential reimbursement requirements.  The SHIP (Pennsylvania Medicare Education and Decision Insight) offers free counseling, focusing on Medicare benefits, treatment options, and plan changes, through their website (www.shiphelp.or g) and phone number.  The program provides vital information to navigate Medicare claims and ensure compliance.
