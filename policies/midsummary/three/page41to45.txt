JeffersonHealth Flex (PPO) plans provide coverage for medical services, dependent on whether you receive covered services through your plan. Medicare covers routine medical services, but clinical research studies are contingent on participation and subject to specific conditions and documentation requirements. Medicare will cover item services, even if you’re not actively involved, but not new services testing or evaluating.  Durable medical equipment (DME) ownership requires a minimum number of consecutive payments, and switching to Original Medicare necessitates a new payment cycle. Oxygen equipment coverage is also included under Medicare, with a five-year cycle after leaving the plan. Contact Member Relations for detailed information and reimbursement policies.
