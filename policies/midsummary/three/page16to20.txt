JeffersonHealthPlans Flex(PPO) offers comprehensive Medicare-eligible medical coverage through a coordination system, primarily utilizing the Coordination of Benefits (COB) process. Each year, patients receive a letter detailing all other insurance they carry. The plan’s primary responsibility is to cover Medicare-eligible medical services, while the secondary payer, typically the employer or group health plan, covers the remaining costs.

Failure to meet payment deadlines results in penalties, and complaints are filed with Chapter 9 for review. Patient support is available through the Member Relations department via phone and website, with interpreter services available. Crucially, the policy requires a call to the designated number (866-901-8000) to initiate the coverage process. Coverage decisions are handled through Chapter 9, a process for reviewing and potentially modifying coverage decisions.  Patient support is available via phone and website, with specific contact details for the Pharmacy Department (866-371-3239) and the Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit (866-901-7096) located at 1101 MarketStreet, Suite 3000, Philadelphia, PA 19107.
