The insurance policy governs expedited medical coverage decisions for Medicare Part B drug benefits, prioritizing immediate review for potential coverage. A 72-hour window follows a decision, requiring an appeal if not met. The Independent Review Entity (IRE) reviews appeals, determining coverage based on specific circumstances.  The policy outlines coverage amounts, limits, exclusions (non-covered drugs, restrictions), and the importance of medical reasons for exceptions.  Payments must be made within 30 days of approval.  The policy also addresses Part D coverage, including crucial definitions, formulary exceptions, cost-sharing adjustments, and the mandatory appeal process. The document also includes information on the IRE’s role, including how to initiate appeals, explain medical justifications, and contact details.
