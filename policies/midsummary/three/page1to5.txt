Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers comprehensive healthcare coverage for Jefferson Health Network members, providing medical and prescription drug benefits. Coverage includes doctor visits, tests, and procedures, with potential for changes in premium, deductibles, and co-pays.  The plan covers medication costs separately, but has restrictions on certain drugs.  Emergency care is available at Jefferson Health.  The plan offers Part D prescription drug coverage, with restrictions on certain medications.  Vaccinations are covered based on delivery method and plan stage.  Quality Improvement initiatives are in place to assist with prescription cost control.  Eligibility is based on the Jefferson Health Network, with potential for changes impacting enrollment. Medicare assistance and the State Health Insurance Assistance Program are available for support.  Termination occurs upon membership expiration.  Members are responsible for maintaining ongoing medical care and continued drug coverage.
