Medicare supplemental benefits for chronic low back pain include DME, diabetic monitoring, emergency room coverage ($100 with medical conditions), homehealthagencycare (for HIV-risk individuals), and remote monitoring (prior authorization required). Hospice care for Medicare-certified patients with a terminal prognosis covers services related to terminal care. Drug costs are shared with hospice, no simultaneous coverage allowed. Immunizations are covered based on risk level, requiring additional vaccinations for high/intermediate risk. Hospital stays start at $250/day for Medicare-covered stays, with 90-day residency, including lab tests & X-rays, with first pint coverage.
