Medicare Supplemental Coverage offers shared payments for Medicare drug and hospice costs, tiered by copays, reserved lifetime, and remaining days. Coverage begins with $0 for Medicare-covered stays, with $800 reserved for 60 years, and remaining days covered. Key services include medical nutrition therapy ($35/visit), physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy. Prescription drug coverage is under Part B, requiring coinsurance and deductibles. Specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services are covered. Prior authorization is required for inpatient admissions, particularly new ones, and specialist visits. Opioid treatment programs are covered through an OTP (Outpatient Treatment Program) with dispensing and administration of MAT medications, counseling, and periodic assessments. Coverage limits apply to specific diagnostic procedures and tests.  Contact your primary care physician for more details.
