The QIO oversees coverage appeals for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) 155. If a Level 1 appeal is denied, patients incur full costs and must initiate a second appeal. Subsequent appeals – Level 2 (Administrative Law Judges) – require specialized legal expertise.  If rejected, patients continue coverage until the designated end date, incurring continued costs.  Level 3 appeals are triggered after a Level 2 decision. Medicare coverage is subject to the Council's final decision, requiring a 60-day deadline to proceed after denial.  Detailed information regarding coverage amounts, limitations, and definitions is provided, with the QIO’s contact information for urgent assistance.
