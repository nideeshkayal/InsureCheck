The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan (MPP) offers monthly premium payments, yearly deductibles, and prescription copayments to eligible individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs. It includes the AIDSDrugAssistance Program (ADAP) for individuals living with HIV, providing medication access through formulary. Employer-sponsored Medicare benefits, including the Railroad Retirement Board (RRB), are crucial for maximizing coverage. The plan covers medical services, equipment, and prescription drugs, with coverage amounts determined by the benefits chart.  Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) utilizes Employer-Sponsored Benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage.  Prior authorization may be required for certain services.  Providers are responsible for coordinating care and ensuring patient records are sent to the provider, with a focus on medically necessary services. Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services.  Resources include the back of this document for specific phone numbers and information.
