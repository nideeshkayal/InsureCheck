JeffersonHealthPlansFlex Medicare coverage for oxygen equipment, supplies, and maintenance covers $7,000 per year for Medicare Part A and Part B services from both networks and out-of-network.  The plan’s maximum out-of-pocket expense is $10,000 annually, with $10,000 coverage for both networks and out-of-network providers, except for Medicare-approved services.  The provider must maintain coverage for five years, extending beyond the initial plan period.  Out-of-network providers incur higher costs, requiring approval; costs are calculated based on the provider’s reimbursement rate.  Members must contact Member Services for balance billing disputes. Coverage for Part D prescription drugs and medically necessary services is subject to prior authorization from Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO).  Preventative services are covered at no cost to the member.  Medicare may add new preventative services during 2025, with this benefit appearing on the benefits chart.
