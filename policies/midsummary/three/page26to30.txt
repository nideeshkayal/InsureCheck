The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (DHS) provides comprehensive assistance to individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs. DHS offers Medicare benefits, including coverage for 65+ individuals, disabilities, and End-Stage Renal Disease.  Social Security handles extra Part D drug coverage for higher incomes. The Pennsylvania Department of Human Services assists with Medicare claims disputes, hospital stay duration adjustments, and home healthcare support. Eligibility is based on Social Security benefits, including 65+ status, disabilities, and End-Stage Renal Disease. Medicaid covers Medicare beneficiaries with limited income through premiums, offering full coverage for QMB+ and qualified individuals. The Pennsylvania Aid for Individuals with Disabilities (ADAP) program helps individuals with HIV access medication through a formulary, requiring state residency, low-income status, and/or under-insurance.  Information on eligibility, covered drugs, and enrollment can be found at 1-800-922-9384 or the DHS website (www.dhs.pa.gov).
