The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Medicare Part D late enrollment penalty begins six months after initial enrollment and is calculated as a percentage of the average monthly premium, adjusting annually. This penalty applies to all Medicare beneficiaries under 65.  It’s crucial to pay promptly to avoid disenrollment and loss of prescription drug coverage, as failure to do so results in significant financial consequences. Payment options include check, electronic funds transfer, phone payment, and pay by phone.  A complaint process is available for situations preventing timely payment, outlined in Chapter 9.  The plan emphasizes the importance of understanding the penalty’s calculation and potential consequences, including the timeframe for review and the liability of remaining members beyond the initial enrollment period.
