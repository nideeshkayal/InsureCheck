Medicare Supplemental Insurance provides \$0 to \$800 coverage for medical services, including physical therapy, psychiatric care, prosthetic device repairs, and COPD benefits. Coverage is phased, with the initial 12-month period covering WelcometoMedicarePreventive Visit (out-of-network exceeding \$50,000 excluded). Key exclusions include cosmetic surgery, experimental procedures, and equipment, and immediate family members' fees. The plan covers urgent care with a \$0 copay up to 36 weeks, but requires in-network authorization.  Prior authorization is required for certain facilities, with network providers preferred. JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) 82 offers a \$0 copay for emergent/urgent services. Contact information is available upon request.
