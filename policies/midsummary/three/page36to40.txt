Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers comprehensive medical coverage through employer-sponsored benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage. Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services. Coverage amounts are determined by the benefits chart, with potential adjustments for out-of-network care costing significantly higher. Key changes include a new 2025 Evidence of Coverage, highlighting updated medical necessity guidelines. Out-of-network providers incur higher costs, necessitating prior authorization. The PCP coordinates care, sending records to providers, with options for prior authorization and provider network adjustments.  A member can request prior authorization; clinical information is required from the provider to approve requests. If a specialist leaves, the plan manages provider changes. If a primary provider leaves, the member receives notification.  Emergency care is covered for work and out-of-network providers, with full costs unless Medicare is involved.  Resources include the plan’s website ([www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare]) for urgent care and disaster-related care.
