The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Part D prescription drug coverage has undergone significant revisions, primarily focusing on enhanced patient safety.  The plan now eliminates non-covered fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments, requiring annual review and proactive adjustments to ensure compliance. A new Drug Management Program (DMP) is implemented for opioid use, mandating provider collaboration for accurate dosage and patient safety through medication therapy management. Mandatory prescription details, including dosage and pharmacy affiliations, are now required for all Part D prescriptions.  The plan introduces a voluntary Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program offering comprehensive medication lists and disposal information, completely free to participate.  Crucially, a transition to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage occurs if out-of-pocket costs exceed $2,000 within a calendar year, impacting payments for drugs not covered or outside the US/territories, or not normally covered in Medicare.  This update significantly expands the scope of coverage and emphasizes rigorous patient safety protocols.
