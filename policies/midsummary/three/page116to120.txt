Jefferson Health Plan Flex Part D mandates mandatory vaccination administration for coverage. Maximum monthly vaccination costs are $35 per supply, tiered based on vaccine price and supply, with strict adherence to Chapter 7 procedures. Providers must bill directly to the plan for services, excluding out-of-network providers.  Patients are fully responsible for full costs, including out-of-network pharmacies, if a bill is received.  The plan considers requests for payment and requires additional patient information.  Failure to comply with rules results in denial of coverage.  A key point is that out-of-network pharmacies bill fully, requiring patient responsibility and a process for appeals.  Detailed coverage details, exclusions, and regulations are outlined in the policy document.
