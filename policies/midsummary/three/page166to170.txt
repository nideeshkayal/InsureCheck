Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers voluntary membership ending with a transition period until January 1st. Members can choose to continue coverage or switch to alternative Medicare Advantage plans, including those with or without prescription drug coverage. The Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period begins January 1st, requiring careful attention to potential changes to coverage amounts, deductibles, and annual maximums. Special enrollment periods are available for eligible members due to circumstances like moving, Medicaid eligibility, contract violations, or PACE enrollment. Members must contact Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE or visit www.medicare.gov to manage their coverage and discontinue the policy.
