The plan offers comprehensive Medicare Part D drug coverage, prioritizing brand-name, generic, and biological medications with a 30-day supply limit, excluding routine chiropractic care. Special pharmacy services extend to home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC), restricted medication, and mail-order pharmacy. Drug coverage includes a temporary supply of medications offering a 90-day membership for new members and extending coverage for long-term care stays with a 31-day emergency supply.  Users must utilize the “Real-Time Benefit Tool” or contact Member Relations to determine coverage, with specific rules regarding prior authorization and exception requests. Detailed coverage details are available in Chapter 9, Section 6.4, detailing procedures for requesting exceptions.
