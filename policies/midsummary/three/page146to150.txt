Medicare oversees Part D drug prescription appeals, with a 24-hour deadline for Level 2 reviews and expedited decisions for physicians. The process begins with contacting the Medicare Quality Improvement Organization to initiate Level 2 review.  The review involves examining the casefile, ensuring all relevant information is considered, and assessing coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) plans.  Critical details include Medicare-covered services, payment arrangements, and a formal discharge notice.  The discharge date must be signed before discharge, and the review considers hospital stay decisions and quality concerns. Medicare’s coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is a key element – detailed evidence of coverage is required.  Failure to meet the deadline results in penalties, necessitating immediate contact with the QIO.
