Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is a Medicare Advantage plan for eligible members, providing coverage for Part A and Part B healthcare services within the designated service area of Pennsylvania counties. The plan offers qualifying health coverage and fulfills the ACA’s individual shared responsibility requirement, with a valid contract until December 31, 2025. Key details include eligibility based on Medicare membership and residing within the defined service area (PA counties: Philadelphia, Bucks, Montgomery, Chester, Delaware, Adams, Franklin, Lancaster, York, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lebanon, Perry, Carbon, Lehigh, Monroe, Northampton, Schuylkill, Berks, Bradford).  Coverage is contingent upon approval by other insurers, with subrogation rights permitting reimbursement for services provided by secondary payers, governed by the plan’s documentation.  Members are required to utilize their membership card for covered services and prescription drug access. Network availability is not guaranteed, and exceptions apply for emergencies and out-of-area services.  Refer to www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare for the most current information and to access the EOC detailing enrollment details and coverage limitations.
