The Plan offers $800 annual coverage for prescription drugs, excluding routine chiropractic care and cosmetic surgery. Coverage applies to all medications, with a $0 copay for emergencies and urgent care, and a $0 copay for accident/improved body member function.  Important exclusions include radial keratotomy, LASIK, and lowvision aids, as well as immediate family members.  The Drug List defines covered medications, requiring approval before coverage is granted. Prescription fulfillment must occur through an approved pharmacy or mail-order service, with reimbursement limited to a 30-day supply.  Contact the Plan directly for details on requesting reimbursement and submitting necessary documentation.
