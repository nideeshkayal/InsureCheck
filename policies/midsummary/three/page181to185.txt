PACEplan offers comprehensive coverage for elderly individuals, including Medicare beneficiaries, Medicaid recipients, and those with disabilities. It provides $2,000 maximum drug costs, a $1/day limit, and specialized coverage for individuals with disabilities, Hospice care, and Medigap.  Crucially, the plan offers “Real-TimeBenefitTool” for accurate formulary information, ensuring timely adjustments to drug coverage.  It’s important to note that certain drugs are excluded from PartD, and annual cost limitations apply.  The plan covers prosthetics and orthotics, QIO checks, quantity limits, and a special enrollment period. Urgent needs are covered, including rehabilitation services, while medically necessary routine visits are not considered urgent.  The plan is currently unavailable due to temporary restrictions.
