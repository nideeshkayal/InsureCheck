The Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhances coverage for individuals with diabetes, expanding into physician services, prosthetics, pulmonary rehab, and medication adherence support. Key coverage includes chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. Emergency medications and experimental treatments are subject to quantity limits and provider assessment. The MDPP requires provider evaluations and drug list verification. Temporary supply options are available up to 30 days for medication costs.  Eligibility for NJ FamilyCare offers “Extra Help” for Medicare Part D prescription drug costs.  Vaccinations are covered. Network pharmacies are critical for drug access, and provider assessments are mandatory.
