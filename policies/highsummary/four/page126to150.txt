The Medicare Part D drug coverage review process begins with a QIO review within 14 days of a denial, focusing on assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals.  Coverage amounts are defined, with significant exclusions including New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance services.  The IRO conducts Level 2 appeals, which can lead to Level 3 appeals if disagreements exist.  The process involves a tiered system – initial decision, then Level 1 or 2 appeals, ultimately culminating in a final service date and potential Level 4 appeals.  Critical details include denial requirements, medical justification deadlines (24-hour responses), and potential for alternative appeals.
