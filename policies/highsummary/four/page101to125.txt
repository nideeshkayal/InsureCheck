Aetna Premier Plus Plus HMO D-SNP offers comprehensive Medicare coverage for diabetes prevention programs (MDPP) enhancements, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices up to 90 days. Coverage includes emergency medications, experimental treatments, quantity limits, and provider evaluations.  Key limitations include deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs. Members must report errors to Aetna, and network providers must bill for covered services and drugs after enrollment.  The policy emphasizes timely access to covered services, particularly emergency care and specialized care. Patient privacy is protected through secure data management, and limitations apply to coverage decisions regarding facility-specific resident rights.  Crucially, it details navigation of potential problems through the Office of the Insurance Ombudsman Program (1-800-446-7467).
