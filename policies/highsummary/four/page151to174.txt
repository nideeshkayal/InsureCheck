Aetna Premier Plus D-SNP’s end-of-service management policy covers Medicare and Medicaid eligibility, with dedicated care manager, care plan, and care team roles. It mandates 60-day denial and enrollment in a new plan following Medicare Part A and B coverage. Key restrictions include drug management, including Medicare Part D coverage, and specific exclusions for drug types and billing practices.  The policy emphasizes hospice care for low-income individuals and medication management, utilizing QIOs for patient support. Significant changes involve specialized rehabilitation and urgent care, potential for translation services, and stringent billing review. Contact Aetna Member Services and Aetna Better Health of New Jersey for eligibility and disenrollment assistance.
