Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+, focusing on behavioral health and mental health services.  It provides prescription drug benefits with limitations and network provider requirements including doctors, nurses, DME, and emergency care.  Key exclusions include out-of-network care, specific service limitations, and prior authorization requirements.  The policy covers Medicare and Medicaid, with zero premiums and no premium.  Enrollment depends on contract renewal and requires Member ID card completion and provider directory enrollment.  Resources include the Evidence of Coverage document, Member Services (1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711)), and the Insurance Ombudsman.  Care Manager and SHIP offer specialized counseling and assistance.  Coverage includes durable medical equipment and transportation, and requires prior authorization for certain services, particularly around dosage limits.
