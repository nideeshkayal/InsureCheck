Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical coverage through a network of providers, covering medical services, prescriptions, and potential copays. Key coverage includes Medicare, State Health Insurance Assistance Program, Quality Improvement Organization, and Social Security. Drug payments are a significant focus, requiring careful monitoring. Eligibility is based on U.S. citizenship or residency, with a crucial “Evidence of Coverage” deadline (1% penalty per month) for unmet coverage. Medicare manages the program through the Federal agency, contracting with Us, and provides detailed eligibility information via the Medicare Complaint Form.  HCBS (Health Care Business Services) eligibility is required, necessitating state notice and approval. Contact Customer Service for questions about coverage and enrollment, including aTTY number and messaging services.
