Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medication coverage, prioritizing controlled substances and vaccines. It utilizes a tiered system with copays ranging from $30 to $30, excluding out-of-network costs.  Significant coverage includes medications, medical services, and device reimbursement, with specific limits for drugs like opioids.  The plan emphasizes timely access to covered treatments, requiring proactive communication with providers.  Medicare Part D coverage is structured with five tiers, prioritizing preferred retail pharmacies and limiting out-of-network access.  Patient responsibilities include premiums, share of cost for Part B, and maintaining compliance with policy guidelines and denial appeals.  SHIP counselors are available for support.
