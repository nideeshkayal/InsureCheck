Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans offer coverage decisions through the independent review organization (IRE) and Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for drug requests and appeals. The plan prioritizes expedited coverage decisions for Part B prescriptions and other medical services, utilizing the IRE for drug requests, focusing on formulary exceptions. Coverage is determined by the IRE’s approval with defined amounts, and denials trigger letters.  A crucial step is immediately notifying the IRE of denials.  Members can terminate via annual enrollment or Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment.  Termination involves adjustments to healthcare coverage and potential switching to Original Medicare or Medicare Advantage.  Members must contact Customer Service for termination policies, including reimbursement responsibilities.  Violation can trigger an Inspector General investigation.
