Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits for Medicare recipients, prioritizing chronic condition care. Key coverage includes physician services, diagnostics, dialysis, cataract surgery, and kidney disease education. Prior authorization is required for oral cancer drugs and dialysis, with a $0/day copay for the first 20 days. Telehealth, including smartphone, telephone, or computer, is available for mental health services, including psychotherapy, telehealth, and nurse practitioner support.  Expedited reviews ensure adherence to drug lists.  Transportation benefits (including $10/visit) are provided. Prescription drug coverage includes $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days.  The Drug List is a critical document, and users must understand its updates.  Customer service is essential for refills, reimbursement, and Medicaid assistance.
