Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) termination pathways utilize Medicare Part D coverage, with maximum out-of-pocket expenses defined by Congress. Medicaid eligibility is crucial, requiring “Member” designation for policy management. Special Enrollment Periods allow for changes in service area or benefits.

Key provisions include defined drug coverage limits, cost-sharing tiers impacting plan payments, and mandatory compliance with Medicare regulations and Social Security Act.  The Social Security Act dictates repayment obligations, while coverage decisions determine plan payment. Special Needs Plans (SNP) offer Medicare coverage for low-income individuals.

Important policy elements include Provider designation for prescription drug access, utilizing a “Real-Time Benefit Tool,” and step therapy/Supplemental Security Income (SSI) programs. Contact Cigna Healthcare customer service for Medicare-related questions and support via the provided portal.
