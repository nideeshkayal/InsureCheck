Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) provides comprehensive cardiovascular disease coverage for up to two diabetes screenings annually, with $125 copay for Medicare ER visits and $50,000 max/year for outside U.S. ER.  The plan covers routine hearing services, fitness benefits (including network access), and remote monitoring for terminal prognoses.  Medicare Part A and B coverage extends up to $5,800 annually for dialysis services, with a 36-month initial period and 24-month DME and oxygen payments.  Prior authorization is required for out-of-network care, with potential for medical and social services costs, excluding durable medical equipment ownership and balance billing.  The policy requires formal inpatient orders and prior authorization for certain medical nutrition therapy, especially for patients with diabetes or renal disease. Medicare Part B coverage exists for medications and medical nutrition therapy, with prior authorization for certain procedures. Contact Cigna Healthcare at 1-888-886-1992 for details.
