First Choice Next offers a $40/month, $0 deductible policy for out-of-network providers, covering standard health plans with lifetime and annual dollar limits.  Coverage is contingent upon mandatory five-day prior authorization and adherence to the Act’s stipulations regarding medical necessity, suitability, and experimental status.  The policy excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions, and does not coordinate benefits with other insurance plans.  A HIPAA Privacy Notice is available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx.  The policy is issued by First Choice Next and is subject to IRS tax identification.  It’s designed for standard health coverage with defined dollar limits.
