South Carolina’s SHSC Ind SC PY25 policy provides comprehensive medical insurance for South Carolina residents, covering outpatient care, surgical procedures (including colorectal cancer screenings and mammograms), Pap tests, newborn hearing screening, and nutritional counseling. Coverage includes pediatric care, including learning disabilities, and ovarian cancer screening. The policy offers limitations on experimental treatments and transplant services. It includes a voluntary membership with an appeal window and expedited review for serious risk. Prior authorization is required for out-of-network services, and failure to file within 180 days results in claim invalidation.  A dedicated review process oversees independent medical necessity, suitability, and testing requirements.  Consumers can report issues via consumers@doi.sc.gov and should utilize the DOI Consumer Services Division at 800-867-2177 for more details.
