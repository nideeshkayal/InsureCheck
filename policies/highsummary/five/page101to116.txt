Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO plan serves 65+ members offering comprehensive coverage for urgent care claims. The policy prioritizes timely benefit review, with expedited external reviews for adverse determinations related to this service, requiring notification and potential appeals.  Key coverage includes defined benefit limits for urgent care, but also includes exclusions for organizations, recovery exceeding benefits, and potential for denial.  The plan utilizes the EOC for claims review and updates, mandates specific notification deadlines (48 hours for initial, 15-day for extension), and includes automatic modification to comply with Nevada and federal law. Payments for overpayments must be returned to Hometown Health.  The plan is governed by the EOC, Group Subscription Agreement, and Schedule of Benefits.
