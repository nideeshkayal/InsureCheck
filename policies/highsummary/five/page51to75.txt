Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, focusing on eye exams, lens options, and contact lenses. It offers coverage for prescription drugs, including Hormone Replacement Therapy, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance.  Coverage excludes conditions like impotence, investigation drugs, and medical treatment.  Artificial lens replacements and contact lens insurance are excluded.  Reimbursement rates for participating pharmacies do not include rebates.  Eligibility is 19 years, with a focus on pediatric vision and limited mail order options.  Out-of-network services exceed limits, and prior authorization is needed for specific treatments.  Independent contractor coverage excludes prescriptions and artificial lens replacements.  The plan requires pregnancy notification, medical record reviews, and a spouse/domestic partner eligibility requirement.  This plan aims to control medical decision-making for Hometown Health's independence.
