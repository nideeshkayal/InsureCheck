Hometown Health’s plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties, focusing on medical services and preventative care. It covers essential medical services including immunizations, screenings, mental health care, and pediatric care, with limitations to medications, fertility treatments, and inpatient hospital care.

Key exclusions include artificial reproduction, donor sperm/eggs, and limited coverage for diabetes management. Policy limits include $600 for routine health, and Medicare coverage for specific conditions. Prior authorization is needed for mental health and some specialist treatments. DME coverage is available for necessary equipment, subject to CMS guidelines, and requires notification within 24 hours for emergency admissions.  Member responsibility encompasses deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Contact Hometown Health for detailed policy details.
