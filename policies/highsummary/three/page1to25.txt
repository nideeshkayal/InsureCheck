Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is a Medicare Advantage plan for eligible Jefferson Health Network members in Pennsylvania counties. It offers Part A and Part B medical coverage, including prescription drug benefits, with limitations on certain medications. Coverage includes doctor visits and procedures, but has restrictions. Emergency care is available at Jefferson Health. Vaccination coverage is based on delivery method and plan stage. The plan uses a coordination system, primarily through COB, and requires timely payment to avoid penalties.  Members must utilize their membership card for covered services and prescription drug access. The plan’s primary focus is to provide comprehensive medical coverage, while secondary payer responsibilities are defined.
