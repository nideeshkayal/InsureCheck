Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers a structured Medicare Advantage plan with a focus on timely coverage decisions. It utilizes a Level 3 appeals process triggered after a Level 2 decision, overseen by both Medicare and the QIO.  Coverage is structured with defined durations and potential for appeals, dependent on the specific decision.  Key coverage areas include prescription drug coverage, with specific limitations and definitions outlined in the policy document. Medicare retains the right to cover services outside its primary payer role, impacting potential Part D shortages.  Members are subject to termination for policy violations, including withholding coverage, impacting drug access, and unauthorized usage.  Crucially, proactive risk mitigation is emphasized to avoid termination.  Contact information for the Quality Improvement Organization and Medicare is provided for assistance, including urgent support.
