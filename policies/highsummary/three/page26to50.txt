JeffersonHealthFlex Medicare plans provide comprehensive medical coverage through employer-sponsored benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage. Medicare covers routine medical services, but clinical research studies and specialist care require prior authorization. Coverage includes medical services, equipment, and prescription drugs, with costs determined by the benefits chart. Out-of-network care incurs higher costs requiring prior authorization, with maximum out-of-pocket expense capped at $10,000 annually. Prior authorization is required for certain services. Oxygen equipment coverage is included under Medicare, with a five-year cycle. Member Services is the primary point of contact for balance billing and reimbursement disputes.  Prior to 2025, prior authorization requirements for specialized services may increase.
