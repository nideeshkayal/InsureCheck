Jefferson Health Plans Flex Part D provides comprehensive prescription drug coverage, significantly expanding the scope. It mandates annual review and proactive adjustments for compliance with the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, excluding drugs outside the U.S., territories, or not covered by the plan. Core coverage includes mandatory Part D vaccine coverage ($35/month) and medication cost tracking with limits of $35 per drug. The plan prioritizes cost management with tiered supply costs ($35 per drug), dependent on slower-priced cases and maximum supply.  Out-of-network pharmacies incur full reimbursement, necessitating patient responsibility. Medicare requires sharing health information, and patient privacy is protected through robust data security measures.  The plan emphasizes patient responsibility for all drug costs and offers support for inquiries.
