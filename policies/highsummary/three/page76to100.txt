Medicare Supplemental Insurance provides comprehensive medical benefits including physical therapy, COPD care, and prosthetic repair. Coverage levels range from $0 to $800, with out-of-network costs exceeding 20% of services. Prescription drug coverage includes a 30-day supply limit, excluding routine chiropractic and cosmetics. Prior authorization is required for certain facilities, with preferred network providers.

The Plan offers Medicare Part D drug coverage focusing on brand-name, generic, and biological medications with a 30-day supply limit, excluding routine chiropractic and cosmetic surgery.  Emergency care is covered at a $0 copay up to 36 weeks, but requires authorization. The Drug List specifies covered medications, requiring approval prior to coverage.

Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides comprehensive Part D drug coverage, prioritizing brand-name, generic, and biological medications.  Coverage extends to 30-day supply limits, excluding routine chiropractic, and offers emergency supplies for long-term care.  Crucially, Medicare certification for hospice patients necessitates specific notification procedures.
