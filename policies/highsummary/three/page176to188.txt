Jefferson HealthPlansFlex(PPO) offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare recipients, including elderly individuals, with a $2,000 annual drug limit.  The plan includes Part D drug coverage, with restrictions on formulary availability and potential shortages. Special Needs Plans (D-SNP) offer Medicare/Medicaid coverage excluding drugs not on the formulary, with eligibility for ExtraHelp and DME support.  

Key policy details include: eligibility requirements, call center, reporting form, and specific limits on drug costs.  The plan covers prosthetics and orthotics, QIO checks, and urgent rehabilitation services.  The policy is currently unavailable due to temporary restrictions.
