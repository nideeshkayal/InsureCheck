Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers enhanced medical benefits for chronic condition patients, including expanded kidney disease coverage. New for 2025, the plan provides $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days.  A new transportation vendor partnership with rideshare services is introduced, offering $10 copays for ambulatory customers.  Glaucoma screening is now covered annually, with a $0 copayment for immediate care, $30 for emergencies & urgent coverage, and $50,000 limit per year for global emergency services.  Vision coverage includes a separate cost share with the PCP/Specialist, and Medicare-covered vision benefits require separate cost sharing.  Routine eye exams are covered annually with a $0 copayment.  Eyewear allowance is applied to the retail value, and there are limitations on the number of covered services.  Careful attention is paid to exclusions related to medical necessity, remote examinations, and cost sharing, ensuring compliance with regulations.
