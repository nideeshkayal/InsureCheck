The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes a 72-hour review process for drug requests, prioritizing fast coverage decisions if a drug is not yet received.  If a request is denied, a letter detailing the reasoning is provided, with deadlines for appeal ranging from 65 calendar days to 14 calendar days after the notice.  A fast appeal requires an answer within 72 hours of receiving the request, and failure to provide an answer within this timeframe triggers escalation to Level 2 of the appeals process.

Level 2 appeals typically require a standard appeal within 7 calendar days, with decisions made to expedite coverage as medically necessary.  A standard appeal for payments for drugs already purchased has a deadline of 14 calendar days.  A crucial point: if the initial decision is denied, the recipient must decide whether to continue the appeal process, which will then proceed to Level 2. The Independent Review Entity (IRE) is the designated review organization.
