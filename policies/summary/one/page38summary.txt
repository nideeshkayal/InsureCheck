Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits with a 36-month initial rent and subsequent 24-month DME and oxygen coverage, up to a maximum of $5,800 per year for Medicare Part A and Part B. Key coverage includes medical necessity, MA coordinated care plan requirements (90-day transition), and provider network requirements.

The new One-time Screening Ultrasound for high-risk individuals allows for a maximum of 12 visits within 90 days, subject to physician referral, with a $15 copayment.  Providers must meet specific requirements including acupuncture licensure and a current, unrestricted license. Acupuncture treatment must be discontinued if the patient isn’t improving or regressing.

A $15 copayment applies for Medicare-covered virtual therapy sessions. Non-emergency ambulance transportation is covered to the nearest facility with a medical necessity, and prior authorization is required for non-emergency services.  Out-of-network ambulance services are reviewed after rendering. Worldwide ambulance services are covered to the closest, most appropriate facility.
