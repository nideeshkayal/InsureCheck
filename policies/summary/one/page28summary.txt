Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks for medical care. This plan covers medically necessary services aligned with Medicare standards. Key exclusions exist regarding out-of-network care, requiring prior authorization and cost-sharing limitations for emergency services.

The current page details how to access specialists and other network providers.  Changes to provider networks are permitted, and members must call Customer Service to verify new provider assignments.  If a provider leaves, the plan provides rights and protections including uninterrupted access to qualified providers, notification of provider departures, and assistance in finding a new provider.  For out-of-network care, contacting your Primary Care Physician (PCP) is recommended, particularly for emergency services.  Additionally, the page highlights coverage for dialysis services for ESRD members, and offers guidance on accessing emergency care during disasters, including dialysis services for geographically displaced members.
