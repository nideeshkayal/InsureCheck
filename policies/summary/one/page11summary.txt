Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members face a crucial “Evidence of Coverage” requirement for timely prescription drug payments. Failure to meet this deadline results in a 1% penalty per month of unfulfilled coverage, escalating to a maximum of 14% for 63+ months. Medicare determines the average monthly premium, currently $36.78, and this premium is linked to the monthly prescription drug bill.

The “Part D Late Enrollment Penalty” applies to those who don’t have coverage after enrollment. Social Security will issue a letter requiring payment, escalating to potential disen enrollment.  To address this, members can pay the penalty via three options: paying by check, online via Cigna Healthcare’s secure system, or having it deducted from their Social Security check.

Crucially, the page emphasizes that the Part D late enrollment penalty is due by the last day of the month, and contacting Customer Service is recommended if facing payment difficulties.  Changes to the monthly plan premium are not allowed during the year, and eligibility for “Extra Help” programs may allow for penalty cessation.
