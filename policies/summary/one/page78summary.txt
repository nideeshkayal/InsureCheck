The chapter focuses on Part D prescription drug coverage.  Medicare has rules regarding out-of-pocket costs, including deductible, copayment, and coinsurance.  The initial coverage stage involves a deductible, while copayment and coinsurance are assessed based on the total cost of drug utilization.  Medicare’s rules dictate payments for drugs during specific stages.  Crucially, the “Real-Time Benefit Tool” provides estimated costs, but it’s essential to refer to Chapter 5 for detailed drug coverage rules and exclusion details.  The chapter emphasizes understanding these rules to accurately track out-of-pocket expenses, ensuring compliance with Medicare’s requirements.
