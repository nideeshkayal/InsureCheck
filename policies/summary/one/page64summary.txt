Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits with $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Transportation services ($10 copays for ambulatory customers) and vision coverage with a separate cost share for PCP/Specialist and Medicare-covered vision benefits are included.

Routine eye exams are covered annually with a $0 copayment.  Cosmetic surgery or procedures are not covered. Experimental medical and surgical procedures and equipment are determined by Original Medicare and are not generally accepted. Full-time nursing care in the home is not covered. Non-routine dental care is covered. Home-delivered meals are not covered.

Key changes include the exclusion of specialized medical and surgical procedures, reversal of sterilization procedures, and significant limitations on personal items in a hospital room.  Furthermore, the definition of "reasonable and necessary" according to Original Medicare is updated to reflect a narrower scope of services covered under the plan.
