This chapter focuses on patient responsibility for healthcare costs under Cigna’s Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan.  Patients are obligated to contribute a portion of their covered medical expenses, including medications, and are required to pay back a portion of the total bill, often reimbursing for expenses exceeding the plan's standard reimbursement rates.

Key considerations include the Drug List – a crucial resource detailing specific vaccine coverage and cost structures – to ensure proper utilization and avoid unmet coverage requirements.  The plan utilizes a tiered system; some vaccines necessitate full reimbursement, while others have reduced reimbursement based on coinsurance or copayments for administration.

Specifically, the chapter addresses situations where patients must request reimbursement, including emergency care, provider billing discrepancies, and retroactive enrollment.  Failure to utilize the Drug List and follow the outlined procedures can result in unmet coverage obligations.  It’s essential to promptly address provider billing issues and submit all necessary documentation for reimbursement.
