Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) provides comprehensive medical benefits for cardiovascular disease, with coverage for up to two diabetes screenings per 12 months. Diabetes self-management training, diabetic services, and supplies are covered with a $20 copay. Prior authorization may be required for specialized diabetes monitoring devices and continuous glucose monitoring.

Chiropractic services are covered with a $20 copay, with prior authorization required for severe diabetic foot disease. Dental services are covered under Medicare, with reimbursement to Cigna Dental Allowance provider.

Depression screening is covered annually with no coinsurance, copayment, or deductible. Routine dental services are expanded beyond Original Medicare, with a Dental Allowance. Cosmetic procedures are not covered.

New additions include expanded cardiac rehab and intensive rehab programs, with prior authorization ($1 visit per year). Durable medical equipment (DME) coverage is expanded, including wheelchairs, crutches, and IV infusion pumps, with a $0 copayment for Medicare-covered therapeutic shoes and inserts, and $0 copayment for Medicare-covered diabetes self-management training. A limited number of DME items have a maximum rental price, and reimbursement ceases after 36 months.
