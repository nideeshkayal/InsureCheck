Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers two pathways for member termination: annual enrollment and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment.  Members can voluntarily terminate their membership during the annual enrollment period (October 15 – December 7) to potentially adjust their healthcare and prescription drug coverage.  If eligibility for “Extra Help” is required, members must be aware of the termination process.

Crucially, termination is triggered upon new coverage plan enrollment, typically January 1st.  The page details options to switch to a different Medicare health plan – Original Medicare with a separate prescription drug plan, or original Medicare without a separate prescription drug plan.  Members can also choose to end their membership due to special circumstances like institution care, or by enrolling in PACE.  To ensure a smooth transition, members should contact Customer Service for further information and utilize the provided handbook and Medicare resources.
