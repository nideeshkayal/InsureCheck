Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits for its members, with a focus on enhanced care for individuals experiencing chronic conditions. Key coverage includes laboratory tests, X-rays, and physician/practitioner services, expanding to include dialysis treatment and home dialysis equipment.  

Crucially, the plan now incorporates enhanced medical benefits related to kidney disease, including education, outpatient dialysis, inpatient dialysis, self-dialysis training, and equipment. Prior authorization is required for renal dialysis, with a cost-sharing structure of $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20 days, and no deductible for the remaining days.  

Supervised Exercise Therapy (SET) is covered for patients with symptomatic peripheral artery disease (PAD) and is offered up to 36 sessions over 12 weeks, requiring qualified auxiliary personnel and physician supervision. Transportation benefits are also included, with a $10 copayment per Medicare-covered visit.
