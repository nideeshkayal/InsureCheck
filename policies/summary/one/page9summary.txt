Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are subject to Medicare HMO rules, requiring review of the Evidence of Coverage document. Key stipulations include eligibility based on U.S. citizenship or lawful presence, and residency within the designated service area. Members must have both Medicare Part A and Part B coverage to remain eligible.

The Provider and Pharmacy Directory lists network pharmacies, where pharmacies are vetted and agreed to fill covered prescriptions. The Provider and Pharmacy Directory also indicates preferred cost sharing for certain drugs, potentially lowering costs.  A Drug List is provided, outlining covered drugs and restrictions.

Your monthly costs include Plan Premium, Medicare Part B Premium, Part D Late Enrollment Penalty, and Income Related Monthly Adjusted Amount.  A crucial element is the Plan Premium which is not required, ensuring eligibility.  Members must continue paying Medicare premiums, including Part B, for continued coverage.
