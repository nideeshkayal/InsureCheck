Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans provide coverage decisions for medical services, subject to a 72-hour appeal window after an appeal is initiated. If a Medicare Part B prescription drug denial occurs, the plan responds within 14 calendar days. The Independent Review Organization (IRE) reviews appeals, and the process can take up to 14 calendar days.

The IRE's decision is written, outlining the decision and reasons. If the review suggests coverage, the plan must authorize or provide services within 72 hours, with expedited options available for 24 hours. If denied, the plan will send a letter explaining the decision and provides a Level 3 appeal option.

To address reimbursement for medical bills, the policy clarifies that requesting reimbursement involves a coverage decision check and rules adherence. The plan will then send payment within 30 days of the request.
