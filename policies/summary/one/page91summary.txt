**Summary**

Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement policy mandates adherence to established guidelines for ensuring culturally competent care. The plan’s core requirement is accurate billing and reimbursement of services, with Cigna evaluating requests based on medical necessity and cost. Denied requests trigger detailed explanations, leading to appeals. Crucially, the plan’s obligation extends to providing direct access to women’s health specialists within the network for routine and preventive care, including translation services and braille formats.  Failure to comply with these guidelines results in denial, a letter detailing the rationale and appeal opportunity.  The policy also includes a dedicated opportunity for female members to access direct specialist access for women’s health services.  Providers are responsible for providing accessible information through customer service, including interpretations and format options.  The plan must consider the benefits of the plan in a format accessible to all members, including those with limited English proficiency.  Finally, a grievance process is available for individuals experiencing challenges receiving information, directing to the Member Grievances department.
