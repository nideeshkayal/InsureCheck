Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers two membership termination pathways: annual enrollment and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment. These pathways are triggered upon new Medicare plan enrollment, typically January 1st.

Termination occurs upon member relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change, as mandated by Cigna Healthcare. Violations, such as providing incorrect information or disrupting care, can trigger an Inspector General investigation and potential disenrollment.

Crucially, Medicare requires explicit authorization before termination. The plan must provide a written reason for termination. Members are strongly advised to contact Customer Service for detailed information regarding termination policies and procedures.  Understanding these stipulations is vital to maintain compliance and avoid potential consequences.
