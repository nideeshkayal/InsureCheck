This section focuses on Part D vaccine coverage, transitioning from the Initial Coverage Stage to Catastrophic Coverage.  Vaccine costs vary significantly, with some requiring a fee for administration, including provider costs.  For most adult vaccines, the plan pays the pharmacy’s cost for the vaccine itself, but reimbursement is determined by the amount paid, potentially requiring recoupment from the plan.  Specifically, for certain vaccines, the plan will reimburse the full amount paid, while for others, reimbursement is reduced by coinsurance or copayments for administration.  The page emphasizes understanding vaccine requirements and utilizing the Drug List for detailed coverage details.
