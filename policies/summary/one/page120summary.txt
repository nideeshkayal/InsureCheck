Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers a voluntary membership termination process. The page details two key termination options:

1.  **Annual Enrollment Period:** Members can voluntarily terminate their membership during this period (October 15 – December 7). During this time, coverage adjustments are necessary, and members must make decisions about their healthcare and prescription drug coverage.

2.  **Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period:** Members can terminate during this period (January 1 – March 31) to switch to another Medicare Advantage plan. This allows for seamless transitions and enrollment in separate prescription drug plans.

Crucially, membership ends upon the commencement of new coverage plans, typically January 1st.
