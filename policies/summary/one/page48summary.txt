Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers cardiovascular disease coverage with $125 copay, $50,000 max/year, and $0 copay. Coverage extends to blood, physician services, and home health, with a 462025 Evidence of Coverage for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) chapter focusing on transplant patient care, including blood storage and administration.

Crucially, the page details the process for inpatient medical services, emphasizing the necessity of a formal inpatient order and the requirement for prior authorization.  It outlines a 190-day lifetime inpatient mental health care limit, with a $320 daily copay for the first 6 days, $0 for the next 90, and a $0 copay per lifetime reserve day.  The plan also covers 60 “lifetime reserve days.”  Expediting hospital admissions requires a readmission policy, with a $0 copay per lifetime reserve day.
