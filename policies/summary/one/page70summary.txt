Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers $0/day copay for the first 20 days of coverage, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. The Drug List is crucial for verifying coverage, as it details which drugs are covered and their associated restrictions. Certain drugs have specific rules regarding coverage, including cost-sharing tiers impacting the cost of each dose.

The plan utilizes tiered cost-sharing, with higher tiers increasing the expense. Drug restrictions are triggered by specific drug rules, requiring prior authorization for certain medications.  If a drug is not covered as desired, there are steps to address this, including contacting Customer Service for approval or exploring alternative options. Furthermore, the Drug List is periodically updated, and steps are taken to address unexpected drug coverage, ensuring medication safety through appropriate utilization.
