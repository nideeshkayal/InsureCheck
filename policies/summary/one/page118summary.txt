Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers a process for addressing complaints regarding coverage decisions, appeals, and related issues. The complaint process is limited to specific problems, primarily quality of care, waiting times, and customer service.

To initiate a complaint, individuals must contact Cigna Healthcare via phone or written correspondence.  A complaint is formally filed as a “grievance” or “grieved.”  The process begins with contacting Customer Service, which will inform you about the next steps.

Step 1 involves promptly contacting Cigna Healthcare, and submitting your complaint to the designated address.  A standard complaint resolution timeframe is 30 calendar days. Expedited grievances require 24 hours, up to 14 more calendar days.  A final response will include reasons for denial.

Critically, the page highlights that a complaint about denied fast coverage decisions or appeals triggers a “fast complaint” automatically.  Additionally, the process allows for complaints about quality of care, and provides options to submit complaints to the Quality Improvement Organization and directly to Medicare.
