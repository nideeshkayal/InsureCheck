Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers medical benefits for cardiovascular disease, including up to two diabetes screenings per year, with a $125 copay for Medicare-eligible ER visits and $50,000 max/year for outside U.S. ER. Coverage includes routine hearing services ($0 copay for one), hearing aid evaluations ($0 copay), and fitness benefits with a standard network.

**Crucially, the 5-year cycle begins with new coverage, and coverage restarts.**  Meals for ESRD customers are provided, with a $0 copay for one per year, and home health services are available once per year, contingent on needing home health.

Prior authorization is often required for medical and social services.  HIV screening is once per year, with home-delivered meals for ESRD members offered monthly.  Hospice care is available for terminal prognoses, with Medicare-certified hospice programs required.  The program also provides remote monitoring services.
