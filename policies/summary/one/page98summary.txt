The chapter details coverage decisions and appeals for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage. Coverage decisions assess medical services and determine whether they are covered under your plan. The process involves requesting coverage decisions and appealing if you disagree with the decision.

Key points include:

*   **Coverage Decisions:** A decision to cover or deny medical services. It’s a formal process regarding benefits and payment amounts.
*   **Appeals:** A formal request to review a coverage decision. Requests will be dismissed if incomplete or if there’s a legal issue.
*   **Level 1 Appeals:** Initial review by the original decision-maker.  A dismissal will be sent if the request is incomplete or lacks proper authorization.
*   **Level 2 Appeals:** An independent review organization initiates a process after a Level 1 appeal, if necessary.

The chapter emphasizes utilizing Customer Service for assistance and contacting SHIP counselors for further support regarding coverage disputes.
