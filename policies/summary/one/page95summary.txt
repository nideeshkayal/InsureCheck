Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers coverage for eligible members, emphasizing patient rights and responsibilities regarding medical care. Patients have the right to refuse treatment and terminate medication, subject to liability. Advance Directives are crucial, enabling authorized medical decisions through document creation.

Key policy responsibilities include understanding covered services, following prescribed protocols for each service, and informing physicians and providers about health conditions. Patients must provide detailed information about their medical services and medication requirements. Coordinating benefits with other insurance plans is essential, requiring communication with the Medicare provider.

Members are responsible for paying premiums, share of cost for Medicare Part B, and must continue to pay a premium for Medicare Part B as a condition of membership.  Moving outside the plan service area may result in membership termination. It is important to understand that the policy covers medical services and drugs, while excluding certain aspects like cosmetic treatments.
