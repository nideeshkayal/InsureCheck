Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks to receive medical care. Providers are essential for coordinating services, including routine physical exams, X-rays, and specialized treatments. The plan covers medically necessary services aligned with Medicare standards. Key exclusions exist regarding out-of-network care, requiring prior authorization and cost-sharing limitations for emergency services.

Specifically, the plan covers routine health care, including breast exams, mammograms, and Pap tests, with network providers.  Emergency care from out-of-network providers is covered, and Medicare-certified dialysis services are provided outside the network's service area, requiring authorization. Changes to PCP assignments are permitted, and members should call Customer Service to verify a new provider is accepting new patients.
