Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Medication coverage is tiered, increasing cost with higher tiers. Drug eligibility is dependent on Drug List verification and prior authorization for specific conditions. Temporary supplies are available for emergencies, with an additional 30-day supply for prescription writing. Changes to the Drug List trigger immediate updates, including additions and removals of drugs and cost-sharing tiers.  The plan may adjust drug lists based on Medicare requirements.  Changes to drug coverage are communicated through website updates and regular Drug List revisions.
