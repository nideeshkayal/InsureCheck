Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members face a critical “Evidence of Coverage” requirement – failure to meet the deadline results in a 1% penalty per month of unfulfilled coverage. This penalty escalates to a maximum of 14% for 63+ months.

To address this, members can pay the penalty via check, secure system, or through Social Security.  Changes to plan membership records (name, address, phone) are required, and liability claims (auto accidents, nursing homes) are also essential.  

Important considerations include: Contacting Customer Service immediately if you experience payment difficulties, and verifying changes to your other insurance coverage through Social Security.  Medicare mandates coordination of benefits when other insurance is involved, requiring a yearly questionnaire.  The plan member ID number is needed to confirm insurance coverage details.
