Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D provides $0/day copays for the first 20 days of coverage, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Drug coverage is tiered, with higher tiers increasing cost. Medications are subject to Drug List verification and restrictions, requiring prior authorization for certain drugs.  A temporary supply is available for drugs not on the Drug List or subject to restrictions, allowing for consultation with a provider.

To request a temporary supply, the drug must no longer be on the plan’s Drug List OR be restricted.  The plan will provide a 30-day supply for drug-related emergencies, with an additional 30-day supply for prescription writing.  If a drug is not covered as desired, contacting Customer Service is the primary action.  For cost-sharing tiers, changing medications is the primary option to mitigate expenses.
