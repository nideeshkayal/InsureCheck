**Summary:**

This chapter details HCBS (Health Care Business Services) eligibility requirements for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members.  Verification is critical, as HCBS eligibility mandates state notice, approved HCBS plan authorization, and documentation.  Key requirements include proof of state residency, HIV status, low income, and uninsured/under-insured status.

The document emphasizes that HCBS coverage is contingent upon meeting these specific criteria.  It details that the application process requires thorough review and validation of eligibility.  Further, this chapter addresses how to contact Railroad Retirement Board for assistance and customer support for questions regarding your specific plan.
