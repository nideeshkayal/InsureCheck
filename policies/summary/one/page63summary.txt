Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers enhanced medical benefits with $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Transportation services are introduced with $10 copays for ambulatory customers, and vision coverage includes a separate cost share with the PCP/Specialist and Medicare-covered vision benefits requiring separate cost sharing. Routine eye exams are covered annually with a $0 copayment. Eyewear allowance is applied to retail value, and there are limitations on the number of covered services.

Cosmetic surgery or procedures are not covered. Experimental medical and surgical procedures and equipment are those determined by Original Medicare to not be generally accepted by the medical community. Full-time nursing care in the home is not covered.  Non-routine dental care is covered. Home-delivered meals are not covered.
