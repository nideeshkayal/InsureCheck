Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage tiers manage cost based on the drug’s tier.  The plan offers five tiers with varying copays and cost-sharing, including “Standard Retail,” “Preferred Retail,” “Non-Preferred,” and “Specialty.”  Tier 1 and Tier 2 drugs have copays up to $30, while Tier 3 and Tier 4 drugs have copays up to $30. For long-term supplies, the cost varies between Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, and Tier 4, with some drugs offering extended supplies up to 100 days.  The “Long-Term Care” (LTC) tier up to 31 days presents a significant cost-sharing, which is up to 31 days.  The plan prioritizes cost savings by allowing for lower costs when utilizing preferred retail pharmacies, and includes limitations on out-of-network pharmacies. The provider has the option to prescribe less than a full month’s supply, which can be helpful for planning refills.
