Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans offer medical coverage decisions, with a 72-hour review process following physician statements. A fast coverage decision is granted if a drug is not yet received or a request is made without a doctor’s support. The IRE reviews drug requests through specific sections, emphasizing formulary exceptions for not-on-list drugs. Requests must be submitted through the plan’s website, phone, or fax, including a detailed request with necessary information. A fast coverage decision is granted if a health condition necessitates it, with a 24-hour deadline for the decision. If denied, a letter explaining the decision is sent, outlining the standard deadlines.

The plan requires a standard coverage decision within 14 calendar days of request, but if it's delayed, it is sent to Level 2 of the appeals process. If the answer is yes to part or all of what’s requested, the plan must provide coverage within 14 calendar days after receiving the request. If the answer is no, a written statement explains the denial and how to appeal.  Level 1 appeals are permitted within 65 calendar days from the date of the written notice.
