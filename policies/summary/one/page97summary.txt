This chapter details two processes for addressing problems with Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage: coverage decisions and complaints. Coverage decisions involve evaluating your specific medical needs and determining whether services are covered under your plan. Complaints are handled through the process of grievances, which are approved by Medicare.  This process focuses on understanding the specific details of your coverage and how to navigate potential issues.

The chapter highlights the importance of understanding the legal terms associated with each process, including “making a complaint” versus “filling a grievance,” and abbreviations like “organization determination” or “coverage determination.”  It emphasizes that the guide in Section 3 is crucial for accurate communication and information retrieval. The key takeaway is that you should always utilize Customer Service for assistance, and if necessary, contact SHIP counselors for further support.
