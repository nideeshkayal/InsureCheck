This chapter details crucial insurance terms related to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) termination pathways. Key elements include defined coverage limits for drugs, services, and cost sharing tiers. The “cost sharing tiers” are structured with higher tiers representing greater prescription costs, impacting plan payment.

The chapter emphasizes “coverage determination” – a decision about covered drugs and required cost sharing – as a critical step. It details coverage decisions, which are termed “coverage decisions” in the document.  Specifically, it defines covered drugs, services, and establishes the importance of “creditable prescription drug coverage” for eligibility. Custodial care is defined as personal care provided in a nursing home, hospice, or similar setting, and doesn’t involve medical care. Exception and Disclosure Information documents outline required information for members, detailing obligations and rights. Emergency coverage defines medical emergencies as requiring immediate attention.
