Part D prescription drug coverage details Medicare’s rules for Medicare beneficiaries. Payments are not included in out-of-pocket costs. Key exclusions include drugs outside the United States, not covered by the plan, and medications from out-of-network pharmacies not meeting plan requirements.

The “Real-Time Benefit Tool” provides estimated costs, but requires Chapter 5 for comprehensive drug coverage rules and exclusions. The current page emphasizes understanding these rules to accurately track out-of-pocket expenses.

The process involves receiving an EOB report – a monthly summary of payments – detailing prescription costs, total payments, and what the plan and you each paid. This report indicates movement to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, triggering the next payment phase. The plan tracks both “Out-of-Pocket Costs” and “Total Drug Costs” to maintain compliance.

The plan has 5 cost-sharing tiers, detailing the varying costs based on drug type and dispensing location.
