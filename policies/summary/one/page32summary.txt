Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are required to utilize provider networks for medical care. Coverage is contingent on participating in a clinical research study, involving significant cost-sharing with Original Medicare. Medicare will cover medically necessary services like hospital stays and surgical procedures after the study’s cost is covered, but participation is not guaranteed.

Denial of coverage necessitates documentation for Medicare-approved studies.  The plan offers limited coverage for DME – durable medical equipment – with ownership rules: members own certain items like prosthetics, and rental is required for others (e.g., oxygen equipment) after paying copays for 13 consecutive payments, even if previously enrolled.  Leaving the plan or not obtaining ownership of DME results in a return of the item.
