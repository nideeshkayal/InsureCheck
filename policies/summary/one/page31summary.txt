Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks for medical care, but are encouraged to notify us in advance for Medicare-qualified clinical trials.  Participating in a clinical research study involves significant cost sharing, with Original Medicare covering routine services and our plan paying the difference between cost-sharing and the participant’s in-network cost. Medicare will pay for medically necessary services, including hospital stays, surgical procedures, and treatment of complications, after the study’s cost is covered.  The plan will pay the cost of participation, but not the additional costs, if the study tests a new item or service, unless Medicare would cover it even without the study.  Denial of coverage requires documentation for Medicare-approved studies.
