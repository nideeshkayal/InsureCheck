**SUMMARY:**

This chapter addresses the responsibility of patients to contribute to healthcare expenses.  You are required to pay a portion of the total bill for covered medical services or drugs.

The plan utilizes a tiered system for vaccine coverage.  Generally, the pharmacy will cover the cost of the vaccine itself, but reimbursement is dependent on the total amount paid. This includes provider costs, potentially requiring recoupment from the plan.

Specifically, some vaccines necessitate full reimbursement, while others have reduced reimbursement based on coinsurance or copayments for administration.  A critical element is understanding the Drug List – a resource detailing specific vaccine coverage details, including cost structures and exclusions.  Failure to properly utilize this resource could result in unmet coverage requirements.
