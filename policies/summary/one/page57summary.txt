Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits including physician services, diagnostic tests, and surgical treatments. Coverage is primarily for Medicare-covered telehealth, with copays ranging from $0 to $350, dependent on setting. Prior authorization is required for oral cancer drugs and medications, with a $0 or $350 copay.

Podiatry services are covered at a $15 copayment per Medicare-covered visit, while prostate cancer screening exams are covered with no cost-sharing. Prosthetic and orthotic devices require prior authorization.

New information includes coverage for cataract removal or cataract surgery, and screening for alcohol misuse (including pregnant women). Lung cancer screening with LDCT is covered for eligible members aged 50-77 who have a history of smoking and receive an order for LDCT.  The Medicare-covered counseling for STIs is now included, with no coinsurance, copayment, or deductible.
