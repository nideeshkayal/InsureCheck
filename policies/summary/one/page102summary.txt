Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans provide coverage decisions for medical services, requiring a formal process for requests.  A fast appeal is initiated within 72 hours after receiving an appeal, and a fast appeal is permitted if a Medicare Part B prescription drug denial is made.  The plan will respond within 14 calendar days for Part B prescriptions or 72 hours for other medical services.

If a fast appeal is requested, the plan will respond within 72 hours, and a fast appeal is permitted if the initial denial is for a Medicare Part B prescription drug. Appeals are initiated when a denial is made regarding medical care decisions, requiring a step-by-step process.

The plan will provide a written explanation of the decision, potentially requesting additional information or contacting you or your doctor.  Deadlines for standard appeals are 30 calendar days after receiving the appeal, and Medicare Part B prescription drug requests have a 7 calendar day deadline.

The independent review organization (IRE) is an independent entity overseen by Medicare that reviews appeals.  The IRE conducts a careful evaluation of the case file and will determine whether to modify the decision.  Fees may be charged for copying and sending case files.
