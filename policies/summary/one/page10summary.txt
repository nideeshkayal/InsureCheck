Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members face a crucial aspect of their coverage: the “Evidence of Coverage” document. This document outlines penalties for not having creditable prescription drug coverage, including a penalty percentage based on months without coverage. The penalty is calculated as 1% per month of unfulfilled coverage, with a maximum penalty of 14% for 63+ months without coverage. Medicare determines the average monthly premium for Medicare drug plans, which is currently $36.78.

A key element is the “Part D Late Enrollment Penalty,” which applies if you don't have coverage after your initial enrollment. The penalty amount is calculated using your modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) as reported on your IRS tax return. Social Security will remit a letter informing you of the extra amount owed, requiring payment to avoid disen enrollment. Failure to pay this penalty results in loss of prescription drug coverage.
