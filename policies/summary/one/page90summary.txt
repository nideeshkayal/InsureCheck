**CHAPTER 8: Your Rights and Responsibilities**

This chapter outlines your rights and responsibilities regarding Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement.  As a patient, you are responsible for accurately completing your reimbursement request, detailing the specific service or drug sought, and providing supporting documentation.

A 12-month timeframe applies to Part C and Part D claims, requiring timely submission. Cigna will evaluate your request based on medical appropriateness and cost, considering both cash pricing and negotiated rates.  Denial of a request triggers a written explanation outlining the reasons for rejection, followed by an appeal process.

Crucially, Cigna’s decision hinges on the medical necessity and financial cost of the requested service.  Failure to adhere to established guidelines will result in denial, leading to a letter detailing the rationale and appeal opportunity.  Always carefully review the policy’s rules and deadlines to ensure compliance.
