Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Coverage includes provider-initiated prescriptions, valid state law, network pharmacy use, and medically accepted indications. The Drug List is crucial for utilizing benefits, defining which drugs are covered, and ensuring reimbursement.

The plan provides long-term supply options via retail pharmacies offering preferred cost-sharing or mail-order programs.  Generics and biosimilars are interchangeable and cost less than brand-name drugs, offering alternatives for many brand-name drugs.  A cost-sharing tier system exists, with higher tiers increasing cost. The Drug List includes 5 cost-sharing tiers, with the lowest tier being preferred generic drugs.

To determine coverage, users must consult the Drug List, visit the website, or contact Customer Service.  The Drug List is updated regularly and includes additional drugs not listed.
