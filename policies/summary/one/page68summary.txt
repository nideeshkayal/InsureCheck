Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D prescription drug coverage offers $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Coverage includes provider-initiated prescriptions, valid state law, network pharmacy use (preferred cost sharing), and medically accepted indications. The Drug List is crucial for utilizing benefits, defining which drugs are covered.

Specifically, the plan provides long-term supply options via two methods: retail pharmacies offering preferred cost-sharing or mail-order programs.  The plan covers out-of-network prescriptions when a network pharmacy is unavailable, and it covers prescriptions for covered Part D drugs if not using a network.  It’s vital to check with Customer Service for pharmacy location information and to review the Drug List for coverage requirements and exclusions, particularly regarding reimbursement.
