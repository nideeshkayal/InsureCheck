Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics. Prior authorization is required for certain medical nutrition therapy, particularly for individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant, ensuring patient care. The plan covers preventive obesity screening and therapy, offering counseling and coordination with primary care. Outpatient mental health care is covered through a state-licensed psychiatrist or doctor, including psychotherapy, telehealth, and nurse practitioner services.  Outpatient rehabilitation services include physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy, with copayments for Medicare-covered individuals or groups. Outpatient substance use disorder services are covered, including Partial Hospitalization Program and outpatient therapy, with copayments for Medicare-covered individuals or groups. Prior authorization may be required for procedures like oral cancer drugs and medication therapies, with $0 or $350 copays depending on the setting.
