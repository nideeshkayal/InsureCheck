Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement policy emphasizes provider adherence to established guidelines for timely access to covered services and drugs. Providers are obligated to ensure timely access to PCPs within the network and to provide services, including telemedicine for women’s health specialists. The policy defines coverage for medications, medical procedures, and devices, outlining specific provider qualifications and network agreements. Key rights include accessing medical records, requesting copies, and understanding coverage details, including exclusions and risk assessments. Crucially, the policy details the right to refuse treatment, including the option to leave a medical facility, and mandates providing clear explanations for denied coverage.
