Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers termination pathways triggered by relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change. Termination occurs upon member relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change, as mandated by Cigna Healthcare. Violations, including incorrect information or disrupting care, can trigger an Inspector General investigation and potential disenrollment.

Medicare requires explicit authorization for termination. The plan must provide a written reason. Members are advised to contact Customer Service for detailed termination policies and procedures. This document details the rights and responsibilities of Cigna Healthcare regarding this process. It encompasses the legal framework governing termination, including the Social Security Act, CMS regulations, subrogation rights, and lien requirements, ensuring compliance with federal laws and the Medicare Secondary Payer rules.
