**2025 Evidence of Coverage for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO)**

Upon leaving the plan and returning to Original Medicare, the five-year cycle begins anew, regardless of continued enrollment with the same provider.  The initial rent period is 36 months, followed by 24 months of equipment and maintenance provided by the oxygen supplier, with subsequent copayments for oxygen payments.

The plan offers limited coverage for Durable Medical Equipment (DME), with ownership rules: members own certain items like prosthetics, but rental is required for others (e.g., oxygen equipment). Leaving the plan results in a return of the DME item, requiring a new cycle of payments.  This cycle restarts with the initial rent period, even after continued enrollment with the same provider.
