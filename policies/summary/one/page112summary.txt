The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for appeals, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions.  Upon denial, a letter mandates a Level 3 appeal, requiring a detailed discharge date explanation.  If denied, a Level 1 appeal is required.

The Notice – a critical document – details the plan’s coverage requirements, including required documentation and reporting concerns. Signing the notice does not signify agreement to a discharge date. The QIO reviews appeals, assessing medical appropriateness and hospital discharge dates, verifying appropriate Medicare service.

If the QIO finds no medical justification for discharge, coverage ends at noon on the day after the appeal. If the QIO decides yes, coverage continues for as long as it’s medically necessary.  If the QIO says no, they uphold the initial decision, and the notice details the required steps for a Level 2 appeal.
