**Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members should contact Virginia Medicaid at 1-833-5CALLVA to understand Medicare’s “Extra Help” program.** This program assists individuals with limited incomes by covering prescription drug premiums, yearly deductibles, and prescription copays. Eligibility is based on income and resources, and “Extra Help” covers monthly premiums for Medicare drug plans. Required documents include Medicaid card details, verification calls, state document confirmation, and electronic enrollment files. Members must contact Customer Service to request assistance or provide documentation for accurate copayment level verification.

**Key details:**

*   **Coverage:** Verify coverage and limits.
*   **Terms:** HCBS eligibility required (state notice, approved HCBS plan, authorization letters, and documentation).
*   **Important:** Medicare Part D drug qualifying for VA MAP assistance.
*   **Requirements:** Proof of state residency, HIV status, low income, and uninsured/under-insured status.  Notify ADAP enrollment worker.
