Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics. Coverage extends to inpatient mental health care, with a 190-day lifetime limit and $320 daily copay for the first 6 days, $0 for the next 90, and $0 per lifetime reserve day.

Crucially, the page emphasizes the necessity of a formal inpatient order and prior authorization for medical nutrition therapy. This benefits individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant, requiring 3 hours of counseling per year.

Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage is available for drugs covered through Part B, including injectable drugs, insulin, and durable medical equipment. Prior authorization may be required for certain procedures.  The plan offers no coinsurance, copayment, or deductible for eligible Medicare-covered medical nutrition therapy services, and outlines limitations for chemotherapy/radiation drugs.
