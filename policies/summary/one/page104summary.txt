Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans offer coverage decisions for medical services, subject to a 72-hour appeal window. The Independent Review Organization (IRE) reviews appeals, potentially taking up to 14 calendar days.  The IRE’s decision determines coverage or denial.  If approved, the plan must authorize or provide services within 72 hours.  If denied, the plan sends a letter explaining the decision and provides a Level 3 appeal option.

Key considerations include reimbursement processes: requesting reimbursement involves a coverage decision check and adherence to rules. The plan will send payment within 30 days of the request.  Part D prescription drug coverage decisions and appeals are crucial.  The section defines coverage determination, an initial decision about benefits and payment amounts, and outlines exceptions for drug requests – including approvals, waivers, and cost-sharing adjustments.  The IRE processes drug requests through specific sections, emphasizing formulary exceptions for drugs not on the plan’s list.
