Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage includes comprehensive medical benefits for cardiovascular disease, with coverage for 60 months for all women, with no copays, copayment, or deductible. Chiropractic services are covered with a $20 copay. Medicare-covered virtual therapy is $15/session.

Chiropractic services are covered with a $20 copay prior to authorization. New policy enhancements include expanded cardiac rehab and intensive rehab programs, with prior authorization ($1 visit per year).

Dental services are covered with $15 copay for Medicare-covered services, reimbursement to a Cigna Dental Allowance network provider.  Coverage for routine dental services is expanded beyond Original Medicare, with a Dental Allowance. Cosmetic procedures are not covered.

Depression screening is covered per year with no coinsurance, copayment, or deductible. Diabetes screening is covered with no coinsurance, copayment, or deductible.
