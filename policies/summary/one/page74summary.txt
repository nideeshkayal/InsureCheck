Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers enhanced drug coverage for eligible prescriptions. The plan prioritizes coverage for certain drugs, including non-prescription medications, fertility drugs, cough and cold remedies, sexual health medications, and treatments for anorexia/weight loss. Notably, off-label use is prohibited, and coverage is limited to certain drugs listed on the Drug List. The plan includes an “Enhanced Drug Coverage” option for select erectile dysfunction drugs with quantity limitations and additional coverage for certain medications, requiring an additional premium.  Expedited drug reviews are conducted to ensure compliance with Medicare requirements.  Important details regarding eligibility, exclusions, and the Catastrophic Coverage Stage are outlined.  Furthermore, the plan emphasizes the importance of verifying membership information and contacting customer service for assistance with prescription refills, reimbursement for unused medications, and potential Medicaid coverage.
