Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement policy mandates meticulous billing and reimbursement of services, requiring providers to adhere to established guidelines for accurate claims processing.  The plan’s core obligation is to ensure timely access to covered services and drugs for members, prioritizing timely access to PCPs within the network.  Providers must ensure timely access to PCP’s office and timely services are provided.

Crucially, Cigna provides direct access to women’s health specialists via telemedicine, including translation services and braille formats. Failure to comply results in denial, demanding detailed explanations and appeal opportunities.  A grievance process is available for individuals experiencing challenges receiving information.

The policy also includes a dedicated opportunity for female members to access direct specialist access for women’s health services.  Cigna Healthcare’s evaluation of new technologies focuses on evaluating medications, medical procedures, and devices to benefit customers, utilizing evidence-based medicine and pharmacoeconomic studies.  Privacy is protected through legally mandated regulations regarding medical record access and confidentiality.
