Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans provide coverage decisions for medical services, requiring a formal process for requests.  A fast coverage decision is initiated when a request for medical items or services is made, subject to specific criteria: requesting only essential items, obtaining necessary approvals, and avoiding harm.  The plan will respond within 14 calendar days for Part B prescriptions or 72 hours for other medical services, with an exception for Medicare Part B.

If a fast coverage decision is requested, the plan will respond within 72 hours, and a fast appeal is permitted if the initial denial is for a Medicare Part B prescription drug. Appeals are initiated when a denial is made regarding medical care decisions, requiring a step-by-step process.

Step 1: Determine if a standard or fast appeal is needed.  A fast appeal is generally initiated within 72 hours for medical items or services.

Step 2: Submit your appeal to the plan, following the specified process.

Step 3: The plan reviews the appeal, considering rules and potential delays. 

Step 4:  If the appeal is denied, you can appeal.

The plan will provide a written explanation of the decision, and may request additional information, potentially contacting you or your doctor.
