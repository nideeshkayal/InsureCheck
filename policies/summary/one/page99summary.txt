The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan offers coverage decisions for medical services, requiring a formal process involving requesting coverage and appealing if necessary.  Level 1 appeals are initiated for incomplete requests, while Level 2 appeals are triggered when necessary.  A crucial step is utilizing Customer Service for assistance and requesting a Representation of Representatives form to designate a person to act on your behalf.  This form allows for a representative to make requests and is essential for requesting a coverage decision.  It’s also important to understand the different types of coverage decisions and appeals, with specific rules for Part B prescription drugs.  Refer to Section 5: Your Medical Care to learn how to request coverage decisions and appeal a coverage decision, particularly for medical services.
