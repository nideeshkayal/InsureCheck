Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) reimbursement requests require a formal process. Patients must submit written requests detailing the requested service or drug, providing supporting documentation. A 12-month timeframe applies to Part C and Part D claims, necessitating timely submission.

Cigna will assess requests based on medical appropriateness and cost, considering cash pricing versus negotiated prices.  If the request is denied, the policy outlines a letter explaining the reasoning and right to appeal.

The policy mandates a decision based on the medical service's coverage, with potential for denial if rules are not followed.  If a denial occurs, a letter is sent detailing the reasons, and an appeal process is initiated, including detailed procedures and deadlines.
