Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D prescription drug coverage offers $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Coverage includes provider-initiated prescriptions, valid state law, network pharmacy use (preferred cost sharing may be available), and medically accepted indications. The Drug List is crucial for utilizing benefits.

The plan provides coverage for all Part D drugs on the Drug List, with restrictions on out-of-network pharmacy usage and specialized pharmacy services (home infusion therapy, LTC facility drug supply). Specialized pharmacy access is required for certain prescriptions. Contact Customer Service for assistance when dealing with pharmacy departures from the network.

New prescriptions receive automatic fulfillment from healthcare providers without prior confirmation. Automatic refills require registration, with potential for cancellation before refill dates expire. A refund is available if you’ve used mail-order services in the past.
