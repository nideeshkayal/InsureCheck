Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics.  Medicare covers inpatient mental health care, with a 190-day lifetime limit and $320 daily copay for the first 6 days, $0 for the next 90, and $0 per lifetime reserve day. Crucially, it emphasizes the necessity of a formal inpatient order and prior authorization for medical nutrition therapy, particularly for individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant. Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage is available for drugs covered through Part B, including injectable drugs, insulin, and durable medical equipment.  Prior authorization may be required for certain procedures, including some oral cancer drugs.  Medicare also covers some antigens if a doctor prepares them and a properly instructed person gives them under supervision, and certain oral anti-cancer drugs if the same drug is available in injectable form.  The plan also covers certain home dialysis drugs and some intravenous immune globulin for primary immune deficiency diseases.
