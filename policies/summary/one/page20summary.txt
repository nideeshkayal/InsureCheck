**Medicare Information:**

Medicare is the Federal agency managing the program. Eligible residents 65+ or with disabilities are eligible for Medicare. Social Security determines eligibility and enrollment for Medicare. Contact Social Security for automatic enrollment if you’re not getting Social Security checks.

**Social Security:**

Social Security determines who pays for extra Part D drug coverage due to income. Call Social Security to review the amount or if your income has changed.

**Medicaid:**

Medicaid is a joint Federal-state program helping with medical costs for eligible individuals. Medicare beneficiaries may be eligible for Medicaid, particularly through Medicare Savings Programs like QMB, SLMB, QI, and QDWI.
