Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) covers cardiovascular disease. Coverage includes: 2 diabetes screenings ($125 copay), ER visits ($50,000 max/year), hearing services ($0 copay), hearing aid evaluations ($0 copay), and fitness benefits.

Coverage cycles begin with new coverage and restarts annually. Meals for ESRD customers are $0 copay per year, and home health services are available once per year, contingent on need. Prior authorization is required for medical and social services. HIV screening is once per year, with monthly home-delivered meals for ESRD members. Hospice care is available for terminal prognoses, requiring Medicare-certified programs. Remote monitoring is provided.

Medicare Part A or B services related to terminal prognosis will be paid by Original Medicare. Medicare Part A and Part B services are NOT covered by hospice. Drug coverage is not included. Emergency services are covered with Original Medicare. Non-emergency services are covered with a fee-for-service basis, while emergency services are covered with Original Medicare.
