The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan offers comprehensive medical coverage through a network of providers. Members receive a monthly premium and must utilize providers within the plan’s network for care.  The plan covers a range of medical services, including doctor visits, hospital stays, and emergency care.  Coverage amounts are defined by the plan's cost-sharing structure, with out-of-pocket expenses varying based on service.  Important details include medication coverage, prescription drug access through the Medical Benefits Chart, and rules for non-medical services.  The plan also addresses Medicare, State Health Insurance Assistance Program, Quality Improvement Organization, and Social Security.  Eligibility for group insurance or employer-sponsored coverage is also outlined.
