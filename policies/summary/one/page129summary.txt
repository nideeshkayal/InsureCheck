This chapter details crucial insurance terms related to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) termination pathways. Key provisions include coverage limits for drugs, services, and cost sharing tiers, establishing a “cost sharing tiers” structure impacting plan payment.  “Coverage determination” is critical, defining covered drugs and formulary exceptions.  “Extra Help” is a Medicare program for low-income individuals to cover prescription drug costs, including premiums, deductibles, and coinsurance. Generic Drug coverage defines drugs with the same active ingredient, simplifying drug selection. “Grievance” addresses complaints about plan providers and pharmacies, excluding coverage disputes. “Home Health Aide” refers to personal care services outside of nursing home care. “Institutional Special Needs Plan” (SNP) allows eligible individuals with long-term care needs to receive services, and “Independent Physician Association” (IPA) facilitates coordination among primary care and specialty physicians. “Initial Coverage Stage” outlines the initial eligibility period, while “Institutional Equivalent Special Needs Plan” ensures uniformity in care delivery within the community.
