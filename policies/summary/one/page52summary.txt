Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics.  Prior authorization is required for certain medical nutrition therapy, particularly for individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant, ensuring proper patient care. Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage is available for drugs covered through Part B, including injectable drugs, insulin, and durable medical equipment.

The plan covers preventive obesity screening and therapy, offering counseling and coordination with primary care. Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapies are covered, including X-rays, radiation, surgical supplies, and laboratory tests. Copayments apply for specific services like EKG, colorectal screenings, genetic tests, and blood services.  A separate PCP/Specialist cost share applies for additional services.

Notably, the page emphasizes the crucial distinction between outpatient and inpatient services. If a physician authorizes admission as an inpatient, the individual is considered an outpatient and pays outpatient hospital cost sharing.  Prior authorization is required for certain procedures, including some oral cancer drugs, and may be required for certain medication therapies.
