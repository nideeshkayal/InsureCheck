Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D prescription drug coverage provides $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Coverage includes provider-initiated prescriptions, valid state law, network pharmacy use (preferred cost sharing may be available), and medically accepted indications. The Drug List is crucial for utilizing the plan's benefits.

The plan covers all Part D prescription drugs on the Drug List, with restrictions on out-of-network pharmacy usage and specialized pharmacy services (home infusion therapy, LTC facility drug supply).  Important to note that specialized pharmacy access is required for certain prescriptions. Contact Customer Service for assistance with accessing your benefits, particularly when dealing with a pharmacy leaving the network.
