Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks for medical care. Emergency care is covered, but out-of-network services require Customer Service, including Emergency Care, anytime within 48 hours. Urgent care centers are available under specific circumstances. Refer to Chapter 7 for questions regarding bill payment.

A clinical research study is a way doctors test new medical care. Medicare-approved studies typically involve volunteer participation, requiring approval and a full understanding of the study’s details. If participating, you are responsible for in-network cost sharing for the study’s services.

Prior authorization is required for out-of-network services. Coverage for medically necessary services is defined in Chapter 4. If you pay more than your plan cost-sharing, you are responsible for the remaining cost. Documentation is required for Medicare-approved clinical research studies, which may include benefits beyond the plan’s standard coverage.
