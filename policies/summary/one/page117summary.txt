**Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) – Complaint Process**

This document outlines the process for addressing complaints related to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) insurance coverage decisions and appeals. A judge at the Federal District Court reviews appeals, with no further appeal levels after the Federal District Court.

**Making Complaints**

Individuals can file complaints regarding quality of care, waiting times, customer service, or other concerns. The complaint process is limited to specific problem types, including issues related to medical care, timeliness (coverage decisions and appeals), and related issues.

**Level 5 Appeal**

A judge will review the appeal, concluding whether to grant or deny the request. The Federal District Court’s role is to finalize the decision, without further appeal levels.

**Important Considerations:**

*   The complaint process is specific to certain problems; quality of care, waiting times, and customer service are key.
*   Requests for coverage decisions or appeals trigger a complaint.
*   If the review finds no medical justification, coverage ends at noon after the appeal.
*   If a decision is favorable, continued coverage is permitted, subject to responsibilities.
