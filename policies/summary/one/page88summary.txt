Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers patient responsibility for healthcare costs, with a tiered system impacting reimbursement. Vaccines necessitate full coverage, while others have reduced reimbursement based on coinsurance or copayments. Requests for reimbursement are triggered by out-of-network pharmacy usage, provider billing discrepancies, or retroactive enrollment. The Drug List is crucial for ensuring appropriate utilization and avoiding unmet coverage requirements.

The current page details how to request reimbursement, including submitting a written request, providing documentation, and adhering to a 12-month timeframe for Part C and Part D claims.  The policy mandates that Cigna assess requests and make a coverage decision based on the medical service or drug’s appropriateness and cost. The plan considers the cash price versus negotiated prices to determine the final reimbursement amount.
