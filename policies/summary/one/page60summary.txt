Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, now expanding to include enhanced care for individuals experiencing chronic conditions. Key changes include increased coverage for kidney disease, including dialysis, home dialysis equipment, and education, with a $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days.  Prior authorization is required for renal dialysis, and transportation benefits are provided at $10/visit.

The plan introduces a new transportation vendor partnership with rideshare services, ensuring ambulatory customers receive transport.  Travel distances exceeding 70 miles per trip incur a $10 copayment, and the transportation vendor is responsible for the entirety of the travel.  Urgent medical services are covered worldwide, with a $0 copayment for immediate care, $30 copayment for emergencies & urgent coverage and $50,000 limit per year for global emergency services, requiring medical necessity. Medicare-covered vision care comes with a separate cost share with the PCP/Specialist.
