Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers enhanced medical benefits for chronic condition patients. **The plan provides $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days.** New for 2025, **transportation services are introduced with $10 copays for ambulatory customers.**  Glaucoma screening is now annually with a $0 copayment for immediate care, $30 for emergencies & urgent coverage, and $50,000 limit per year for global emergency services. Vision coverage includes a separate cost share with the PCP/Specialist, and Medicare-covered vision benefits require separate cost sharing. Routine eye exams are covered annually with a $0 copayment. Eyewear allowance is applied to retail value, and there are limitations on the number of covered services.  **The plan does not cover both eyeglass lenses/frames and contacts.**  Careful attention is paid to exclusions related to medical necessity, remote examinations, and cost sharing, ensuring compliance with regulations.
