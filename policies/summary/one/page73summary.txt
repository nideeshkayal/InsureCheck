Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers $0/day copays for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days. Medication coverage is tiered, increasing cost with higher tiers, and eligibility is based on Drug List verification. Temporary supplies are available for emergencies, with a 30-day supply for prescription writing. Changes to the Drug List trigger immediate updates, including additions and removals of drugs and cost-sharing tiers.

The plan may adjust drug lists based on Medicare requirements. Changes to drug coverage are communicated through website updates and regular Drug List revisions.  Specifically, changes to the Drug List affect the provision of medications, potentially removing drugs, moving them to different tiers, or adding restrictions.

Drug eligibility is dependent on Drug List verification and prior authorization for specific conditions.  For example, some drugs are withdrawn from the market. The plan will notify you at least 30 days before any change affecting your prescription, and you’ll need to discuss options with your prescriber regarding coverage decisions, including exceptions.  Drugs not covered include those covering Medicare Part A or Part B, or purchased outside the US.
