**Summary**

This chapter details critical insurance terms related to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) termination pathways. Crucially, termination triggers relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change, necessitating full repayment of benefits, including third-party payments.  The Social Security Act, CMS regulations, subrogation rights, and lien requirements govern this process.  Members are obligated to fully repay benefits, including all applicable third-party payments.

Subrogation and reimbursement rights are prioritized over other claims, directly impacting plan payment.  Reporting fraud, waste, or abuse requires immediate reporting to the Compliance and Ethics Hotline at 1-800-472-8348 – anonymously.

The chapter emphasizes the importance of compliance with federal laws and Medicare Secondary Payer rules, establishing these obligations for effective insurance practice.
