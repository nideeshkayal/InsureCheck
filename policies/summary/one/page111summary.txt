The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Independent Review Organization (IRE) for drug requests, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions.  Immediately notifying the IRE of any denial is crucial, with a 72-hour deadline for appeals and a 7-calendar-day deadline for standard appeals. The IRE’s decision dictates coverage amounts, which are determined by approval.  If denied, a letter explains the decision, offering a Level 3 appeal.

The page emphasizes a vital document – the Notice – which documents your rights regarding Medicare-covered services, including required documentation and reporting concerns. Signing the notice does not signify agreement to a discharge date. The Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) reviews appeals, examining whether the planned discharge date is medically appropriate.  The QIO assesses hospital discharge dates for Medicare beneficiaries, verifying appropriateness through review of medical information and doctor consultations.  Contact the QIO via phone or Customer Service for immediate review and a Detailed Notice of Discharge.
