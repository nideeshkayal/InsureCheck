Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are covered under this plan with drug payments. Drug payments are determined by the plan’s cost-sharing structure, and members must closely monitor these costs.

The “Part D Drug Coverage” section details payment calculations, specifying how each member’s share is determined based on their plan’s cost-sharing.  This includes defined cost-sharing percentages.

Crucially, the page highlights the importance of reviewing eligibility to terminate your membership.  Failure to do so can result in policy discontinuation.  This is a vital step to maintain your coverage.
