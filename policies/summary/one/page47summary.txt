Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers cardiovascular disease coverage. Coverage amounts are $125 copay, $50,000 max/year, $0 copay, $0 copay, and $0.05 transportation and lodging. Key terms include annual restarts, meals for ESRD customers ($0 copay/year), home health services (once/year, need-based), prior authorization for medical & social services, HIV screening (annual, monthly meals), hospice care for terminal prognoses (Medicare-certified programs), remote monitoring, and drug coverage is not included. Emergency services are covered with Original Medicare. Non-emergency services are fee-for-service.  Transferring medical equipment requires Medicare-approved transplant centers.
