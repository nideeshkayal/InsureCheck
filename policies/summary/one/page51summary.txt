Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) provides comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, surgical treatments, and prosthetics.  It emphasizes the necessity of a formal inpatient order and prior authorization for medical nutrition therapy, particularly for individuals with diabetes, renal disease, or after kidney transplant. Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage is available for drugs covered through Part B, including injectable drugs, insulin, and durable medical equipment. Prior authorization may be required for certain procedures, including some oral cancer drugs.

The plan covers preventive obesity screening and therapy, offering counseling and coordination with primary care.  Opioid treatment program services are offered for members with OUD, including medication-assisted treatment, counseling, toxicology testing, and intake activities. Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapies are covered, including X-rays, radiation, surgical supplies, and laboratory tests.  Copayments apply for specific services like EKG, colorectal screenings, genetic tests, blood services, and radiology tests.  A separate PCP/Specialist cost share applies for additional services.
