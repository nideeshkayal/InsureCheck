Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits, including physician services, diagnostic tests, and surgical treatments. Coverage is primarily for Medicare-covered telehealth services utilizing a smartphone, telephone, or computer, with copays ranging from $0 to $350 depending on the setting. Prior authorization is required for procedures like oral cancer drugs and medications, with a $0 or $350 copay setting.

The page details the evaluation process, including consultation with a physician by phone, internet, or electronic health record, and second opinion by another network provider prior to surgery. Medicare covers services provided by other health providers, such as physician assistants, nurse practitioners, and psychologists.  Podiatry services are covered with a $15 copayment for each Medicare-covered visit, while prostate cancer screening exams are covered with no cost-sharing. Prosthetic and orthotic devices and related supplies require prior authorization.
