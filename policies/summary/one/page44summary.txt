Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers medical benefits for cardiovascular disease. Coverage includes up to two diabetes screenings per year. Emergency care is worldwide with a $125 copay for Medicare-eligible ER visits and $50,000 max/year for outside U.S. ER.

The 5-year cycle begins with new coverage, and coverage restarts. Observation services are for emergencies, and DME provides wheelchairs, crutches, and IV infusion pumps with a $0 copay for Medicare-covered items and $0 copay for Medicare-covered training. Fitness benefits include a standard network, workout videos, coaching, and digital resources. Non-standard services are excluded and require additional fees.

Coverage includes routine hearing services: one hearing exam per year, fitting evaluation, and hearing aid evaluations with a $0 copayment for one. Hearing aid devices are external use only; they must be initiated by the hearing vendor.

For HIV screening, coverage is once every 12 months. Home-delivered meals are available to members released from hospitals/nursing homes with a $0 copay.
