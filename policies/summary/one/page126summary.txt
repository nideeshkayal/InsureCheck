**SUMMARY**

This chapter defines critical insurance terms pertaining to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) – specifically, termination pathways.  Termination is triggered by relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change, as dictated by Cigna Healthcare’s regulations. This action disrupts care and triggers an Inspector General investigation.

Crucially, the termination process is governed by the Social Security Act, CMS regulations, subrogation rights, and lien requirements.  Members are obligated to fully repay benefits, including all applicable third-party payments.

Subrogation and reimbursement rights are prioritized over other claims, with payment directed directly to the plan.  Reporting fraud, waste, or abuse requires immediate reporting to the Compliance and Ethics Hotline at 1-800-472-8348 – reporting anonymously. This chapter establishes these obligations, ensuring compliance with federal laws and Medicare Secondary Payer rules.
