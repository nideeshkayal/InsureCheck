Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans offer medical coverage decisions. The Independent Review Organization (IRE) reviews appeals up to 14 days. Approval means authorization or services within 72 hours. Denial results in a letter explaining the decision and a Level 3 appeal.

Key details include reimbursement: coverage decision check & rules. Payments are sent within 30 days of requests. Part D prescription drug decisions and appeals are crucial. The IRE processes drug requests through specific sections, emphasizing formulary exceptions for drugs not on the plan’s list.

Request an exception if a drug isn’t covered. Exception types include: covering a Part D drug for you that’s not on our Drug List, removing restrictions for a covered drug, changing coverage to a lower cost-sharing tier, or covering alternative drugs.
