The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the independent review organization (IRE) for drug requests, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions.  A critical step involves immediately notifying the IRE of any denial, with a 72-hour deadline for appeals and a 7-calendar-day deadline for standard appeals.  The IRE’s decision dictates coverage amounts, which are determined by the review’s approval.  If the IRE denies a request, a letter explains the decision, allowing a Level 3 appeal if insufficient coverage is found.
