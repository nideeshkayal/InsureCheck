Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) provides comprehensive medical coverage through a network of providers.  Members receive a monthly premium and utilize providers within the network for care. Coverage amounts are defined by the plan’s cost-sharing structure, varying based on service. Key changes include a new section addressing drug payments, including potential cost increases due to drug safety measures.  A crucial addition is the “What if your coverage changes” section, emphasizing the need to monitor drug payments.  The “Part D Drug Coverage” section details how payments are determined.  The plan also covers Medicare, State Health Insurance Assistance Program, Quality Improvement Organization, and Social Security.  Eligibility includes group insurance and employer-sponsored coverage.  The “Asking us to pay our share” section highlights specific situations where members should seek payment.  Finally, the page discusses ending your membership in the plan.
