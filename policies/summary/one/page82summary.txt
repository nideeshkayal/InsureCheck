Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage tiers are designed to manage cost. The plan offers five tiers – Lowest Cost Sharing, Preferred Generic, Preferred Brand, Non-Preferred, and Specialty – each with varying copayments and cost-sharing requirements.

**Key Points:** A one-month supply of a covered Part D prescription drug costs up to $35 regardless of the tier.  The “Standard Retail” cost sharing is up to 30 days, while “Preferred Retail” is up to 30 days. 

**Important Considerations:** The plan includes limitations on out-of-network pharmacies – coverage is limited to certain situations.  Specifically, the “Long-Term Care” (LTC) cost sharing tier, up to 31 days, is a significant cost sharing.  It’s crucial to review the full Chapter 5 for details on cost-sharing for Part D vaccines, as it details how coverage changes for specific vaccines.
