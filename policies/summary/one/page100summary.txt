The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan offers coverage decisions for medical services, requiring a formal process.  Users are required to initiate coverage decisions when experiencing a problem or complaint regarding coverage, including seeking a coverage decision for incomplete requests, a coverage decision for needed medical care, or an appeal.  

The process involves a step-by-step procedure: first, determine whether a standard coverage decision or a fast coverage decision is appropriate.  If a fast coverage decision is sought, it requires meeting specific criteria:  requesting only medical items/services, obtaining the necessary approvals, and avoiding causing serious harm to health.  After requesting coverage, the plan will respond with a letter explaining the standard deadline and the possibility of a fast decision.  User interaction includes calling, writing, or faxing the plan to request coverage.  The plan then considers the request and provides a response within 14 calendar days for Part B prescriptions or 72 hours for other medical services.
