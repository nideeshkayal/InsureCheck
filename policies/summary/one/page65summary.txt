**CHAPTER 5: Prescription Drug Coverage**

Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers Part D prescription drug coverage with a $0/day copay for the first 20 days, $214 for the next 20, and no deductible for remaining days.  Transportation services ($10 copays for ambulatory customers) and vision coverage with a separate cost share for PCP/Specialist and Medicare-covered vision benefits are included.

Key changes include a revised definition of “reasonable and necessary” under Original Medicare, focusing on more specific services covered.  Specifically, the exclusion of specialized medical and surgical procedures, sterilization procedures, and full-time nursing care in the home has been updated.  Additionally, the definition of “reasonable and necessary” has been narrowed to encompass current medical and surgical procedures, reflecting a narrower scope of benefits.  Non-routine dental care remains covered.
