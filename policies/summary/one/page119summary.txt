**Summary:**

Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers a grievance process for resolving coverage-related issues. Initial complaints must be submitted to Cigna Healthcare via phone or written correspondence, initiating a formal “grievance” or “grieved” process.

Critically, a complaint regarding denied fast coverage decisions or appeals triggers a “fast complaint” automatically, requiring expedited resolution.  Furthermore, complaints regarding quality of care are now available for submission to the Quality Improvement Organization.

Additionally, the page details options for submitting complaints directly to Medicare, supplementing the standard process.  The process includes a 30-day standard resolution timeframe, with expedited grievances offering 24 hours and up to 14 additional days.  A final response will detail the denial reason for the claim.
