Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers enhanced drug coverage for eligible prescriptions, prioritizing non-prescription medications. Coverage is restricted to specific drugs on the Drug List and includes limitations for erectile dysfunction drugs with quantity limitations.  The “Enhanced Drug Coverage” option provides quantity limitations and additional coverage for opioid pain medications.

A Drug Management Program (DMP) is implemented to help members safely utilize their prescription opioids and benzodiazepines.  This program involves reviewing prescriptions to ensure appropriate use, limiting access to certain medications, and providing a Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program.  Members with certain medical conditions, such as cancer-related pain or hospice care, are prioritized in the DMP.  Contact Customer Service for questions regarding these programs.
