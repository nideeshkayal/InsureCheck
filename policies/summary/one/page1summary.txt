Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan offers comprehensive health benefits and prescription drug coverage for members of Cigna’s Medicare program. This plan is offered by Cigna HealthcareSM and is available through the provider network.

**Coverage:** The plan provides Medicare Health Benefits and Prescription Drug Coverage.  Coverage includes medical services, prescriptions, and potentially copays/coinsurance.

**Benefits:**  Patients receive access to a wide range of medical and prescription services.  The plan covers eligible medical expenses as defined by Medicare.

**Important Notes:**  This plan’s benefits and conditions are subject to change on January 1, 2026. Formulary, network, and provider information may be updated.  A complaint process exists for dissatisfaction with services or treatment, with advance notice required.  Contact Cigna Customer Service for assistance or further information.
