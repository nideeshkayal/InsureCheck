Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers termination pathways triggered by relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change. Termination occurs upon member relocation, incarceration, or citizenship change, as mandated by Cigna Healthcare. Violations, including incorrect information, disrupt care, trigger an Inspector General investigation, and can lead to disenrollment.  The plan’s termination process is governed by the Social Security Act, CMS regulations, subrogation rights, and lien requirements, ensuring compliance with federal laws and Medicare Secondary Payer rules.  Members are obligated to cooperate and fully repay benefits, including any applicable third-party payments, directly to the plan. Subrogation and reimbursement rights are prioritized over other claims, with the highest priority being payment to the plan. Reporting fraud, waste, or abuse requires immediate reporting to the Compliance and Ethics Hotline at 1-800-472-8348, and the hotline operates anonymously.
