The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the independent review organization (IRE) to review drug requests, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions. The IRE’s role is to determine if the initial decision is correct or requires modification, overseen by Medicare.

A key step involves contacting the IRE immediately if a denial occurs – the IRE will provide instructions on Level 2 appeals and the deadline for answering the request. Fast appeals require a response within 72 hours of the request, while standard appeals have deadlines of 7 calendar days.

The IRE’s decision impacts coverage amounts; coverage is determined based on the review’s approval. If the IRE denies the request, a letter explains the decision, outlining the right to a Level 3 appeal if the drug coverage is insufficient. The IRE must notify you of the decision, including the right to a Level 3 appeal.
