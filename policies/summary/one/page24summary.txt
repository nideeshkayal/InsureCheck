**Summary:**

This document outlines crucial coverage details for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members. Verify coverage and limits, essential for understanding potential benefits. HCBS eligibility is required, necessitating state notice, approved HCBS plan, authorization letters, and documentation.
