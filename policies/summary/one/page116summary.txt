The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for appeals. Level 1 appeals are mandatory for denial decisions, requiring expedited review. The QIO conducts an independent review of the case, examining medical information and provider opinions to determine coverage continuation.

If the QIO finds no medical justification, coverage ends at noon on the day after the appeal. If the QIO decides coverage continues, it’s subject to five levels of appeal, with a maximum duration of five. A fast-track appeal is available for specific services like Home health, skilled nursing, and CORF.

If the review results in a denial, the Quality Improvement Organization provides a Detailed Explanation of Non-Coverage outlining the reasons for ending coverage. If the review is favorable, continued coverage is permitted with costs remaining the insured’s responsibility, potentially with limitations.

If the review organization says no, it means they agree with the decision, necessitating a decision to take further appeals – three levels exist (Level 3, 4, and 5) following Level 2, and further appeal may be needed if the dollar value exceeds a minimum threshold.
