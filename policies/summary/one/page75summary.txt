Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Part D offers enhanced drug coverage for eligible prescriptions, prioritizing non-prescription medications, fertility drugs, cough and cold remedies, sexual health medications, and anorexia/weight loss treatments. Off-label use is prohibited, with coverage limited to certain drugs on the Drug List. The plan includes an “Enhanced Drug Coverage” option for erectile dysfunction drugs with quantity limitations and additional coverage for certain medications.

Expedited drug reviews are conducted to ensure compliance. Key considerations include eligibility, exclusions, and the Catastrophic Coverage Stage. Medicare-certified hospice patients require specific notification from their hospice provider regarding drug coverage, especially for related conditions.

Drug safety reviews are performed regularly to identify potential errors, unnecessary drugs, unsafe combinations, and age/gender-related issues. Prioritize reviewing your prescription for potential problems and immediately contact customer service for refills, reimbursement, and Medicaid coverage.
