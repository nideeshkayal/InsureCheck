The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for appeals, prioritizing expedited coverage decisions. If denial occurs, a Level 1 appeal is required, necessitating a detailed discharge date explanation. The QIO reviews appeals to determine medical appropriateness and hospital discharge dates, verifying Medicare service.

If the QIO finds no medical justification for discharge, coverage ends at noon on the day after the appeal. If the QIO decides yes, coverage continues for as long as medically necessary, with a maximum duration of five levels of appeal.  A fast-track appeal is available for three specific services: Home health care, skilled nursing facility care, and CORF services.  Requests for a faster appeal must be submitted in writing with a specific timeframe and require the signer’s signature.  The process involves following established deadlines and seeking assistance when needed.
