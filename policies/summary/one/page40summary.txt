**Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Coverage Summary**

This page details comprehensive coverage for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage. Key stipulations include:

*   **Cardiovascular Disease Testing:** Coverage for all women is 60 months, with no copays, copayment, or deductible. Chiropractic services are covered with a $20 copay.
*   **Colorectal Cancer Screening:** Coverage includes colonoscopy (120 months), flexible sigmoidoscopy (120 months), screening fecal-occult blood tests (12 months), multi-target stool DNA tests (3 years), and blood-based biomarkers (3 years) – excluding barium enemas (with coinsurance).
*   **Medicare-Covered Virtual Therapy:** $15 copay for virtual therapy sessions.
*   **Chiropractic Services:** Medicare-covered, with $20 copay for manual manipulation of spine – prior authorization required.
*   **New Policy:**  Enhanced cardiovascular disease risk reduction visit ($10 copay), expanded cardiac rehab, and intensive rehab programs with prior authorization ($1 visit per year).
