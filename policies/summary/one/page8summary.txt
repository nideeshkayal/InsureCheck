Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are subject to Medicare HMO rules, requiring review of the Evidence of Coverage document. Key stipulations include eligibility based on U.S. citizenship or lawful presence, and residency within the designated service area.  Crucially, members must have both Medicare Part A and Part B coverage to remain eligible.

The Evidence of Coverage details contract terms, including formulary changes, enrollment requirements, and potential riders. Medicare annually updates the contract, impacting cost and coverage.  Members must contact Customer Service to determine their area of coverage and address any changes.

The Provider and Pharmacy Directory lists network providers – doctors, medical groups, and durable medical equipment suppliers – essential for receiving medical services.  Unauthorized use of outside providers results in full payment, with exceptions for emergencies, urgent care, dialysis, and out-of-network providers.  Requests for a copy of the Provider and Pharmacy Directory are available electronically or by mail.
