Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members must utilize provider networks for medical care, requiring prior authorization for out-of-network services. Coverage for emergencies outside the U.S. is provided, with ambulance services for potential danger.  The plan covers medical emergencies, with doctors determining stability after the emergency.  Out-of-network care necessitates calling Customer Service, including Emergency Care services, anytime, and within 48 hours.  Emergency care is covered if the provider deems it necessary, but follow-up care is required after the emergency concludes. Urgent care centers are available under specific circumstances, including temporary geographic limitations and unusual circumstances.  Refer to the Provider and Pharmacy Directory (www.CignaMedicare.com/resources) for location information.
