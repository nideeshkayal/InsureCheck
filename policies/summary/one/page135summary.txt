Cigna Healthcare offers comprehensive Medicare Advantage HMO and PPO plans, including eligibility requirements for individuals moving outside the service area or seeking extra support.  The “Special Enrollment Period” allows for expanded coverage options.  A “Quality Improvement Organization” (QIO) and “Rehabilitation Services” provide supplemental coverage for medical device needs, alongside “Step Therapy” and “Supplemental Security Income” (SSI) program components impacting patient care.  Cigna emphasizes Member designation for benefit management.  The Cigna Healthcare Customer Service portal provides contact information, including telephone numbers, language interpreter services, andTTY access.  Information regarding the Virginia Insurance Counseling & Assistance Program (VICAP) is provided, assisting individuals with Medicare-related questions and support, located at https://www.vda.virginia.gov/vicap.htm.
