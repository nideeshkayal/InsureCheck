Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers two membership termination pathways: annual enrollment and Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment.  Members must continue using their plan’s network providers for medical services and prescriptions, adhering to specific rules regarding hospital stays and coverage duration.

Termination triggers upon new Medicare plan enrollment, typically January 1st.  Cigna Healthcare mandates termination if member moves out of the service area, is incarcerated, or becomes a U.S. citizen.  Violations of the membership agreement, such as providing incorrect information or disrupting care, can lead to investigation by the Inspector General and potential disenrollment.

Medicare requires explicit authorization before terminating the membership, and the plan must provide written reasons for termination.  Members are advised to contact Customer Service for further information and guidance.
