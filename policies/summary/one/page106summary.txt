Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plans offer medical coverage decisions, with a standard review process of 72 hours following physician statement.  To obtain a fast coverage decision, patients must meet two criteria: 1) a drug not yet received, and 2) a request without a doctor’s support.  

The IRE reviews drug requests through specific sections, emphasizing formulary exceptions for not-on-list drugs.  If a drug is not covered, exceptions include covering a Part D drug for you that’s not on the plan.  

Requests must be submitted through the plan's website, phone, or fax, including a detailed request with necessary information.  A fast coverage decision is granted if a health condition necessitates it, with a 24-hour deadline for the decision.  If denied, a letter explaining the decision is sent, outlining the standard deadlines.
