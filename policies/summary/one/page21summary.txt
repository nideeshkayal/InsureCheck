Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members should contact Virginia Medicaid at 1-833-5CALLVA to understand Medicare’s “Extra Help” program. This program assists individuals with limited incomes and resources by providing help paying prescription drug premiums, yearly deductibles, and prescription copays. Eligibility is determined by income and resources, with “Extra Help” covering monthly premiums for Medicare drug plans. The program offers assistance for prescription drug costs through the Medicare gov website and Social Security Office.  Documentation required for “Extra Help” includes Medicaid card details, verification calls, state document confirmation, and electronic enrollment files.  Members should contact Customer Service to request assistance or provide required documentation for accurate copayment level verification.
