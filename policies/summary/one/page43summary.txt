Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits for cardiovascular disease, including coverage for up to two diabetes screenings per 12 months.  Emergency care is covered worldwide, with $125 copay for Medicare-eligible emergency room visits and $50,000 maximum per year for outside U.S. emergency care.  

The 5-year cycle begins upon joining or leaving the plan, with new coverage starting over.  Observation services are provided for emergency evaluations, and DME expands with wheelchairs, crutches, and IV infusion pumps, with a $0 copayment for Medicare-covered items and $0 copayment for Medicare-covered training.

Fitness benefits include participation in a standard fitness network, access to workout videos, personalized coaching, and digital resources.  Non-standard services are excluded, requiring additional fees.  Contact the Cigna Healthcare fitness vendor at 1-888-886-1992 for more details.
