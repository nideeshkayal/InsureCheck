**CHAPTER 9: Addressing Problems and Complaints**

This chapter focuses on navigating issues with Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage.  You are responsible for understanding covered services, adhering to prescribed protocols for each service, and promptly informing physicians and providers about your health conditions.

Crucially, you have the right to refuse treatment and terminate medication under specific conditions – subject to liability.  Advance Directives are essential; they document authorized medical decisions, enabling physicians and providers.

The policy covers medical services and medications, but excludes cosmetic treatments.  Moving outside the plan service area can result in membership termination.

You must pay premiums, share of cost for Medicare Part B, and continue to pay a premium for Medicare Part B.  Communication with the Medicare provider is vital for coordinating benefits across different insurance plans.  Understanding your responsibilities is key to ensuring a smooth and compliant experience.
