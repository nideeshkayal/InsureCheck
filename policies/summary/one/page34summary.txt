**Medical Benefits Chart – Summary**

This chart outlines medical benefits for Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO). The initial cycle begins after leaving the plan and returns upon returning to Original Medicare, regardless of continued enrollment.  The initial rent period is 36 months, followed by 24 months of DME maintenance and oxygen supplier payments.

Durable Medical Equipment (DME) coverage is limited. Ownership rules apply to certain items like prosthetics; members own these, but rental is required for others.  Leaving the plan results in a return of the DME item, triggering a new cycle of payments.

Important considerations include exclusions regarding certain medical equipment and limitations on DME ownership. This cycle restarts with the initial rent period, even after continued enrollment with the same provider.
