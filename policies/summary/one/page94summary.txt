**Rights and Responsibilities Regarding Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Coverage**

This document outlines patient rights and responsibilities related to Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) coverage. Patients possess the right to refuse treatment and terminate medication, accepting full liability for consequences.  Advance Directives are crucial; patients can create these documents to authorize medical decisions, specifying who can make decisions on their behalf.  These documents, such as living wills and power of attorney for health care, are legally binding.

The policy mandates providing detailed instructions to doctors regarding medical care, including how to handle decisions if unable to act.  Patients can request copies of these documents and, upon hospitalization, present them to the hospital.  A complaint process is available through Virginia Department of Health if concerns arise.

If a doctor fails to follow instructions, a complaint can be filed, and the hospital is required to treat you fairly.  If rights are violated due to race, disability, or other protected characteristics, contact the Office for Civil Rights.
