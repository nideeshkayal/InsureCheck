**Summary:**

Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members face a critical “Evidence of Coverage” deadline, triggering a 1% penalty per month for unmet coverage. Failure to meet this deadline results in increased penalties for 63+ months, impacting member financial responsibility.

To comply, members must pay the penalty via check, secure system, or Social Security.  Changes to plan records are mandatory, including name, address, and phone number.

Medicare mandates coordination of benefits when other insurance is involved, requiring an annual questionnaire. Coverage typically pays first for services related to each type: No-fault, liability, black lung benefits, and workers' compensation. Medicaid and TRICARE do not pay first for Medicare-covered services.

Contact Customer Service immediately if facing payment difficulties, as the deadline is approaching.  Ensure all necessary documentation is readily available for verification.
