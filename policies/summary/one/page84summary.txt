**Medicare Part D Prescription Drug Coverage**

This page details Medicare Part D’s coverage for prescription drugs, transitioning from the Initial Coverage Stage to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage. The Catastrophic Coverage Stage offers zero out-of-pocket costs for covered Part D drugs.

The plan prioritizes cost savings through preferred retail pharmacies and limitations on out-of-network access. Crucially, coverage for certain drugs, notably those recommended by the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) and offered by pharmacies, is covered under Part B.

Vaccine coverage is structured around three cost components: vaccine cost itself, provider cost for administration, and the provider’s portion of the cost.  Some vaccines are fully covered, while others require a fee.  Payment for Part D vaccinations is variable, potentially requiring reimbursement from the plan. The page emphasizes the importance of understanding vaccine coverage requirements and utilizing the plan’s Drug List for specific details.
