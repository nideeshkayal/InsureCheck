Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers comprehensive medical benefits with a 36-month initial rent and subsequent 24-month DME and oxygen coverage, up to a maximum of $5,800 per year for Medicare Part A and Part B.  Key coverage details include:

*   **Medical Benefits Chart:** Details medical necessity, MA coordinated care plan requirements (90-day transition), and provider network requirements.
*   **Exclusions:** Durable medical equipment ownership rules apply, and balance billing is prohibited.
*   **Medicaid/QMB Eligibility:** Medicaid or QMB eligibility is required for provider proof.
*   **Preventive Services:** Covered preventive services are free, but existing medical conditions require a copayment.

**New Information:** The plan now includes a one-time screening ultrasound for individuals at risk, with a maximum of 12 visits in 90 days, subject to physician referral.  Providers must meet specific requirements, including acupuncture licensure and a current, unrestricted license.  Acupuncture treatment must be discontinued if the patient is not improving or regressing.  A $15 copayment applies for each Medicare-covered virtual therapy session.
