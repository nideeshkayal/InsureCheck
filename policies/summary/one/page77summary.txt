**CHAPTER 6: What You Pay for Your Part D Prescription Drugs**

This section outlines the cost structure for your comprehensive Part D coverage.  The primary focus is on managing your prescription drug expenses.  Current charges for your medications are determined by the Drug List, which contains a curated selection of medications.

The Enhanced Drug Coverage option offers enhanced coverage for specific medications, including non-prescription medications and opioid pain medications.  However, this coverage is subject to quantity limitations.

Importantly, this program includes a Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program for members with specific medical conditions, such as cancer-related pain or hospice care, to ensure appropriate medication use.  **Contact Customer Service immediately** if you have questions about these programs or any related aspects of your Part D plan.  Review your coverage details for specific limitations and exclusions.
