Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members are covered under this plan, which is a Medicare HMO. Cost sharing and provider access differ from Original Medicare, governed by Medicare and private company. This plan offers Qualifying Health Coverage (QHC) and requires review of the Evidence of Coverage document. It details coverage specifics, including the definition of “covered services” and required compliance.

The Evidence of Coverage document outlines the plan’s contract with members, including enrollment details, formulary, and potential riders.  Crucially, the contract is subject to changes by Medicare annually, impacting costs and coverage.  Members must have both Medicare Part A and Part B coverage to remain eligible, and reside within the designated service area.
