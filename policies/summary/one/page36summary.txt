Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) offers a comprehensive medical benefits plan. Coverage begins with a 36-month initial rent and subsequent 24-month DME and oxygen, with a maximum out-of-pocket cost of $5,800 per year for Medicare Part A and Part B.

Key coverage details include:

*   **Medical Benefits Chart:** This chart details covered services, costs, and eligibility requirements for each service. It outlines Medicare-covered services, medical necessity, MA coordinated care plan requirements (minimum 90-day transition), and network provider requirements.
*   **Exclusions:** Durable medical equipment ownership rules apply, and balance billing is prohibited.
*   **Medicaid/QMB Eligibility:** Medicaid or QMB eligibility is required for provider proof.
*   **Preventive Services:** Covered preventive services are free, but existing medical conditions require a copayment.

The plan emphasizes that all services are covered under Original Medicare, with potential additional costs from the plan.
