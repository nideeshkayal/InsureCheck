The Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) plan utilizes the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) for appeals. Level 1 appeals are mandatory for denial decisions, requiring expedited review. The QIO conducts an independent review of the case, examining medical information and provider opinions to determine if coverage should continue.

If the QIO finds no medical justification, coverage ends at noon on the day after the appeal. If the QIO decides coverage continues, it’s subject to five levels of appeal, with a maximum duration of five. A fast-track appeal is available for specific services: Home health, skilled nursing, and CORF.

If the review results in a denial, the Quality Improvement Organization will provide a Detailed Explanation of Non-Coverage outlining the reasons for ending coverage.  If the review is favorable, continued coverage is permitted, with costs remaining the responsibility of the insured.  A Level 2 appeal is permitted if the initial appeal is denied.
