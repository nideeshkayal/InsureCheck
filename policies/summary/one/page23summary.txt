**Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) Members:**  Contact Virginia Medicaid at 1-833-5CALLVA to understand Medicare’s “Extra Help” program, which provides assistance to individuals with limited incomes, covering prescription drug premiums, yearly deductibles, and prescription copays. Eligibility is based on income and resources, and “Extra Help” covers monthly premiums for Medicare drug plans.  Required documents include Medicaid card details, verification calls, state document confirmation, and electronic enrollment files.  Members must contact Customer Service to request assistance or provide documentation for accurate copayment level verification.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Coverage:** Verify coverage and limits – this is a crucial initial step.
*   **Terms:** HCBS eligibility required (state notice, approved HCBS plan, authorization letters, and documentation).
*   **Requirements:** Proof of state residency, HIV status, low income, and uninsured/under-insured status.  Notify ADAP enrollment worker.
*   **Method:** Railroad Retirement Board – Call 1-877-772-5772 for assistance.
