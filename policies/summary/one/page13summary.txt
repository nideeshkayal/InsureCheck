Cigna Preferred Medicare (HMO) members face a critical “Evidence of Coverage” deadline (1% penalty per month) for unmet coverage. Failure results in increased penalties for 63+ months.

To comply, members must pay the penalty via check, secure system, or Social Security. Changes to plan records (name, address, phone) are necessary, and liability claims are essential.

The plan member ID number is required for verification. Contact Customer Service immediately if facing payment difficulties.

Medicare mandates coordination of benefits when other insurance is involved, requiring an annual questionnaire.  Coverage typically pays first for services related to each type: No-fault, liability, black lung benefits, and workers' compensation. Medicaid and TRICARE do not pay first for Medicare-covered services.
