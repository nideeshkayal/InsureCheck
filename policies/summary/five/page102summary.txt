H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers a Group PPO Plan for members aged 65+ or entitled to Medicare benefits. This plan’s primary responsibility remains with Medicare, regardless of employment status. Recovery is permitted for exceeding benefit amounts, including organizations, subject to policy terms.

The Policy remains primary for the first 30 months and then secondary to Medicare. ESRD (Extended Resident Elderly Program) coverage remains the primary responsibility for the first 30 months, based on age and employment.

Eligibility for Medicare benefits is based on age and employment status, and adverse benefit determinations include rescission of coverage, denial, reduction, or termination of benefits, failure to provide or make payment, utilization management program denials, or experimental/investigational services. 

An authorized representative is required to submit a request for external review in case of an adverse benefit determination.  The authorized representative must be designated in writing unless the claim involves an Urgent Care claim, requiring consent from the insured.
