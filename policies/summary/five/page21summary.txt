Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It includes medically necessary services, including prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance. Key coverage details include data breach reporting obligations and restrictions on certain health information, specifically concerning confidential information under Federal laws. Hometown Health maintains medical information of former members; the information is used for various purposes, even after termination, adhering to legal retention requirements. Individuals have rights to inspect and copy medical information, requiring written requests to Hometown Health’s Customer Services.
