Hometown Health’s Provider Relationship Plan offers a comprehensive policy for Hometown Health’s independent contractor providers. **Coverage is defined as excluding prescriptions, investigation drugs, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services.**  The plan’s core focuses on **exclusions:**  These exclusions include pregnancy notification, concurrent review of medical records, retrospective review for authorization, and second opinions for diagnosis uncertainty, treatment response failure, or conflicting test results.  Enrollment requires meeting eligibility requirements, including US citizenship, residency, employment status, and payment obligations. Dependents may enroll if listed on the application, subject to employer eligibility rules.  The plan’s primary objective is to ensure Hometown Health’s control over medical decision-making and outlines conditions for coverage.
