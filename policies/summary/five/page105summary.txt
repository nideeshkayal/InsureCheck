H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers Group PPO Plan for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review. Key policy details include Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, with notification and a response period, including required details of the claim. Notifications are delivered within 48 hours of receipt, but not later than 72 hours after the claim. Concurrent care decisions involve notifying the insured of reduction or termination of coverage. Pre-service claims require notification within a reasonable timeframe, up to 15 days after receipt, with extensions granted for unforeseen circumstances, with notification before the initial 15-day period expires. Informal appeals are permitted within 60 days of adverse determination, with the customer services department recording information including the claimant’s name, complaint details, and appeal date. A 1st level formal appeal is initiated within 180 days of adverse determination.
