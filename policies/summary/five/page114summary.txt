Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan focuses on timely benefit review for 65+ members. It incorporates expedited external reviews for adverse determinations, guaranteeing appeals. New requirements mandate submission of external review requests within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review period for conditions jeopardizing health or function. Authorization is required for all claims.

The EOC supersedes all previous agreements, emphasizing representation rather than guarantees. Clerical errors or delays will not invalidate coverage, but will lead to equitable adjustments to premiums. The EOC, Group Subscription Agreement, and Schedule of Benefits constitute the entire contract. Changes to the EOC must be endorsed by an authorized officer.

Crucially, the plan includes automatic modification to comply with federal and Nevada law. Members legally capable of contracting agree to all terms, and the representative of all members cannot contract. Notice is to be sent to Hometown Health's Customer Services Department at 10315 Professional Circle, Reno, Nevada 89521.
