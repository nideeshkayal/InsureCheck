Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan provides coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday. Coverage includes annual eye exams, lens options (yearly and calendar), and contact lenses, but specific requirements for pediatric vision conditions are necessary. Specialty Pharmaceutical coverage is limited to participating pharmacies and requires a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription. The plan offers a formulary alternative for Hormone Replacement Therapy, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. A maximum cost of $100 for prescription drugs is established.

Coverage for Diabetic Supplies is medically necessary and includes deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Medical, pharmacy, and pediatric vision benefits are excluded for services not covered by the EOC or explicitly listed as covered elsewhere.  Excluded services include not Medically Necessary, complications resulting from procedures, treatment for injury/illness, copies of records, and loss related to a riot or criminal act.
