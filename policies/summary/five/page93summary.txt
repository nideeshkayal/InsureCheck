**Summary**

This H.O.METOWN HEALTH Small Group PPO Plan establishes a constructive trust relationship, ensuring the Member’s funds remain secure for medical expenses incurred due to a third-party’s illness or injury. The policy mandates the Member to maintain funds in a separate account and grants equitable lien on those funds.

Crucially, the policy defines reimbursement obligations to the Member, contingent upon the third party’s payment. This includes reimbursement for all damages – including personal injury, property loss, and medical expenses – immediately upon receiving compensation. The policy also dictates reimbursement obligations even if the Member recovers no-fault benefits, ensuring full coverage.

Furthermore, the policy explicitly requires the Member to serve as a constructive trustee, controlling the funds to the extent the third party pays. The policy outlines notification requirements for any claims and designates representatives to handle claims related to the illness or injury.
