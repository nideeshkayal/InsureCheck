Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. The plan includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases.  Hometown Health manages Prescription Drug benefits, contracted with a Pharmacy Benefit Manager (PBM) for cost-effective management.

The Schedule of Benefits details costs, restrictions, and rights, emphasizing cost-effectiveness. Telehealth or telemedicine services are offered, and Urgent Care Center services are covered with In-Network Copayments and Coinsurance, with potential balance billing for Out-of-Area Non-Participating Providers. Importantly, the policy prioritizes the Member's Out-of-Pocket Maximum.
