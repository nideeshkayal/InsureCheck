H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers Group PPO Plan for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review. The policy mandates Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, with a 48-hour notification period and response requirement. Concurrent care decisions require notification of reduction or termination of coverage. Informal appeals are permitted within 60 days of adverse determination, with customer service recording claimant details.

A formal appeal must include name, address, and telephone number, membership number, and a brief statement of the matter, appeal, and reason. Support documentation – medical records, physician letters, and relevant information – is essential.

Expedited appeals are not available for post-service claims. If the physician requests an expedited appeal, or supports it, the policy will automatically grant it, subject to 15-day delay.  If an appeal is denied, the policy will provide a 15-day response.  Access to relevant documentation is provided, including internal guidelines, to assist in reviewing the adverse benefit determination.
