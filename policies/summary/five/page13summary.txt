Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases, excluding cost-effective interventions and prescription drug costs exceeding allowed limits. Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-sharing for non-preferred providers and denied claims. Criminal activity is covered, and Custodial care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring continued medical personnel.

The Schedule of Benefits outlines costs, restrictions, and rights, with a focus on cost-effectiveness. Telehealth or telemedicine services are offered, and Urgent Care Center services are covered with In-Network Copayments and Coinsurance, with potential balance billing for Out-of-Area Non-Participating Providers.  Urgent Care Center services from Non-Participating Providers in Nevada are covered at the Out-of-Network Cost Sharing rate.  The policy emphasizes the Member’s Out-of-Pocket Maximum.
