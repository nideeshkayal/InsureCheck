**Summary:**

This policy grants underwriting rights to third parties liable for injuries or illnesses, triggering subrogation and reimbursement obligations.  It establishes a right of recovery for damages paid to the third party, contingent on the Member's medical expenses.  Specifically, the policy allows reimbursement when a Member’s injury is caused by a third party’s negligence directly impacting the Member’s policy coverage.

The core of the policy’s structure centers on subrogation.  If a third party’s negligence results in an Illness or Injury, the policy’s subrogation rights are activated, assigning the right to the Member to recover compensation from the third party.  This includes reimbursement for all damages – including personal injury, property loss, and medical expenses – regardless of fault.  Furthermore, the policy defines reimbursement obligations even if the Member recovers no-fault benefits, ensuring full reimbursement. The policy stipulates equitable lien, requiring reimbursement of payments made by the Member against any recovery from the third party.
