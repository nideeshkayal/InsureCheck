Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan offers coverage for children aged 0-19 years, with a defined eligibility period of achieving 19th birthday.  Coverage includes an eye exam annually at no cost to members, lenses for a set yearly and calendar lens count, and contact lenses with specific requirements for conditions.  Specialty Pharmaceutical coverage is limited to participating pharmacies and requires a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription.  Deductible and Out-of-Pocket Maximums are defined, with a maximum cost of $100 for prescription drugs, and the plan includes a formulary alternative for Hormone Replacement Therapy.  Coverage for Diabetic Supplies is medically necessary and includes deductibles, copays, and coinsurance.
