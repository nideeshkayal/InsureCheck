Hometown Health’s Plan excludes prescriptions for conditions like impotence, investigation drugs, and medical treatment. The Formulary Exclusions List specifically restricts rebate programs for pediatric vision coverage, excluding replacements of lenses/contacts, surgical treatments, and contact lens insurance.  Furthermore, it limits reimbursement for “artificially painted or non-prescription lenses,” “additional office visits for contact lens pathology,” and “contact lens modification” – crucial for understanding the scope of coverage limitations. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices, appliances, and services are excluded, as are services performed by a dentist compensated by a facility for similar covered services. Coverage for specialized procedures and techniques is excluded. Costs for Out-of-Network Covered Services exceeding the Allowed Amount are excluded.
