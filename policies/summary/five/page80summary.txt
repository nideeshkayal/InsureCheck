**Small Group PPO Plan Coverage**

This Small Group PPO Plan provides coverage for eligible individuals, triggering upon birth, adoption, or placement. Coverage is contingent on verifying required documentation, including notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, birth certificate, marriage certificate, domestic partnership certificate, qualified medical child support order, court order, proof of residency, and Social Security number.

Changes to enrollment based on application submissions *must* be corrected to avoid coverage loss and financial responsibility. Medicare eligibility may apply, with Medicare primary payer for group plans with fewer than 20 employees.  

Refer to 2. Medicare-Eligible Members and 17, 42 CFR § 411.100(a)(i) for details.
