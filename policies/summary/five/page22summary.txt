**Hometown Health Small Group PPO Plan**

This plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services such as prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance.

**Key Rights and Responsibilities:**

*   **Right to Amend:** Individuals can request amendments to medical information.
*   **Accounting of Disclosures:** A list of disclosures made regarding medical information is required.
*   **Restrictions:** You can request restrictions on medical information use or disclosure.

**Important Considerations:** Hometown Health maintains former member data. This data is used for various purposes, including legal retention requirements. Access to medical information is limited to authorized personnel.
