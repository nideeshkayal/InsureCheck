Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases, excluding cost-effective interventions and prescription drug costs exceeding allowed limits. Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-sharing for non-preferred providers and denied claims. Criminal activity is covered, and Custodial care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring continued medical personnel.

The Schedule of Benefits details costs, restrictions, and rights, emphasizing cost-effectiveness. Telehealth or telemedicine services are offered, and Urgent Care Center services are covered with In-Network Copayments and Coinsurance, with potential balance billing for Out-of-Area Non-Participating Providers. The policy prioritizes the Member's Out-of-Pocket Maximum.
