Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Key coverage includes diabetic management, including infusion pumps and supplies, medication, and equipment for diabetes care, as well as self-management training and education. Coverage is capped at $600 for routine health services and excludes FDA-approved medications or devices. Family Planning provides vasectomy and tubal ligation coverage. Gastric Restrictive Services are covered for obesity/morbid obesity with a maximum of one surgery per lifetime, excluding complications resulting from other illnesses. Genetic Counseling/Testing covers medically necessary genetic disease testing, adhering to standard medical criteria.
