Hometown Health’s Plan excludes prescriptions, investigation drugs, medical treatment, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices are not covered.  Out-of-network services exceed allowed amounts and costs exceeding allowed amounts for inpatient/outpatient surgery are not covered. Concurrent review and case management follows admission to a facility, requiring notification of pregnancy for benefit management.  Discharge planning begins immediately upon post-discharge needs identification.  The plan requires notification of pregnancy for benefit management.  Retrospective review evaluates medical records for authorization, Prior Authorization, and concurrent review.  Second opinions are authorized upon request, including scenarios involving diagnosis uncertainty, treatment response failure, or conflicting test results.
