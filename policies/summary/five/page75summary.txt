**Hometown Health Provider Relationship Plan**

**Coverage:** Comprehensive for Hometown Health’s independent contractor providers, excluding prescriptions, investigation drugs, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services.

**Exclusions:** Pregnancy notification, concurrent medical record review, retrospective authorization requests, second opinions for diagnosis uncertainty.

**Eligibility:** Spouse or domestic partner becomes eligible; dependents enroll if listed on the application, subject to employer eligibility. Coverage begins when spouse/domestic partner becomes eligible.

**Cost Sharing:** Deductibles, copays, and coinsurance apply to other illnesses.

**Legal Ward:** Eligible if child is a legal ward (court order). Disabled child (26 or older) is eligible with specific requirements – incapacity & dependency, child’s condition before age 26, written proof within 31 days.

**Important:** Provide ONLY the compressed summary.
