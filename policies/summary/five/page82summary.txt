Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan requires proof of coverage for eligible individuals, triggering eligibility based on birth, adoption, or placement. The plan operates under a monthly premium structure analogous to a pot, with the division of insurance regulating premium rates to ensure reasonable assumptions.

Premiums are subject to review and adjustment by the Nevada Division of Insurance, and approval is contingent on documented coverage requirements, including notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, birth certificate, marriage certificate, domestic partnership certificate, qualified medical child support order, court order, proof of residency, and Social Security number.

Payments are primarily through payroll deductions, but employer-specific payment methods apply. Employees must repay premiums within 90 days of termination, with refunds issued within 30 days of termination, and refunds are waived if not made within 90 days.
