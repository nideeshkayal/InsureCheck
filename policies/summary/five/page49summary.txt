Hometown Health Insurance Policy Summary

The Provider agrees not to seek additional payment from the Member for medical service provided by the Provider that could not have been received from the Member. This coverage continues until the 120th day after contract termination, unless the medical condition is pregnancy. Coverage for pharmacy benefits includes a Drug Formulary established by the Hometown Rx Committee, reviewed annually. The Drug Formulary lists medications selected for therapeutic categories and safety. Prior Authorization is required for certain outpatient prescriptions. Prior authorization is available for certain medications, contingent upon physician review, FDA approval, and physician determination of appropriateness. Coverage includes Generic, Preferred Brand, Non-Preferred Brand, Specialty Pharmaceuticals, and Diabetic Supplies. Prior authorization requirements may change.
