Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Emergency Services

**Policy Number:** [Policy Number]

**Coverage:** Medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals, including immunizations and screenings. Emergency services are medically necessary for acute care, semi-private rooms, maternity/newborn care, and alcohol/substance abuse rehabilitation. **Exclusions:** Artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, surrogacy, inpatient hospital care, institutions focused on rest, elderly, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.

**Important Terms:** Notifications within 24 hours required unless medically necessary cannot be addressed. Balance billing is a practice when non-participating providers bill you for amounts exceeding allowed levels.

**Important Notes:** Radiation therapy & chemotherapy are medically necessary, delivered appropriately.
