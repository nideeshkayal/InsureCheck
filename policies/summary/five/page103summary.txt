H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers Group PPO Plan for 65+ members. The policy remains primary for 30 months and then secondary to Medicare.  Expedited Appeal is available for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims.  A Grievance Committee oversees coverage decisions, and Informal Appeal is a voluntary route for resolving issues.  Failure to obtain prior authorization triggers notification and a response period, including required details of the claim.  The policy emphasizes the physician’s judgment in determining Urgent Care claims, prioritizing timely benefit review.
