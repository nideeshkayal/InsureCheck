COBRA continuation coverage ends when eligibility criteria are met, including becoming covered under another plan, eligibility for Medicare, premium payment delays, or employer cessation of coverage. USERRA provides continued coverage for employees on leave with or without pay, offering the same benefits as COBRA, with 102% of employer premium. This coverage is consistent with the Employer Group’s benefits during leave.  The policy terminates on a 12-week period, with a total of 12 weeks of benefits being covered.  It is crucial to understand that this coverage is contingent on meeting the defined conditions outlined in the Employer Group’s policy document.
