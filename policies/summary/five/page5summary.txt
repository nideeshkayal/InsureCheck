Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO plan provides comprehensive coverage for employees in Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. This plan includes prescription drug, pediatric vision, and dental services, subject to network requirements.

The policy emphasizes crucial terms, notably subrogation, coordination of benefits, and reimbursement rights.  It defines the plan's exclusions – medical, general, pharmaceutical, pediatric vision, and pediatric dental exclusions – impacting coverage.  Crucially, the plan mandates a 31-day eligibility period following birth/adoption.

The document details the schedule of benefits encompassing professional services, hospital care, skilled nursing, emergency services, and outpatient surgical procedures.  It also covers continued coverage after termination of provider contracts.  Understanding these terms and exclusions is vital for utilizing the plan effectively.
