Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan requires proof of coverage for eligible individuals, triggering eligibility based on birth, adoption, or placement. Premiums are subject to review and adjustment by Nevada Division of Insurance, contingent on documented coverage, including notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, birth certificate, marriage certificate, domestic partnership certificate, qualified medical child support order, court order, proof of residency, and Social Security number. Payments are primarily through payroll deductions, with employer-specific payment methods applicable. Termination occurs upon death, dependents’ coverage ends on the last day of the month of death, or a request to terminate.  Retransission may occur if Hometown Health ceases operations, following established regulations.  A crucial point is that fraud or material misrepresentation can result in termination or rescission, requiring retroactive termination.
