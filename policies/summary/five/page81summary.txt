Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan offers coverage for eligible individuals, triggered by birth, adoption, or placement. Premiums are monthly charges to establish and maintain coverage, analogous to a pot.  The Nevada Division of Insurance requires a certain cash reserve to cover claims, impacting premium affordability.  

The plan’s rate setting process is rigorously regulated by the Nevada Division of Insurance, ensuring assumptions are reviewed and adjusted.  Premiums are approved only after the division confirms reasonable assumptions. Coverage is contingent on required documentation including notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, birth certificate, marriage certificate, domestic partnership certificate, qualified medical child support order, court order, proof of residency, and Social Security number.  

Payments are typically through payroll deductions but employer-specific payment methods apply.  Employees must pay their portion of the premium directly to their employer by the due date, or coverage may be terminated retroactively. Refunds are issued within 30 days after termination, with medical costs refundable.
