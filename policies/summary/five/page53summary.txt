Hometown Health’s Policy outlines comprehensive prescription drug coverage, emphasizing member responsibility for costs. Coverage includes generic, preferred brand, non-preferred brand, specialty pharmaceuticals, and diabetic supplies. Significant changes are anticipated regarding reimbursement, potentially increasing Member costs due to evolving requirements.

Specifically, Specialty Pharmaceuticals require a certified immunizing pharmacist’s prescription, and coverage is limited to participating pharmacies. Diabetic supplies are covered medically necessary, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance applicable. Hormone Replacement Therapy requires FDA approval or state/federal law. Topical ophthalmic products have limitations on refills and require prior authorization. Cancer treatment drugs are covered when medically necessary and approved by the FDA.

Orally Administered Chemotherapy has a maximum cost of $100 per prescription, with a formulary alternative option available for Members on high deductible plans.
