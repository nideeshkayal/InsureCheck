H.O.METOWN HEALTH provides comprehensive health benefits across various plans, including group insurance, student coverage, and labor management plans. Key coverage includes group services, student benefits, labor management benefits, and governmental programs. Coordination of benefits is central to the plan, ensuring reimbursement for covered services from other plans.

When coordination occurs, it defines which plan will pay for a covered service. Secondary Plans may reduce benefits based on the Primary Plan's payment, requiring a claim to the Primary Plan for reimbursement. Hometown Health pays benefits to other insurers when overpayment occurs, discharging liability. The plan prioritizes payment to members contingent on coordination.
