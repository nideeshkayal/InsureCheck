Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  The plan includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases.  It provides Telehealth services and Urgent Care with In-Network Copayments and Coinsurance, with potential balance billing for Out-of-Area Providers.  Crucially, the policy prioritizes the Member’s Out-of-Pocket Maximum.  Hometown Health emphasizes cost-effectiveness through the schedule of benefits, offering free aids and services to individuals with disabilities, including interpreter services for diverse language needs.  A grievance process is available to address potential discrimination or concerns regarding service provision, with Jessica Marini acting as a resource.
