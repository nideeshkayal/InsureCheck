The Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan offers comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday. Coverage includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lenses, but specific pediatric vision conditions are required. Specialty Pharmaceutical coverage is limited to participating pharmacies, requiring a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription.

The plan incorporates a formulary alternative for Hormone Replacement Therapy, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Maximum prescription drug cost is $100.

Crucially, the plan excludes services not covered by the EOC, such as complications resulting from procedures, injury, or loss related to a riot or criminal act, and the exclusion of cosmetic surgery, including removal of sagging or extra skin (except for breast reduction as specified in the EOC).
