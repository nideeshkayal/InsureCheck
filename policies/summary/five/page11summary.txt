Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. The plan includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization for many cases, excluding cost-effective interventions and prescription drug costs exceeding allowed limits.  Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-sharing for non-preferred providers and denied claims. Criminal activity is covered. Custodial care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring continued medical personnel.  The policy emphasizes cost-effectiveness compared to alternative interventions.  Authorization procedures may result in coverage denials, excluding services, costs, and ancillary charges. Out-of-Network services incur more than In-Network rates; coverage is determined by the Member’s Out-of-Pocket Maximum.  Extended coverage for dependents requires the Family Out-of-Pocket Maximum to be paid, or no further payments are required.
