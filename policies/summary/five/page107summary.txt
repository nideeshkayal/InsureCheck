H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers the 2018 Small Group PPO Plan for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review. The policy mandates Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, with a 48-hour notification period and response requirement. Concurrent care decisions require notification of reduction or termination of coverage. Informal appeals are permitted within 60 days of adverse determination, with customer service recording claimant details.

The Grievance Committee handles 2nd level formal appeals to the 2018 Small Group PPO Plan. The policy’s 2nd level appeal is voluntary for Urgent Care claims; the policy will grant it if the physician requests it and supports it.  The Grievance Committee schedules formal presentations at a mutually convenient time, after a 1-week notification period.

Conflicts of interest are addressed to ensure independent decisions. Decisions regarding hiring, compensation, or termination will not be impacted by the likelihood of support for denial.
