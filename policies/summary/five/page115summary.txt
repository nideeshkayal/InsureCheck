Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan offers expedited external review for adverse determinations.  This plan includes mandatory external review requests within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review period for conditions jeopardizing health or function. Authorization is required for all claims.

The EOC supersedes previous agreements, emphasizing representation rather than guarantees. Clerical errors or delays do not invalidate coverage but will result in equitable adjustments to premiums. The EOC, Group Subscription Agreement, and Schedule of Benefits constitute the entire contract. Changes must be endorsed by an authorized officer.

This plan incorporates automatic modification to comply with federal and Nevada law. Members legally capable of contracting agree to all terms, and the representative of all members cannot contract.  Payment for overpayments must be returned to Hometown Health.
