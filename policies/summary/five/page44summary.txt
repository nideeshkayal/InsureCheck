**Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Emergency Services**

This policy provides medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals, including immunizations and screenings. Emergency services are medically necessary for acute care, semi-private rooms, maternity/newborn care, and alcohol/substance abuse rehabilitation. **Exclusions:** Artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, surrogacy, inpatient hospital care, institutions focused on rest, elderly, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.

**Important Terms:** Notifications must be provided within 24 hours unless medically necessary cannot be addressed. Balance billing occurs when providers bill exceeding the allowed amount; this typically happens during Emergency or Out-of-Area Urgent Care Center visits. Non-participating providers may bill more than the allowed amount.  Emergency Care may cost more; refer to your Schedule of Benefits.

**Key Changes:** The page highlights the critical exclusion of specific procedures and the potential for balance billing, emphasizing that Hometown Health doesn’t contract with non-participating providers, leading to potential billing exceeding allowed amounts.
