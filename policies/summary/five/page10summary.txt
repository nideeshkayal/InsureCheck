Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Key coverage includes medically necessary services, requiring prior authorization, covering illnesses, injuries, and symptoms, excluding non-preferred provider charges. Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding cost-effective interventions, prescription drug costs exceeding allowed amounts, ancillary charges, denied claims, and prescription drug from a Non-Participating Pharmacy.  Criminal activity is covered. Custodial care assistance is medically necessary, not requiring continued medical personnel. HDHP requirements include pre-tax HSA contributions. Network limitations exist; services must originate from Hometown Health and OneHealth providers at the Preferred Provider In-Network rate. The policy emphasizes cost-effectiveness compared to alternative interventions.
