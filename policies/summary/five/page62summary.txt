Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday. This plan includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lens services. Crucially, it excludes services related to reproductive technologies, including surrogacy, embryo transfer, and donor sperm/eggs unless specifically covered and described within this EOC.

The current page details exclusions related to pharmacy benefits. Specifically, drugs not medically necessary, or not required in accordance with medical practice or law, are excluded.  Cosmetics, drugs used for cosmetic purposes, dietary supplements, and appetite suppressants are also excluded.  Drugs dispensed by non-participating pharmacies are excluded unless medically necessary for an emergency or urgent care condition.  Drugs listed on the Formulary Exclusions List, designated as Non-Formulary, or not included on the Formulary are excluded. Finally, prescriptions from providers not acting within their license are excluded.
