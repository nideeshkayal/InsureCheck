H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for 65+ members, with a critical emphasis on timely benefit review. Expedited External Review is permitted for adverse determinations related to Urgent Care, requiring notification and potential appeals.  The IRO reviews documents and provides final determination within 15 days, informing both the insured and physician. Authorization for Release of Medical Records is required for external review.  An independent third-party reviews claims anew, and the right to appeal any denial is guaranteed.  Individuals can submit requests for external review within 180 days of receiving a denial, with a 45-day timeframe for expedited review for conditions jeopardizing health or function.  Refer to the Office for Consumer Health Assistance and the Nevada Division of Insurance for further details.
