Hometown Health’s Policy outlines coverage for outpatient prescription drug purchases, emphasizing member responsibility for costs. Coverage includes generic, preferred brand, non-preferred brand, specialty pharmaceuticals, and diabetic supplies. Reimbursement is subject to deductibles, copays, and coinsurance, with potential for less-than-expected reimbursement. Specialty pharmaceuticals require a specific pharmacy designation and limited supply. Preventive medications are covered at participating pharmacies.

Importantly, the policy details potential for changes to coverage terms and requirements. Prescription submission with card is mandatory. Mail order prescriptions are limited to 90-day supplies unless otherwise specified. Specialty Pharmaceutical coverage requires a prescription from a certified immunizing pharmacist. The policy addresses potential for less-than-expected reimbursement, increasing Member costs.
