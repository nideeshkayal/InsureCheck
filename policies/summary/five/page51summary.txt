Hometown Health’s Policy outlines coverage for outpatient prescription drug purchases, emphasizing member responsibility for costs. Prior authorization is required for certain medications, including those for chronic conditions, and can change. Coverage includes generic, preferred brand, non-preferred brand, specialty pharmaceuticals, and diabetic supplies.

The policy clarifies that Hometown Health reimburses for cost-sharing, including deductibles, copayments, and coinsurance. It also specifies that prescriptions must be presented with the card. Mail order prescriptions are limited to a 90-day supply unless otherwise specified. Specialty Pharmaceuticals are unique, requiring a specific pharmacy designation and limited supply. Preventive medications are covered at participating pharmacies. 

Important considerations include the potential for less than expected reimbursement, potentially increasing Member costs. The policy also highlights the potential for changes to coverage terms and requirements.
