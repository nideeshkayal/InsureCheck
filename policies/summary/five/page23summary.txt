Hometown Health Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services, including prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance.

**Key Rights and Responsibilities:** Individuals can request amendments to medical information and limit disclosures related to surgeries. Restrictions can be requested in writing to Hometown Health Customer Services (10315 Professional Circle, Reno, NV).

**Important Considerations:** Hometown Health maintains former member data, used for legal retention. Access is limited to authorized personnel.

**Right to Request Confidential Communications:** You can request confidential communications at work or by mail.

**Right to a Paper Copy:** You have the right to a paper copy of the notice.

**Changes to Notice:** Hometown Health reserves the right to revise the notice.

**Complaints:** If privacy concerns arise, file a complaint with Hometown Health Customer Services at 775-982-3112.
