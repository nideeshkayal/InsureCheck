Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Summary

**Coverage:** Medical pharmacy benefits, immunizations (influenza, pneumococcal, measles), hearing/vision screenings, evidence-based preventive care, and radiation therapy.

**Exclusions:** Artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy.

**Repair Limits:** Limited to medically necessary stabilization after injury. Orthopedic devices & prosthetics covered only when medically necessary.

**Covered Services:** Skin lesion removal (port wine lesions), spinal manipulation (up to 20 visits/lifetime), transplant services for organ cases (bone marrow, cornea, heart, lung, intestinal & liver, kidney, pancreas, and kidney), and radiation therapy.

**Important Notes:** Policy excludes facilities not designated as Hometown Health Center of Excellence, organ donor services, organ purchases/sales, transportation of the donor, and services at non-Hometown Health Center of Excellence.
