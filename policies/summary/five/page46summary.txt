**Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Emergency Services**

This policy covers medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals experiencing acute care needs, including immunizations and screenings for maternity, alcohol rehab, and recovery from illness.  Coverage is based on the generally accepted cost of durable medical equipment (DME) essential for the patient’s medical needs, guided by national standards.

**Key Exclusions:** The policy excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, inpatient hospital care, elderly care, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.  Also excluded are exercising equipment, vibratory or negative gravity equipment, swimming or therapy pools, spas, and whirlpools, except when medically necessary for a medical condition.  Furthermore, the policy does not cover enteral formulas or special food products unless medically necessary for an inherited metabolic disease.

Hearing aids are covered for repair and replacement every three years for each ear requiring a hearing aid, and only if obtained from approved providers. Home health care is covered for skilled nursing care and therapies, *excluding* Bluetooth and GPS-enabled devices.
