Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  The plan emphasizes crucial terms, including subrogation, coordination of benefits, and reimbursement rights.

Key definitions include: Acute conditions are short-term illnesses, while Chronic conditions continue for six months and are expected to persist. Chronic Pain encompasses ongoing pain due to non-life-threatening causes, requiring ongoing treatment.

Coinsurance applies after deductibles are paid, with Member costs sharing based on the In-Network Out-of-Pocket Maximums. Copayments are dollar amounts paid to providers, and Cost Sharing refers to Deductible, Copayment, and Coin-Share expenses. The eligibility period begins after enrollment and varies with circumstances.  Understanding these terms and exclusions is vital for utilizing the plan effectively.
