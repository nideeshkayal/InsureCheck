**COBRA Continuation Coverage**

COBRA continuation is triggered by a qualifying event – termination or reduced work hours – impacting dependent coverage.  If dependents become eligible after termination, coverage is extended to 36 months, then potentially to Medicare.  A qualifying event *after* initial 18 months triggers a second qualifying event – potential loss of dependent coverage.  Notification requirements mandate 60 days from notification or 60 days from coverage end.  The plan includes exclusions for benefits received after termination.  Individuals must contact Hometown Health or their employer to initiate COBRA continuation.
