Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan requires diligent record-keeping. Material misrepresentation, including revealing identity, allowing unauthorized use, or engaging in fraudulent activity, results in termination or rescission. Employers must ensure eligibility requirements are met, and coverage may be rescinded back to the initial enrollment date.

Dependent coverage termination follows established law, with employer coverage options available to spouses, domestic partners, and children as defined in the Group Subscription Agreement. Coverage for dependent children terminates on the last day of the month when the child reaches age 26, contingent on whether the child seeks to become a Subscriber. Hometown Health reserves the right to recoup benefits beyond the termination date.
