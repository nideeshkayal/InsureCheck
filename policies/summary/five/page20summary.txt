Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It offers medically necessary services, including prior authorization requirements, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance. Key coverage details include defined access to medical information restricted to authorized personnel, ensuring patient privacy. The plan addresses disclosures to family members and specific situations like military and veterans, public health risks, and potential threats to health.  It also includes data breach reporting obligations, law enforcement release requests, and disaster relief scenarios.  Specifically, the policy outlines disclosures for abuse, neglect, and domestic violence, requiring consent or legal authorization.  Finally, it addresses national security and intelligence activities, ensuring data is handled responsibly.
