Hometown Health’s Plan offers a Provider relationship as an independent contractor, meaning Hometown Health is not liable for claims arising from medical services provided by any Provider.  Providers retain full authority over medical decisions, although Hometown Health’s determination of treatment isn’t a Covered Service.

The plan’s core remains focused on exclusions: prescriptions, investigation drugs, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services are not covered.  Concurrent review and case management follows admission, requiring pregnancy notification.  Retrospective review evaluates medical records for authorization, prior authorization, and concurrent review.  Second opinions are authorized upon request, including scenarios involving diagnosis uncertainty, treatment response failure, or conflicting test results.
