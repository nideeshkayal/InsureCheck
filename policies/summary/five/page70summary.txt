Hometown Health’s Plan excludes prescriptions for impotence, investigation drugs, medical treatment, and pediatric vision. Artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services are also excluded. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices are not covered.

Out-of-Network Services exceed allowed amounts, and costs exceeding allowed amounts for inpatient/outpatient surgery are not covered. Prior authorization is required for cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation, chemotherapy, and infusion therapy, determined by the Utilization Management Committee. Notifications are required at least five business days before elective inpatient or outpatient surgery. Providers must confirm notification within 24 hours.

Concurrent review and case management follows admission to a facility. The plan requires notification of pregnancy for benefit management.  Covered healthcare services and supplies are authorized only as outlined in the policy.
