H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan offers 65+ members comprehensive coverage for health services. The policy mandates timely benefit review, with Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, a 48-hour notification period, and a requirement for reduction or termination of coverage upon disagreement. The Grievance Committee oversees voluntary appeals to ensure independent decisions. External Review is available for adverse benefit determinations, including Urgent Care denials, requiring a 30-day notification period. Expedited External Review is permitted for conditions jeopardizing life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, and when a request for internal appeal is made. Compliance with U.S. regulations (regulation18, 26 CFR, 29 CFR, 2560.503-1, 2590.715-2719, 45 CFR) is essential.
