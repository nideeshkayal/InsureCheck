Hometown Health’s Plan excludes prescriptions for specific conditions like impotence, investigation drugs, medical treatment, and pediatric vision coverage, limiting rebate programs for pediatric vision. It also excludes artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and services performed by a dentist compensated by a facility. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices are excluded.  Costs exceeding allowed amounts for Out-of-Network Services are not covered. Prior authorization is required for certain services, including cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation, chemotherapy, and infusion therapy, as determined by the Utilization Management Committee.  Hometown Health requires notification of pregnancy for better benefit management.  The utilization management program mandates provider notification before elective hospital admissions, ensuring coverage based on defined criteria.
