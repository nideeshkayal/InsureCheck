Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Summary

This policy, number [Policy Number], provides comprehensive medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals, including immunizations, screenings, and mental health care. It covers inpatient hospital care, with a maximum duration of 60 days, and outpatient services.

**Key Coverage:**  The policy explicitly excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy.  Inpatient hospital care is medically necessary for acute care, semi-private rooms, maternity/newborn care, and alcohol/substance abuse rehabilitation.  Emergency admissions require notification within the next business day unless medically necessary cannot be addressed.

**Important Considerations:**  Coverage includes radiation therapy and chemotherapy, but these services are delivered in the most appropriate clinical manner.  Services not covered include institutions primarily focused on rest, the elderly, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.  Emergency services are available 24/7, requiring immediate attention.
