Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO plan offers comprehensive coverage for employees in Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. This plan includes prescription drug, pediatric vision, and dental services, subject to network requirements.

Key details include minimum essential coverage, eligibility starting at 31 days following birth/adoption, and ACA, HIPAA, and Nevada telehealth regulations adherence. The policy incorporates a schedule of benefits, detailing services for professional services, hospital, skilled nursing, emergency services, and outpatient surgical procedures. It also covers continued coverage after termination of a provider contract. 

Crucially, the plan excludes medical and general exclusions, pharmaceutical benefits exclusions, pediatric vision exclusions, and pediatric dental benefits exclusions.  Information about utilization management, relationship of parties, and enrollment procedures are also presented.  The summary emphasizes the crucial policy terms and the importance of understanding the benefits offered and limitations.
