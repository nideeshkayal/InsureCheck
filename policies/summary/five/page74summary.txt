Hometown Health’s Provider Relationship Plan offers comprehensive coverage for Hometown Health’s independent contractor providers.  **Coverage excludes prescriptions, investigation drugs, pediatric vision, artificial lens replacements, contact lens insurance, and dentist services.**  The plan’s core focuses on **exclusions** – pregnancy notification, concurrent review of medical records, retrospective authorization requests, and second opinions for diagnosis uncertainty, treatment response failure, or conflicting test results.

**Enrollment requires meeting eligibility requirements, including US citizenship, residency, employment status, and payment obligations.** Dependents may enroll if listed on the application, subject to employer eligibility rules. **Eligibility for coverage begins when the spouse or domestic partner becomes eligible.**  The plan’s primary objective is control over medical decision-making, ensuring Hometown Health’s authority over medical decisions.

**Key Definitions:** “Placement for adoption” signifies a situation where a Subscriber assumes parental responsibility, triggering coverage eligibility. **NRS 689B.033** regulates placement for adoption.  The plan emphasizes medical care for injury and sickness, with cost-sharing requirements including deductibles, copays, and coinsurance applicable to other illnesses.
