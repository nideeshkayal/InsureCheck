Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Key coverage includes diabetic management, including infusion pumps and supplies, medication, and equipment for diabetes care, as well as self-management training and education.  Genetic counseling is now offered for medical and scientific information about inherited conditions, birth defects, or genome-related effects.

The Genetic Counseling/Testing coverage expands to encompass pregnancy management, specifically for parents of children born with genetic disorders, birth defects, inborn errors of metabolism, chromosome abnormalities, or potential complications. Testing is permissible after consultation with a certified genetic counselor and/or, as approved by a Physician, and will be covered when the results directly impact clinical decision-making.  Testing is limited to one lifetime for inherited disorders, and the current literature suggests that genetic testing for inherited diseases should only be conducted once per lifetime of the member.
