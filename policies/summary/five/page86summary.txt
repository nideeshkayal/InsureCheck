Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan requires diligent record-keeping to avoid termination. COBRA mandates that employers offer continued medical coverage for eligible employees and dependents, subject to standard claims procedures. Coverage is contingent upon meeting eligibility requirements, including a qualifying event—such as a reduced work schedule or termination for a non-gross misconduct reason—that triggers COBRA continuation.  This plan outlines specific exclusions: Hometown Health is not liable for benefits received after termination.  COBRA continuation eligibility is determined by a “Qualified Beneficiary” definition, including employees, dependents, former spouses, and former employers.  To maintain coverage, individuals must contact Hometown Health or their employer to initiate the process.
