**Hometown Health Insurance Policy - Current Page Summary**

The Current Page details coverage for Hometown Health Insurance, focusing on genetic counseling, medical pharmacy benefits, mental health services, and dental care.  

**Coverage Details:** The policy provides coverage for genetic counseling for inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, and limited testing. Medical pharmacy benefits are included for specialized medications. Mental health services are subject to Schedule of Benefits limits, and newborn/maternity care is covered with transportation costs.

**Important Exclusions:** The policy excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy.  Repairs and restorations are limited to medically necessary stabilization/repair of sound teeth after injury. Orthopedic devices and prosthetics are covered only when medically necessary.

**Key Definitions:** The policy defines “Partial Hospitalization Services” as a 12-hour treatment period, and “Podiatry Services” as treatment for acute foot conditions.  Preventive services include periodic physical examinations and routine immunizations.
