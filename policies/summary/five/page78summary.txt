**Hometown Health Provider Relationship Plan – Coverage Changes**

This document details enrollment procedures for dependent status, death, termination of employment, and other key events. The page emphasizes that proof of other coverage is required for eligibility. Coverage is effective based on the event itself (birth, adoption, or placement) or court order.

The following events are not Qualifying Life Events: a change in citizenship status, incarceration status, loss of Advance Premium Tax Credits or Cost Sharing Reductions, status as an American Indian or Alaska Native, or completion of a non-Calendar year health insurance plan.

Enrollment requires completing an application, including documentation and payment.  Application submission is subject to employer guidelines. Coverage is effective upon the event’s completion, with dates following the event or the 1st of the following month as determined by your employer.
