H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers a Group PPO Plan for members aged 65+ or entitled to Medicare benefits. This plan is designed for coordination of health benefits, contingent on birthday and dependent/parent benefits. Coverage is determined by the member's birthday and eligibility for Medicare.

The Policy’s primary responsibility for Medicare benefits remains with Medicare, regardless of employment status.  Recovery is permitted for exceeding benefit amounts, including organizations, and is subject to policy terms.

The Policy remains primary for the first 30 months and then secondary to Medicare.  ESRD (Extended Resident Elderly Program) coverage remains the primary responsibility for the first 30 months.  Eligibility for Medicare benefits is based on age and employment status.
