Hometown Health offers comprehensive coverage for genetic counseling related to inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, and testing limitations for one lifetime. This includes mastectomies for breast cancer survivors, reconstructive surgery for breast cancer, and related complications.  Coverage includes medical pharmacy benefits for specialized pharmaceuticals, which include injectables, oral medications, and medications given by other routes of delivery, with exceptions for dosage issues.  Mental Health Services are covered, including medically necessary mental health care, with limits defined in the Schedule of Benefits. Newborn and Maternity Care is covered, including prenatal and postpartum care for eligible members, and includes transportation costs for newborn care.  The policy explicitly excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and maternity services related to surrogacy.  Prior Authorization is required for certain mental health services, and gynecological care is accessible with a minimum 48-hour stay for vaginal deliveries and 96-hour stay for Cesarean sections.
