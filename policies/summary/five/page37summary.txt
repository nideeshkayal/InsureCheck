Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Current Page Summary

This policy offers comprehensive coverage for Hometown Health Insurance, including genetic counseling, medical pharmacy benefits, mental health services, and dental care. Coverage levels for genetic counseling are determined by the American Cancer Society’s guidelines, and coverage for mental health services is subject to schedule limits.

**Key Coverage Details:** The policy covers medical pharmacy benefits, immunizations (including influenza, pneumococcal, and measles), and hearing and vision screenings. It also provides evidence-based preventive care, including recommendations from the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force.

**Important Exclusions:** The policy explicitly excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy. Repairs are limited to medically necessary stabilization after injury. Orthopedic devices and prosthetics are covered only when medically necessary.

Furthermore, the policy includes radiation therapy services, medically necessary and covered for.
