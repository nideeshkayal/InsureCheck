The Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan excludes prescriptions for conditions like impotence, investigation drugs, and medical treatment. The Formulary Exclusions List specifically restricts rebate programs for pediatric vision coverage, excluding replacements of lenses/contacts, surgical treatments, and contact lens insurance.  Furthermore, the document limits reimbursement for “artificially painted or non-prescription lenses,” “additional office visits for contact lens pathology,” and “contact lens modification” – crucial for understanding the scope of coverage limitations.  State or territorial taxes on dental services are excluded, and those submitted by a dentist for the same services performed on the same member by another dentist are also excluded. Duplicate, provisional, and temporary devices, appliances, and services are also excluded, as are services performed by a dentist who is compensated by a facility for similar covered services performed for members.  Coverage for specialized procedures and techniques is excluded.
