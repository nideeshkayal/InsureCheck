H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan prioritizes timely benefit review for 65+ members. The plan covers urgent care, with expedited external review permitted for adverse determinations, requiring notification and potential appeals.  Independent third-party reviews of claims are anew conducted, guaranteeing appeals.  Individuals can submit requests for external review within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review timeframe for conditions jeopardizing health or function. Authorization is required for all claims.

The EOC supersedes all other agreements, stating that statements are representations and not warranties.  Clerical errors or delays will not invalidate coverage, but will result in equitable adjustments to premiums.  The EOC, Group Subscription Agreement, and Schedule of Benefits constitute the entire contract. Changes to the EOC must be endorsed by an authorized officer.
