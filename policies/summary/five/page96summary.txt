H.O.METOWN HEALTH Small Group PPO Plan’s Coordination of Benefits provision governs health care coverage across various plans, including group insurance, hospital services, and government programs.  It facilitates payment coordination with other insurers, ensuring reimbursement for covered services. This coordination is essential for containing costs, particularly when multiple plans offer coverage. The plan prioritizes payment to the member or eligible dependents, contingent on the coordination of benefits.

Key definitions include “Allowable Expense” (representing all medically necessary expenses), “Claim Determination Period” (the calendar year for claims liability calculation), and “Plan” (defined as an entity providing health benefits).  The provision’s purpose is to ensure that all health benefits are coordinated with other valid coverage plans, maintaining cost control and facilitating payment.
