Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan offers comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19. The plan includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lens services. Crucially, it excludes reproductive technologies, including surrogacy, embryo transfer, and donor sperm/eggs unless specifically covered. Drugs dispensed by non-participating pharmacies are also excluded, except under emergency or urgent care conditions. Furthermore, prescription medications not acting within their license are excluded. The Formulary Exclusions List, designated as Non-Formulary, and drugs listed on the FDA’s Caution –limited by Federal Law to Investigation list are also excluded. Non-medical supplies and substances are excluded, regardless of intended use. Medications for impotence or erectile dysfunction are also excluded.  Drugs consumed outside the United States are excluded unless from a participating Canadian pharmacy.
