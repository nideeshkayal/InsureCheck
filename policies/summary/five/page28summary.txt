Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  The plan’s core coverage includes allergy testing and treatment, ASD screening and diagnosis, autism behavior intervention, and therapy limits.  Treatment requires Prior Authorization and is subject to specific limitations, including maximum benefit for ASD habilitation and rehabilitation, with a maximum of 600 hours (130 visits) per calendar year.  Coverage is capped at $600 for routine health services and excludes FDA-approved medications or devices.  The plan also limits coverage for extraneous expenses related to the clinical trial or study.
