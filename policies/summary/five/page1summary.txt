Here is a concise summary of the Hometown Health Providers Insurance Policy, based on the provided page content:

This Hometown Health Providers Insurance Policy is an open access Preferred Provider Organization (PPO) plan designed for employees of employers within Carson City and Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  This policy provides access to a network of Preferred Providers who offer services to Hometown Health members.

The policy offers coverage for prescription drugs, pediatric vision, and dental coverage, subject to network requirements. Members must utilize the Hometown Rx Premier Pharmacy Network for prescription drugs, and work with their doctors to select drugs included in the HometownRx Standard Prescription Drug List. The policy does not cover drugs outside this list. Coverage is available to employees and eligible dependents of employers with a primary business location within these counties.
