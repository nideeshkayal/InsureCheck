Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO plan provides comprehensive coverage for employees within the specified counties – Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  This plan offers prescription drug, pediatric vision, and dental services, subject to network requirements.

The policy incorporates Minimum Essential Coverage (IRS Form 1095-B) and eligibility for dependent coverage, starting at 31 days following birth/adoption.  It adheres to ACA, HIPAA, and Nevada telehealth regulations.
