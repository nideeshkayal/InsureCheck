Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Summary

This policy, number [Policy Number], provides comprehensive medical and pharmacy benefits, including immunizations, screenings, and mental health care. It covers inpatient hospital care, including acute care, semi-private rooms, nursing, and short-term rehabilitative services, with a maximum duration of 60 days.  **The policy explicitly excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy.**

**Crucially, inpatient hospital care is medically necessary for acute care, semi-private rooms, and maternity/newborn care, with prior authorization required.**  Outpatient services cover hospital care, nursing care, and diagnostic services, contingent upon coverage outlined in the Schedule of Benefits. **The policy also emphasizes that emergency admissions require notification within the next business day unless medically necessary is unable to be addressed.**
