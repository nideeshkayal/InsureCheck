COBRA continuation provides coverage for individuals impacted by termination or reduced work hours. It’s triggered by qualifying events like divorce, loss of dependent coverage, or notification of a qualifying event extending continuation.  **Crucially, notification requirements are stringent:** 60 days from notification or 60 days from coverage end, with potential failure to notify leading to plan administrator non-obligation.  **The Trade Act of 2002** modifies COBRA, offering a 60-day extension for specific employees receiving Trade Adjustment Assistance (TAA).  Eligibility for this extension is limited to those receiving TAA, starting six months after coverage ends.  **Failure to comply with notification deadlines results in lost COBRA rights.**  Contact Hometown Health or your employer to initiate continuation coverage.
