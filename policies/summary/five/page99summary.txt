H.O.METOWN HEALTH provides comprehensive health benefits across various plans, including group insurance, student coverage, and labor management plans. The policy emphasizes coordination of benefits to ensure accurate payment.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Coordination:** The plan prioritizes payment to members contingent on coordination.
*   **Benefit Allocation:** Determination methods include no coordination, Dental Plans, Dependent/Parent, and Inactive Employee.
*   **Coverage:** Coverage is determined based on the individual’s birthday, and dependent/parent benefits are prioritized.
*   **Recovery:**  The policy has the right to recover payments exceeding the required benefit amount, including individuals, insurance companies, and other organizations.

This page specifically focuses on the critical elements of benefit allocation and the policy’s right to recover payments, ensuring clarity and compliance in the coordination of health benefits.
