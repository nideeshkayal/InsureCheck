H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers a Group PPO Plan for members aged 65+ or entitled to Medicare benefits, with a focus on coordination of health benefits. Medicare is the primary payer for these members, regardless of employment status with an employer.

**Key Details:**

*   **Coordination:** Payments are contingent on coordination, not duplication of Medicare benefits.
*   **Coverage:** Coverage is determined by birthday and dependent/parent benefits.
*   **Recovery:** Policy has the right to recover exceeding benefit amounts, including organizations.
*   **Eligibility:** Eligibility for Medicare benefits depends on age and employment status.
*   **ESRD:**  For ESRD, the Policy remains primary for the first 30 months, then secondary to Medicare.
