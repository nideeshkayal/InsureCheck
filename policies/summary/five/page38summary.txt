Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Current Page Summary

This policy offers comprehensive coverage for Hometown Health, encompassing genetic counseling, medical pharmacy benefits, mental health services, and dental care. Key coverage details include medical pharmacy benefits, immunizations (influenza, pneumococcal, measles), hearing and vision screenings, evidence-based preventive care, and radiation therapy.

Coverage is restricted by exclusions: artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy. Repairs are limited to medically necessary stabilization after injury, and orthopedic devices and prosthetics are covered only when medically necessary.

The policy also covers skin lesion removal (port wine lesions), spinal manipulation (up to 20 visits/lifetime), transplant services for specific organ cases (bone marrow, cornea, heart, lung, intestinal and liver, kidney, pancreas, and kidney), and radiation therapy. Medical necessity is assessed based on the setting of the service.
