This H.O.METOWN HEALTH Small Group PPO Plan establishes a constructive trust relationship for medical expenses resulting from a third-party’s illness or injury. The policy mandates the Member to maintain funds in a separate account and grants equitable lien on those funds. Crucially, reimbursement obligations are contingent upon the third-party’s payment; this includes all damages – including personal injury, property loss, and medical expenses – immediately upon compensation. The policy also covers future expenses, regardless of fault, ensuring full coverage.

The policy’s core provisions include distinct requirements for serving as a constructive trustee, overseeing funds, and coordinating claims. Representatives must provide necessary information and execute forms, and the Member must cooperate fully with the insurer. Importantly, the policy explicitly avoids the “Make-Whole Doctrine,” ensuring the Member’s responsibilities remain unchanged, regardless of claim classification or the insurer's actions. The example of Mr. Smith’s recovery illustrates this – the recovery is reduced based on the third-party’s expenses, preventing the Member from repaying the full amount.
