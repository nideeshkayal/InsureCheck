Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday.  The plan includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lens services. Crucially, the plan excludes services not covered by the Excluded Conditions, such as complications resulting from procedures, injury, or loss related to a riot or criminal act. Cosmetic surgery is also excluded, including sagging or extra skin removal. The plan excludes psychological and physical factors, including self-image, social issues, and medical problems impacting functionality. Furthermore, it excludes medications related to erectile function, sensitive areas, and conditions affecting the body’s appearance. Rarely, the plan excludes natural remedies related to marijuana use. Specifically, it excludes treatments for the removal of varicose veins, ablation, or injections, or treatments for appetite control or obesity.  The plan also excludes experimental or investigational medical, surgical, or other health care procedures and transplants, except as described within the Excluded Conditions.
