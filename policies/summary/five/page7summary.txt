Hometown Health’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Key terms include subrogation, coordination of benefits, and reimbursement rights.

The plan emphasizes crucial terms:

*   **Costs:** Member responsibilities are limited to Covered Services, excluding Non-Preferred Provider charges, Coinsurance, prescription drug costs exceeding the Allowed Amount, ancillary charges, denied claims, and prescription drug from a Non-Participating Pharmacy.
*   **Services:** Covered services require Prior Authorization, are rendered by licensed providers within the state, and are medically necessary or specifically listed as a benefit.
*   **Criminal Acts:** Any criminal activity is covered under this policy.
*   **Custodial Care:** This coverage includes assistance in daily living activities, with a cure requirement, and does not require continued medical personnel.

**Important:** The family deductible is twice the individual deductible, and the family deductible is set at twice the individual deductible.
