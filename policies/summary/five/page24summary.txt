Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services like prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance.

**Key Details:** Hometown Health retains former member data for legal retention, limiting access to authorized personnel. Individuals have the right to request confidential communications – through work or mail – and a paper copy of the notice.  **Important:**  Medical information disclosures are subject to your written authorization, allowing for revocation at any time. The plan covers medically necessary services, but explicitly states that other uses and disclosures will be made with your consent.  Finally, Hometown Health maintains records of provided care.
