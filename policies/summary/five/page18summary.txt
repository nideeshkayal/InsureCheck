Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It includes medically necessary services requiring prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care with copays and coinsurance. Crucially, Hometown Health emphasizes cost-effectiveness through schedule of benefits.

The policy defines access to medical information as restricted to authorized personnel, maintaining physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards. Hometown Health will use and disclose medical information for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations, including providing information about medical services for treatment, referral, eligibility reviews, and utilization review. It also allows Hometown Health to use and disclose your information for certain purposes as permitted by law, such as for treatment, payment, and healthcare operations.  Access to your medical information is subject to privacy regulations, including the Privacy Office’s responsibility for safeguarding medical data.
