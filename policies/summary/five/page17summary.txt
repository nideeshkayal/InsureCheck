Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It includes medically necessary services requiring prior authorization, offers Telehealth and Urgent Care with copays and coinsurance, and prioritizes the Member’s Out-of-Pocket Maximum. The policy includes a grievance process. Crucially, Hometown Health emphasizes cost-effectiveness through schedule of benefits, offering free aids and services for individuals with disabilities, including interpreter services.  It also provides a balance billing option for out-of-area providers.
