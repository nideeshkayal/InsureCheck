H.O.METOWN HEALTH Small Group PPO Plan provisions center on constructive trust and reimbursement obligations.  The policy mandates a constructive trust relationship, ensuring funds are held in a separate account.  Reimbursement is contingent upon third-party compensation – including damages – immediately upon recovery.

Crucially, the policy explicitly avoids “Make-Whole Doctrine,” meaning responsibilities remain unchanged regardless of claim classification or insurer actions.  The document details allocation of recovered amounts, prioritizing payment to the Member or eligible dependents.

The policy establishes a crucial right to release information to insurance companies, requiring Member cooperation for subrogation and reimbursement obligations.  Termination is possible if information is not provided, and the policy holder's coverage is subject to this discretionary power.  Finally, rights for heirs and estates are retained.
