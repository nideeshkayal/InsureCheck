H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive coverage for 65+ members.  The policy mandates timely benefit review, subject to Expedited External Review for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, a 48-hour notification period, and reduction or termination of coverage upon disagreement. Expedited External Review is permitted for conditions jeopardizing life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, and when a request for internal appeal is made. Compliance with U.S. regulations is crucial.

The IRO (Independent Review Organization) reviews documents and provides final determination within fifteen days (48 hours for expedited requests) to either approve, modify, or reverse the adverse determination.  The IRO notifies you, your physician, and the OCHA of its decision.  Authorization for Release of Medical Records allows for the release of your records for external review. An independent third-party reviews claims anew.  You have the right to appeal any decision denying payment.
