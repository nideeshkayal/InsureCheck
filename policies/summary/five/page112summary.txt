H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review. The plan covers urgent care, with expedited external review permitted for adverse determinations related to this service.  This review necessitates notification and potential appeals.

Independent third-party reviews of claims are conducted anew, and appeals are guaranteed.  Individuals can submit requests for external review within 180 days of denial, with a 45-day expedited review timeframe for conditions jeopardizing health or function.  Authorization for external review is required for all claims.  Refer to the Office for Consumer Health Assistance and the Nevada Division of Insurance for further details.
