Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Emergency Services covers medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals with acute care needs, including immunizations and screenings. Emergency services are medically necessary for conditions like maternity, alcohol rehab, and recovery from illness.

**Exclusions:** The policy excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, inpatient hospital care, elderly care, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.

**Balance Billing:** Balance billing occurs when providers bill exceeding the allowed amount, particularly during Emergency or Out-of-Area Urgent Care Center visits. Non-participating providers may bill more, and Emergency care may cost more.

**Durable Medical Equipment (DME):** Coverage is provided for DME deemed medically necessary, including oxygen equipment, wheelchairs, hospital beds, glucose monitors, and monitoring devices for infants. Coverage requirements are outlined in CMS guidelines.
