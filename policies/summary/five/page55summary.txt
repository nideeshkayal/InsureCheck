Hometown Health’s Pediatric Vision Plan offers coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday. Coverage includes annual eye exams, lens options (yearly and calendar), and contact lenses, with specific requirements for conditions. Specialty Pharmaceutical coverage is limited to participating pharmacies and requires a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription.

The plan includes a formulary alternative for Hormone Replacement Therapy, with deductibles, copays, and coinsurance. Coverage for Diabetic Supplies is medically necessary and includes deductibles, copays, and coinsurance.  The plan provides a maximum cost of $100 for prescription drugs.
