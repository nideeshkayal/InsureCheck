Hometown Health Providers Insurance Policy provides coverage for employees within Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It’s an open access PPO plan covering prescription drugs, pediatric vision, and dental services, subject to network requirements.

**Key Details:**

*   **Minimum Essential Coverage:** IRS Form 1095-B reports coverage to the IRS and taxpayers.
*   **Dependent Eligibility:** Employer-defined dependents are eligible for coverage, with eligibility criteria starting at 31 days following birth/adoption.
*   **Ongoing Regulation:** The policy complies with ACA, HIPAA, and Nevada telehealth laws.
*   **Document Access:** Documents (EOC, Schedule of Benefits, etc.) are available online at hometownhealth.com.
