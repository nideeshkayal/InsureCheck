**Hometown Health Provider Relationship Plan – Coverage Changes**

Enrollments for coverage are governed by specific rules concerning open enrollment periods. During the Open Enrollment Period, eligible spouses or domestic partners and dependents can enroll in the Benefit Plan. Coverage is effective on the Group’s initial effective date or on the Policy Renewal Date.

Significant changes occur when a Qualifying Life Event triggers enrollment. Specifically, birth, marriage, or adoption require 31 or 30 days, respectively, for special enrollment. Failure to complete the enrollment application within this timeframe may result in denial of coverage.

Changes to coverage, including deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums, are not counted toward new plans.  The page highlights the importance of adhering to these enrollment deadlines to protect against adverse selection.
