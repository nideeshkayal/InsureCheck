**Hometown Health Small Group PPO Plan**

**Coverage:** Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.

**Key Terms:** Diabetic management (infusion pumps, supplies, equipment), genetic counseling (inherited conditions, birth defects, genome effects), prenatal care (pregnancy management, parental support), and testing.

**Conditions/Exclusions:** Genetic counseling for inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, complications, testing is limited to one lifetime for inherited diseases; only approved by a physician after consultation. Testing is permissible after consultation with a certified genetic counselor. Covered tests are for direct clinical impact. Excludes reproduction by artificial means (except for maximum benefit limit), fertility promotion (including IVF, embryo transplants), reversal of infertility, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and maternity services related to surrogate mothers.

**VERY IMPORTANT:** Gifts, services, or drugs related to artificially induced pregnancy or assisted reproductive technology (except for artificial insemination benefit limits) are not covered.
