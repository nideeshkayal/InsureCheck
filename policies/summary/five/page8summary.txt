**Summary:**

The Hometown Health 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties.  The Family Deductible is twice the individual deductible, and the Family Deductible is set at twice the individual deductible, including a requirement to satisfy it each year.

**Key Policy Details:**

*   **Coverage:** Covered services are medically necessary or listed as benefits, requiring prior authorization.
*   **Cost:** Member responsibilities are limited to Covered Services, excluding Non-Preferred Provider charges, Coinsurance, prescription drug costs exceeding allowed amounts, ancillary charges, denied claims, and prescription drug from a Non-Participating Pharmacy.
*   **Criminal Acts:** Criminal activity is covered.
*   **Custodial Care:** Assistance in daily living activities with a cure requirement, not requiring continued medical personnel.

The Evidence of Coverage (EOC) document details the policy, exclusions, limitations, and administrative policies governing the plan.
