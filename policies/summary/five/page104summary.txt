H.O.METOWN HEALTH offers Group PPO Plan for 65+ members, prioritizing timely benefit review.  This policy includes Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, with notification and a response period, including required details of the claim.  Notification of benefit determination is delivered within 48 hours of receipt, but not later than 72 hours after the claim.

**Key Policy Details:**

*   **Urgent Care Claims:** Benefit determination is issued as soon as possible, with timely notification of medical exigencies and sufficient information to complete the claim.  A 72-hour notification window applies, with an exception for information receipt and end of the period provided.
*   **Concurrent Care Decisions:**  Approval of ongoing treatment reduces/terminates coverage, notifying the insured of the reduction or termination. The insurance provider will extend the period for treatment beyond the initial authorization.
*   **Pre-Service Claims:** Notification of benefit determination is issued within a reasonable time, up to 15 days after receipt of the request.  Extension may be granted due to unforeseen circumstances, with notification before the initial 15-day period expires.
