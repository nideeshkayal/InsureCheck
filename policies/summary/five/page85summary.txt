Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan requires diligent record-keeping to avoid termination. Upon termination of a Member’s coverage, Hometown Health provides a Certificate of Creditable Coverage identifying the length of coverage. Hometown Health will continue to pay claims incurred during the Member’s coverage period, subject to standard claim payment procedures. Importantly, Hometown Health will not pay for services provided after termination, even with prior authorizations, and is not responsible for expenses incurred after termination. Hometown Health is not liable for benefits received after termination, regardless of circumstances, including accidental events, hospitalizations, or retroactive benefits.  The contract outlines specific exclusions: Hometown Health is not responsible for services received after termination.
