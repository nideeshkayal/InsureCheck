H.O.METOWN HEALTH provides comprehensive health benefits across various plans, including group insurance, student coverage, and labor management plans. Coordination of benefits is crucial; Hometown Health pays benefits to other insurers when overpayment occurs, discharging liability. The plan prioritizes payment to members contingent on coordination.

The policy establishes a determination method for benefit allocation:

*   **No Coordination:** Benefits are determined by the first applicable rule – either no coordination or Dental Plans.
*   **Dental Plans:** If a surgical procedure is submitted, the Member is covered by a Dental Plan.
*   **Dependent/Parent:** Benefits are determined based on parent’s birthday, prioritizing the parent with the earlier birthday.
*   **Dependent Child:** For dependent children, the plan with the parent with custody benefits first.
*   **Inactive Employee:** Coverage is primary for eligible employees.

This page emphasizes the importance of coordination and clarifies specific rules related to coverage and liability.
