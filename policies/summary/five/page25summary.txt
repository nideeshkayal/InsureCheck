Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Coverage includes medically necessary services such as prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance.  The plan mandates a deductible, copays, and coinsurance, with the specific amounts outlined in the Schedule of Benefits. Medical information disclosures require written authorization, with revocation possible. Hometown Health maintains records of provided care, and the plan covers medically necessary services, though other uses and disclosures are subject to consent. Prior authorization is required for many services, and limitations apply depending on the specific treatment and provider. This chapter is essential for reading alongside V (Exclusions and Limitations) and your Policy-specific Schedule of Benefits, as it details Cost Sharing information not listed here.
