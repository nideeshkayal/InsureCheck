H.O.METOWN HEALTH’s 2018 Small Group PPO Plan offers comprehensive health coverage for 65+ members.  The policy mandates timely benefit review, subject to Expedited Appeal for adverse benefit determinations related to Urgent Care claims, a 48-hour notification period, and reduction or termination of coverage upon disagreement.  Expedited External Review is permitted for conditions jeopardizing life, health, or ability to regain maximum function, and when a request for internal appeal is made.  Compliance with U.S. regulations is crucial.  The plan includes a process for expedited external review if an adverse determination involves denial of coverage, requiring a 30-day notification.  The OCHA (Office of Consumer Health Assistance) provides toll-free assistance at 888-333-1597.  The IRO (Independent Review Organization) will review documents and provide additional information within five business days of receiving the request.
