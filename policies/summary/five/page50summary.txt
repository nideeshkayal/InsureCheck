Hometown Health Insurance Policy Summary

This policy outlines coverage for outpatient prescription drug purchases, ensuring member responsibility for costs. Prior Authorization is required for certain medications, contingent upon physician review, FDA approval, and physician determination of appropriateness. Coverage includes Generic, Preferred Brand, Non-Preferred Brand, Specialty Pharmaceuticals, and Diabetic Supplies. Prior authorization requirements may change.

Hometown Health reimburses the Member for the cost of prescription drugs meeting eligibility requirements, less deductible, copayments, coinsurance, and ancillary charges. Members are responsible for their deductible, copayments, and coinsurance, and for the pharmacy’s cost of filling the prescription.

Hometown Health maintains the right to review requests and can deny reimbursement for unused policy. Members are required to present their Hometown Health card when filling prescriptions. Non-participating pharmacies can refuse to fill prescriptions, with payment remaining the Member’s responsibility. The policy highlights that Hometown Health’s payment amount could be less than if a Member used the policy, potentially resulting in higher costs for the Member.
