Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. This plan covers medically necessary allergy testing and treatment, alternative medicine (including acupuncture, holistic medicine, and herbal therapies), Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD) screening and diagnosis, and autism behavior intervention.

**Key Details:**

*   **ASD Coverage:** Coverage is for ASD screening and diagnosis, treatment with a licensed autism behavior interventionist, and treatment plans prescribed by licensed physicians or psychologists.
*   **Prior Authorization:** Treatment for ASD requires Prior Authorization.
*   **Therapy Limits:** Maximum benefit is 600 hours (130 visits) for habilitation and 600 hours (130 visits) for rehabilitation per calendar year.
*   **Copays, Deductibles, and Coinsurance:** Coverage is subject to these conditions.
