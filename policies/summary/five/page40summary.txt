**Hometown Health Insurance Policy – Summary**

This policy, number [Policy Number], covers medical pharmacy benefits, immunizations, hearing/vision screenings, evidence-based preventive care, and radiation therapy. Key exclusions include artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy.

The policy defines medically necessary inpatient hospital care, including acute care, semi-private room, general nursing, diagnostic services, surgical procedures (including obstetrical and orthopedic), maternity and newborn care (up to 48 hours), and short-term rehabilitative services (limited to 60 days).  Inpatient alcohol/substance abuse treatment is subject to medical necessity and maximum treatment duration. Provider visits are covered for medically necessary inpatient care, outpatient care, partial hospitalization, and hospitalization for mental health conditions.
