Hometown Health Insurance Policy

**Coverage:** Medical and pharmacy benefits for acute care, including immunizations & maternity screenings. Coverage is based on durable medical equipment (DME) cost, following national standards.

**Exclusions:** Artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, inpatient hospital care, elderly care, custodial facilities, and psychosocial rehabilitation. Hearing aids are covered for repair/replacement every three years for each ear needing a hearing aid, *only* from approved providers. Home health care is covered for skilled nursing & therapies, *excluding* Bluetooth and GPS. Hospice services cover 6 months or less life expectancy, with limited mental health benefits. Kidney dialysis is not covered by Medicare or federal programs except Medicaid. Lab/diagnostic services include x-rays, fluoroscopy, ultrasounds, electrocardsograms, and imaging (CT, CTA, MRI, PET).

**Contract Termination:** If Provider contract is terminated, Member can continue treatment from Provider if medically necessary, agreed continuity, and Provider agrees.
