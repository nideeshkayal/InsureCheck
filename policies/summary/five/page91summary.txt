**Subrogation and Reimbursement Rights**

This policy grants underwriting rights to third parties liable for injuries or illnesses, potentially triggering subrogation and reimbursement obligations.  A key aspect of this coverage is the right to be reimbursed for damages paid to the third party, provided the policyholder has incurred medical expenses. This includes situations where a third party’s negligence directly causes an injury or illness, resulting in reimbursement.

Specifically, the policy allows for reimbursement when a Member is injured in an accident caused by a third party, and the Member subsequently recovers damages in a lawsuit.  The policy’s objectives center around conserving resources for all members, requiring the third party to bear the costs associated with the injury or illness and preventing unjust enrichment. This is particularly relevant for situations where the third party’s negligence directly impacts the Member’s policy coverage.
