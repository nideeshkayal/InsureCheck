**COBRA Continuation Coverage**

COBRA continuation coverage ends when: 1) You or your covered dependent becomes covered under another plan; 2) You or your dependent becomes eligible for Medicare; 3) Premium payment is delayed; or 4) Your employer ceases offering coverage.

**USERRA** provides continued coverage for employees with USERRA leave, offering the same benefits as COBRA, including eligibility for service in the United States uniformed service up to 24 months.

**Leaves of Absence** provides coverage for employees on leave with or without pay. This coverage is consistent with the Employer Group’s benefits during the period of leave.

**Important Notes:**  The Premium for USERRA continuation coverage is 102% of the employer’s premium, and eligibility requirements are outlined in the Employer Group’s policy document. Contact your employer for specific details regarding election and payment obligations.
