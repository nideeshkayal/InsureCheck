The Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan provides comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, with a defined eligibility period of 19th birthday. The plan includes annual eye exams, lens options, and contact lens services. Specialized Pharmaceutical coverage is limited to participating pharmacies, requiring a certified immunizing pharmacist prescription.

Crucially, the plan excludes services not covered by the EOC (Exclusionary Conditions), such as complications resulting from procedures, injury, or loss related to a riot or criminal act. Cosmetic surgery is excluded, including sagging or extra skin removal.  Furthermore, the plan excludes psychological and physical factors, including self-image, social issues, and medical problems impacting functionality.  

The plan also excludes medications related to erectile function, sensitive areas, and conditions affecting the body’s appearance, like aesthetic treatments.  Rarely, the plan excludes natural remedies related to marijuana use.  Specifically, the plan does not cover treatments for the removal of varicose veins, ablation, or injections, or treatments for appetite control or obesity.
