The Hometown Health Insurance Policy covers medical and pharmacy benefits for individuals experiencing acute care needs, including immunizations and maternity screenings. Coverage is based on durable medical equipment (DME) cost, guided by national standards.  The policy excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, inpatient hospital care, elderly care, custodial facilities, personal items, and psychosocial rehabilitation.  Hearing aids are covered for repair and replacement every three years for each ear requiring a hearing aid, *only* if obtained from approved providers. Home health care is covered for skilled nursing and therapies, *excluding* Bluetooth and GPS-enabled devices. Hospice services cover six months or less life expectancy, with limited eligibility for mental health benefits. Kidney dialysis services are not covered under Medicare or other federal programs except Medicaid, and lab and diagnostic services include x-rays, fluoroscopy, ultrasounds, electrocardsograms, and imaging, including CT, CTA, MRI, and PET.
