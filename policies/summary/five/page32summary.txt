Hometown Health Small Group PPO Plan offers coverage for genetic counseling related to inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, and testing limitations to one lifetime for inherited diseases.  Coverage includes mastectomies for breast cancer survivors, reconstructive surgery for breast cancer, and related complications.  The plan excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, reversal of infertility, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and maternity services related to surrogacy.  Medical care is covered, including office visits, hospital services, ambulatory surgical center services, home health, surgery, and other professional services.  Medical pharmacy benefits apply to these services, with exceptions for separate deductible and medical deductible benefits.
