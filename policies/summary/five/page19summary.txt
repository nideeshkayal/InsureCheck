Hometown Health’s Small Group PPO plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. It includes medically necessary services requiring prior authorization, telehealth, and urgent care, with copays and coinsurance. Key coverage details include defined access to medical information restricted to authorized personnel, ensuring patient privacy and security. The plan’s operations encompass disease and case management programs, risk management, auditing, and underwriting. Transitioning policies allows for updates to other health plans.  The policy also addresses disclosures to family members and specific situations like military and veterans, public health risks, and potential threats to health and safety.  It’s crucial to understand the implications of medical information disclosures, including potential for use for quality assessment, fraud detection, and legal compliance.
