**Hometown Health Provider Relationship Plan – Coverage Changes**

This document details dependent status, death, termination of employment, and other key events. Coverage is triggered by the event itself – birth, adoption, or placement.  **Proof of other coverage is required for eligibility**, regardless of the event’s nature.  Coverage is effective upon completion, with dates following the event or the 1st of the following month, as determined by your employer.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Eligibility Verification:** Required forms include a notice of creditable coverage, coordination of benefits, birth certificate, marriage certificate, domestic partnership certificate, qualified medical child support order, court order, proof of residency, and Social Security number.
*   **Notification:** You must provide requested forms within 30 days of the request. Failure to do so can result in loss of enrollment.
*   **Enrollment Changes:** Changes to enrollment based on application submissions *must* be corrected.  This could lead to coverage loss and financial responsibility.
