**Hometown Health Insurance Policy**

**Coverage:** Genetic counseling for inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, and testing limitations. Medical pharmacy benefits for specialized pharmaceuticals (injectables, oral medications, etc.). Mental Health Services with limits defined in Schedule of Benefits and newborn/maternity care with transportation costs.

**Conditions:** Prior authorization required for certain mental health services. Gynecological care: 48-hour stay for vaginal deliveries & 96-hour stay for Cesarean sections – referral required. Dental services: 48-hour stay for jawbone repair & congenital defects.

**Exclusions:** Artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy. Repairs & restorations except medically necessary stabilization/repair of sound teeth after injury. Orthopedic devices & prosthetics limited to Medically necessary braces.
