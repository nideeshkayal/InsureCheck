Hometown Health offers comprehensive coverage for genetic counseling related to inherited disorders, birth defects, chromosome abnormalities, and testing limitations, including mastectomies for breast cancer survivors, reconstructive surgery, and related complications. Coverage includes medical pharmacy benefits for specialized pharmaceuticals, including injectables, oral medications, and medications delivered by other routes. Mental Health Services are covered with limits defined in the Schedule of Benefits, and newborn and maternity care are provided with transportation costs.

The policy explicitly excludes artificial reproduction, fertility promotion, donor sperm/eggs, surrogate mother services, GIFT/ZIFT procedures, low tubal transfers, donor egg retrieval, and surrogacy. Prior Authorization is required for certain mental health services.  Gynecological care is accessible with a 48-hour stay for vaginal deliveries and a 96-hour stay for Cesarean sections, contingent on a referral.  The policy also addresses dental services, including emergency jawbone repair and treatment for congenital defects.
