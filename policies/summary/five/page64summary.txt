Hometown Health Pediatric Vision Plan offers comprehensive coverage for children aged 0-19, excluding reproductive technologies. Prescription medications not acting within their license are excluded. Drug dispensed by non-participating pharmacies are also excluded. Quantities exceeding a 30-day supply are excluded, requiring refill.  The Formulary Exclusions List, including Non-Formulary, FDA-Limited by Federal Law to Investigation drugs and medications for impotence, excludes prescriptions for these conditions.  Members must present ID cards for refills, and failure to do so results in non-reimbursement.  Hometown Health retains the right to review requests for reimbursement, with cost adjustments potentially limiting member benefits compared to utilizing the policy.
