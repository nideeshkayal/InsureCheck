The Hometown Health 2018 Small Group PPO Plan provides comprehensive coverage for Carson City, Churchill, Clark, Douglas, Eureka, Esmeralda, Humboldt, Lander, Lincoln, Lyon, Mineral, Nye, Pershing, Storey, and Washoe counties. Key details include:

*   **Coverage:** Covered services are medically necessary or listed as benefits, requiring prior authorization.
*   **Cost:** Members are responsible for Covered Services, excluding Non-Preferred Provider charges, co-insurance, prescription drug costs exceeding allowed amounts, ancillary charges, denied claims, and prescription drug from a Non-Participating Pharmacy.
*   **Criminal Acts:** Covered criminal activity.
*   **Custodial Care:** Assistance in daily living activities with a cure requirement, not requiring continued medical personnel.
*   **HDHP:** An HDHP plan, IRS Publication 969, or Revenue Procedure 2017-37, requiring pre-tax HSA contributions.
*   **Hospital:** A legally operated facility defined as an Acute care or Tertiary Care Hospital.
*   **Network Limitation:** Members in Nevada must receive services from Hometown Health and OneHealth network providers at the Preferred Provider In-Network rate.
