Medicare provides crucial contact information for filing complaints regarding JeffersonHealthPlans Flex (PPO) coverage. The page emphasizes the importance of understanding your coverage details and potential reimbursement obligations. Medicare offers 24/7 support through the phone at 877-486-2048 (TTY) and website at www.Medicare.gov.

It highlights the need to review Chapter 7 for reimbursement details and offers Medicare contact information: 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) and 24/7 support through the website (www.Medicare.gov).  The page also introduces the Medicare Complaint Form (www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx) for submitting complaints effectively.
