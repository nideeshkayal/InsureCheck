Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides affordable healthcare coverage through eligibility based on the Jefferson Health Network. It covers medical services, including doctor visits, tests, and procedures, with out-of-network charges separate. Emergency care is available at Jefferson Health. Drug costs are covered, but separate costs apply.

The plan offers Part D prescription drug coverage, with restrictions on certain drugs. Coverage is based on the plan’s coverage stage, with a deductible and nothing paid during the initial coverage stage. Vaccines are covered with payment dependent on the delivery method. Quality Improvement is emphasized to help control prescription costs. Resources for Medicare assistance and the State Health Insurance Assistance Program are available.

You have the right and responsibility to ask about cost stops, appeals, and complaints.  To address problems, use the basic decision and appeal processes.  You must keep medical care through your plan, and you must keep your medical items, services, and drug coverage ongoing.  Termination occurs when your membership ends.
