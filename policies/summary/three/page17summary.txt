**SECTION 7: How Other Insurance Work With Your Plan**

Other insurance coverage is coordinated through Coordination of Benefits.  Each year, you’ll receive a letter listing all other insurance you have.  It’s crucial to review this letter carefully as it dictates how your plan and other coverage will be handled.

**Important:**  Medicare pays first for eligible services, while the primary payer pays the limit.  The primary payer is the employer or group health plan that provides coverage to the employee.

**Key Details:**

*   **Penalty:** Failure to meet payment deadlines results in a penalty.
*   **Payment Options:**  Check, EFT, Phone, and Pay by Phone are required.
*   **Deadline:** Pay within 90 days to avoid penalties.
*   **Emergency:**  Complaints are filed with Chapter 9 if payment is delayed.
*   **Membership End:** Remain liable for penalty upon membership termination.
*   **Coverage:**  Your plan pays first for Medicare-covered services, but the secondary payer pays the remaining costs.
