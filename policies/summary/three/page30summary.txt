The Pennsylvania DHS Office’s ExtraHelp program provides monthly premium payments, yearly deductibles, and prescription copayments to eligible individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs.  Crucially, this program covers individuals through savings and assets, not home or vehicle.  Overpayment is permitted through reimbursement, either forward checks or offset future payments.

The program also includes the AIDSDrugAssistance Program (ADAP), specifically for individuals living with HIV, offering medication access through formulary.  Eligibility is determined by state residency, low-income status, and/or under-insurance.

Individuals can access more information and enrollment details on the Health Department’s website, at 1-800-922-9384 or 711.
