The QIO will initiate a Level 3 appeal following a Level 2 decision. The appeal process involves three independent review organizations, the Independent Review Entity (IRE), which assesses the case’s medical appropriateness. Medicare oversees the IRE’s review, ensuring it determines whether to approve or deny the decision.

A patient must file a Level 2 appeal within 24 hours of requesting a fast review, and if the QIO denies, the patient must continue paying their share of the cost. The IRE reviews the patient’s hospital discharge, looking for medical necessity. If the IRE approves, reimbursement is provided, and coverage extends for inpatient services as long as medically necessary. If the IRE denies, the patient must continue to pay their share of costs and coverage limitations apply.
