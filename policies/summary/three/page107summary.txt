**Summary:**

This page details the Part D drug cost tracking and management for the Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program. It highlights the transition from Initial Coverage to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, which includes exclusions for drugs outside the U.S., territories, or not covered by the plan, as well as drugs at out-of-network pharmacies not meeting plan requirements.

Crucially, the page emphasizes the new requirement to track all Part D drug costs, including payments made, and provides examples of situations where out-of-pocket costs must be submitted. It reinforces the importance of providing receipts for all payments, specifically for coverage-related cases.  The year-to-date information summary and specific instructions on how to contribute data are presented.  This page consolidates the essential information regarding Part D drug costs and reimbursement policies for the plan.
