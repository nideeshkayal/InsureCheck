Medicare supplemental coverage offers a wide range of benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, and psychiatric services with coverage levels from $0 to $800. Prosthetic and orthotic device coverage is expanded, including repair and replacement. COPD members receive $0 copay for pulmonary rehabilitation. Telehealth services are covered at $0 copay, and alcohol misuse screening is offered with four counseling sessions per year.

The SET program provides supervised exercise therapy for patients with PAD and referral for PAD treatment, with $0 copay for sessions up to 36 over 12 weeks. Blood-including storage and administration services are covered beginning with the first pint used, and all other components are covered beginning with the first pint used. Prior authorization is required for certain facilities. Network providers are preferred, but cost-sharing may apply for out-of-network services.

Medicare-covered urgent care visits are not waived, and plan must cover urgently needed services and only charge in-network cost sharing. For coverage outside the United States, consult “Worldwide Emergency/Urgent Coverage”.
