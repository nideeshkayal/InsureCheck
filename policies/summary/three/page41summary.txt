The current page details medical service coverage during emergencies, with a focus on the JeffersonHealth Plans Flex (PPO) plan. **You are billed for the full cost of your services,** regardless of whether you have paid more than your plan’s cost sharing for covered services or received a bill for the full cost of covered medical services. **Coverage is dependent on whether you receive services covered by your plan.**

**Clinical research studies** are covered by Medicare for approved studies. **Participants** must meet specific requirements and understand the study’s nature before participating. **If you participate in a Medicare-approved study, Original Medicare typically pays most of the costs.** If you are ineligible for a study, you are responsible for in-network costs. **Documentation is required** to show how much you paid.
