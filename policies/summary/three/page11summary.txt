Medicare members of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) require card-based utilization for covered services and prescription drug access. The Provider & Pharmacy Directory lists network pharmacies, crucial for receiving medical care. Network providers accept payment, and utilizing them is essential for receiving benefits.

Exceptions exist for emergencies and urgent care, but network availability is not guaranteed. Out-of-area dialysis and unauthorized out-of-network providers are permitted under specific conditions.  It is important to note that the Provider & Pharmacy Directory is vital for finding the appropriate pharmacies.  To obtain the most current information, access the plan’s website at www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare.

The Plan’s List of Covered Drugs (Formulary) outlines which prescription drugs are covered under Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO). The plan’s drug list is selected by doctors and pharmacists, meeting Medicare standards.  You can obtain a copy of the Drug List at the plan’s website or Member Relations.
