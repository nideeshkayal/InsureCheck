CHAPTER 3: Using the Plan for Your Medical Services

This chapter focuses on utilizing the plan’s benefits to access necessary medical care. Individuals with employer-sponsored Medicare coverage may benefit from additional support through the Railroad Retirement Board (RRB). Contact the benefits administrator for comprehensive questions regarding your coverage, premiums, and enrollment period.

The plan offers several options for supplemental benefits:

*   **Employer-Sponsored Benefits:**  If you receive benefits from your employer or retirement group, contact the benefits administrator for specific details.
*   **RRB:**  If you receive benefits from an RRB, contact that group's benefits administrator for relevant guidance.
*   **Prescription Drug Coverage:**  If you have other prescription drug coverage through your employer or retirement group, contact that group’s benefits administrator.

Refer to the back of this document for precise phone numbers and resources.  This chapter outlines how to leverage the plan’s resources to ensure optimal healthcare coverage.
