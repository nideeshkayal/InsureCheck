**Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Drug List Updates**

This page details updates to the Drug List, requiring immediate attention. Changes are made annually, starting January 1st, to ensure consistent coverage.  The plan mandates timely request handling for unforeseen drug removals, and will remove unsafed drugs and others withdrawn from the market.

Significant changes occur when adding a new generic version of a brand-name drug or expanding biosimilar options. These changes impact cost-sharing tiers and may restrict drug use.  The plan will notify you at least 30 days before any adjustment, covering a 30-day fill of the drug version you’re utilizing.  Existing changes will not affect you during the current year, except for withdrawal, generic replacement, or other changes.

Changes affecting drugs you are currently taking, except for market withdrawals, generics, or biosimilars, will not affect your usage until January 1st of the following year.
