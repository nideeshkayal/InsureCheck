**Summary:**

This plan mandates full patient responsibility for all drug costs, regardless of provider.  Strict adherence to plan guidelines is crucial, ensuring culturally competent services and accessibility.  Mandatory vaccination administration is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers.

The plan prioritizes providing culturally competent services, offering translation services and Braille materials for non-English speakers.  Interpretation services are available to address questions.  Direct access to specialists is offered for women’s routine and preventive health care, with a plan to locate specialists outside the network for specialized care.

Patients have the right to choose their provider and receive timely access to covered services, including timely prescriptions.  Grievance procedures are available for any information challenges, including filing complaints with Medicare and the Office for Civil Rights.
