**Medicare Information & Assistance**

This page provides crucial information for submitting complaints regarding JeffersonHealthPlans Flex (PPO) coverage.  To file a complaint, contact the Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit at 866-901-7096.  It’s essential to understand your coverage details and potential reimbursement obligations.

The page highlights key payment request procedures, requiring a call to the Pharmacy Department at 866-371-3239.  It also emphasizes the Medicare contact information: 1-800-MEDICARE or 1-800-633-4227.  This information is vital for navigating the Medicare system.  Remember to review Chapter 7 for reimbursement details.
