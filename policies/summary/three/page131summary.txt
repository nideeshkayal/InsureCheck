The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) coverage is subject to medical care decisions and appeals. Seeking medical items, services, and Medicare Part B prescription drugs is required as evidence of coverage. The initial review begins with determining the covered decision. If a decision is made, a Level 1 appeal is initiated, which is dismissed if not fully agreed with. A Level 2 appeal can be requested if the initial decision is unsatisfactory.

For Part D drug appeals, if the request is not fully agreed with, a Level 2 appeal is requested. Part D appeals are discussed further in Section 6 of this chapter. If you are not satisfied with the decision at Level 2, you can continue through additional appeal levels (Section 9).

To help with coverage decisions or appeals, resources are available at Member Relations, Medicare’s website (www.CMS.gov/Medicare/CMS-Forms/CMS-Forms/downloads/cms1696.pdf), or your doctor can request a representative. You can also request a decision through the Member Relations website (www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare/form).  It is crucial to provide a signed form to ensure proper processing.
