**Summary:**

Secondary payer coverage is permitted under Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) for eligible members, contingent upon approval by other insurers. This arrangement enables coverage for additional services rendered by secondary payers.

Subrogation rights exist, granting the plan the potential to seek reimbursement from the primary insurer if the insured incurs costs associated with services provided by the secondary payer.  This is a crucial element of the coverage model.

Specific terms governing these rights and obligations are outlined in the plan’s documentation. Thorough review of the plan’s specific terms is vital to fully understand responsibilities and potential liabilities.
