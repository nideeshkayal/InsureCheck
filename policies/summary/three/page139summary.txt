**Medicare Part B Prescription Drug Insurance Policy**

**Coverage:** Coverage amounts and limits are defined. 72-hour authorization deadline applies for requests. Appeals lead to Level 2, then Level 3, and finally, a dollar value determination and letter outlining steps.

**Appeal:** Three levels: Level 2, then Level 3, culminating in a dollar value determination and letter.  Don’t appeal if the IRE approves.

**Payment:** Payments must be sent within 30 days upon approval. Payment within 60 days after appeal submission.

**Section 6: Part D Coverage:** Drugs must be medically accepted for their indication. Consult Chapter 5 for detailed information on Part D drugs, rules, restrictions, and costs. The Drug List represents the current permitted drug list.

**Important:** IRE decisions must be paid within 30 days.
