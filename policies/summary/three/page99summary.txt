Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Part D prescription drug coverage updates reflect significant changes. The plan now excludes coverage for drugs not covered by Medicare, Part B, or federally approved programs like the American Hospital Formulary Service Drug Information System. Non-prescription drugs, fertility treatments, cosmetic drugs, and sexual dysfunction treatments are not covered.

Specifically, the plan clarifies that “ExtraHelp” programs do not cover drugs not typically covered under the plan.  Users must proactively check the Drug List annually and contact their state Medicaid program for detailed coverage information.

For long-term care (LTC) facilities, prescriptions are typically provided through the facility’s pharmacy or a network pharmacy, ensuring residents can continue receiving their medication.  The Provider & Pharmacy Directory (www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare) is recommended for further details, particularly regarding LTC facility pharmacy networks.
