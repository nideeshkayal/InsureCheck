Medicare will not cover new clinical research studies unless Medicare covers item services, even if the participant is not actively involved. Medicare covers item services for inpatient, skilled nursing, or facility care, but this coverage is limited and doesn’t extend to non-participating trials.

Section 7 outlines ownership of durable medical equipment (DME). Members must make a certain number of payments under the plan before owning DME items, even if they make up to 12 consecutive payments under Original Medicare.  If you switch to Original Medicare, you’ll need to make 13 new consecutive payments to own the DME item.

Existing payments made under Original Medicare do not count toward ownership of the item.  Member Relations should be contacted for further details.
