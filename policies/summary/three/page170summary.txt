Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides a transition period until January 1st for members to choose between continuing coverage or switching to alternative Medicare Advantage plans.  A key change is the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, beginning January 1st, for new beneficiaries.  Members must understand potential changes to coverage amounts, deductibles, and annual maximums.  

Eligible members can participate in special enrollment periods triggered by specific circumstances, such as moving, Medicaid eligibility, potential contract violations, care within an institution, or enrollment in the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE).

To end your membership, members should enroll in a new plan, either a new Medicare health plan or a separate Medicare prescription drug plan.  The new plan will automatically deactivate your existing Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) policy when coverage begins.  Contact Member Relations or Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE (24/7) for more information and to request discontinuation of coverage.
