Medicare supplemental coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, and psychiatric services. Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring a 20% coinsurance.

Medicare-covered ambulatory surgical center services cost $245. Telehealth services, including diagnosis, evaluation, and treatment of acute illnesses or stroke, are covered for patients with an in-person visit within 6 months prior to telehealth. Exceptions are permitted for certain circumstances. Virtual check-ins are available for new patients, and evaluations aren’t directly related to office visits within 7 days.

Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) includes stipulations regarding coverage for other plans. Podiatry services are covered, with $35 copay per visit and $35 copay per routine visit. Prostate cancer screening exams, digital rectal exams, PSA tests, and prosthetic/orthotic devices are covered annually for members with age 50 and older.
