**New & Important Information**

This page details Medicare supplemental coverage for hospice care, including DME, diabetic monitoring, emergency room coverage, and homehealthagencycare.  Emergency room visits are $100 with medical conditions contingent on coverage, while homehealthagencycare is covered for Medicare-certified individuals at risk for HIV.

**Key Coverage Details:**

*   **Hospice:**  Payments are shared, with PartD drug costs potentially being covered if unrelated to hospice.  Both hospice and the plan’s PartD benefit are not covered simultaneously.
*   **Immunizations:** Coverage includes pneumonia, influenza, flu/influenza shots, Hepatitis B, and COVID-19 vaccines, based on risk level.  High/intermediate risk individuals receive additional vaccinations.
*   **Inpatient Hospital Care:** In-network and out-of-network hospital care is covered, starting with a $250 daily copay. Hospital stay starts the day you are admitted.
