**Insurance Policy Summary**

This document covers Medicare Part D late enrollment penalties. Starting six months after initial enrollment, the penalty resets annually based on premium changes. Failure to meet payments results in disenrollment and loss of coverage.

**Key Details:**

*   **Penalty Calculation:** Percentage of average premium, annual adjustments.
*   **Payment Options:** Check, EFT, Phone, Pay by Phone.
*   **Deadline:** Pay within 90 days to avoid penalties.
*   **Emergency:** Complaint (agrievance) filed with Chapter 9 if payment is delayed.
*   **Membership End:** Remain liable for penalty upon membership termination.
*   **Research:** Consent required for clinical research; disclose interest.

**Important:** Name, address, or phone number changes required. Address any changes to other insurance coverage.
