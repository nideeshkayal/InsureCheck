Jefferson Health Plans Flex(PPO) offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare-eligible medical services, with the primary payer – typically employer or group health plans – covering the remaining costs.  Coverage is coordinated through Coordination of Benefits (COB), where each year, you receive a letter detailing all other insurance you carry. Failure to meet payment deadlines results in penalties, and a formal appeal process is available for delayed payments.

The plan’s coverage is structured with a focus on Medicare-eligible services, with the secondary payer responsible for remaining costs.  A key point is that coverage decisions, appeals, and complaints are handled through Chapter 9, a process for reviewing and potentially modifying coverage decisions.  Patient support is available through the Member Relations department via phone and website, with options for interpreter services for non-English speakers.
