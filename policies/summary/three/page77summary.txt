Medicare supplemental coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, and psychiatric services. Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring a 20% coinsurance.

Key new details include:

*   **Prosthetic and Orthotic Devices:** Coverage for prosthetic and orthotic devices, including repair and replacement, is included with Medicare-covered care.
*   **Pulmonary Rehabilitation:** Coverage for Medicare-covered pulmonary rehabilitation services is $0 copay per visit for COPD members.
*   **Remote Access Technology (Teladoc):** $0 copay for Teladoc services, offering 24/7 access to credentialed doctors.
*   **Alcohol Misuse Screening:** A crucial new component – Medicare provides screening and counseling to address alcohol misuse, with potential for four face-to-face counseling sessions per year for positive results.
