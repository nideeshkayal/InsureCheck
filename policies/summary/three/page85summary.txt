This Medicare Supplemental Insurance plan provides coverage for medical services, including physical therapy, psychiatric care, prosthetic device repairs, and COPD member benefits, up to \$800 annually.  Coverage is phased: WelcometoMedicarePreventive Visit is covered for the first 12 months with out-of-network services exceeding \$50,000 per year being excluded.

Crucially, the plan does not cover cosmeticsurgery or procedures not specifically listed. It includes cases of accidental injuries or improvement of a deformed body member’s functionality, covering all stages of reconstruction, including breast reconstruction and symmetrical breast appearance.  Experimental medical and surgical procedures, equipment, and medications are excluded.  Fees charged for care by immediate relatives or members of the household are not covered.  The JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) 82 plan is covered with a \$0 copay for emergent/urgent services.
