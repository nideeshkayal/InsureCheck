**Coverage for Clinical Research Studies**

Under JeffersonHealth Plans Flex (PPO) coverage for clinical research studies is contingent on whether you participate. Medicare-approved clinical research studies are covered by Original Medicare, typically paying most costs. However, if you are ineligible, you’re responsible for in-network costs.

Documentation is mandatory to show your portion of the cost for participation. This includes room and board, operation, treatment of side effects, and other medical services as part of the study.

Original Medicare covers routine services, but your plan’s benefits may add additional costs. You’ll receive the difference between the study’s cost and your in-network costsharing, reflecting your plan’s standard rates.  It’s crucial to submit documentation, such as provider bills, to confirm your participation and receive payment.
