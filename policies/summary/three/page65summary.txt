Medicare supplemental coverage for hospice includes shared payments between PartD drug costs and hospice costs, with no simultaneous coverage for both. Immunizations are covered for risk levels, with additional vaccinations for high/intermediate risk individuals. Inpatient hospital care is covered, starting at $250/day for Medicare-covered stays, with a 90-day residency, and includes mental health services requiring a hospital stay. Prior authorization is required for all inpatient admissions, especially for new admissions.

The policy covers medical necessity, requiring physician approval for all admissions, particularly for new admissions. Costs are tiered, with $0 copays per day for days 1-6 for Medicare-covered stays, $800 copays per lifetime reserved for 60 life-time years, and $0 copays per day for the remaining days.  Coverage extends to diagnostic tests, X-rays, radium therapy, and specialist visits, including technician material and services.  Physician visits are covered, and the policy extends to SNF facilities.
