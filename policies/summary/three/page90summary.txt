The plan offers Part D prescription drug coverage up to $800 annually, subject to specific rules. Coverage is for all medications, excluding routine chiropractic care. Cosmetic surgery, non-listed procedures, and emergency/urgent care are excluded. The Drug List defines covered medications requiring approval, and prescriptions must originate through an approved pharmacy or mail-order service.

Importantly, the plan now includes specialized pharmacy services for home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC) facilities, restricted medications, and mail-order pharmacy services requiring special handling. Coverage expands for Part D Prescription Drugs Flex (PPO), outlining key reimbursement considerations.

To get coverage for out-of-network prescriptions, you must submit a detailed receipt and explanation to the Plan. Reimbursement is limited to a 30-day supply. The plan provides guidance on requesting reimbursement from the plan, utilizing Chapter 7 for specific payment procedures.
