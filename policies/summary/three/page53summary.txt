This Medicare Part A and B plan offers coverage for chronic low back pain treatment with a maximum annual out-of-pocket expense of $10,000.  Coverage includes ambulance services at $255 per-way for Medicare-covered ground ambulance and $20% coinsurance for air ambulance.  Additional eight sessions are covered for improvement, with no systemic causes, surgery, or pregnancy.

The plan covers annual wellness visits, with screening lab services as needed, costing $0 copay.  Annual physical examinations are covered per year, with screening laboratory services as needed, with costs of $0. Annual wellness visits are free, and coverage is once every 12 months.

Notably, there is no insurance, copayment, or deductible for qualified individuals at risk of osteoporosis; the following Medicare-covered bone mass measurement services are covered every 24 months or more frequently if medically necessary. Breast cancer screening (mammograms) is covered for qualified individuals at ages 35 and 39, and one screening mammogram every 24 months for women aged 40 and older. Cardiac rehabilitation services are covered for members who meet certain conditions with a $0 copay per service, with rigorous, more intensive programs typically requiring more oversight.
