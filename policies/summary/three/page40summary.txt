This section details how to obtain medical services during an emergency.  Emergency care is covered for work and out-of-network providers, but out-of-network providers bear full costs, excluding Medicare emergencies.  Care is prioritized when the PCP’s office is unavailable; calling the PCP’s office is required unless directed otherwise.  During a state of disaster, individuals remain entitled to care, and the plan provides this support.  For emergencies outside the United States, coverage is permitted under specific circumstances.  Resources include a website ([www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare](https://www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare)) for finding in-network urgent care centers and detailed information on disaster-related care.
