**Summary of Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO)**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare Part D drug prescriptions, extending to brand-name, generic, and biological medications. Coverage is 30-day supply limited, except for routine chiropractic care.

A crucial new feature is a temporary supply of medications, offering a 90-day membership for new members and extending coverage for members with more than 90 days of plan participation. For long-term care stays, a 31-day emergency supply is provided.

Specifically, Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers detailed coverage levels for 92 sections, including a significant 93 percentage segment. Users must utilize the “Real-Time Benefit Tool” or contact Member Relations for coverage determination.  Changes in care levels necessitate an exception request.

The plan emphasizes requesting exceptions through Chapter 9, Section 6.4, detailing procedures and deadlines for fair and prompt request handling.
