The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers medical coverage through employer-sponsored benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage.  A new 2025 Evidence of Coverage is implemented, signifying updated guidelines for medical necessity.  Out-of-network providers incur significantly higher costs, necessitating prior authorization.  The PCP is responsible for coordinating care and sending records to providers, with the option to request prior authorization.  A member can request prior authorization, but clinical information is required from their provider to approve requests.  If a specialist leaves, the plan adjusts provider networks. If a primary care provider leaves, the member receives notification.  If other providers leave, the member receives assignment to a new provider or choice of provider.  Contact the plan for assistance with finding a new provider.  Emergency care is covered for work or out-of-network providers, but out-of-network providers are responsible for full costs, except for Medicare emergencies.
