**SECTION 3: Secondary Payersubrogation rights**

This section details the rights and responsibilities regarding secondary payers and subrogation.  Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) allows for secondary payer coverage, requiring the plan to be approved by other insurers to cover additional services. Subrogation rights exist, meaning the plan may seek reimbursement from the primary insurer if the insured is responsible for the cost of services. This process is governed by established procedures.  Careful review of the plan’s specific terms is crucial to understand these rights and obligations.
