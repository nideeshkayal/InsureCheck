**Medical Benefits Chart – Medicare Part A & B**

This plan offers maximum out-of-pocket expense of $10,000 annually for Medicare Part A and Part B services, specifically for chronic low back pain treatment. Coverage is 12 weeks or longer, with no systemic causes, surgery, or pregnancy. Additional eight sessions are covered if patient demonstrates improvement.

Coverage includes ambulance services: $255 per-way for Medicare-covered ground ambulance and $20% coinsurance for Medicare-covered air ambulance.  This coverage is not waived if readmitted to the hospital. Ambulance services, whether emergency or non-emergency, include fixed wing and rotary wing ambulances, to the nearest appropriate facility. Medicare ambulance benefits are transportation benefits, and without transportation, no treatments are covered. Costs are the member’s responsibility. Multiple providers may be paid only to the ambulance provider. Prior authorization is required for non-emergency ground or air ambulance trips. Annual physical examination is covered per year, with screening laboratory services as needed. $0 copay for annual physical exams. Annual wellness visit is free, and coverage is once every 12 months.
