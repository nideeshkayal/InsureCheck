**Summary**

JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) provides comprehensive coverage for patients in the United States.  Full patient responsibility for all drug costs is a core requirement, necessitating adherence to plan guidelines for culturally competent services and accessibility. Mandatory vaccination administration is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers. Patients have the right to choose providers and timely access to covered services, including prescriptions.

Key priorities include privacy protection, with all medical records secured by law. Unauthorized individuals cannot view patient information. Exceptions exist for quality-of-care reviews, requiring written permission. Medicare mandates sharing health information, including PartD prescriptions, following Federal statutes and regulations.

Patient rights include reviewing records, requesting copies, and modifying coverage. Member Relations provides support at 2025EvidenceofCoverage for JeffersonHealth Plans Flex (PPO).
