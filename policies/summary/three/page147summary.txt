**Appeal Process Summary**

This page details the appeal process for Part D drug prescriptions. Medicare oversees reviews, with a 24-hour deadline for Level 2 appeals and expedited decisions for physicians.

**Step 1:** Contact the IRE for Level 2 appeal instructions. If not completed, the claim will be forwarded. The IRE reviews the casefile to ensure all information is considered.

**Step 2:** Review the casefile for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) coverage. The IRE will evaluate the request based on available evidence.

**Step 3:** Decision & Response: The independent review organization provides an answer to your Level 2 appeal. If favorable, provide drug coverage within 24 hours.

**Step 4:** Further Appeals: If denied, you can appeal further. Additional appeal levels are available after Level 2, with detailed instructions in the notice.
