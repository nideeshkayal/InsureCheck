Medicare Part A and B plans offer coverage for chronic low back pain with a maximum annual out-of-pocket expense of $10,000.  Coverage includes ambulance services ($255 per-way for Medicare-covered ground ambulance and $20% coinsurance for air ambulance), eight sessions of improvement for chronic conditions, excluding surgery or pregnancy. Wellness visits are covered, including lab services ($0 copay), annual physical exams ($0), and annual wellness visits (free, 12-month coverage).  Colorectal cancer screening is covered for qualified individuals 35 and 39 years old, with a minimum limit of 120 months and every 24 months thereafter. Cardiac rehabilitation services are covered for members meeting conditions, with a $0 copay per service.  Barium enema is an alternative to colonoscopy for high-risk patients, and coverage details are in the Medical Benefits Chart. Priorauthorization is required for supplemental services.  Depressionscreening is offered, with a $200 maximum coverage amount per year.
