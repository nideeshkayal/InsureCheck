Jefferson Health Plans provide a structured process for addressing grievances, emphasizing timely responses. When a patient files a complaint, it’s classified as a “grievance,” requiring immediate action. The formal procedure involves contacting Member Relations via phone or written request, with a 60-day response time after receiving the complaint.

The process then initiates an Investigation by an Appeals Specialist, which must be completed within 30 days of receiving the complaint.  Expedited grievance processes are available for situations requiring faster resolution, up to 14 days, and may extend this timeframe if justification exists.  Furthermore, the patient has the right to request expedited grievance resolution for decisions on coverage, appeals, or drug requests, including plans with Part D benefits.
