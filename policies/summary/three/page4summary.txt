Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides affordable healthcare coverage through eligibility based on the Jefferson Health Network. It covers medical services, including doctor visits, tests, and procedures, with out-of-network charges separate. Emergency care is available at Jefferson Health. Drug costs are covered, but separate costs apply.

The plan offers Part D prescription drug coverage, with restrictions on certain drugs.  Coverage is based on the plan’s coverage stage, with a deductible and nothing paid during the initial coverage stage.  Vaccines are covered with payment dependent on the delivery method.  The plan emphasizes Quality Improvement, which helps with prescription costs.  It also provides resources for Medicare assistance and the State Health Insurance Assistance Program.
