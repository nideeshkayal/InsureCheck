This page details Medicare supplemental benefits for chronic low back pain, including DME, diabetic monitoring, and emergency room coverage. Medicare-covered emergency room visits are $100, contingent on medical conditions.

Supplemental services, such as DME, bed orders, and IV infusion pumps, require prior authorization, with out-of-network costs being the same as in-network. Routine hearing exams are covered at $35, while specialist visits may add $1,000.

For HIV screening, coverage is $0 copay for every 12-month screen, and women during pregnancy receive up to three screenings. Home healthcare services, including home health agency care, are covered for Medicare-covered individuals who are at increased risk for HIV infection.
