**Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO – Policy Analysis**

**Coverage:** $2,000 max drug cost, $1/day limit.

**Special Needs Plans (D-SNP):** $1000 exception for drugs not on formulary, eligibility for “ExtraHelp.” Home Health Aide support, Hospice care.

**Medicaid:** Joint federal/state coverage for low-income individuals.

**Medically Accepted Indication:** FDA drug information required.

**Medicare:** 65+, disabilities, end-stage renal disease. OpenEnrollment allows plan changes. MedicareAdvantage OpenEnrollment allows plan changes. Part D prescription drug coverage for eligible individuals.

**Key Terms:** D-SNP, Medicare, Medicaid, FDA, OpenEnrollment, HMO, PPO, MSA.
