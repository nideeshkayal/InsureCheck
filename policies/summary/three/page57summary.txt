This page details Medicare Part A and B coverage for chronic low back pain, emphasizing diabetes screening. Individuals with risk factors like high blood pressure, abnormal cholesterol and triglyceride levels, obesity, or a history of high blood sugar are eligible for screenings every 12 months.

Diabetes screening is covered for qualified individuals 35 and 39, with a minimum of 120 months and annual physical exams costing $0. Coverage includes fast glucose tests, annual physical exams ($0), and annual wellness visits (free, 12-month). Priorauthorization is required for supplemental services.

Special considerations are given to diabetes self-management training, diabetic supplies, and diabetic foot disease coverage. Prior authorizations are required for monitoring supplies from non-preferred manufacturers.  Medicare-covered diabetic therapeutic shoes or inserts are covered at 20% coinsurance, while Medicare-covered diabetic monitoring supplies are covered at 0% coinsurance.
