**JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) 82**

**Coverage:** Up to \$800 annually. Coverage includes physical therapy, psychiatric care, prosthetic device repairs, and COPD member benefits.

**Key Terms:** Phase 1: WelcometoMedicarePreventive Visit (first 12 months, out-of-network exceeding \$50,000 is excluded). Cosmetic surgery and procedures not listed are excluded.

**Conditions:** Emergency/urgent care is \$0 copay. Accident/improved body member function (breast reconstruction, symmetrical breast appearance) is covered. Experimental procedures, equipment, and medications are excluded. Immediate relatives/household members are not covered. Dentalcare is required for deformed body member functionality.

**Excluded:** Routine chiropractic care is not reasonable/necessary. Radial keratotomy, LASIK, and other lowvision aids are excluded.
