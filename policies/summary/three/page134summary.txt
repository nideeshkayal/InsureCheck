**Summary:**

This document details the process for coverage decisions, starting with requesting coverage for medical care.  Coverage decisions are triggered within 14 calendar days for medical item coverage, or 72 hours for medical services.  For Medicare Part B, this is expedited to 72 hours.

If a fast coverage decision is requested without doctor approval, standard deadlines apply.  If a complaint is filed, a letter stating standard deadlines will be sent.

To appeal, you must explain the decision and present a request for additional time to ensure adequate coverage.  The appeal process differs from decisions and appeals, and a fast complaint is different.

If you believe the decision should not be taken, file a fast complaint.  The process involves explaining the decision and requesting further time to review it, and will send a written statement explaining why coverage was denied.
