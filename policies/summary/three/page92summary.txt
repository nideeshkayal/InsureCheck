The Plan’s Drug List outlines covered medications for Medicare Part D, prioritizing brand-name drugs, generic drugs, and biological products. Coverage extends to all medications except routine chiropractic care, with a 30-day supply limit.

Key differences include specialized pharmacy services for home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC), restricted medication, and mail-order pharmacy services.

To determine which drugs are covered, users can check the Drug List electronically, visit the website (www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare), call Member Relations, or utilize the “Real-Time Benefit Tool.”

Certain drugs have specific restrictions regarding how and when they are covered. The plan’s rules are designed to encourage the most effective use of medications, prioritizing lower-cost options.
