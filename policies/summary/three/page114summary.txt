**Part D Prescription Drug Coverage**

Under the Catastrophic Coverage Stage of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO), members experience no Part D drug costs.  Once the Catastrophic Coverage Stage is reached, coverage is reduced to the *full* cost of each drug until the end of the year.

A significant change is the requirement for Part D vaccines.  Maximum monthly costs are $35 for a single supply of each drug, tiered based on slower-priced cases and maximum supply amounts.  Vaccine coverage is determined by a two-part system: the vaccine’s cost itself, and administrative costs.  Members must pay for vaccine administration; the cost varies based on circumstances.

For adult Part D vaccinations, reimbursement is typically the *full* amount paid. For other vaccinations, reimbursement amounts are reduced by coinsurance or copayment.  Vaccination procedures, as outlined in Chapter 7, are crucial to follow.  For most adult Part D vaccinations, reimbursement is fully applicable.
