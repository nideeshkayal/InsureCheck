The PACEplan offers comprehensive coverage for elderly individuals, including Medicare beneficiaries, Medicaid recipients, and those with disabilities. It provides $2,000 maximum drug cost, a $1/day limit, and specialized coverage for individuals with disabilities, Hospice care, and Medigap.

Key coverage details include prosthetics and orthotics, quality improvement organization (QIO) checks, quantity limits for drugs, and “Real-TimeBenefitTool” for accurate formulary information. SpecialEnrollment Period is critical for changes in health or drug plans.

The plan also covers Rehabilitation Services, including physical therapy, speech therapy, and occupational therapy. It emphasizes that certain drugs are excluded from PartD, and annual cost limitations apply. Urgent Needed Services are also covered.
