The plan offers coverage for Medicare Part D drug prescriptions, prioritizing brand-name, generic, and biological medications. Coverage extends to all medications except routine chiropractic care, with a 30-day supply limit. Users must utilize the “Real-Time Benefit Tool” or contact Member Relations to determine coverage.

**New Information:** The plan now covers a temporary supply of medications, offering a 90-day membership to new members and extending coverage for members with more than 90 days of plan participation. For members with significant long-term care stays, the plan provides a 31-day emergency supply.  Specific coverage levels for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) are detailed, including a 92 section.
