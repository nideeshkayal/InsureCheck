**Summary:**

This chapter details crucial insurance terms for Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO members, particularly focusing on termination due to policy violations. The core issue is the potential Part D shortage, necessitating immediate member contact regarding health concerns. The “Appeal” process allows members to dispute coverage denials.

Coinsurance, a percentage required to pay for services or prescriptions, is crucial. Combined Maximum Out-of-Pocket (COPO) amounts define the maximum annual cost for Part A and Part B services from both providers and out-of-network, with a maximum of $2,000 spent on Part D. Cost-sharing refers to the amount a member pays when receiving medical services or prescriptions.

Coverage Determination dictates whether a drug is covered, and if not, the determination is required. Creditable Prescription Drug Coverage ensures prescriptions meet a minimum standard of payment.
