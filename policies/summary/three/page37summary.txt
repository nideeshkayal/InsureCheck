Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers medical coverage through Employer-Sponsored Benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage. Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services.  The plan utilizes a benefits chart to determine coverage amounts.  Key changes include a new 2025 Evidence of Coverage for the plan, signifying updated guidelines.  Out-of-network providers incur significantly higher costs, necessitating prior authorization.  The PCP is responsible for coordinating care and sending records to providers.  A member may request prior authorization, but clinical information is required from their provider to approve requests.  If a specialist leaves, the plan will adjust provider networks.  If a primary care provider leaves, the member receives notification.  If any other providers leave, the member receives assignment to a new provider, or has the right to choose a new provider.  It is crucial to contact the plan for assistance in finding a new provider.
