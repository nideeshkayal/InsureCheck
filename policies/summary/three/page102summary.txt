The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Part D prescription drug coverage has revised exclusions, significantly altering prior limitations.  Non-covered drugs like fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments are now excluded, necessitating proactive annual review.  A new Drug Management Program (DMP) is implemented for opioid use, requiring provider collaboration to determine appropriate dosages.  The plan now mandates accurate prescription details, including dosage and pharmacy affiliations, before coverage is applied.  Furthermore, members are now flagged for potential errors, medication use errors, or unsafe dosage levels.  Appeal rights are now guaranteed if you believe a decision was made incorrectly.  If you appeal, the plan will review your case and provide a new decision.  Enrollment in the Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program is now voluntary and free. Members who qualify for the program receive a comprehensive medication list and information on safe disposal.
