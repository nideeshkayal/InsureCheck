The plan provides coverage for Part D prescription drugs up to \$800 annually, subject to specific rules. Coverage is available for all medications, excluding routine chiropractic care. Cosmetic surgery and other non-listed procedures are excluded. Emergency/urgent care is covered at a \$0 copay. Accident/improved body member function is covered, but experimental procedures, equipment, and medications are not. Immediate relatives/household members are not covered. Dentalcare is required for deformed body member functionality.

The plan’s Drug List defines covered medications, requiring medical justification and approval before coverage is provided. Prescription fulfillment must occur through an approved network pharmacy or plan mail-order service. Network pharmacies are essential for receiving covered prescriptions, and the Drug List represents all Part D prescription drugs on the plan.
