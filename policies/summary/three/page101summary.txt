The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Part D prescription drug coverage has updated, significantly altering exclusions. Previously, non-covered drugs like fertility treatments and sexual dysfunction treatments were excluded, requiring proactive annual review and contact with the State Medicaid program.

Crucially, “ExtraHelp” programs now do not cover drugs not typically covered under the plan, necessitating increased vigilance. Long-term care facilities receive drug coverage through their pharmacy or network, a secondary aspect to employer or retiree group coverage.

Prescriptions for drug-related errors, potential medication use errors, and unsafe dosage levels are now flagged.  The Drug Management Program (DMP) is implemented to ensure safe opioid use, requiring provider collaboration to determine appropriate medication choices when necessary.  Furthermore, the plan now requires accurate prescription details, including dosage and pharmacy affiliations, before coverage is applied, and detailed letter communication regarding limitations on coverage.
