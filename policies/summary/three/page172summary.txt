To end your Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) membership, Medicare may deactivate your account if you withhold information about other insurance coverage, engage in disruptive behavior, or allow unauthorized use of your membership card.  If you are incarcerated or not a U.S. citizen/lawfully present, or if you lie about other coverage, your membership will be terminated. Medicare may investigate your case with the InspectorGeneral.  If you experience a shortage in Part D, you will lose prescription drug coverage.  It’s crucial to contact MemberRelations immediately if you feel your membership is being ended due to a health-related reason.  Do not leave your plan without prior approval from Medicare.
