The Plan's Drug List outlines covered medications, prioritizing Medicare Part D prescription drugs. It includes brand-named drugs, generic drugs, and biological products, with a focus on those approved by Medicare. Coverage extends to all medications, excluding routine chiropractic care, and is limited to a 30-day supply.

Importantly, the plan now offers specialized pharmacy services for home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC), restricted medications, and mail-order pharmacy services requiring special handling.

Coverage expands for Part D PPO, with specific reimbursement considerations.  The Drug List excludes all other prescription drugs, and in some cases, Medicare does not cover certain types of drugs.  For more detailed information regarding specific exceptions, consult Chapter 9.2025.
