The page details Level 3 appeals, specifically for Medicare coverage, following a Level 2 appeal decision.  If the Council denies the appeal, the beneficiary must continue coverage within 60 days.  Accepting the Council’s decision results in the appeal process terminating, and the beneficiary must proceed to the next level of review (Level 4) or continue with the next level.

Level 3 appeals are handled by Administrative Law Judges within the Federal government, who review the appeal and provide an answer.  If the answer is ‘yes,’ the appeal process is over.  The Judge must authorize or provide coverage, within 72 hours, or make a payment later than 30 calendar days after receiving the decision. If the answer is ‘no,’ the appeal process may continue.

The page highlights a crucial point: for most situations, Level 2 appeal decisions lead to the next level of review.  Specifically, a Level 2 appeal concerning a Level 1 appeal, and both appeals have been turned down, requires further review, and the beneficiary receives a notice explaining the next steps (Level 4 appeal).
