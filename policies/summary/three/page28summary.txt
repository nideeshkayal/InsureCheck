Pennsylvania Department of Human Services ( DHS )provides assistance to individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs. The Medicare.gov website offers information on drug costs, including help with lowering expenses.

Medicare’s “ExtraHelp” program offers financial support for prescription drug costs for eligible individuals. It provides monthly premium payments, yearly deductibles, and prescription copayments. Qualified individuals receive this assistance through savings and assets, not their home or vehicle.

To apply, call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or visit www.dhs.pa.gov.  The DHS Office at 1-800-772-1213 offers assistance with evidence of proper copayments and provides member relations services.
