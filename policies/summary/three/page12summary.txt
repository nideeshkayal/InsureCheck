The Plan’s Monthly Medicare PartBPremium is required for all members of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO). This premium includes the PartB premium, potentially plus a PartA premium, which affects eligibility for premium-free PartA.

Section 4.3 details the late enrollment penalty – a significant additional premium incurred if coverage is not creditable after six months without coverage. The penalty is calculated as a percentage per month, beginning six months after initial enrollment.

The Plan emphasizes that creditable prescription drug coverage meets Medicare minimum standards and is expected to pay at least as much as Medicare’s standard prescription drug plan.  It’s crucial to note that prescription drug discounts, free clinics, and drug discount websites are not covered.

The Plan also states that prescription drug coverage is defined as expected to pay, and that non-creditable coverage is not permitted. This information is essential for understanding the penalty’s calculation.
