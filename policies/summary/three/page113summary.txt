The Part D prescription drug cost policy outlines a crucial shift for members utilizing Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO).  Beginning with the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, members experience no Part D drug costs.  Once the Catastrophic Coverage Stage is reached, members are required to pay nothing for their covered Part D drugs until the end of the year.

A key change is the requirement for Part D vaccines, with a maximum monthly cost of $35 for a single supply of each drug, tiered based on slower-priced cases and maximum supply costs.  Vaccine coverage is determined by a two-part system: the vaccine’s cost itself, where the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) recommends most vaccinations, and where the vaccine is administered.

Members must pay for the vaccine’s administration, and the cost can vary depending on the circumstances of receiving the vaccine.  For most adult Part D vaccinations, the cost is reimbursed the entire amount paid.
