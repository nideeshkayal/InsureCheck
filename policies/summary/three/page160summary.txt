SECTION 9: Level 3 Appeals – Medicare Coverage

If you’ve received a Level 2 appeal and subsequently receive a Level 3 decision, you are now subject to additional appeals. The Medicare Appeals Council (Council) will review your case, and if they determine a favorable Level 3 appeal, the appeal process may not be over.  Medicare will send a Level 4 appeal request with supporting documentation.

Crucially, the Council’s decision is final.  If the Council denies your appeal, you *must* continue coverage within 60 days of receiving the Council’s decision.  If you reject the Council's decision, you must proceed with the next level of review, and then, if you choose, continue coverage.  The Council’s review determines whether to appeal to Level 5 decisions.
