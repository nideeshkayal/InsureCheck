**Pennsylvania Medicare Education and Decision Insight** is a state-funded government program offering free, local health insurance counseling to Medicare beneficiaries. It’s designed to assist individuals with Medicare coverage, including understanding rights, making complaints, and resolving issues with their claims.

**Accessing the Program:** Visitors can access the SHIP through the website at www.shiphelp.or g.  To find the specific contact information for their state, they must select their state from a dropdown menu, which will take them to a page with phone numbers and resources specific to their location.

The SHIP provides counseling on Medicare benefits, medical care, treatment options, and plan changes.  The Quality Improvement Organization serves as a resource for Medicare beneficiaries in Pennsylvania, with the focus on Livanta.
