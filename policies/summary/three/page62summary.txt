**Hospice Care Coverage**

Medicare supplemental benefits for chronic low back pain include DME (device equipment), diabetic monitoring, and emergency room coverage. Emergency room visits are $100 with medical conditions, contingent on coverage. Homehealthagencycare is covered for Medicare-covered individuals at risk for HIV infection with 58 CHAPTER 4 detailing specific coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO).  Homeinfusiontherapy is covered for Medicare-covered individuals, requiring 8 hours of skilled nursing and home health aide services. Medical equipment and supplies are covered, with 20% coinsurance for Medicare drugs. Remote monitoring is also covered. Prior authorization is required for all services. Hospice care is available for Medicare-certified patients with a terminal prognosis, and services related to terminal care are covered by any Medicare-certified hospice program.
