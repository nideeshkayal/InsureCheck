**SECTION 3: How to Get Services During an Emergency**

This section outlines procedures for addressing medical emergencies.  When a medical emergency occurs, immediate action is crucial.  You are obligated to seek help quickly, regardless of the circumstance.  Calling 911 is recommended for immediate assistance, followed by contacting an ambulance if needed.  If you have a medical emergency, you do not need to obtain prior authorization from your PCP.  You are covered for ambulance services in situations where reaching the emergency room otherwise would be dangerous. Medical services are covered during the emergency, and doctors will determine if the condition is stable, ensuring continued care.  Follow-up care is covered under the plan, but out-of-network providers incur higher costs.  If a specialist leaves, you are assigned to a new provider or choice of provider.  Contact the plan for assistance with finding a new provider.  Emergency care is covered for work or out-of-network providers, but out-of-network providers are responsible for full costs, except for Medicare emergencies.
