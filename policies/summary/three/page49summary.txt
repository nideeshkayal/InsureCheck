This plan offers a maximum out-of-pocket expense of $10,000 during the year for Medicare Part A and Part B services from both networks and out-of-network providers.  Members are required to contact Member Services for balance billing disputes.  The Medical Benefits Chart details coverage for services including Part D prescription drugs, and it’s crucial to meet Medicare-approved coverage requirements.  You must provide medically necessary services, and coverage is contingent on prior authorization from Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) when necessary.  Out-of-network providers incur higher costs, and approval is often not required for these services.  Cost sharing percentages are calculated based on the provider type; network providers charge coinsurance, while out-of-network providers charge the coinsurance percentage multiplied by the plan’s reimbursement rate.
