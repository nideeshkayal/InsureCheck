The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan (MPP) provides monthly premium payments, yearly deductibles, and prescription copayments to eligible individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs. This program covers individuals through savings and assets, not home or vehicle.

The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan also includes the AIDSDrugAssistance Program (ADAP), specifically for individuals living with HIV, offering medication access through formulary. Eligibility is determined by state residency, low-income status, and/or under-insurance.

Individuals can access more information and enrollment details on the Health Department’s website, at 1-800-922-9384 or 711. The Railroad Retirement Board (RRB) administers comprehensive benefit programs for railroad workers and their families.  If receiving Medicare through the RRB, it's crucial to inform the agency of any changes in mailing address and benefit inquiries.  Phone numbers for the RRB include 1-877-772-5772 and 1-312-751-4701.
