**Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO - Summary**

**Coverage:** $2,000 max Part D. Cost-sharing: Medical services/prescriptions.

**Conditions:** Appeal process, coinsurance (Part A & Part B), Custodial care (nursing home, hospice, facility), Daily drug cost ($1/day). Deductible: Amount to pay before plan pays.

**Deductible:** Amount to pay before plan pays. Disenrollment: Ending membership. Dispensing fee: Drug dispensing time. Dual-eligible special needs plans (D-SNP) cover Medicare & Medicaid. DME: Medical equipment for home use (walkers, etc.).

**Emergency:** Medical emergencies requiring immediate attention.

**EOC & Disclosure:** Document explaining coverage, rights, and responsibilities. Exception: Drug not on formulary. 2025 Evidence of Coverage.

**Key:** Medicare, Part D, COPO, Cost-sharing, Deductible, EOC, Disclosure
