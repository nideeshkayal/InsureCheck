**Chapter 9: Addressing Issues & Concerns**

Members should proactively manage their coverage through the “Create a Problem or Complaint” process. This includes reviewing records, requesting copies, and modifying coverage as needed.  Pay-as-you-go policies necessitate continued Medicare Part B premiums, requiring ongoing engagement with your healthcare providers.

Crucially, members must actively engage with their physicians to clarify care plans, address concerns, and follow up on treatment recommendations.  Regular updates regarding membership status are required, including location changes.  

Pay attention to any potential enrollment penalties if they are triggered.  Failure to promptly address issues with your coverage can lead to significant financial ramifications.  The goal is to maintain a seamless and effective healthcare experience by proactively managing your plan’s requirements.
