This chapter details crucial insurance terms for Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO members, emphasizing termination due to policy violations.  A critical point is the potential Part D shortage impacting prescription drug coverage, necessitating immediate member contact regarding health concerns.  The “Appeal” process allows members to dispute coverage denials.  Balance Billing is vital; providers must charge the agreed-upon cost-sharing amount. Benefit Periods define the timeframe for hospital and skilled nursing facility (SNF) services, ending after 60 days. Biological Product refers to prescription drugs made from natural sources. Biosimilars are alternative forms of biological products, offering safe and effective substitutes.  The Catastrophic Coverage Stage begins when $2,000 is spent on Part D drugs.  CMS administers Medicare, ensuring compliance with regulations.
