To end your Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) membership, you must enroll in a new Medicare health plan or a separate prescription drug plan. This occurs when your coverage begins after Medicare Part A and Part B eligibility is determined.  Members must continue to obtain medical services and prescriptions through Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) until their membership ends.  The plan will deactivate upon coverage beginning.

Specifically, the plan must end if you no longer have Medicare Part A and Part B coverage, you move outside of the service area, you are away from the service area for more than six months, you move or travel, or you withhold information about other insurance coverage.  Crucially, if you become incarcerated or are not a U.S. citizen or lawfully present, or if you lie or withhold information about other insurance, your membership will end.
