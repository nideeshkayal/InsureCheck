The plan offers Part D prescription drug coverage up to $800 annually, subject to specific rules. Coverage is for all medications, excluding routine chiropractic care. Cosmetic surgery, non-listed procedures, and emergency/urgent care are excluded.  This plan also includes coverage for deformed body member functionality and dentalcare, requiring medical justification for certain prescriptions.

The Drug List defines covered medications, requiring approval before coverage is provided. Prescription fulfillment must occur through an approved pharmacy or mail-order service. Network pharmacies are essential for receiving covered prescriptions and are vital for timely medication delivery.

Importantly, the plan now covers specialized pharmacy services for home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC) facilities, restricted medications, and mail-order pharmacy services that require special handling. The plan also provides new coverage for Part D Prescription Drugs Flex (PPO), outlining key considerations for timely responses to pharmacy requests.
