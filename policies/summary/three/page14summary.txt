**Summary**

The Part D late enrollment penalty is a crucial aspect of Medicare coverage, starting six months after initial enrollment. If you are under 65 receiving Medicare benefits, the penalty resets when you change plans. Failure to pay results in disenrollment and loss of prescription drug coverage.

The penalty is calculated as a percentage of the average monthly premium, with annual adjustments based on the premium average.  There are four payment options:

*   **Check:** Monthly bill sent via check, payable to JeffersonHealthPlans.
*   **Electronic Funds Transfer:** Payment deducted from your checking/savings account.
*   **Phone Payment:** One-time payment via bank or credit card.
*   **Pay by Phone:** Call 1-833-915-1450 to set up your account.

Ensure you understand the specific terms and conditions of each option and the potential consequences of non-payment to avoid disenrollment.
