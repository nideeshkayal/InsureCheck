Medicare supplemental coverage offers comprehensive medical benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, and psychiatric services.  Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring 20% coinsurance.

A significant new addition is prosthetic and orthotic device coverage, including repair and replacement.  Medicare provides $0 copay for COPD members’ pulmonary rehabilitation. Telehealth services, including 24/7 access to credentialed doctors via Teladoc, are covered at $0 copay. Alcohol misuse screening is offered, with four counseling sessions per year for positive results.

For kidney disease, Medicare covers six kidney disease education services per lifetime at $0 copay.  Outpatient dialysis treatments, including dialysistreatments temporarily unavailable, are covered with 20% coinsurance.  Homedialysis equipment and supplies are covered, and certain drugs are covered under Medicare Part B.  For more information on coverage, consult the dedicated Medicare Part B prescription drugs section.
