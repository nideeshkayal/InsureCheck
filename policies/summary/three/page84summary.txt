**Summary:**

This Medicare Supplemental Insurance plan offers coverage for medical services, including physical therapy, psychiatric care, prosthetic device repairs, and COPD member benefits, up to \$800 annually.  Coverage is phased:  WelcometoMedicarePreventive Visit is covered for the first 12 months, with out-of-network services exceeding \$50,000 per year being excluded.

**Key Details:**

*   **Worldwide Emergency/Urgent Coverage:**  Coverage for emergent and urgently needed services outside the U.S. is permitted up to \$50,000 annually, with a \$0 copay.
*   **Excluded Services:** The plan does not cover services that are not specifically listed.  A chart details excluded services, requiring self-payment unless a specific condition applies.
*   **JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) 82:** This PPO plan is covered with a \$0 copay for emergent/urgent services.
