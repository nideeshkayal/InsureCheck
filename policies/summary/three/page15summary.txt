**Summary**

This document details the Part D late enrollment penalty, a crucial aspect of Medicare coverage. Starting six months after initial enrollment, the penalty resets when you change plans. Failure to pay results in disenrollment and loss of prescription drug coverage.

The penalty is calculated as a percentage of the average monthly premium, with annual adjustments based on the premium average.  There are four payment options: Check, Electronic Funds Transfer, Phone Payment, and Pay by Phone.

**Important Considerations:**

*   **Payment Deadline:** Ensure timely payment to avoid penalty.
*   **Timeframe:** If not paid within 90 days, you'll receive a notice with potential penalties.
*   **Emergency Circumstances:** If an emergency prevents timely payment, you can file a complaint (agrievance) as outlined in Chapter 9.
*   **Membership End:** If your membership ends, you remain liable for the penalty.
