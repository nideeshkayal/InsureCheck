**Summary:**

This document outlines coverage decisions and appeals for Part D drug coverage. Initial decisions determine benefits based on specific circumstances, requiring exceptions for non-covered drugs, restrictions, or prioritization. Appeals are mandatory following a decision.

The Drug List defines coverage amounts based on cost-sharing tiers. Alternatives drugs are requested for conditions not covered, and cost-sharing amounts apply to alternative drugs. Changes to coverage can be requested to lower cost-sharing tiers.

If an exception is requested, the physician must provide a medical explanation, and the request should include relevant medical information from the doctor.

If multiple tiers are available, exceptions are generally not granted unless the alternative drugs are ineffective and cause adverse reactions.  For issues, contact the appropriate channel for coverage decisions and appeals.
