**Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Coverage**

This plan offers medical coverage through Employer-Sponsored Benefits, RRB, and Prescription Drug Coverage. Coverage amounts are determined by the benefits chart, and Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services. Providers are eligible for Medicare coverage, but out-of-network providers incur higher costs.

The PCP is responsible for coordinating care and ensuring patient records are sent to the provider. Choosing a PCP involves selecting a physician based on state requirements and the provider’s office location. Prior authorization may be required for certain services.

**Key Changes & Important Details:**

*   **Medicare Coverage:** Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services.
*   **Out-of-Network Costs:** Out-of-network providers incur higher costs.
*   **New Coverage Details:** 2025 Evidence of Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is now available.
