**SUMMARY**

Medicare allows you to file a complaint regarding Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) through the Medicare Complaint Form on www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx. You can also submit a complaint to the Quality Improvement Organization and/or the Appeals Unit.

For quality of care complaints, the plan offers expedited resolution, up to 44 days, and a responsive response if a quick resolution isn’t possible.  The current page details specific coverage details for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO), encompassing coverage amounts, conditions, and exclusions as outlined in Chapter 9.  Ensure you understand these provisions to ensure appropriate benefits and remedies.
