Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Part D prescription drug coverage has updated. It excludes coverage for drugs not covered by Medicare, Part B, or federally approved programs like the American Hospital Formulary Service Drug Information System.  Non-prescription drugs, fertility treatments, cosmetic drugs, and sexual dysfunction treatments are not covered.

Crucially, “ExtraHelp” programs now do not cover drugs not typically covered under the plan, requiring proactive annual review and contact with the State Medicaid program.

Long-term care facilities receive drug coverage through their pharmacy or network, ensuring medication continuity.  Employer or retiree group coverage is secondary to this.

Special attention is paid to creditable coverage – each year the employer or retiree group should notify you of expected coverage amounts. If coverage is not received, request a copy from the provider or benefits administrator immediately.

Medicare-certified hospice patients require separate notification before prescriptions are filled due to coverage limitations. Revocation or discharge of hospice eligibility signifies drug coverage closure, and documentation is crucial for pharmacy verification.
