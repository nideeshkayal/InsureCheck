Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare Part D drug prescriptions, extending to brand-name, generic, and biological medications. The plan includes 30-day supply limits except for routine chiropractic care, with a 90-day supply for new members and an emergency supply for long-term care.

Changes to the Drug List occur annually, starting January 1st, with additions and removals of medications. Changes affect cost-sharing tiers, and sometimes, brand-name medications are replaced with generic versions.  The plan mandates exceptions through Chapter 9, Section 6.4, emphasizing timely request handling.

Important considerations include the requirement to follow Medicarerequirements before any changes.  Changes to the Drug List are immediately posted on the website with detailed information.  The plan utilizes regular Drug List updates to ensure clarity.
