**Summary of Recent Policy Changes & Grievance Procedures**

Jefferson Health Plans emphasize swift resolution of grievances through expedited processes for critical decisions, including coverage, appeals, and drug requests. Expedited processes are available up to 14 days, subject to justification.  The Appeals Specialist will promptly investigate complaints within 24 hours if a fast decision is needed.

If a complaint necessitates extra time, up to 14 additional days can be added, up to a total of 44 days.  If no quick resolution can be achieved, the plan will provide a responsive response outlining reasons for denial.

For quality of care complaints, two additional options exist: direct complaint to the Quality Improvement Organization, or a formal appeal to the Appeals Unit.

Refer to Chapter 2 for detailed information on Coverage Decisions, Appeals, and Complaint Handling.
