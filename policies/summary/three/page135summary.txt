**Summary**

This document details the process for medical coverage decisions, emphasizing expedited review for faster decisions.  A fast appeal is initiated within 72 hours following the initial decision, generally for Part B drug coverage.  If a fast appeal is requested without doctor approval, standard deadlines apply.

A key step involves deciding if a fast appeal is needed, and if so, submitting a written request within 65 calendar days of the decision date.  The request must be submitted and the plan will provide an answer.

If a complaint is filed, a letter will be sent stating standard deadlines.  The appeal process differs significantly; a fast appeal is handled within 72 hours.  To appeal, explain the decision and request additional time for review, and a written statement explaining the denial will be sent.

The document also covers the necessary steps: assessing the appeal, gathering additional information, and providing a final answer.
