Medicare supplemental coverage offers comprehensive medical benefits, including medically-necessary care, physical therapy, and psychiatric services. Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring 20% coinsurance.

Significant changes include prosthetic and orthotic device coverage, including repair and replacement. COPD members receive $0 copay for pulmonary rehabilitation services. Telehealth (24/7 access via Teladoc) is covered at $0 copay. Alcohol misuse screening is offered, with four counseling sessions per year for positive results.

For kidney disease, six kidney disease education services per lifetime are covered at $0 copay. Outpatient dialysis treatments are covered with 20% coinsurance. Blood-including storage and administration services are covered, beginning with the first pint used. All other components of blood are covered beginning with the first pint used.

Prior authorization is required for certain facilities.  Network facilities are preferred, but cost-sharing may apply for out-of-network services.
