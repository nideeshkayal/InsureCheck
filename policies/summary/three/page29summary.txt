The page details eligibility for Pennsylvania’s DHS Office’s ExtraHelp program, assisting individuals with limited income, including prescription drug costs. Individuals with ExtraHelp coverage receive monthly premium payments, yearly deductibles, and prescription copayments.

Crucially, the program covers eligible individuals through savings and assets, not home or vehicle. If overpaid, ExtraHelp provides reimbursement, either forward check or offset future payments.

The page also introduces the AIDSDrugAssistance Program (ADAP), a program specifically for individuals living with HIV, who receive access to life-saving medications through the ADAP formulary.  This program assists with prescription drug costs and eligibility requirements are met with proof of state residency, low-income, and/or under-insurance status.

Individuals can access more information on eligibility criteria, covered drugs, and enrollment at 1-800-922-9384.
