This plan offers maximum out-of-pocket expense of $10,000 annually for Medicare Part A and Part B services from both networks and out-of-network providers. Members must contact Member Services to resolve balance billing disputes. The Medical Benefits Chart details coverage for Part D prescription drugs, requiring medically necessary services and prior authorization from Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) for out-of-network providers.  Out-of-network services incur higher costs, and approval is not usually required. Cost sharing percentages are based on provider type; network providers charge coinsurance, while out-of-network providers charge the coinsurance percentage multiplied by the plan’s reimbursement rate.  For preventative services covered by Original Medicare, we cover them at no cost to you.  Medicare may add coverage for new services during 2025, and you will see this benefit next to the preventive services in the benefits chart.  Medicare will cover any new services during 2025.
