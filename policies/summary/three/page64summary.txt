Medicare supplemental coverage for hospice includes shared payments between PartD drug costs and hospice costs, with no simultaneous coverage for both. Immunizations are covered for risk levels, with additional vaccinations for high/intermediate risk individuals. Inpatient hospital care is covered, starting at $250/day for Medicare-covered stays, with a 90-day residency. Drug and medication coverage includes lab tests and X-rays, charged per stay.

Blood transfusions, including storage and administration, are covered with a first pint needed. Prior authorization is required for all inpatient admissions. The page highlights the importance of medical necessity and requires physician approval for all admissions, especially for new admissions.
