**Section 7.2 Rules for Oxygen Equipment**

JeffersonHealthPlansFlex(PPO) covers oxygen equipment, supplies, and maintenance as part of Medicare coverage. Rental, delivery, tubing, and maintenance are covered. However, if you leave the plan, the supplier must return the equipment.

Original Medicare requires a minimum number of consecutive payments for DME ownership – 13 payments after switching to Original Medicare. If you leave the plan, the supplier will provide five years of equipment support.

The five-year cycle begins again after you remain with the same company, requiring ongoing payments.  The provider must cover oxygen services for five years, even if you stay with the same company.
