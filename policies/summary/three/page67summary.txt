Medicare Supplemental Coverage provides shared payments for Medicare drug and hospice costs. Coverage levels are tiered: $0 copays for Medicare-covered stays, reserved $800 lifetime, and remaining days.

Services include medical nutrition therapy ($35/visit), physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy. Prescription drug coverage is under Part B of Original Medicare, but requires coinsurance and deductibles.

The MDPP benefits are designed to enhance long-term dietary and lifestyle changes.  It’s structured health behavior change intervention.

The plan covers drug costs through provider, but includes additional scans and tests if needed.  Specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services are covered.
