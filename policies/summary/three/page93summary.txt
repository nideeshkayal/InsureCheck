The Plan’s Drug List prioritizes Medicare Part D coverage for medications, focusing on brand-name, generic, and biological products.  Coverage extends to all medications except routine chiropractic care, with a 30-day supply limit.  The plan utilizes specialized pharmacy services for home infusion therapy, resident long-term care (LTC), restricted medication, and mail-order pharmacy services.

Crucially, drugs have restrictions; certain drugs require extra steps to be covered, potentially involving prior authorization.  A steptherapy requirement may apply if a drug doesn’t work, and the plan may not cover another drug. Quantity limits exist, restricting the amount of medication a patient can receive.  The plan also addresses situations where a drug isn’t covered, such as generic versions or specific formulations.  To determine coverage, users must utilize the “Real-Time Benefit Tool” or contact Member Relations.
