**Summary**

This chapter details prescription drug (Part D) coverage within the Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program.  A critical change involves the elimination of non-covered fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments, necessitating annual review and proactive adjustment to ensure compliance.  The plan introduces a new Drug Management Program (DMP) for opioid use, requiring provider collaboration for accurate dosage and patient safety.  Mandatory prescription details – including dosage and pharmacy affiliations – are now required for all Part D prescriptions.

Furthermore, diligent review of prescription information is crucial to flag potential errors, medication misuse, or unsafe dosages. Members are now flagged and have the right to appeal decisions. A Voluntary Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program is available, offering medication list and disposal information, and is free to participate.
