JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) provides comprehensive coverage for U.S. patients prioritizing patient responsibility for drug costs. Mandatory vaccination is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers. Patients have the right to choose providers.

Key priorities include privacy protection, secured by law, and unauthorized access to patient records – requiring written permission for reviews and modifications. Medicare mandates sharing health information, including PartD prescriptions, adhering to Federal statutes and regulations.

Patient rights include reviewing records, requesting copies, and modifying coverage. Member Relations provides support.
