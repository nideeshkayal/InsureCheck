The page details the Step-by-Step process for appealing coverage decisions from the QIO. After Level 2, patients face three additional levels of appeal – Level 3 (Administrative Law Judges) – each requiring specialized legal counsel.  A critical requirement is immediate contact within 24 hours of the decision, or a complaint can be filed.  If the QIO denies the initial appeal, the patient must decide whether to proceed with further appeals, incurring additional costs.  If the QIO rejects the appeal, the patient must continue to cover care for as long as it’s medically necessary, with full reimbursement obligations continuing until the designated end date.  If the QIO rejects the appeal, the patient must decide if they want to escalate to a Level 3 appeal.  The page emphasizes coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) 157, highlighting that the independent review organization's decision is final.
