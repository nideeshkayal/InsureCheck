Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers comprehensive healthcare and prescription drug coverage for members. This plan provides coverage as a member of Jefferson Health Plans, specifically through their Flexible Plan option. The plan offers benefits encompassing medical and prescription drug coverage, with potential for changes in premiums, deductibles, and co-pays/coinsurance, as outlined on the formulary, pharmacynetwork, and provider network.

Key policy details include the plan premium and cost-sharing, medical and prescription drug coverage specifics, complaint procedures, and Medicare law requirements.  Important notice regarding changes to the formulary, provider network, or other regulations will be provided at least 30 days prior to changes affecting enrollees. This document serves as a guide to understanding your benefits and rights, and is crucial for navigating your healthcare plan effectively.
