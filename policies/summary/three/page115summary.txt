**Part D Prescription Drug Coverage – Updated**

Under Jefferson Health Plan Flex (PPO) Part D coverage, there is no cost in the Catastrophic Stage.  Once reached, coverage is reduced to the full cost of each drug until the end of the year.

A significant new requirement is the mandatory administration of Part D vaccines. Maximum monthly costs are $35 per supply for each drug, tiered based on vaccine price and supply quantity.  Vaccine coverage is determined by a two-part system: vaccine cost and administrative charges.

For adult Part D vaccinations, reimbursement is typically the full amount paid. Other vaccinations have reduced reimbursement amounts – coinsurance or copayments apply based on circumstances.  Strict adherence to Chapter 7 procedures is critical for vaccination administration.  Fully applicable reimbursement is expected for most adult vaccinations.
