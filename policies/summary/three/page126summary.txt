**JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) Member Responsibilities**

This plan emphasizes patient responsibility for drug costs. Mandatory vaccination is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers. Members must call Member Relations for problem resolution, coverage decisions, or appeals. Medicare mandates health information sharing, including PartD prescriptions, adhering to Federal statutes.

Key responsibilities include reviewing records, requesting copies, and modifying coverage.  Crucially, members must actively engage with their doctors, asking questions, and following up on their care.  Pay-as-you-go policies are in place, requiring continued Medicare Part B premiums to remain a member.  Furthermore, members are responsible for paying any enrollment penalties if required.  Regular updates are needed regarding membership status, and updates are needed when members move to new areas.
