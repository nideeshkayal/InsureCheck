**Summary:**

This plan mandates full patient responsibility for all drug costs, regardless of provider.  Strict adherence to plan guidelines is crucial, ensuring culturally competent services and accessibility. Mandatory vaccination administration is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers.  Patients have the right to choose their provider and timely access to covered services, including timely prescriptions.

Key priorities include privacy protection – all medical records must be protected by law – and the right to access your information.  Unauthorized individuals cannot see your records. Exceptions exist for quality-of-care checks, but require written permission first. Medicare requires sharing health information, including PartD prescriptions, adhering to Federal statutes and regulations.  You can review your records, request copies, and ask for modifications.
