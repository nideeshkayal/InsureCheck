Jefferson HealthPlansFlex(PPO) Member Relations – Contact Information and Policy Details.

Jefferson HealthPlans, located at 1101 MarketStreet, Philadelphia, PA, offers comprehensive Medicare care support through PAMEDI, a state program providing free local health insurance counseling to eligible seniors.
