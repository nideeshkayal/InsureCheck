The PACEplan offers comprehensive coverage for elderly individuals, including Medicare beneficiaries, Medicaid recipients, and those with disabilities. It provides $2,000 maximum drug cost, a $1/day limit, and specialized coverage for individuals with disabilities, Hospice care, and Medigap.

PartD provides voluntary Medicare Prescription Drug Benefit coverage, while PartC is for Medicare Advantage plans.  PPO plans require prior authorization for most services, with higher out-of-pocket costs for out-of-network providers.  PriorAuthorization is critical for services needing approval. The plan emphasizes that certain drugs are excluded from PartD, and there are annual cost limitations, particularly for Out-of-Network providers.
