The Medicare Part A and B plan offers coverage for chronic low back pain with a maximum annual out-of-pocket expense of $10,000. Coverage includes ambulance services ($255 per-way for Medicare-covered ground ambulance and $20% coinsurance for air ambulance) and eight sessions of improvement for chronic conditions, excluding surgery or pregnancy.

Wellness visits, including lab services ($0 copay), annual physical exams ($0), and annual wellness visits (free, 12-month coverage) are covered.  There is no insurance, copayment, or deductible for colorectal cancer screening.  Colonoscopy has a minimum limit of 120 months for patients not at high risk and 48 months after a previous screening. Mammograms are covered for qualified individuals 35 and 39 years old, and every 24 months thereafter for women 40 and older. Cardiac rehabilitation services are covered for members meeting conditions with a $0 copay per service, with rigorous, intensive programs requiring more oversight.

Barium enema is an alternative to colonoscopy for high-risk patients, and coverage details are listed in the Medical Benefits Chart.
