JeffersonHealthPlansFlex (PPO) provides comprehensive coverage for patients in the United States, prioritizing patient responsibility for drug costs through plan guidelines. Mandatory vaccination is required, with exceptions for out-of-network providers, and patients have the right to choose providers.

Key priorities include privacy protection, with all medical records secured by law. Unauthorized access to patient information is prohibited, requiring written permission for reviews and modifications. Medicare mandates sharing health information, including PartD prescriptions, following Federal statutes and regulations.

Patient rights encompass reviewing records, requesting copies, and modifying coverage. Member Relations provides support at 2025EvidenceofCoverage.

Advanced Directives are crucial – individuals must fill out a legally-required form to give instructions to medical decisions. These documents are used when patient self-determination is limited.  If instructions are not followed, a complaint can be filed with the Office of Quality Assurance, Pennsylvania Department of Health, Health and Welfare Building.
