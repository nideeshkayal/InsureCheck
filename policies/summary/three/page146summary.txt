The page details the process for appealing insurance coverage decisions, particularly for Part D drug prescriptions, with a 24-hour deadline for Level 2 appeals and expedited decisions for physicians.  Medicare oversees the review process, and if the review is not completed within the specified timeframe, the claim is forwarded to the Independent Review Entity (IRE).

If an appeal is not received within 24 hours, a written statement explaining the lack of response is required.  The IRE’s role is to independently review the request and determine whether to change the decision.  

Step 1 involves contacting the IRE and receiving instructions on how to initiate a Level 2 appeal.  If the initial appeal is not completed, the claim will be forwarded.  The IRE reviews the casefile, ensuring all information is considered.

Step 2 focuses on the review process, where reviewers carefully examine the information presented. Deadlines for fast appeals are 2025, and the page emphasizes evidence of coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) plans.
