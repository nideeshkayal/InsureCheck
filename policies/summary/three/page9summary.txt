Medicare requires approval of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) annually, with the contract valid from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025. Eligibility is based on being both a Medicare member and living within the designated service area defined in Section 2.2. The service area includes Pennsylvania counties: Philadelphia, Bucks, Montgomery, Chester, Delaware, Adams, Franklin, Lancaster, York, Cumberland, Dauphin, Lebanon, Perry, Carbon, Lehigh, Monroe, Northampton, Schuylkill, Berks, Bradford.

If you leave the service area, you cannot remain a member.  To maintain coverage, you must continue to reside within the service area during the SpecialEnrollment Period, allowing you to switch to Original Medicare or other eligible plans.  It is crucial to contact Member Relation for information regarding your new location and potential disenrollment.
