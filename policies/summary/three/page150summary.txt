**Summary**

This page details Medicare’s process for Level 3 appeals following a Part D drug prescription. Medicare provides detailed rights regarding Medicare-covered services during and after hospitalization, including necessary services, payment arrangements, and a formal discharge notice.  The discharge date is critical; it must be signed before discharge to ensure timely coverage.

A key aspect is the right to participate in decisions regarding hospital stay and quality concerns. Medicare’s coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) requires detailed evidence of coverage.  If issues arise with Medicare’s coverage, the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) will contact the state.  Immediate action is recommended – contact the QIO within a midnight deadline to avoid penalties.

If you don't meet the deadline, contact the QIO. The QIO conducts an independent review of your case, reviewing medical information, doctor’s opinions, and hospital records. If the review indicates coverage should continue, the QIO will provide a written notice of discharge with the planned discharge date. If the review suggests denial, you must pay the full cost of hospital care after the designated discharge time.
