This page details the Part D drug cost tracking and management for the Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program. It emphasizes the crucial requirement to track all Part D drug costs, including payments and receipts for coverage-related cases.  The plan stipulates that all Part D drug costs must be reported, with a maximum monthly supply cost of $35 per insulin product per tier.

The Plan offers five cost-sharing tiers, with the highest tier (Non-Preferred) for specialty drugs, and the lowest (Preferred Generic) for generic drugs.  Each drug tier has different cost-sharing requirements, determined by the drug's cost and prescription location.  The page highlights that Part D prescriptions can be filled from network retail pharmacies, non-network pharmacies, or out-of-network pharmacies, with specific circumstances for out-of-network prescriptions.  It is imperative to review the Drug List to determine the cost-sharing tier for each drug.
