The document outlines the process for appealing insurance coverage decisions, particularly for Part D drug prescriptions. A 24-hour deadline exists for appeals to Level 2 of the appeal process, with expedited decisions permitted for physicians requesting them. PPO flex plans also receive coverage, and a fast appeal is required within 72 hours if health necessitates it.

If a written response is not received within 24 hours, a written statement explaining the lack of response is required.  You must submit a written request, including your name, contact information, and claim details, using the CMS Model Coverage Redetermination Request Form.

For fast appeals, a response is required within 72 hours of receipt.  If not, the request will be forwarded to Level 2 of the appeal process.  The Level 2 review organization will review the request and provide an answer.

Deadlines for standard appeals for drug coverage are 7 calendar days after receiving the request.  For payment appeals, deadlines are 14 calendar days after receiving the request.
