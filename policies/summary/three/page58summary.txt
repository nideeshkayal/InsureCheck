Medicare Part A and B coverage for chronic low back pain includes durable medical equipment (DME) and related supplies. Coverage is provided for wheelchairs, crutches, powered mattresses, diabetes supplies, hospital beds, IV infusion pumps, speech generating devices, and walkers. Medicare-covered DME and related supplies cost $0, while Medicare-covered diabetic monitoring supplies are $0.

Prior authorization is required for supplemental services, with specific coverage for DME and related supplies, bed orders, and IV infusion pumps.  Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) covers any DME covered by Original Medicare brands and manufacturers on their list, with a 36-month enrollment period. New brands and manufacturers will be covered for up to 90 days.  If you disagree with the plan’s coverage, you can file an appeal.
