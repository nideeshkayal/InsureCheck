**Medicare Complaint Form – JeffersonHealth Plans Flex (PPO)**

To file a complaint regarding your JeffersonHealthPlans Flex (PPO) coverage, contact the Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit at 866-901-7096.  You can submit an online complaint at www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx.

**Pharmacy Department:** Call 866-371-3239 for prescription drug assistance.

**Important Phone Numbers:**

*   **866-901-7096:** Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit
*   **866-371-3239:** Pharmacy Department

This page details requirements for submitting complaints about JeffersonHealthPlans Flex (PPO) coverage.  Specifically, ensure you understand the process of submitting an online complaint via Medicare's website.  If you've received a bill exceeding your coverage amount, you may need to request reimbursement from the provider. Refer to Chapter 7 for details on seeking reimbursement.
