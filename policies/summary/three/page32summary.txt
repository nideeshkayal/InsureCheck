Individuals with employer-sponsored Medicare coverage may have additional benefits through various groups, including the Railroad Retirement Board (RRB).  If you receive benefits from your employer or a retirement group, contact the benefits administrator for questions about your coverage, premiums, and enrollment period.  Call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227; TTY:1-877-486-2048) for assistance with Medicare coverage.  If you have other prescription drug coverage through your employer or retirement group, contact that group’s benefits administrator.  Refer to the back of this document for specific phone numbers and resources.
