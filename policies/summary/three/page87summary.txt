**CHAPTER 5: Using the Plan’s Coverage for Prescription Drugs**

This chapter outlines the coverage for prescription drugs, utilizing the plan’s specific provisions. Coverage is up to \$800 annually.  Crucially, this coverage applies to all prescription drugs, excluding routine chiropractic care. Cosmetic surgery and procedures not listed are excluded.

Emergency/urgent care is covered at a \$0 copay. Accident/improved body member function (breast reconstruction, symmetrical breast appearance) is covered. Experimental procedures, equipment, and medications are not covered. Immediate relatives/household members are not covered. Dentalcare is required for deformed body member functionality.

The plan excludes Radial keratotomy, LASIK, and other lowvision aids. Routine chiropractic care is not reasonable/necessary.  This chapter also defines exclusions such as cosmetic surgery and procedures not listed, as well as the requirement for immediate relatives/household members.
