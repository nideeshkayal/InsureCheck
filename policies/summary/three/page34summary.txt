The plan offers multiple avenues for medical coverage: utilizing Employer-Sponsored Benefits, RRB (Rural Benefit Resources), and Prescription Drug Coverage.  Employer-sponsored benefits require administrator contact for specifics.  The plan covers medical services, equipment, and prescription drugs, with coverage amounts determined by the benefits chart.  As a Medicare health plan member, Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) covers medically necessary services, including prevention, diagnosis, and treatment.  Care received from providers eligible under Original Medicare is considered medically necessary.  The benefits chart, particularly Chapter 4, details covered services and amounts.
