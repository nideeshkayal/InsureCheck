**PACEplan – Urgent Coverage**

PACEplan offers comprehensive coverage for elderly individuals, including Medicare beneficiaries, Medicaid recipients, and those with disabilities.  The plan provides $2,000 maximum drug cost, a $1/day limit, and specialized coverage for individuals with disabilities, Hospice care, and Medigap.

Crucially, the plan now offers “Real-TimeBenefitTool” for accurate formulary information, ensuring timely adjustments to drug coverage.  It includes prosthetics and orthotics, QIO checks, quantity limits for drugs, and a special enrollment period.

Important to note, medically necessary routine visits are not considered urgent, and the plan is temporarily unavailable. Urgent needed services are covered, including rehabilitation services. The plan also excludes certain drugs under PartD, and annual cost limitations apply.
