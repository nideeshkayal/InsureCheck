**Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO – Policy Analysis**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for eligible individuals, including Medicare beneficiaries, Medicaid recipients, and those with medical needs. Key features include $2,000 maximum drug cost, $1/day limit, and specialized plans for Individuals with disabilities, Hospice care, and Medigap coverage for supplemental insurance.

The plan includes Medicare Prescription Drug Coverage (Medicar ePartD) – covering outpatient prescription drugs, vaccines, biologics, and supplies not covered by Medicare Part A or B. Network Pharmacy is essential for access to prescription drugs; members must fill them at Network Pharmacies.

The organization determination process establishes coverage decisions, ensuring certain services are covered or billed according to the plan. Original Biological Product, a FDA-approved drug reference, is also utilized.
