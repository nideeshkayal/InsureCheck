**Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) – Evidence of Coverage**

This policy offers affordable healthcare for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO). It covers medical services like doctor visits, tests, and procedures, with eligibility based on the Jefferson Health Network. Out-of-network charges are separate. Emergency care is available through Jefferson Health. Drug costs are covered, but separate costs apply. Information on Medicare assistance and resources is provided. Contact Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) and Member Relations for details. Medicare assistance and the State Health Insurance Assistance Program are also available. Quality Improvement helps with prescription costs.
