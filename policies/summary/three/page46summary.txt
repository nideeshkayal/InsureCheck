**Oxygen Equipment Coverage Summary**

JeffersonHealthPlansFlex covers oxygen equipment, supplies, and maintenance as part of Medicare coverage. Rental, delivery, tubing, and maintenance are covered.  The provider must return the equipment upon plan termination.

Crucially, the five-year cycle begins anew after a company remains with the provider, necessitating ongoing payments.  The provider must continue to cover oxygen services for five years, even if the company changes.  This extended period is critical for maintaining coverage.  The current page emphasizes that equipment ownership and support extend beyond the initial plan period.
