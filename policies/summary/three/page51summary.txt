The plan offers maximum out-of-pocket expense of $10,000 annually for Medicare Part A and Part B services, encompassing chronic low back pain treatment.  Coverage is defined as lasting 12 weeks or longer, with no systemic causes, no surgery, and no pregnancy.  Additional eight sessions are covered if the patient demonstrates improvement.

Physician qualifications for acupuncture are stringent, requiring a licensed acupuncturist with ACAOM certification and unrestricted licensure within a specified state/territory.  Auxiliary personnel must also meet specific supervision levels as outlined in 42CFR §410.26 & 410.27.2025.  The Medical Benefits Chart details coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) and requires proof of coverage for this plan.
