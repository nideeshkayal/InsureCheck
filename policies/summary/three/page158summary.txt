The page details the process for appealing coverage decisions from the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO). If your Level 1 appeal is denied, you must pay the full cost of care, with potential limitations. If approved, reimbursement continues until the designated care period ends.

A second appeal is required if your initial appeal is denied. Three additional levels of appeal – Level 3 (Administrative Law Judges) – are available following Level 2, each handled by a specialized legal specialist. Urgent contact with the QIO is critical within 24 hours of the appeal decision, or you’ll be directed to a different appeal method.

The page emphasizes coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) 155, stressing prompt attention to coverage issues. If the QIO rejects your appeal, you must decide whether to proceed with further appeals. If you use this other method, the first two levels of appeal differ. The page clarifies that if you continue home healthcare, nursing facility care, or CORF services after the date when coverage ends, you will incur the full cost.
