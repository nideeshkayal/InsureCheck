**Jefferson Health Plans Flex PPO – Summary**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare recipients, including Part D, cost-sharing, and deductibles. Coverage is $2,000 max per year, with a $1/day drug cost.  The deductible is the amount to pay before the plan covers all costs.

Special Needs Plans (D-SNP) provide Medicare & Medicaid coverage, including DME (medical equipment).  An exception exists for drugs not on the formulary.  2025 Evidence of Coverage is required.

The plan includes a grievance process and eligibility for “ExtraHelp” programs for limited income.  HomeHealth Aide supports independent living, while Hospice provides specialized care for those nearing end-of-life.  Initial Coverage limits are established, and a significant income-related adjustment is required.
