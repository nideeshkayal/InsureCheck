Level 4 appeals are initiated when a Medicare Level 2 appeal is denied. The Federal government’s Administrative Law Judges review the appeal and provide a response. If the answer is ‘yes,’ the process concludes; otherwise, the appeal continues.  The Council will authorize or provide coverage within 72 hours, or make a payment after 30 calendar days. Acceptance triggers a process that may continue to Level 4.

If the answer is ‘no,’ the appeal remains open. If you decide to accept the decision, the appeal process will proceed to Level 5, requiring review by a Federal District Court judge. The Federal District Court judge will review the information and decide whether to grant or reject the request.
