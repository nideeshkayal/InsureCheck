**Summary of Part D Late Enrollment Penalty**

The Plan mandates a monthly penalty for delayed prescription drug coverage under the Part D late enrollment penalty. This penalty is calculated as a percentage of the average monthly premium, beginning six months after initial enrollment. The penalty can change annually based on the average monthly premium.

Specifically, if you are under 65 and currently receiving Medicare benefits, the penalty resets when you change plans.

If you disagree about the penalty, you can request a review within 60 days of the first letter.  However, if you are paying the penalty before joining the plan, you may not be able to request a review.

Social Security will send a letter detailing the additional amount owed if payment is insufficient.  Failure to pay results in disenrollment from the Plan and loss of prescription drug coverage.

For more information, visit https://www.medicare.gov/drug-coverage-part-d/costs-for-medicare-drug-coverage/monthly-premium-for-drug-plans.
