**Summary of Drug List Updates**

The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) Drug List undergoes annual revisions, beginning January 1st, to ensure consistent coverage.  Significant changes occur with new generic versions of brand-name drugs and biosimilar expansions, impacting cost-sharing and drug utilization.  These changes *immediately* affect existing patients unless a withdrawal, generic replacement, or other change is made.

Specifically, the plan excludes coverage for drugs not covered by Medicare, Part B, or other federally approved programs like the American Hospital Formulary Service Drug Information System.  Non-prescription drugs, fertility drugs, cough/cold remedies, cosmetic drugs, and sexual dysfunction treatments are not covered.  Furthermore, “ExtraHelp” programs don’t cover drugs not normally covered under the plan.

To ensure accurate coverage, users should proactively check the Drug List annually and contact their state Medicaid program for additional coverage details.
