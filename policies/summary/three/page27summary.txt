Social Security provides contact information (800-772-1213 or www.ssa.gov) for individuals seeking Medicare benefits and assistance. This includes claims disputes, hospital stay duration adjustments, and home healthcare support. The program offers eligibility criteria based on Social Security benefits, including 65+ individuals, disabilities, and End-Stage Renal Disease. Medicaid is a Federal and State Government program that assists with medical costs for individuals with limited incomes and resources, providing coverage for Medicare premiums. Qualified Medicare Beneficiaries (QMB), Specified Low-Income Medicare Beneficiaries (SLMB), and Qualifying Individual (QI) programs offer benefits based on Medicare Part A and Part B premiums, including full Medicaid benefits (QMB+).  For more information, contact the Pennsylvania Department of Human Services (1-800-692-7462) or visit www.ssa.gov.
