**Summary:**

This chapter addresses termination of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) memberships due to policy violations. Medicare may deactivate your account if you withhold information about other insurance coverage, engage in disruptive behavior, or allow unauthorized use of your membership card. Specifically, incarcerated individuals or those not U.S. citizens/lawfully present are subject to termination.

A key condition is lying about other insurance coverage, which will result in membership cancellation. Medicare will investigate any suspected misrepresentation.

Crucially, the document highlights a potential Part D shortage, leading to loss of prescription drug coverage.  **Immediately contact MemberRelations** if you believe your membership is being terminated due to a health-related issue.  **Do not leave your plan without prior approval from Medicare.**  The chapter emphasizes the importance of proactively addressing any potential issues to avoid termination.
