The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program introduces a new Drug Management Program (DMP) for opioid use, requiring annual review and proactive adjustment to ensure compliance.  A critical change is the elimination of non-covered fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments, necessitating revised annual review and adjustments to maintain compliance.

Moving to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, if out-of-pocket costs exceed $2,000 within a calendar year, members will transition from Initial Coverage to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage. This stage includes exclusions for drugs outside the United States, territories, or not covered by the plan, and drugs at out-of-network pharmacies not meeting plan requirements. Payments for drugs not normally covered in Medicare are also excluded. Payments for Veterans Health Administration (VA) drugs, third-party manufacturers, and Drug Manufacturers’ Discount Program are covered.
