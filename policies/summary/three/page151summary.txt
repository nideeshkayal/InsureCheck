**Summary**

This page details the Medicare Level 3 appeal process following a Part D drug prescription. Following a Level 1 appeal, a patient can initiate a Level 2 appeal. If the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) denies the appeal, the patient remains in the hospital after the designated discharge date.  A Level 2 appeal requires a second review by the QIO, typically within 60 days of the initial denial.  Reviewers will determine the decision and notify the patient of their decision. If the QIO approves, the patient must reimburse for their share of hospital care received since the initial denial.  If the QIO denies, the patient continues to pay their share of the cost and coverage limitations.
