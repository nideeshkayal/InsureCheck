**Summary:**

This page details the appeal process following coverage decisions from the Quality Improvement Organization (QIO). If your Level 1 appeal is denied, you must pay the full cost of care, with potential limitations. If approved, reimbursement continues until the designated care period ends.

If the QIO denies your appeal, you are responsible for paying remaining costs and must initiate a second appeal. If the review concludes no, you must decide whether to proceed with further appeals – there are three additional levels of appeal (Level 3) following Level 2, each handled by an Administrative Law Judge.  Ensure you receive a written notice detailing the decision and subsequent actions. The page also addresses coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) 155, emphasizing the need to address coverage issues promptly.
