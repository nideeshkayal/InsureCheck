Medicare Supplemental Coverage provides shared payments for Medicare drug and hospice costs, tiered by copays, reserved lifetime, and remaining days. Coverage levels are $0 for Medicare-covered stays, with $800 lifetime reserved, and remaining days covered.

Key services include medical nutrition therapy ($35/visit), physical therapy, occupational therapy, and speech therapy. Prescription drug coverage is under Part B, requiring coinsurance and deductibles. Specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services are covered.

Part B insurance covers transplant/immun immunosuppressive drugs if Medicare pays for organ transplant, while Part A covers transplants if you have Part A at the time of the transplant. Antigens are covered if a doctor certifies proper supervision. Certain oral anti-cancer drugs cover some oral cancers and certain anti-nausea drugs cover them if available. Part B doesn’t cover them, in which case Part D covers them.

Priorauthorization is required, and obesity screening and therapy promotes sustained weight loss through counseling. Opioid treatment program services are covered through an OTP.  Avoid coverage for individuals with a body mass index of 30 or more.  Talk to your primary care physician to find out more.
