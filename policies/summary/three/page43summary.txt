**Coverage for Clinical Research Studies**

Medicare will not cover new services that are testing, unless Medicare covers the item services even if you are not participating. Items and services provided only to collect data, and not used directly in your healthcare, such as monthly CT scans, are covered.

Medicare would cover item services even if you are not participating. However, if you are not participating, Medicare would not cover them. The policy emphasizes that Medicare will cover item services even if you are not enrolled in the trial.

To obtain more information, visit the Medicare website at www.medicare.gov/Pubs/pdf/02226-Medicare-and-Clinical-Research-Studies.pdf. You can also call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or 24/7 via TTY at 1-877-486-2048.
