**Summary**

Medicare allows you to file a complaint about Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) directly through the Medicare Complaint Form on www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx. You can also submit a complaint to the Quality Improvement Organization and/or the Appeals Unit.

For quality of care complaints, you can file a complaint with either the Quality Improvement Organization or the Appeals Unit.

The plan provides specific coverage details for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO), including coverage amounts, conditions, and exclusions as outlined in Chapter 9. The current page highlights the process for submitting complaints, emphasizing expedited resolution options up to 44 days and a responsive response if a quick resolution cannot be achieved.
