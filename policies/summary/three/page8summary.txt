Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is a Medicare Advantage Plan, approved by Medicare and managed by a private company. It’s designed for members enrolled in Medicare, providing coverage for Part A and Part B healthcare services. This plan offers qualifying health coverage and fulfills the PatientProtection and Affordable Care Act’s (ACA) individual shared responsibility requirement.

The Evidence of Covered Document (EOC) details the plan’s coverage, including required medical care and prescription drug access. It explains your rights and responsibilities, what services are covered, and how to file a complaint if unsatisfied. Key elements include your enrollment details, coverage limits, and potential for changes to your plan after December 31, 2025.

Understanding the EOC is crucial for managing your coverage. The contract is valid from January 1, 2025, to December 31, 2025, allowing for annual policy modifications.
