**Summary:**

The plan’s coverage is coordinated through Coordination of Benefits (COB). Each year, you’ll receive a letter detailing all other insurance you carry.  Medicare initially pays for eligible services, while the primary payer, typically the employer or group health plan, covers the remaining costs.

Failure to meet payment deadlines results in a penalty.  Payment options include check, EFT, phone, and pay by phone.  Pay within 90 days to avoid penalties.

Complainants can file with Chapter 9 if payment is delayed. Remain liable for penalties upon membership termination.  The plan’s primary responsibility is to cover Medicare-eligible services first, with the secondary payer covering the remaining costs.
