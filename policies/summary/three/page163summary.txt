The page details the process for addressing quality of care, waiting times, customer service, and other concerns related to Jefferson Health plans.  A complaint can be filed if the patient experiences delays, poor cleanliness, or unmet expectations regarding coverage decisions or appeals.  The complaint process is structured by sections, emphasizing the key triggers for filing – timeliness of actions, unmet deadlines for coverage decisions, and failure to forward cases to review organizations.  Specifically, the page highlights that a complaint is classified as a “grievance,” and it is encouraged to make a complaint if the plan is not responding quickly enough.  It clarifies that the complaint process uses the same method as filing a grievance to address issues like coverage decisions, appeals, and delays.
