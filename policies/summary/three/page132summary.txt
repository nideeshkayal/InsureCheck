The Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) coverage mandates medical care decisions and appeals. Seeking medical services or Medicare Part B prescription drugs requires a signed form to initiate the process.  The current section details coverage decisions for four specific situations: medical care decisions, Part D prescription drug appeals, hospital stay coverage decisions, and outpatient services coverage decisions.

Specifically, Section 5 addresses how to request a coverage decision when you’re not receiving desired medical care.  It outlines the process for each situation, including requesting coverage for medical care or appealing coverage decisions. Crucially, the page emphasizes that specific Part D prescription drug appeals have distinct rules compared to other medical services covered under this plan.  If you experience difficulties with coverage, contact Member Relations or your lawyer.
