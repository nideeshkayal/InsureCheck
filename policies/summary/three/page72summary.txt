Medicare Supplemental Coverage offers comprehensive medical benefits, with coverage levels ranging from $0 for Medicare-covered stays to $800 lifetime reserved, and remaining days covered.  Key services include Outpatient Hospital Observation, costing $375 per stay, and diagnostic tests/therapies at $275 per visit.

Priorauthorization is required for specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services, requiring written documentation.  Out-of-network services incur a 20% coinsurance. Blood testing begins with a pint, necessitating prior authorization.  Observation services are covered when medically necessary and hospitalization coverage depends on provider authorization.
