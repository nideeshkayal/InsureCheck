Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers voluntary ending of membership with a transition period until January 1st.  Members can end their membership during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7), allowing them to choose between continuing coverage or changing to a new plan.  Changes to coverage must occur before January 1st.

A key change is the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period (2025 Evidence of Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO)) allows for a single change to the health covered during this period.  The plan’s membership ends on January 1st, and members must understand the implications of their new plan choice.
