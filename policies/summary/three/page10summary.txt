Medicare members of Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) must utilize their membership card for covered services and prescription drug access. Using the card is crucial for receiving benefits, particularly when accessing network pharmacies.

The provider and pharmacy directory lists current network providers, including doctors, medical groups, durable medical equipment suppliers, and hospitals. Network providers are required to accept payment, and using network providers is essential for receiving medical care.

Exceptions exist for emergencies and urgently needed services, but network availability is not guaranteed. Out-of-area dialysis services and cases where Jefferson Health Plans Flex authorizes out-of-network providers are exceptions to the network rules.

To obtain the most current information, visit www.JeffersonHealthPlans.com/medicare.
