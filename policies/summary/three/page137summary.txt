The Independent Review Entity (IRE) is a Medicare-appointed organization tasked with reviewing appeals and decisions regarding insurance coverage for Medicare Part B prescription drug benefits. Step 1 involves reviewing the appeal, sending information to the IRE, and granting a fast appeal (Level 2) within 72 hours of receipt. If the request is not met, the IRE will forward it to Level 2, requiring a review.

If the request is for a medical item or service, the IRE will provide an answer within 30 calendar days. However, if additional information is needed to make a decision, the IRE may extend this timeframe up to 14 calendar days. The IRE's decision must be written, detailing the reasoning behind the outcome.

Finally, the IRE will provide information on how to handle problems or complaints, including filing a fast complaint within 24 hours.
