**SUMMARY:**

This chapter focuses on Prescription Drug (Part D) coverage within the Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program. Key changes include the elimination of non-covered drugs, specifically fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments, necessitating annual review and proactive adjustment.

A new Drug Management Program (DMP) is introduced for opioid use, requiring provider collaboration to ensure appropriate dosages and patient safety. Accurate prescription details, including dosage and pharmacy affiliations, are now mandatory for coverage.

Furthermore, the plan now mandates diligent review of prescription information to flag potential errors, medication use errors, or unsafe dosage levels. Members are now flagged for potential issues, and appeal rights are guaranteed if a decision is incorrect.

A voluntary Medication Therapy Management (MTM) program is offered, providing a comprehensive medication list and safe disposal information. Eligibility for this program is free.
