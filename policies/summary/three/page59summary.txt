Medicare Part A and B coverage for chronic low back pain includes durable medical equipment (DME) and related supplies, with costs varying by provider. Medicare-covered DME and related supplies cost $0, while Medicare-covered diabetic monitoring supplies are $0. Medicare-covered emergency room visits are covered at $100, and coverage is dependent on emergency conditions.  Prior authorization is required for supplemental services, including DME, bed orders, and IV infusion pumps, with the 36-month enrollment period for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO).  Disagreements with coverage require appeal, and the out-of-network cost-sharing is the same as in-network.  Health and wellness education programs are offered through SilverSneakers®, with access to a network of fitness locations.
