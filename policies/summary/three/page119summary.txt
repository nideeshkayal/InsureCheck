Jefferson Health Plan Flex (PPO) Part D mandates mandatory vaccination administration as a condition of coverage.  Providers must bill directly to the plan for services received, with exceptions for out-of-network providers.  If an out-of-network pharmacy bill is received, the patient will be fully responsible for the full cost, regardless of provider.  The plan will consider requests for payment and will provide additional information from the patient if necessary.  If medical care is not covered, the provider will not pay for their share of the cost.  The plan will send a letter explaining the decision and providing an appeal process.  It is crucial to follow all rules and regulations to ensure compliance.
