**Part D Coverage & Appeals**

Part D coverage decisions and appeals are crucial. Initial coverage decisions involve determining benefits and amounts for drug coverage, requiring a decision based on specific circumstances. Seeking exceptions for non-covered drugs, restrictions on coverage, or prioritization of alternative drugs are common actions.

Asking for coverage of drugs not on the Plan’s List necessitates a formulary exception, while requesting adjustments to cost-sharing is another possible exception. Appeals are mandatory following a decision.

If disagreements arise, an appeal can be initiated. The section details how to ask for decisions and how to request appeals, emphasizing the need to explain medical reasons for exceptions and consult with a physician or prescriber.
