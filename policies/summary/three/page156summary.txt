The Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) conducts an independent review of the appeal, assessing medical appropriateness to determine coverage decisions.  A Level 1 appeal initiates a process requiring a written notice outlining end-date and continuation timeframe. The IRE reviews the case, and the QIO will assess medical information, doctor’s opinions, and plan details to determine if coverage should continue.

If the IRE denies, the patient must pay the full cost of care, with potential limitations.  If approved, reimbursement continues until the designated care period ends.  If the reviewers deny the appeal, the patient must pay the remaining costs, and another appeal is possible. If the Level 1 appeal is unsuccessful, the patient must make another appeal.
