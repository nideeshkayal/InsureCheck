**Livanta Pennsylvania Quality Improvement Organization** provides free counseling to Medicare beneficiaries in Pennsylvania. It’s an independent organization that focuses on Liventa’s quality improvement efforts, specifically targeting the Liventa program.

Livinta offers assistance with Medicare benefits, medical care, treatment options, and plan changes. It’s not connected to any plan and should be contacted at 888-396-4646 or through their website (www.livantaqio.com) for complaints, coverage issues, or enrollment.

Specifically, Livanta assists individuals with:

*   Claims disputes
*   Hospital stay duration adjustments
*   Home healthcare, skilled nursing facility, or CORF services adjustments.

Eligibility for Medicare is determined by Social Security, focusing on 65+ individuals, individuals with disabilities, and those with End-Stage Renal Disease. Social Security also handles extra Part D drug coverage amounts for higher incomes.

It is important to contact Social Security or visit your local Social Security office to apply for Medicare benefits.
