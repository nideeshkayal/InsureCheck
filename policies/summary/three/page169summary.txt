Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) offers a transition period until January 1st, allowing members to choose between continuing coverage or changing to a new plan.  During the Annual Enrollment Period, members can switch to alternative Medicare Advantage plans, including those with or without prescription drug coverage, or obtain separate prescription drug plans.

A key change is the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, beginning January 1st, for new beneficiaries enrolled in MA plans.  During this period, members must understand the implications of their new plan choice, including potential changes to coverage amounts, deductibles, and annual maximums.  

Special Enrollment Periods are available for eligible members under specific circumstances. These periods are triggered by circumstances like moving, Medicaid eligibility, potential contract violations, care within an institution, or enrollment in the Program of All-Inclusive Care for the Elderly (PACE). Members must contact Medicare at 1-800-MEDICARE or visit www.medicare.gov to determine eligibility and to change their Medicare health coverage and prescription drug coverage.
