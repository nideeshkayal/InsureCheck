The current plan provides a maximum out-of-pocket expense of $10,000 during the calendar year for Medicare Part A and Part B services received from both in-network and out-of-network providers.  This maximum covers the most significant cost during the year for covered services.  The cost of copayments and coinsurance is calculated based on the plan’s reimbursement rates, with out-of-network providers incurring higher costs.  Specifically, the plan limits out-of-pocket costs to $7,000 per calendar year for Part A and Part B services from both networks and out-of-network providers.  If you have paid $10,000, you will have 100% coverage, with no further out-of-pocket expenses.  Members are required to contact Member Services for balance billing disputes.
