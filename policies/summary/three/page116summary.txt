**Summary:**

Under Jefferson Health Plan Flex (PPO) Part D, mandatory vaccination administration is now required. Maximum monthly costs are $35 per supply for each drug, tiered based on vaccine price and supply.  Strict adherence to Chapter 7 procedures is crucial for vaccination administration.

The plan mandates that patients pay back any portion of the cost when they’ve paid more than their share of the cost for covered services or medications. This includes reimbursement for vaccinations, while stricter rules apply for other vaccinations.

Providers are obligated to bill directly to the plan for services received, with exceptions for out-of-network providers.  Patient responsibility remains for the full cost of services received even when billed by an out-of-network provider. Remember, you must never pay more than the plan-allowed cost sharing.
