**Step 4: Initiate Level 3 Appeal**

Following a Level 2 appeal, a patient can proceed with a Level 3 appeal. This process involves three additional levels of review after the initial denial. The detailed instructions for each level are available in the written notice received after the Level 2 decision. The Administrative Law Judge handles the appeals.

**Step 2: Review Discharge Date**

The QIO will review your planned discharge date to ensure it’s medically appropriate. This includes checking for appropriate hospital stay length and any necessary medical considerations.

**Step 3: Decision & Notification**

Within 72 hours of requesting a fast review, the QIO will issue a decision. If the QIO approves, reimbursement for covered hospital costs will be made. If the QIO denies, the patient will continue to pay their share of the cost, and coverage limitations apply.
