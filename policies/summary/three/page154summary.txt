**Summary:**

This chapter details the process for appealing Medicare coverage decisions.  The QIO initiates a Level 3 appeal following a Level 2 decision, requiring a patient to file within 24 hours of requesting a fast review.  The appeal process involves three review organizations: the Independent Review Entity (IRE), which assesses medical appropriateness, and Medicare, overseeing the IRE’s review.  If the IRE denies, the patient must continue paying their share of costs.  If the IRE approves, reimbursement is provided, extending inpatient services until medically necessary.  Coverage ends when the designated care period concludes, requiring advance notice.  The chapter details how to request a fast-track appeal, including receiving a written notice specifying the stop date and timeframe for coverage continuation.  It also clarifies Medicare’s Non-Coverage Notice regarding appeals, ensuring proper procedure.
