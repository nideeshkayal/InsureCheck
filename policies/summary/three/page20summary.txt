JeffersonHealthPlans Flex(PPO) offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare-eligible medical services through a coordination system. The plan’s coverage is structured with a focus on the secondary payer, responsible for remaining costs.  Coverage decisions are handled through Chapter 9, a process for reviewing and potentially modifying coverage. Patient support is provided through the Member Relations department via phone and website, offering interpreter services.

Crucially, the page emphasizes Coverage Decisions for MedicalCare, requiring a call to the designated number (866-901-8000) to initiate the process. Appeals are available for medical care coverage, with a specific timeframe for submissions.  Important phone numbers include the Pharmacy Department (866-371-3239) and the Complaints, Grievances & Appeals Unit (866-901-7096) located at 1101 MarketStreet, Suite 3000, Philadelphia, PA 19107.
