**Section 5: Requesting Coverage Decisions**

When coverage decisions are triggered, you must initiate an appeal within 14 calendar days for Part B drug coverage or 72 hours for medical services.  A fast coverage decision is expedited, typically within 72 hours, but requires specific requirements: only request coverage for medical items/services, not requests for payment.  If your doctor advises a fast coverage decision, automatically grant it.  If you request a fast coverage decision without doctor approval, we will use standard deadlines.

If coverage is reduced or stopped, you must appeal. The appeal process involves explaining the decision and setting standard deadlines.  If your doctor asks for the fast coverage decision, automatically grant it.  If you file a complaint about a decision, we will send a letter stating standard deadlines.
