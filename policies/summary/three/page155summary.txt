The current page details the process for appealing Medicare coverage decisions, focusing on a Level 1 appeal. It initiates a process involving three organizations: the Independent Review Entity (IRE) and Medicare, overseeing the IRE’s review.  A Level 1 appeal requires a patient to file within 24 hours of requesting a fast review. The process includes requesting a written notice stating the coverage end date and timeframe for continuation, ensuring proper procedure.

The Quality Improvement Organization (QIO) reviews the appeal, assessing medical appropriateness. If the IRE denies, the patient must pay the full cost of care. If approved, reimbursement is provided until the designated care period concludes.  The page emphasizes the importance of timely action, highlighting the deadline for coverage continuation and the potential for appeals rights if the decision is not satisfactory.  It also directs the user to the Quality Improvement Organization for contact information.
