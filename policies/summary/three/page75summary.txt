Medicare supplemental coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits, encompassing medically-necessary care, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, and psychiatric services.  Coverage levels range from $0 to $800 lifetime reserved, with out-of-network services incurring a 20% coinsurance.  Medicare-covered ambulatory surgical center services cost $245.  Telehealth services, including diagnosis, evaluation, and treatment of acute illnesses or stroke, are covered for patients with an in-person visit within 6 months prior to telehealth. Exceptions are permitted for certain circumstances.  Virtual check-ins are available for new patients, and evaluations aren't directly related to office visits within 7 days.  Coverage for Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) is included, with stipulations regarding coverage for other plans.
