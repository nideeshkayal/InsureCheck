**Summary**

This chapter details prescription drug (Part D) coverage within the Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) program. A crucial change involves eliminating non-covered fertility and sexual dysfunction treatments, necessitating annual review and proactive adjustment to ensure compliance. The plan introduces a new Drug Management Program (DMP) for opioid use, requiring provider collaboration for accurate dosage and patient safety. Mandatory prescription details – including dosage and pharmacy affiliations – are now required for all Part D prescriptions.

Moving to the Catastrophic Coverage Stage, if out-of-pocket costs exceed $2,000 within a calendar year, members will transition from the Initial Coverage Stage to this stage. Payments are excluded from out-of-pocket costs for drugs purchased outside the United States and territories, drugs not covered by the plan, or drugs at out-of-network pharmacies not meeting plan requirements. Payments for drugs not normally covered in Medicare are also excluded. Payments for prescription drugs not normally covered in Medicare are also excluded. Payments made by the Veterans Health Administration (VA), third-party drug manufacturers, and Drug Manufacturers’ Discount Program are also covered.
