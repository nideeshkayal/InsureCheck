Under Jefferson Health Plan Flex (PPO) Part D, mandatory vaccination administration is now required. Maximum monthly costs are $35 per supply for each drug, tiered based on vaccine price and supply. Strict adherence to Chapter 7 procedures is crucial for vaccination administration. Providers are obligated to bill directly to the plan for services received, with exceptions for out-of-network providers. Patient responsibility remains for the full cost of services received, even when billed by an out-of-network provider.  **A key change is that if you get a bill from an out-of-network pharmacy, you must pay the full cost.** Remember that we only cover out-of-network pharmacies in limited circumstances. Chapter 5, Section 2.5 details these circumstances.
