Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) provides medical coverage through Employer-Sponsored Benefits, RRB (Rural Benefit Resources), and Prescription Drug Coverage. Coverage amounts are determined by the benefits chart, and Medicare coverage is medically necessary for medically necessary services.

Providers are eligible for Medicare coverage under the plan, but out-of-network providers incur higher costs.  The PCP is responsible for coordinating care and ensuring patient records are sent to the provider.  Choosing a PCP involves selecting a physician based on state requirements and the provider’s office location.  Prior authorization may be required for certain services.  The Provider&Pharmacy Directory provides current provider information.
