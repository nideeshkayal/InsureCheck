**SECTION 2: Where to Get More Information and Assistance**

SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) offers government-sponsored counseling services for individuals experiencing coverage disputes. SHIP provides trained counselors who can assist with understanding the appropriate process for addressing complaints, including appeals and resolutions.  The program is independent from insurance companies and health plans. Counselors can offer guidance on selecting the correct process based on specific situations.

Specifically, SHIP is available to address complaints related to Jefferson Health Plans Flex (PPO) coverage. SHIP counselors can help determine which process to utilize for handling issues, answer questions, and offer more information.  Understanding the process is crucial for navigating coverage decisions effectively.
