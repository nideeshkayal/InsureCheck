**CHAPTER 8: Your Rights and Responsibilities**

This chapter outlines key rights and responsibilities regarding Part D prescription drug coverage under the Jefferson Health Plan Flex.  Mandatory vaccination administration is a requirement for coverage, with exceptions for out-of-network providers.

If an out-of-network pharmacy bill is received, the patient is fully responsible for the entire cost, regardless of the provider’s responsibility.  The plan will address this situation through requests for payment, providing additional information to the patient as needed.

**Important:**  Strict adherence to all rules and regulations is crucial.  Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in denial of coverage.  The plan will inform you of the decision and provide an appeal process.  Consult the policy document for complete details and specific procedures.
