Medicare Supplemental Coverage offers medical benefits, with coverage levels ranging from $0 for Medicare-covered stays to $800 lifetime reserved, and remaining days covered. Opioid treatment programs are covered through an OTP, utilizing dispensing, counseling, and assessments.

Services covered include Outpatient Hospital Observation, which costs $375 per stay.  For out-of-network services, coinsurance is 20%. Diagnostic tests and therapies are covered at $275 per visit.  Priorauthorization is required for specialist visits and ambulatory surgical center services.

Blood testing begins with the first pint, requiring prior authorization.  Observation services are covered when medically necessary, and hospitalization coverage depends on provider authorization.  You must pay for out-of-network services with a 20% coinsurance.
