**Summary**

This page details the process for ending membership within a Medicare plan, including appeals and grievances.  The policy emphasizes timely submissions of complaints within 60 days of denial. Expedited grievances resolve within 24 hours for members, and Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services offers assistance through phone, email, and website resources.

Crucially, Liventa, a federal government-funded QIO, investigates complaints regarding quality of care within the Medicare program.  The QIO's goal is to ensure patient well-being through continuous reviews and improvements.

The page outlines termination rules regarding membership, including restrictions on asking members to leave, rights to a complaint, and information about Medicare and NJ FamilyCare services.  It details the timeline for ending membership and the process for accessing additional information.
