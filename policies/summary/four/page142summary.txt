Medicare Part D drug decisions require stringent scrutiny due to Medicare’s restrictions on medication coverage. New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance (DBI) drugs are subject to complex 24-hour PA requirements for denial. Expedited redetermination is available after denial, with expedited coverage options. If denied, an explanation letter is sent. Payments must be made within 30 days. The Independent Review Organization (IRO) reviews requests within 30 calendar days, and expedited redetermination is also available.

To initiate a Level 1 Appeal, contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) within 60 days of your coverage ending. The QIO reviewers will analyze your appeal, including a decision within 14 calendar days.

If the QIO approves your appeal, you must pay for hospital services until medically necessary. If denied, the QIO maintains the original decision. An Alternate Appeal process is available if you disagree with the initial denial.
