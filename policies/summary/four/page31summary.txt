Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. Key coverage includes health care, medical benefits, MLTSS (medical, dental, and surgical expenses), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and DME. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly kidney dialysis, and changes to the PCP necessitate notification. The plan offers coverage for MLTSS, including home and community-based services for members with nursing facility needs, such as assisted living and transportation.

Coverage for MLTSS includes non-emergency healthcare related tasks through the Personal Preference Program (PPP). You can engage personal care providers through a process involving a care manager.  Approval for Personal Care Assistant Services (PCA) is required, and coverage is typically for at least six months.  The plan offers transportation services, including MAVs and basic life support ambulance, for non-emergency transportation, paid directly by Medicaid.
