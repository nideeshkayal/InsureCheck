**Summary**

This Medicare and NJ FamilyCare Medicaid policy offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care with transfer approval and coverage for behavioral health and prescription drugs. Key limitations include coverage limits, dates, and eligibility requirements. Coverage for out-of-network care is permitted under specific conditions involving a network provider. Outside the service area, coverage is available for urgent medical services and ambulance transportation.

The policy defines eligibility requirements for both programs, mandates medical records from emergency providers, and covers eligible individuals. Coverage for clinical research studies is permitted for volunteers, with Medicare covering study-related costs. Payment for services in clinical research studies is typically non-refundable, with Medicare paying for covered services, routine costs, and potential side effects. Prior authorization is required for certain benefits, including investigations and device trials.

To ensure coverage, individuals should contact Member Services to understand appeal rights and benefits details.
