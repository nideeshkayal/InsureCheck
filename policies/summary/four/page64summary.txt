Aetna Premier Plus Plus provides comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays, including inpatient care, outpatient mental health, and nutritional therapy. Coverage extends to 28 meals after discharge, contingent upon provider confirmation.

Crucially, specialist services, particularly for mental health, require prior authorization from the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease, including training on dietary changes and physical activity.

Prior authorization is required for specialist services, including mental health, and is delegated to the provider. IV immune globulin and other drugs for home dialysis are covered. The plan also includes nursing facility care, including meals, nursing services, physical therapy, occupational therapy, speech therapy, respiratory therapy, and drug administration.
