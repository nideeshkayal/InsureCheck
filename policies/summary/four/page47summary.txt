This Medicare plan offers comprehensive coverage for 24 months, contingent upon medical necessity. Key benefits include annual routine physical, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction, and cervical/vaginal cancer screening. Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services require prior authorization and are covered for non-Medicare benefits.

The plan covers colorectal cancer screening with options for colonoscopy (no minimum age), flexible sigmoidoscopy, fecal occult blood tests, and Pap tests. Chiropractic, genetic testing (multitarget stool DNA), and dental services are included. Coverage extends to diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.

Notably, the plan emphasizes the importance of prior authorization for ASD services, with providers responsible for the process.  Diabetes screening, including fasting glucose testing and risk factor considerations, is covered.  The remaining portion of the page details continued coverage for depression screening and diabetes screenings – ensuring continued benefits for eligible individuals.
