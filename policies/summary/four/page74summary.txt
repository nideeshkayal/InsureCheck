**Aetna Premier Plus Plus Coverage**

**Coverage:** Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries & therapy/nursing individuals. Key benefits include physician services (doctor’s office, limited services), prosthetics (breast prostheses, repairs), pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors).

**Conditions:** Must have a primary care provider session, SNF care (semi-private, private, meals, PT, OT, speech therapy), and drug administration (blood clotting factors). Asexually receptive sex infections (STIs) screening & counseling are covered.

**Important:** Requires qualifying session through Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) – 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP. Two quit attempts allowed within a 12-month period. Covers OTC smoking cessation products and SET for PAD.

**Policy Details:** Qualified personnel for 30-60 minute sessions for SET.
