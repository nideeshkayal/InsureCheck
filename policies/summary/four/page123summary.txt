**Coverage Decisions & Appeals**

This chapter details coverage decisions and appeals, particularly for medical care. It outlines five key situations: medical care problems, Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage, continued coverage for specific services (home health, skilled nursing, and CORF), and questions regarding coverage decisions.

**Key Considerations:**

*   **Integration:** Coverage decisions are often handled by a member services representative or SHIP.
*   **Request Process:** You can request a coverage decision via phone (1-844-362-0934 or TTY 711), faxing, or writing.
*   **Specific Rules:** Hospital, home health, and CORF services have special rules.
*   **Standard & Fast Deadlines:** Standard coverage decisions are 14 calendar days, while Medicare Part B prescription drug is 72 hours after request. Extended time may be granted if additional information is needed.
*   **Decision Communication:** Written communication confirms the decision within 14 calendar days.
