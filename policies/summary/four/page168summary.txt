This Aetna Premier Plus D-SNP policy offers comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, including patient care, care manager, care plan, and care team roles. Key coverage includes Medicare and Medicaid eligibility, with a dual eligibility structure. Medicare Part D covers outpatient prescription drugs, vaccines, and supplies, subject to exclusions for specific drug types.

The policy mandates careful review of billing practices to avoid unapproved costs. Individualized Care Plans (ICPs) outline services, encompassing medical, behavioral health, and long-term support. The Drug List dictates specific drug requirements and limitations, ensuring adherence to accepted medical standards. Eligible patients participate in hospice care and receive directed care. Low-income subsidies (LIS) support long-term care needs.

The policy utilizes Network Pharmacy for prescription fulfillment; Network providers, including doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals, are essential for covered services. This policy also includes a Member Services department to assist members with benefits and inquiries.
