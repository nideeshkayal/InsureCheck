Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+. The plan provides zero monthly premiums and no premium, covering behavioral health and mental health services with prescription drug costs. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME suppliers, and pharmacies are required to accept full payment for covered prescriptions.

Key coverage includes prescription drug coverage, with potential limitations on dosage or usage. The Member ID Card and Evidence of Coverage document are required for eligibility verification. Members must report any changes to their membership record promptly, ensuring accurate information for providers and pharmacies.

This includes name, address, phone number updates, changes to employer-sponsored insurance, liability claims (e.g., auto accidents), nursing facility admissions, hospital care, caregiver changes, and clinical research participation.
