Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+. The plan provides zero monthly premiums and no premium, covering behavioral health and mental health services with prescription drug costs. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME suppliers, with pharmacies required to accept full payment for covered prescriptions.

Key coverage includes prescription drug benefits, with potential limitations on dosage or usage.  The Member ID Card and Evidence of Coverage document are required for eligibility verification.  Members must report any changes to their membership record promptly.

The page also provides contact information for Member Services (1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711)) for updates, and links to AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP. Important resources include Care Manager contact, SHIP, QIO, Medicare, NJ FamilyCare (Medicaid), Office of the Insurance Ombudsman, and the New Jersey Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman.
