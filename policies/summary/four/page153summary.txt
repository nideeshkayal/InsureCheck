**Summary of Current Page Content**

This page focuses on patient grievances and complaints related to Medicare coverage, specifically regarding appeals and denials. Medicare provides a process for addressing these issues, with tiered appeals (IRO review -> Level 3 -> Level 4) leading to potential drug requests and expedited grievances.  

The policy details the process for filing complaints, requiring timely submission within 60 days of denial.  Expedited grievances resolve within 24 hours if applicable, and the process is free for members.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services offers assistance through phone, email, and website resources.

Crucially, the page details the QIO’s role – Livanta, a federal government-funded organization, investigates complaints about quality of care.  The QIO’s objective is to ensure patient well-being through ongoing reviews and improvements to the care provided, using a complaint-driven approach.
