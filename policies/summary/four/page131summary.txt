**Insurance Policy - IURO Appeal**

**Coverage:** Coverage amounts and limits apply. Important terms include assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals. **Exclusion:** New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance is not covered.

**Conditions:** Fair Hearing possible for Level 3 or higher appeals. Must submit a Fair Hearing application form within 60 days of decision letter. Expedited Fair Hearing: decision within 72 hours of receipt. Service stops require written request within 10 days.

**Decision:** Written decisions and reasons provided. Payment issues addressed; provider contacted for billing. Additional appeal rights if Level 2 appeal denied.
