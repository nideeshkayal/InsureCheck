**Summary:**

This Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP policy provides coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Coverage extends up to 90 days for long-term care facility stays.

Key terms include Emergency Medications, Experimental Treatments, Quantity Limits, Provider Evaluation, Temporary Supply Options (up to 30 days), EOB details (drug usage, costs, and price changes), NJ FamilyCare (Extra Help for Medicare Part D), provider and pharmacy directories, and payment sources.

Important conditions include Exception Requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage, which impacts deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs. Provider and Pharmacy Directory listings are essential for finding network pharmacies. Provider assessments are mandatory.

Vaccinations are covered as health benefits; Medicare Part D vaccines are free to you.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services is required if errors are identified.
