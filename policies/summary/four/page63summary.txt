Aetna Premier Plus Plus provides comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays, including inpatient care, outpatient mental health, and nutritional therapy. Coverage extends to 28 meals after discharge, contingent on provider confirmation.

Crucially, the plan covers specialist services, particularly for mental health, requiring prior authorization from the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services, including training on dietary changes and physical activity, are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease.

Prior authorization is required for specialist services, including mental health, and is delegated to the provider. Coverage for IV immune globulin and other drugs for home dialysis is included.  The page also highlights the necessity of verifying coverage details with your provider and directs users to AetnaBetterHealth.com/new-jersey-hmosnp/providers/prior-authorization.html for further details.
