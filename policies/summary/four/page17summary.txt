Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid eligible individuals 19+. Key coverage includes prescription drug benefits, with limited dosage/usage. The policy covers behavioral health and mental health services, offering zero premiums and no premium. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME. Members must report changes promptly.

The policy emphasizes dosage/usage limits impacting prescription drug costs. Appeals are available for coverage disputes. A Care Manager is assigned to each member, providing specialized health care support. Contact Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for assistance.

The State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) provides free counseling to Medicare recipients, including assistance with Medicare changes and navigating SHIP services. SHIP is not affiliated with insurance companies. Contact SHIP for help with Medicare-related questions, including changing plans and understanding rights.
