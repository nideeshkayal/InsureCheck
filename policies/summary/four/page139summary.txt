**Medicare Part D Drug Decisions – Policy Summary**

This policy covers Medicare Part D drug decisions for assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals. Significant exclusions apply to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs. Prior authorization (PA) is required for certain medications. Appeals are available after denial, with expedited coverage (24-hour response, 24-hour complaint) and fast appeal (60 days after initial letter) options. Expedited redetermination is also expedited. If denied, an explanation letter is sent. Payments must be made within 30 days. The Independent Review Organization (IRO) reviews requests, requiring payment within 30 calendar days. A Level 2 Appeal process exists for health needs, while a standard appeal is required for repayment.
