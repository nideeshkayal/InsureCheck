Aetna Assurance Premier Plus D-SNP provides comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, including patient care and support. Key features include Care Manager, Care Plan, and Care Team roles, necessitating engagement with CMS. The coverage includes exclusions for specific drug types, and the policy requires careful review of billing practices to avoid unapproved costs. The Independent Review Organization (IRO) oversees drug coverage decisions. Individualized Care Plans (ICPs) outline services, encompassing medical, behavioral health, and long-term support. The Drug List, a formulary, dictates specific drug requirements and limitations.  Eligible patients must participate in hospice care and are directed towards hospice providers.  The policy also includes the opportunity for Low Income Subsidy (LIS) to support long-term care needs.
