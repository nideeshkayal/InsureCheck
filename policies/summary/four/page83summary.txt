Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for patients with diabetes, including increased medication adherence support through physician-driven prescriptions, specialized pharmacy access, and new requirements for hospital stays. Updated rules address non-covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures, limiting coverage to drug lists and physician-driven prescriptions.  New provisions now cover chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease.  The MDPP introduces new hospital stay requirements and hospice program coverage, strengthening patient care.
