**Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) - Medicare & NJ FamilyCare Medicaid**

**Coverage:** Medical, dental, and surgical expenses, supplies, behavioral health, prescription drugs, and DME. Coverage includes emergency care, including urgent situations and disasters, with transfer approval needed.

**Key Terms:** MLTSS (Medical, Dental, and Surgical Expenses), PCA (Personal Care Assistant Services), transportation (MAVs & ambulance for non-emergency).

**Conditions:** Requires medical records from emergency providers for claims processing & reimbursement.

**Policy Details:** Effective date, limits, dates – please see the full policy summary.

**Important:** This policy covers eligible individuals in Medicare and NJ FamilyCare Medicaid.
