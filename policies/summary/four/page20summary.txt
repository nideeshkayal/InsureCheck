**Summary**

The Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage, offering dosage/usage limits impacting prescription drug costs. Coverage is dependent on provider participation and is available 24/7 via phone at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) and through the AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP website.

The policy includes specialized care support through a Care Manager and counseling services through SHIP. Coverage extends to behavioral health and mental health services with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME.  The call is free.

The Office of the Insurance Ombudsman and the New Jersey Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman offer assistance to address potential issues and provide guidance on service and billing problems.  Important resources are available on the Aetna website and Medicare.gov, detailing strategies for cost reduction.
