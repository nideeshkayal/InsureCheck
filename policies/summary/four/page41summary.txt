**Summary of NJDSNP Plan Details**

The Aetna Medicare’s NJDSNP plan provides comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care and behavioral health with transfer approval. Coverage limits apply, and eligibility is crucial. This plan covers medical, dental, and surgical services with specific conditions, notably potential out-of-network charges.

Key restrictions include medical record requirements, eligibility rules, and coverage limitations for outside-network services. Oxygen equipment rental is a 36-month contract with obligations and potential renewal. Members must call Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for assistance and access to the detailed benefits chart at AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.

The Benefits Chart details covered services, excluding out-of-network care and outlining the necessity of medically necessary services. It emphasizes the importance of understanding coverage limits and potential for PA. Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) participates in a special assistance program (LIS VBID) for health care needs, offering WHP services and ACP assistance.
