**Summary**

This Aetna Premier Plus Plus plan offers comprehensive coverage for routine medical services, including annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services. It includes an Extra Benefits Card providing $305/month transportation, utilities, personal care, and rent/mortgage assistance.

**Key Details:**

*   **HIV Screening:** The plan covers up to three HIV screening tests per pregnancy for women at risk.
*   **Home Health:** Prior authorization is required for home health services.
*   **Home Infusion Therapy:** Covered as a continuing benefit, requiring specific conditions to be met.
*   **Coverage Exclusions:**  Lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent payments are not covered.
