**Summary:**

This Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals, including specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. Key coverage areas encompass health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). Transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care are covered.

The policy mandates prior authorization for specialist services, particularly for kidney dialysis when outside the network’s service area for a short period.  A PCP plays a crucial role in coordinating care, consulting with other providers, and overseeing the process of obtaining prior authorization for services. Members must choose a Care Manager and inform them of their provider network.  Changes to PCP can occur at any time, requiring member notification.  A new PCP can be assigned, and the member receives a new membership record.  Specialty providers are covered up to four months after the termination of the notice period.
