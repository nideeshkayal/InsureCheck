Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices for eligible members up to 90 days. This policy includes emergency medications, experimental treatments, quantity limits, provider evaluations, temporary supply options (up to 30 days), and EOB details covering drug usage, costs, and price changes.

Crucially, the plan addresses NJ FamilyCare Extra Help, provider and pharmacy directories, and payment sources.  A key condition is Exception Requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage, impacting deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs.  Vaccinations are covered, with Medicare Part D vaccines being free.

Members are required to contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for any errors identified.  Visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for further information.
