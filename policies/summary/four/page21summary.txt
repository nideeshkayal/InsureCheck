**Summary**

The Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage, with dosage/usage limits impacting prescription drug costs. Coverage is dependent on provider participation and is available 24/7 via phone at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) and through the AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP website.

Key features include specialized care support through a Care Manager and counseling services through SHIP. Coverage extends to behavioral health and mental health services with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME. The call is free.

Important resources are available on the Aetna website and Medicare.gov regarding cost reduction strategies. Social Security determines eligibility, while Social Security handles enrollment for Medicare recipients aged 65+ or those with disabilities.  Address changes require notification to Social Security.
