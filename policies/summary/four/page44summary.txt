The Aetna Medicare NJDSNP plan offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care and behavioral health.  Coverage is based on a 24-month timeframe with potential for more frequent payments if medically necessary.  This plan includes coverage for annual routine physical benefits, covering bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening (mammograms), cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction visits, and blood tests for cervical and vaginal cancer. Importantly, Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services require prior authorization, and coverage extends to non-Medicare benefits.
