Medicare Part D Drug Decisions policy requires careful attention to medication exclusions.  New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs are subject to significant restrictions, impacting Medicare coverage. Prior authorization (PA) is mandatory for many medications, requiring a 24-hour response or 24-hour complaint for denial. Appeals are available after denial with expedited coverage options or fast appeal.  Expedited redetermination is also expedited.  If denied, an explanation letter is sent. Payments must be made within 30 days. The Independent Review Organization (IRO) reviews requests, requiring payment within 30 calendar days. The QIO, Livanta, oversees appeals and ensures medication appropriateness.  Contacting the QIO before discharge is crucial, and missed deadlines can result in payment for unapproved services after discharge.
