**Summary**

This document details member rights and responsibilities concerning Medicare Part D coverage, emphasizing crucial rights regarding medical record access, consent for PHI sharing, and treatment decisions. Members possess the right to modify their medical records, including updating them with physician consent.  The plan stipulates timely access to covered services, including specialized care and emergency medical treatments, with restrictions on direct billing for these services. Network providers cannot bill for covered services; balance billing or unapproved services are prohibited.

The document outlines coverage limitations – exclusions for specific services, research trials, and the right to refuse experimental treatments. It clarifies decision-making responsibilities, including the ability to refuse treatment, second opinions, and stopping prescribed medications. Members are entitled to request coverage decisions, utilize advance directives, and document their healthcare.  If an advance directive is obtained, the document emphasizes keeping a copy of the form.  If a signed advance directive is not available, the hospital will request it.

If instructions are not followed, members should contact Member Services for further information.  A complaint can be filed with the state agency overseeing advance directives, and the SHIP program is available at 1-800-792-8820 (TTY: 711) for more details.
