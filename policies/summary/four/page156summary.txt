**Summary:**

This document outlines the termination process for Medicare membership within the plan, emphasizing critical details.  If you choose to terminate your membership, you must request a disenclosure within 60 days of denial. This process involves two options: enrolling in a new Medicare plan or switching to Original Medicare.  

Specifically, you can request a disenclosure via written request to Member Services at 1-800-MEDICARE (TTY: 711) or by calling 24/7.  Alternatively, you can enroll in a new Medicare health plan.  The document details three options for Medicare services, with the option to automatically terminate your membership when a new plan begins coverage. The SHIP program is also available for assistance.
