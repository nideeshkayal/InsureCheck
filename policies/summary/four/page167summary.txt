**Summary:**

This policy, Aetna Premier Plus D-SNP, offers comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, including patient care and support. Key features include Care Manager, Care Plan, and Care Team roles, necessitating engagement with CMS. Coverage includes exclusions for specific drug types, and careful review of billing practices is vital to avoid unapproved costs. Individualized Care Plans (ICPs) outline services, encompassing medical, behavioral health, and long-term support. The Drug List dictates specific drug requirements and limitations, ensuring adherence to accepted medical standards. Eligible patients participate in hospice care and are directed toward hospice providers. Low Income Subsidy (LIS) is available to support long-term care needs. The policy requires comprehensive coverage of Medicare and Medicaid, establishing a dual eligibility structure.  Medicare Advantage (MA) plans offer private coverage through companies, while Medicare Part A covers essential medical services.
