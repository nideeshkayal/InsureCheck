**Comprehensive Coverage for Medicare NJDSNP**

This plan provides comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage for 24 months, contingent upon medical necessity. Key coverage includes annual routine physical benefits prioritizing bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening (mammograms), cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction visits, and blood tests for cervical and vaginal cancer.

Notably, Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services require prior authorization. Coverage extends to non-Medicare benefits, and the plan continues with the next page. Prior authorization responsibilities lie with the provider.

The page details colorectal cancer screening, including colonoscopy (no minimum age limit), flexible sigmoidoscopy (120 months), and fecal occult blood tests (twice per year). Cervical and vaginal cancer screening (Pap tests and pelvic exams every 12 months) are also covered. Chiropractic services, genetic testing (multitarget stool DNA - once every 3 years), and dental services are included.

The plan covers diagnostic, preventive, restorative, and surgical services, including x-rays and other diagnostic imaging, and fluoride treatments.
