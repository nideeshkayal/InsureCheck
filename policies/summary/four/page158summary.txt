**Summary**

This document outlines termination procedures for Medicare membership within the Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) plan. Members must request a disenclosure within 60 days of denial and enroll in a new plan or switch to Original Medicare. Options include enrolling in a new plan, a Dual-Eligible Special Needs Plan (D-SNP), or voluntary plan selection. Automatic disenclosure occurs upon Original Medicare beginning. SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) offers assistance. The document provides instructions for contacting SHIP (1-800-792-8820 (TTY: 711)) and NJ FamilyCare (1-800-701-0710) for support.  Important considerations include Medicare’s drug coverage enrollment requirements, which necessitates separate enrollment or switching to a drug plan if no other sources are available. Disenrolling triggers automatic enrollment into the affiliated NJ FamilyCare plan, Aetna Better Health of New Jersey, for your NJ FamilyCare benefits.  The plan continues to provide coverage until the membership ends, with ongoing access to network providers for medical care, pharmacy services, and hospital coverage.
