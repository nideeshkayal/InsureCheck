The document outlines termination procedures for Medicare members within the Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) plan. Members must request disconnection within 60 days of denial, enrolling in a new plan or switching to Original Medicare. Automatic disenrollment occurs upon Original Medicare commencement.

Key considerations include Medicare Part A and Part B coverage requirements; if Medicaid eligibility is lost, the plan provides a grace period of up to three months, with automatic enrollment in the NJ FamilyCare plan. Moving out of the service area or prolonged absence triggers disenrollment.

The document addresses potential issues like incorrect information during enrollment, disruptive behavior, unauthorized use of Member IDs, and requests to leave the plan for any health-related reason. Medicare can be contacted at 1 800 MEDICARE (1 800-633-4227) or Aetna Better Health of New Jersey (1-844-362-0934) for assistance with eligibility and disenrollment.
