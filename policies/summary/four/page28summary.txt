**Summary:**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. Key coverage areas include health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). Transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care are covered.

The policy mandates prior authorization for specialist services, particularly for kidney dialysis when outside the network's service area for a short period. The PCP plays a central role in coordinating care, consulting with other providers, and obtaining prior authorization.  Changes to the PCP are required with member notification. A new PCP can be assigned, and the member receives a new membership record. Speciality providers are covered up to four months after the termination of the notice period. Network providers may leave the plan, requiring notification to ensure uninterrupted access to qualified providers.
