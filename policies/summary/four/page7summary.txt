**Summary:**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage for eligible individuals aged 19 and over within Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid contracts. The plan provides coverage for behavioral health and mental health treatment, encompassing prescription drug costs for Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. Enrollment is subject to contract renewal and change. Key eligibility requirements include reviewing contract renewal status and establishing a Member ID Card, Provider & Pharmacy Directory, and Explanation of Benefits. 

Coverage extends to in-network providers, including independent contractors, and includes DME suppliers. Out-of-network providers are not obligated to treat members. Mail order prescriptions are available, and calls are available for delivery within 10 days. Privacy of personal health information is crucial.

To access coverage, call Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for more information.
