**Summary:**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. The policy offers specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. Key features include medication dosage limits, impacting prescription drug costs, and coverage for behavioral health and mental health services – with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, DME, and other healthcare professionals.

The policy covers health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). It includes transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care. Coverage extends to clinical research studies, payments for durable medical equipment, and includes all services covered by Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare. 

Important details include limitations on out-of-network care, requiring prior authorization for emergency or urgently needed services. The policy covers kidney dialysis when outside the network's service area for a short period.
