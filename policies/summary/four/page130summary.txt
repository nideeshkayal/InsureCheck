**Summary of Insurance Policy – IURO Appeal**

The current page details the process for appealing insurance decisions, particularly through the Independent Review Organization (IRO).  Authorization is required within 72 hours of an appeal or external appeal.  The IURO review process is optional; you can request an appeal and wait for a decision, or initiate it simultaneously.

The IURO’s decision can be upheld, or an appeal can be granted, depending on the level of appeal and the medical necessity of the case.  A Level 3 appeal or Fair Hearing may be possible.

The page clarifies the services *not* covered by the IURO, including assisted living programs, caregiver training, chore services, community transition services, and home-delivered meals.  It highlights the importance of requesting the External Appeal Application form within 60 days of the decision letter for your Level 1 appeal.  The New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance (1‑888‑393‑1062 or 609‑777‑9470) offers assistance with the IURO appeal.
