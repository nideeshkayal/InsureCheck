Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage has strict deadlines for appeals – a 10-day window after initial request. Failure to appeal within this timeframe results in coverage cessation. Medicare Part B offers two levels of appeal: a fast appeal for quicker resolution, and a Level 2 appeal requiring a thorough review.

The insurance company must authorize or provide coverage within 72 hours of the appeal, regardless of whether it’s a fast appeal or a Level 2 review. A Level 2 appeal follows, with the insurance provider assessing the request within 72 hours.

If the request is for a Medicare Part B prescription drug, the insurance provider must provide an answer within 7 calendar days after the appeal.

If the request is denied, the appeal is routed to the IRO (Independent Utilization Review Organization), and the insurance company sends a letter. For Medicaid services or items, a Level 2 appeal can be filed with the state as soon as the time is up.
