Medicare Part D drug coverage decisions prioritize evidence of coverage to determine eligibility. Key coverage elements include assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals. Significant exclusions apply to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs, requiring prior authorization (PA) for certain medications. Requests for formulary exceptions necessitate a medical justification and physician’s statement.

The policy emphasizes the doctor’s responsibility to provide a detailed medical explanation for each request. If denied, an appeal process is available. Deadlines for standard coverage decisions are 72 hours after request or 14 calendar days after doctor’s statement. Expedited coverage decisions are available with a 24-hour response, and a fast complaint can be filed within 24 hours.

Deadlines for a standard coverage decision about a drug you didn’t get are 72 hours after request. If we don’t meet this deadline, we send your request to Level 2 of the appeals process for review by an IRO. An appeal to our plan about a Medicare Part D drug coverage decision is called a plan “redetermination.” Ensure you submit your appeal within 60 calendar days of the initial letter.
