Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage. The policy emphasizes dosage/usage limits, impacting prescription drug costs, and includes appeals for coverage disputes.  It offers specialized health care support through a Care Manager and counseling services through SHIP.  The policy covers behavioral health and mental health services with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME.  Coverage is dependent on provider participation, and the call is available 24/7 at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) and via the AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP website.  The website provides resources for Medicare recipients, including information on Medicare changes and services.
