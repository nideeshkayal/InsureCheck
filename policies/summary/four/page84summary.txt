Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements significantly expand coverage for patients with diabetes, including enhanced physician-driven prescriptions, specialized pharmacy access, and updated hospital stay requirements. Key changes address non-covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures, limiting coverage to drug lists and physician-driven prescriptions. New provisions now cover chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. The MDPP introduces new hospital stay requirements and hospice program coverage, strengthening patient care.
