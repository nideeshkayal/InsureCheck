Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays, including inpatient, outpatient, and nutritional therapy. Coverage extends to 28 meals after discharge, contingent on provider confirmation.

Crucially, specialist services, particularly for mental health, require prior authorization from the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease, including dietary and physical activity guidance.

Prior authorization is required for specialist services, including mental health, and is delegated to the provider. IV immune globulin and other drugs for home dialysis are covered. Nursing facility care, including meals, nursing services, and therapy, is also included.

The plan also covers obesity screening and therapy, continuing this benefit on the next page.  Prior authorization may be required and is the responsibility of your provider.  Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services and supplies are covered, including x-rays, radiation therapy, and supplies. Surgical supplies, such as dressings, are also covered, with prior authorization required.
