**Policy Overview:** This document outlines the rights and responsibilities of Medicare Part D members, emphasizing understanding coverage and ensuring proactive care management.

**Key Obligations:** Members are responsible for all costs, including unapproved services, and must provide necessary information to their Care Manager. Accurate information regarding health conditions, medications, and support systems is crucial.

**Service Request & Communication:** Members can request service changes at any time, including adding, decreasing, or discontinuing services, and are required to receive a list of Care Manager personnel.

**Facility Rights & Responsibilities:**  The document details the importance of adhering to facility-specific resident rights, including receiving facility-specific resident rights when admitted.

**Important Considerations:**  Transfer and discharge must be justified for medical necessity, requiring reasonable notice of nonpayment and proper communication with the facility.
