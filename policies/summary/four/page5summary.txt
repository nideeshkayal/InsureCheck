Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage for individuals aged 19 and over through December 31, 2024. It’s a Dual Eligible Special Needs Plan with Medicare and New Jersey Medicaid contracts. Key coverage includes Medicare and NJ FamilyCare prescription drug costs, excluding certain services.

The plan covers behavioral health and MLTSS, including mental health and substance use disorder treatment.  Enrollees must use in-network providers and DME suppliers.  Enrollment depends on contract renewal and is subject to change.

Provider referrals are not required for in-network providers. Participating healthcare providers are independent contractors. Network composition can shift.  Out-of-network providers are under no obligation to treat plan members.  Mail order prescriptions are available through the network mail order delivery program, and call 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for delivery within 10 days.
