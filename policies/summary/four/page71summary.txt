**Summary:**

Aetna Premier Plus Plus Coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits for members aged 50-77, Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries, and individuals/groups undergoing therapy and nursing. Coverage includes outpatient diagnoses, therapy, nursing, and medication, with prior authorization required for surgery and partial hospitalization.

The plan covers physician/provider services, including doctor’s office visits, and limited services like jaw surgery, fracture treatment, and foot care. Podiatry services are also covered.

**Important Note:** Prior authorization is required for all services, including prostate cancer screening exams, and is the member’s responsibility.  Coverage details are outlined in the Evidence of Coverage chapter and the Aetna Medicare.com/NJDSNP website.
