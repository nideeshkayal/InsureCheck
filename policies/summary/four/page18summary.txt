**Summary:**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage, including prescription drug benefits with zero premiums and no premium. Coverage extends to behavioral health and mental health services, offering limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME, with coverage dependent on provider participation.

The policy emphasizes dosage/usage limits, impacting prescription drug costs. Appeals are available for coverage disputes. A Care Manager is assigned to each member, providing specialized health care support.

SHIP offers free counseling to Medicare recipients, including Medicare changes and SHIP services. Liventa, a group of healthcare professionals, offers quality improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing care quality for Medicare recipients.
