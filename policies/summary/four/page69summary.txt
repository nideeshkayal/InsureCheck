**Coverage Summary - Aetna Premier Plus Plus**

This plan provides comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays, utilizing Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services. Key coverage includes outpatient diagnostic tests, therapeutic services, and nursing facility care. Prior authorization is required for provider-controlled services like surgery and partial hospitalization.

The plan includes personalized care assistance (PCA) for home-based healthcare, offering extended-release Naltrexone (Vivitrol) treatment for substance use disorder.  Mental health services are covered including individual and group sessions, and psychiatric services (individual and group).  Telehealth services, including primary care physician consultations, are offered, provided via telehealth for specific conditions.

**Important Note:** This coverage is a supplement to telehealth services, including addiction treatment, and includes urgency services.  Coverage is contingent on provider availability.
