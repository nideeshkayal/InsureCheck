**Drug Coverage Summary**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Coverage extends up to 90 days for long-term care facility stays, requiring supply notifications and provider assessments. Emergency medications and experimental treatments are subject to quantity limits and provider evaluation. Temporary supply options are available up to 30 days.

The Policy emphasizes proactive drug utilization through exception requests referencing Chapter 5 of the Evidence of Coverage. Eligibility for NJ FamilyCare results in “Extra Help” for Medicare Part D prescription drug costs, impacting premiums, deductibles, and copays. Provider and pharmacy directories list crucial network pharmacies for covered drugs, and provider assessments are mandatory.

The Drug List limits coverage and identifies essential drug costs.  The EOB provides details of drug usage, costs, and price changes.  NJ FamilyCare provides additional coverage for prescription drugs not covered under Medicare, including some over-the-counter drugs.
