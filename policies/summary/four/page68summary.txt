Aetna Premier Plus Plus provides comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly mental health, delegated to the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease, including dietary and physical activity guidance.

The plan includes outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services, including x-rays, radiation therapy, and supplies. Nursing facility care, including meals and therapy, is covered, and obesity screening and therapy are also included. Independent practitioner networks are utilized for mental health care, offering extended-release Naltrexone (vivitrol) treatment. Substance use disorder treatment services are covered, including alcohol misuse screening, referrals, prescription drugs, and treatment for conditions.

Prior authorization is required for provider-controlled services, such as outpatient surgery and partial hospitalization services. Personalized Care Assistance (PCA) provides home-based healthcare assistance under supervision, requiring provider authorization.
