**Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) Enhancements**

The MDPP now offers wigs for chemotherapy hair loss, a significant benefit for patients. Crucially, it includes supervised exercise therapy (SET) training, transportation, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs) for terminal prognoses, aligning with hospice care eligibility. Emergency care is covered.

The MDPP extends coverage to chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for the feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. Veterans receive VA-specific coverage.

A key exclusion is not covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, surgical treatment for morbid obesity, or items considered not “reasonable and medically necessary.” Refer to Chapter 9 of your Evidence of Coverage for appeals.

The MDPP’s outpatient drug coverage rules emphasize doctor-written prescriptions, valid under state law, and using a network pharmacy for the “Drug List” (Plan’s List of Covered Drugs). Drug use must be medically accepted, approved by the FDA, or supported by medical references.
