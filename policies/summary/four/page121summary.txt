Medicare provides guidance on prescription drug requests, including key terms defining the process. Coverage decisions involve reviewing benefits and coverage for medical services or drugs, potentially resulting in denials or appeals. The document emphasizes navigating problems and complaints, including Office of the Insurance Ombudsman Program (1-800-446-7467) and coverage decision appeals.  If a coverage decision is made, the user or their doctor can request a coverage decision, which includes appealing denial, Level 1 Appeal, and alternative appeals. If a decision is denied, the user can appeal, and if unsuccessful, can request expedited or “fast coverage” decisions.  Support resources include Member Services and SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) for assistance.
