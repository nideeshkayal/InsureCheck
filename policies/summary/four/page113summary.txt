Medicare Part D coverage includes medical record access, consent for PHI sharing, and treatment decisions.  The plan stipulates timely access to covered services, including specialized care and emergency medical treatments, with restrictions on direct billing for these services. Network providers cannot bill for covered services; balance billing or unapproved services are prohibited.  Members must follow instructions, request coverage decisions, utilize advance directives, and document their healthcare.  The Ombudsperson Program (1-800-446-7467) and the New Jersey Medicaid program offer assistance.  Members are responsible for covering all costs, including any services not covered, and must communicate with Member Services regarding questions and coverage.  The plan emphasizes respecting other members' rights and expectations.
