**Summary**

This Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) plan offers comprehensive coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+ within Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid contracts. Coverage includes behavioral health and mental health treatment, encompassing prescription drug costs for both Medicare and NJ FamilyCare.

Key eligibility requirements include verifying contract renewal status and establishing a Member ID Card, Provider & Pharmacy Directory, and Explanation of Benefits. The plan extends coverage to in-network providers, including independent contractors and DME suppliers. Out-of-network providers are not obligated to treat members. Mail order prescriptions are available, with delivery within 10 days.

Privacy of personal health information is paramount. The plan’s service area covers counties in New Jersey – Atlantic, Bergen, Burlington, Camden, Cape May, Cumberland, Essex, Gloucester, Hudson, Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex, Monmouth, Morris, Ocean, Passaic, Salem, Somerset, Sussex, Union, Warren. Individuals must reside within this service area to join, and eligibility is contingent on Medicare Part A and Part B coverage, being a US citizen or lawfully present, and being currently eligible for full NJ FamilyCare benefits. Call Member Services for more details.
