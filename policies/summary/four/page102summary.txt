Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP offers comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices up to 90 days. Key conditions include Exception Requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage, impacting deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs.  Vaccinations are covered, with Medicare Part D vaccines free. Members must contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) to report any errors and visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for further details.  The policy dictates that network providers must bill the plan for covered services and drugs after enrollment, and Aetna directly handles provider payments.  Providers are prohibited from billing for services not covered.
