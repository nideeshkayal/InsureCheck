**Medicare Part D Coverage Review Process**

This page outlines the process for addressing coverage decisions related to Medicare Part D. If denied, a QIO review within 14 calendar days determines initial decisions. If approved, the QIO pays for hospital services until medically necessary, maintaining the original discharge date.  The QIO maintains the original decision if denial is upheld and an Alternate Appeal process is available. A Level 2 Alternate Appeal is possible if disagreement exists with the initial denial, requiring a review of the case. Contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) to initiate the process, and visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for more information.

Reviewers at the QIO will examine your medical information, including your plan’s details, and any relevant information they receive.  They’ll provide a detailed explanation of the denial within one full day.  If approved, the QIO will cover your services for as long as medically necessary. If denied, your coverage ends on the date the QIO communicated the decision, with the cost of care still being borne by you.
