Aetna Premier Plus Plus provides comprehensive medical coverage, including routine medical services like annual physicals, bone mass measurements, breast cancer screenings, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screenings, chiropractic care, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services. Crucially, the plan covers up to three HIV screening tests per pregnancy for women at risk, including home health services requiring prior authorization, and hospice care (eligible for Original Medicare). Coverage exclusions include lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent payments.  The plan also covers drug therapy and spiritual counseling, continuing the benefits on the next page.
