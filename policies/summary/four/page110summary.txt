**Summary of Evidence of Coverage – Chapter 8**

This chapter details member rights and responsibilities concerning coverage and benefits. Members have the right to access, correct, and update their medical records.  Crucially, they must obtain written consent before sharing PHI with government agencies or providing it to others.

The document emphasizes timely access to covered services, including specialized care and emergency services without prior approval. Members have the right to leave the plan if they choose, and are entitled to receive most of their healthcare through Medicare or other MA plans.  Network providers cannot bill directly for covered services, and must refrain from balance billing or charging for services they don't cover.  The chapter also details limitations to coverage, including exclusions and specific rules for obtaining covered services.
