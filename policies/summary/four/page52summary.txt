**Summary**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for routine medical care, including annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.  Prior authorization is required for ASD services, coverage for Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) is provider-determined with limits on monitoring, and continued coverage for depression screening and diabetes screenings is included.

The plan provides an Extra Benefits Card with a $305 monthly benefit, covering transportation, utilities, personal care items, and assistance with rent or mortgage.  Partnership with NationsBenefits ensures this benefit is delivered before or shortly after the plan begins. A $150 annual benefit is available for approved home and bathroom safety devices, with a limit of three orders per year.

Important notes include that the plan is not responsible for lost or stolen cards, late utilities, or rent payments.  Coverage for Fall Prevention provides a $150 annual benefit towards safety devices, while a limit of 3 orders per year applies. This benefit continues on the next page.
