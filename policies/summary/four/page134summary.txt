Medicare Part D drug coverage decisions prioritize evidence of coverage to determine eligibility. The policy outlines coverage limits and exclusions, focusing on covered services like assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals.  Significant exclusions apply to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance drugs, requiring prior authorization (PA) for certain medications.  Requests for formulary exceptions – such as covering alternative drugs or removing restrictions – necessitate a medical justification and must be submitted by a physician.  The policy emphasizes that the doctor must provide a detailed medical explanation for the request.  If denied, an appeal process is available.  Refer to Section G5 for details on requesting coverage decisions and exceptions.
