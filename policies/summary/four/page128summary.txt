The Insurance Company requires authorization within 72 hours of an appeal, regardless of whether it’s a fast or Level 2 review.  If the request is for a Medicare Part B prescription drug, the provider must provide a response within 7 calendar days. A Level 2 appeal initiates a review by the Independent Review Organization (IRO), which can take up to 14 calendar days to complete. If the request is denied, the appeal is routed to the IRO, and the insurance company sends a letter.  For Medicaid or NJ FamilyCare services, a Level 2 appeal can be filed with the state within 10 calendar days of the appeal’s completion.  If the request is for a service covered only by Medicare, the benefits continue during the IRO review. The IRO reviews appeals for independent organization, ensuring compliance with Medicare rules.  A fast appeal can be a Level 2 appeal, and a standard appeal can be a Level 2 appeal.
