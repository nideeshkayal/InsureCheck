**Summary:**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for routine medical care, including annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services. Prior authorization is required for ASD services, coverage for Continuous Glucose Monitors (CGMs) is provider-determined with limits on monitoring, and continued coverage for depression screening and diabetes screenings is included.

Emergency care is defined as services provided by trained medical professionals in emergencies, requiring immediate treatment to mitigate severe pain, serious injury, or life-threatening conditions. It includes transfers to other hospitals when necessary to ensure patient safety, specifically addressing pregnant women in active labor when timely transfer is critical. The plan also offers an Extra Benefits Card with a monthly $305 benefit to cover daily expenses.
