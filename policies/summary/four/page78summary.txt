Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries receive comprehensive coverage for physician services, prosthetics, pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors) due to a primary care provider session and SNF care. The plan mandates two quit attempts within a 12-month period and covers OTC smoking cessation products and SET for PAD. Crucially, the plan includes supervised exercise therapy (SET) training, transportation for services, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs). Urgent care is covered.

A new benefit is now offered: wigs for hair loss related to chemotherapy. This service is for up to $400 annually and is offered for hair loss resulting from chemotherapy. Medicare certified hospice care is available for individuals with a terminal prognosis, and the plan supports finding these programs.

The page highlights exclusions: “Excluded” benefits are not covered by Medicare, Medicaid, or NJ FamilyCare. The benefits include: not covered medical benefits (except under specific conditions), experimental medical treatments, surgical treatment for morbid obesity, and items considered not “reasonable and medically necessary.”  Refer to Chapter 9 of your Evidence of Coverage for appeals.
