Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. Key coverage areas include health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). Transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care are covered.

**Important Policy Details:**

*   **Prior Authorization:** Specialist services, particularly kidney dialysis, require prior authorization from the PCP. Changes to the PCP are required with member notification.
*   **Network Coverage:** Network providers can leave the plan, necessitating notification to ensure uninterrupted access to qualified providers.
*   **Out-of-Network Coverage:** If a provider leaves, an out-of-network specialist may be provided for up to four months after termination.
*   **Quality of Care:**  Members have the right to file a quality of care complaint if they believe care is inadequate.
*   **Provider Transition:** If a provider leaves, members should contact us to assist in finding a new provider.
