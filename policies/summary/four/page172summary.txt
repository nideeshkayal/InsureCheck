**Summary:**

This D-SNP policy provides comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, encompassing Medicare and Medicaid eligibility. Dual eligibility is required for both, with meticulous billing review to avoid unapproved costs. Key features include dedicated hospice care for low-income individuals, medication management, and access to specific medications. The policy emphasizes quality improvement organizations (QIOs) and referral systems, requiring a PCP’s approval for providers other than the PCP. Rehabilitation services are covered, with Chapter 4 detailing treatment pathways. Significant changes include the introduction of specialized care for recovery, ensuring continuous, skilled rehabilitation. Urgent care, SSI, and Step Therapy are covered, but not social security benefits.  The policy utilizes a translation service for English-speaking individuals, offering free interpretation for questions about health or medication plans.
