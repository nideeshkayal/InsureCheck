Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage through December 31, 2024. The Member Handbook details coverage for health care services including behavioral health, prescription drugs, and Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS). Key terms define services offered, such as mental health and substance use disorder treatment. The plan covers prescription drug costs, but exclusions apply.  The document is available in Spanish and other formats.  Aetna emphasizes understanding the plan through the handbook.  Member Services is available for free support.  Interpreter services are available for those who require assistance in different languages.
