Aetna Premier Plus Plus Coverage provides comprehensive medical benefits for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries and individuals/groups undergoing therapy and nursing. Coverage includes outpatient diagnoses, therapy, nursing, and medication, with prior authorization required for surgery and partial hospitalization. The plan covers physician/provider services, including doctor’s office visits, and limited services like jaw surgery, fracture treatment, and foot care. Prosthetic devices and related supplies are a key benefit, including breast prostheses and repairs. Pulmonary rehabilitation services are available for members with moderate to very severe COPD, requiring a doctor's order. Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) screening and counseling are covered, including chlamydia, gonorrhea, syphilis, and hepatitis B screenings for pregnant women and at-risk individuals.  Prior authorization is mandatory for all services, including prostate cancer screening exams.  Coverage details are outlined in the Evidence of Coverage chapter and the Aetna Medicare.com/NJDSNP website.
