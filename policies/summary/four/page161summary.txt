Aetna Assure Premier Plus members in HMO D-SNP coverage must request disconnection within 60 days of denial or enrollment in a new plan or Original Medicare. Automatic disenrollment occurs upon Original Medicare commencement.  

Crucially, Medicare Part A and B coverage requirements include grace periods for Medicaid eligibility loss and disenrollment triggers. Members should contact Aetna Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or Aetna Better Health of New Jersey (1-844-362-0934) for assistance with eligibility and disenrollment.
