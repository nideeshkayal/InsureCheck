The Aetna Medicare NJDSNP plan offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage for a 24-month period, contingent on medical necessity. Coverage includes annual routine physical benefits, prioritizing bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening (mammograms), cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction visits, and blood tests for cervical and vaginal cancer.

Notably, Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services require prior authorization. Coverage extends to non-Medicare benefits, and the plan continues with the next page.  Prior authorization responsibilities lie with the provider.

The page details coverage for colorectal cancer screening, including colonoscopy (no minimum age limit), flexible sigmoidoscopy (120 months), and screening fecal occult blood tests (twice per year).  Special considerations include Cervical and vaginal cancer screening (Pap tests and pelvic exams every 12 months), Chiropractic services (spine adjustments), and genetic testing (multitarget stool DNA - once every 3 years).
