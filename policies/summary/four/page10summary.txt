Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage to eligible individuals aged 19+ within designated areas. Coverage includes behavioral health and mental health treatment, with prescription drug costs for both Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. Key eligibility requirements include contract renewal status verification, Member ID card completion, Provider & Pharmacy Directory enrollment, and DME supplier inclusion.

The plan’s Health Risk Assessment (HRA) assesses care needs, tailored to medical, behavioral health, and functional needs. Monthly costs are zero, and the plan offers no premium. Members are required to follow all rules outlined in the Evidence of Coverage document, including appeals procedures. Access to the Evidence of Coverage can be obtained by calling Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 or visiting AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.
