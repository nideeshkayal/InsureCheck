**Medicare Part D Coverage Review**

This document details the process for addressing Medicare Part D coverage decisions. A QIO reviews within 14 days determines initial decisions. Approved, the QIO pays for hospital services until medically necessary, maintaining the original discharge date. QIO maintains the original decision if denial is upheld and an Alternate Appeal is available. A Level 2 Alternate Appeal is possible if disagreement exists with the initial denial, requiring a case review.
