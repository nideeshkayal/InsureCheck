Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers coverage for Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid eligible individuals 19+. Key coverage includes prescription drug benefits, with limited dosage/usage. The policy covers behavioral health and mental health services, offering zero premiums and no premium. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME. Members must report changes promptly.

The coverage decision involves dosage/usage limits, impacting prescription drug costs. The policy offers evidence of coverage, emphasizing coverage decisions about prescription drugs. Appeals are available for coverage disputes. A Care Manager is assigned to each member, providing specialized health care support and guidance.
