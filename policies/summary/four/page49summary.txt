**Medicare Plan Coverage Summary**

This plan offers 24-month coverage for Medicare Plan Coverage Summary.  It includes annual routine physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, cardiovascular disease risk reduction, cervical/vaginal cancer screening, colorectal cancer screening (colonoscopy, sigmoidoscopy, Pap tests), chiropractic, genetic testing (multitarget stool DNA), dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.

**Important Considerations:**

*   Prior authorization is required for ASD services.
*   Diabetes screening (fasting glucose, risk factors) is covered. Continued coverage for depression screening and diabetes screenings is included.
*   Supplies for diabetic management, including training, devices, and tests, are covered.
*   Non-life scan monitoring is not covered.

This plan emphasizes prior authorization for ASD services. Coverage for continuous glucose monitors (CGMs) is determined by the provider, with limits on the number of monitors per year and test strips. Prior authorization is required for more than one blood glucose monitor per year.
