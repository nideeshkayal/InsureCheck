**Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) Enhancements**

The MDPP now offers wigs for chemotherapy hair loss, a new benefit for up to $400 annually. This addition provides crucial coverage for hair loss related to chemotherapy, addressing a significant need for patients.

Crucially, the MDPP includes supervised exercise therapy (SET) training, transportation, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs) for individuals with terminal prognoses, aligning with hospice care eligibility.  Emergency care is covered.

The page highlights exclusions: not covered medical benefits (except under specific conditions), experimental medical treatments, surgical treatment for morbid obesity, and items considered not “reasonable and medically necessary.” Refer to Chapter 9 of your Evidence of Coverage for appeals.

The MDPP also includes coverage for chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for the feet – exceptions for diabetic foot disease.  Veterans receive VA-specific coverage.
