Medicare Part D drug decisions require stringent scrutiny due to Medicare’s restrictions. Expedited redetermination is available after denial, with expedited coverage options. If denied, a QIO review, within 14 calendar days, determines the initial decision.

If approved, the QIO will pay for hospital services until medically necessary, maintaining the original discharge date.  The QIO maintains the original decision if denial is upheld, and an Alternate Appeal process is available for disagreement.

The IRO reviews requests within 30 calendar days, and expedited redetermination is also available.  A Level 2 Alternate Appeal is possible if you disagree with the initial denial, ensuring a review of the case.
