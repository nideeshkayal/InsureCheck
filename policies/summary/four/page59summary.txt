Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage, including routine medical services, cardiac rehabilitation, and dental care. Coverage includes inpatient hospital care, outpatient services, including mental health care, including psychiatric hospital stays, and dialysis treatments.  The plan covers drug therapy and spiritual counseling. Crucially, it includes prior authorization for inpatient services in psychiatric hospitals, acute inpatient hospitalization, and short-term care facilities, as well as kidney disease care and supplies.  Coverage exclusions include lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent payments.  Prior authorization is required for specialist services, including mental health care, and is managed by the provider.
