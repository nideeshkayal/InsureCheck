**Policy Summary:**

**Coverage:** Coverage amounts and limits are outlined. Key terms include “Level 3 appeal,” “Drug requests,” and “Expedited grievances.”

**Conditions & Exclusions:**  Appeals are tiered (IRO review -> Level 3 -> Level 4) and can lead to drug requests. Grievances must be filed within 60 days of denial. Expedited grievances resolve within 24 hours if beneficial.

**Policy Details:** Appeals and Greivances (PO Box 818070, Cleveland, OH 44181) address complaints. Member Services (1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711)) can be contacted within 60 days of denial. OCR available for complaints, with a complaint form at www.medicare.gov/MedicareComplaintForm/home.aspx and a phone number 1-800-368-1019.

**Important:**  Medicare answers complaints within 30 days of a decision if further information is needed.
