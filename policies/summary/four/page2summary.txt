Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage through December 31, 2024. The plan covers behavioral health, prescription drugs, and Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS). Key terms define services like mental health and substance use disorder treatment, which are covered under the plan.

Crucially, the policy covers prescription drug costs, but exclusions apply for certain services.  Aetna emphasizes understanding the plan through the handbook, with free interpreter services available for individuals needing assistance in different languages.  The document is available in Spanish and other formats.
