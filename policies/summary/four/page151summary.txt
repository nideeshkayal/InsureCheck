Medicare Part D denial review involves tiered appeals, starting with an initial IRO review followed by Level 1/2 appeals escalating to Level 3.  If a Level 3 appeal is denied, the appeals process continues, potentially leading to a Level 4 appeal. Level 4 appeals focus on drug requests involving dollar thresholds, requiring a final service date. The Council reviews appeals, providing an answer. If the Council denies the appeal, the appeals process continues, potentially culminating in a Level 5 appeal for drug requests.  To file a grievance, members can submit written complaints to Appeals and Greivances (PO Box 818070, Cleveland, OH 44181) within 60 calendar days of the problem, or call Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711).  Expedited grievances can be resolved within 24 hours if they benefit the claimant.
