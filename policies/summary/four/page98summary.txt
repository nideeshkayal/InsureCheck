**Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP** provides coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Coverage extends up to 90 days for long-term care facility stays.

**Key terms:** Emergency medications, experimental treatments, quantity limits, provider evaluation, temporary supply options (up to 30 days), EOB details (drug usage, costs, price changes), NJ FamilyCare (Extra Help for Medicare Part D), provider and pharmacy directories, and payment sources.

**Important conditions:** Exception requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage impact deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs. Provider/Pharmacy directories list essential network pharmacies. Provider assessments are mandatory.

**EOB:** Detailed drug usage, costs, and price changes.
