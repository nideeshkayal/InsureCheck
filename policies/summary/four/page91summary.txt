Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Key coverage includes emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures, all subject to quantity limits and provider assessment. Temporary supply options are available for up to 30 days, extending coverage beyond the initial 90 days.

The Drug List is the primary source for coverage determination, with quantity limits and provider evaluation required. An exception request is possible for supply runs, but providers must notify the insurance company. The Drug List may be updated, potentially resulting in generic drug substitutions or changes to brand name drugs. Coverage extends beyond the initial 90 days for long-term care facilities, and a one-time prescription override is needed for supply runs.

The policy emphasizes proactive management of drug utilization through exception requests, referencing Chapter 9 of the Evidence of Coverage for detailed exceptions.
