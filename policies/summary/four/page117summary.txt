**Summary:**

This document outlines responsibilities for Medicare Part D members, emphasizing proactive care management.  Members are obligated to cover all costs associated with health services, including unapproved treatments. Accurate health condition, medication, and support system information is vital for effective care planning.

The document details requirements for facility-specific resident rights, ensuring compliance with these rights when admitted. It’s crucial to justify transfer and discharge decisions for medical necessity, requiring timely notification of non-payment and appropriate communication with the facility.

The chapter addresses various issues including service changes, disagreements with plan decisions, and concerns about long-term services and supports.  It provides guidance on navigating potential problems and complaints, including the Office of the Insurance Ombudsman Program – contact information is available at 1-800-446-7467.
