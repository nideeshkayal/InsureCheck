**Summary**

This plan offers comprehensive coverage for routine medical care, including annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.  It includes an Extra Benefits Card providing a $305 monthly benefit covering transportation, utilities, personal care, and rent/mortgage assistance.

Key coverage includes Fall prevention (continued), family planning services, and the law’s choice of provider – either network or out-of-network.  Coverage for fertility services like family planning exams, lab tests, methods, supplies, counseling, and STI testing is provided, but depends on the provider’s network status.  The plan pays for medical treatment related to infertility and HIV/AIDS, genetic testing, and some other family planning services.

Important details include the $150 annual home and bathroom safety device benefit, with a limit of three orders per year, and the continued coverage for depression screening and diabetes screenings.  All services are subject to provider network requirements.  The plan does not cover lost or stolen cards, late utility payments, or rent payments. This benefit continues on the next page.
