Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays. Coverage includes inpatient hospital care, outpatient services (mental health, dialysis), and nutritional therapy. 

Crucially, the plan covers 28 meals over 14 days after discharge, contingent on provider confirmation. Medical nutrition therapy is a key benefit, specifically for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease without dialysis, and for after a kidney transplant when ordered by a physician.

Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly mental health, and managed by the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered, including MDPP training on long-term dietary changes and physical activity.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services are available at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711), and more information is available at AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.  Be sure to verify coverage details with your provider.
