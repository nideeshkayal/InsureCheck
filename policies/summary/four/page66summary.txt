Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays, including inpatient, outpatient, and nutritional therapy. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly mental health, delegated to the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease, including dietary and physical activity guidance.

Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services are covered, including x-rays, radiation therapy, and supplies, with prior authorization required. Nursing facility care, including meals and therapy, is included, and obesity screening and therapy are also covered.  The plan covers outpatient mental health care, utilizing licensed psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers, nurses, and other qualified professionals, as well as independent practitioner networks. Prior authorization is crucial for provider-controlled services.
