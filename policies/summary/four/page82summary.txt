Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for patients with diabetes through increased medication adherence support, including doctor-written prescriptions, drug list medications, and specialized pharmacy access.  Updated rules address non-covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures, limiting coverage to drug lists and requiring physician-driven prescriptions.  New provisions now cover chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease.  The MDPP introduces new requirements for hospital stays, skilled nursing facilities, and hospice programs, strengthening patient care.
