**Evidence of Coverage – Chapter 7, 3, & 5** governs Aetna Premier Plus Plus Member Services’ obligations regarding patient data privacy. Chapter 7, 3, and 5 detail the rights of members regarding PHI, including the right to access, correct, and request updates to medical records.  Crucially, Members must obtain written consent before sharing PHI with government agencies (court orders, Medicare) or providing it to others.  The document emphasizes the need for timely access to covered services, including specialized care, and highlights the right to emergency services without prior approval and prescription refills.  Aetna prioritizes protecting member privacy by ensuring secure data management.
