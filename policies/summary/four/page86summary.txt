Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. The plan now covers emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services is available at 1-844-362-0934 or visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for more details.  The Drug List is updated regularly, including brand name drugs, generic drugs, and biosimilars.  To find a drug's inclusion, visit the plan's website, call Member Services, or use the “Real Time Benefit Tool.”  Drugs not on the list are called excluded drugs and require separate coverage, and appeals are possible if a drug is not covered.
