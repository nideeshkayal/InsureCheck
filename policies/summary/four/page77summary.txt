Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries receive comprehensive coverage for physician services, prosthetics, pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors) due to a primary care provider session and SNF care. This plan mandates two quit attempts within a 12-month period, and covers OTC smoking cessation products and SET for PAD. Crucially, the plan includes supervised exercise therapy (SET) training, transportation for services, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs). Urgent care is covered.

Coverage includes vision care, including annual eye exams for diabetic retinopathy and age-related macular degeneration. The plan also covers one pair of glasses/contact lenses after cataract surgery and one glaucoma screening every year. There are restrictions on the number of pairs of glasses/contacts allowed – two pairs after each surgery, and no pair after the second surgery.

The page highlights a new benefit: wigs for hair loss related to chemotherapy. This service is offered for hair loss resulting from chemotherapy.  The plan pays up to $400 annually for covered wigs. Medicare ‑certified hospice care is available for individuals with a terminal prognosis, and the plan supports finding these programs.
