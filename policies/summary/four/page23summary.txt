**Summary**

The Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) policy offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage, with dosage/usage limits impacting prescription drug costs. This policy is dependent on provider participation and is available 24/7 via phone at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) and through AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP. Key features include specialized care support through Care Manager and counseling through SHIP. Coverage extends to behavioral health and mental health services with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME. Call is free.

Important resources are available on the Aetna website and Medicare.gov regarding cost reduction strategies. Social Security determines eligibility, while Social Security handles enrollment for Medicare recipients aged 65+ or those with disabilities. Address changes require notification to Social Security.
