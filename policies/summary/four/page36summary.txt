Medicare and NJ FamilyCare Medicaid provide comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care and behavioral health with transfer approval. Coverage limits apply, and dates and eligibility are key considerations. Out-of-network care is permitted under specific conditions involving a network provider. Outside the service area, urgent medical services and ambulance transportation are covered.

The policy defines eligibility requirements for both programs, mandates medical records from emergency providers, and covers eligible individuals. Clinical research studies are permitted for volunteers, with Medicare covering study-related costs, but reimbursement is typically non-refundable.

Individuals should contact Member Services to understand appeal rights and benefits details.  The page emphasizes the importance of verifying coverage and understanding limitations, particularly regarding medical records, eligibility, and the limitations of coverage for out-of-network services.
