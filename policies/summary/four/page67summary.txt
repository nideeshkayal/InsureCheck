Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly mental health, delegated to the provider. Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) services are covered for individuals with diabetes or kidney disease, including dietary and physical activity guidance.

The plan covers outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services, including x-rays, radiation therapy, and supplies. Nursing facility care, including meals and therapy, is also covered, and obesity screening and therapy are included. Prior authorization is crucial for provider-controlled services.

Outpatient mental health care, including psychiatric consultations, group/individual counseling, and rehabilitation, is covered, utilizing licensed psychiatrists, psychologists, and social workers. Independent practitioner networks are utilized. Substance use disorder treatment services are covered, including alcohol misuse screening, referrals, prescription drugs, and treatment for conditions.  The plan includes extended-release Naltrexone (vivitrol) treatment.
