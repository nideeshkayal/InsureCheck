**Summary:**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals, offering specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. The policy encompasses health care, Managed Long-Term Services and Supports (MLTSS), supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (DME). Transportation, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care are covered.

Key features include limitations on out-of-network care requiring prior authorization for emergency/urgent services, and coverage for kidney dialysis when outside the network's service area for a short period.  The policy covers a range of providers including doctors, nurses, DME, and other healthcare professionals.  Prior to enrollment, you must choose a Care Manager and must inform them of your chosen provider network.
