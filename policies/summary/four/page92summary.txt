Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices. Coverage extends beyond the initial 90 days for long-term care facilities, requiring supply run notifications and provider assessment.  Emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures are subject to quantity limits and provider evaluation. Temporary supply options are available up to 30 days.

The Drug List is the primary source for coverage determination, with quantity limits and provider evaluation required. An exception request is possible for supply runs, but providers must notify the insurance company. Drug updates can impact generic substitution or brand name changes. Coverage extends beyond the initial 90 days for long-term care facilities, requiring a one-time prescription override.

Refer to Chapter 9 of the Evidence of Coverage for detailed exceptions.  The policy emphasizes proactive drug utilization through exception requests, referencing Chapter 5 Getting your outpatient prescription drugs.
