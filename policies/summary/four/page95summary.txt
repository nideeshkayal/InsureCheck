**Summary**

This policy, the Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP, provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices.  Coverage extends beyond 90 days for long-term care facilities, requiring supply run notifications and provider assessments. Emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures are subject to quantity limits and provider evaluation.  Temporary supply options are available up to 30 days.

Key terms include the Drug List, which details which drugs are covered, limits, and provider evaluations.  The policy emphasizes proactive drug utilization through exception requests, referencing Chapter 5 of the Evidence of Coverage.  Eligibility for NJ FamilyCare results in “Extra Help” for Medicare Part D prescription drug costs, impacting premiums, deductibles, and copays.  The provider and pharmacy directory lists network pharmacies crucial for obtaining covered drugs, and provider assessments are mandatory.
