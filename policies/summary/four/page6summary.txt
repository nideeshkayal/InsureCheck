Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive health and drug coverage for individuals aged 19 and over within the Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid contracts. It provides coverage for behavioral health and mental health treatment, encompassing a range of services including prescription drug costs for Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. Enrollment is subject to contract renewal and change. Key benefits include eligibility for Medicare and Medicaid, covering essential services.

The plan’s coverage extends to in-network providers, including independent contractors, and includes DME suppliers. Providers must use in-network service.  Out-of-network providers are not obligated to treat members. Mail order prescriptions are available, and calls are available for delivery within 10 days.

Eligibility for membership involves reviewing contract renewal status. Key terms include the Member ID Card, Provider & Pharmacy Directory, and the Explanation of Benefits.  Privacy of personal health information is crucial.
