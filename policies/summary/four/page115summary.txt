**MLTSS Member Rights & Responsibilities**

As a member of Medicare Part D, you have the right to request information about available services, understand your care needs, and work with your Care Manager to develop a care plan. You are responsible for all costs, including unapproved services, and must provide necessary information to your Care Manager.  Understanding your health conditions, medications, and support systems is crucial, and you must adhere to the plan's guidelines.  It’s vital to provide accurate information to your Care Manager regarding changes to your health, medications, living arrangements, and support systems.  Prioritize seeking clarification on your rights and responsibilities.  The plan emphasizes respecting other members’ rights and expectations.
