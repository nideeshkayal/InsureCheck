**Policy Summary**

This summarizes the Medicare membership termination process. It outlines coverage limits, key terms (like denial deadlines, expedited grievances, and Aetna Assure Premier Plus services), conditions and exclusions, and policy numbers/dates. **Timely submission of complaints within 60 days of denial is crucial.** Liventa investigates quality of care complaints. Termination rules include restrictions on members leaving, rights to a complaint, Medicare and NJ FamilyCare services information, and deadlines based on Special Enrollment Periods (January-March, April-June, July-September, Annual Enrollment Period - October 15-December 7). Changes in enrollment occur due to moving, eligibility changes, or new care locations. Refer to Section C1, C2, and Member Services for complete details.
