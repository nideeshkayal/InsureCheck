This chapter addresses coverage decisions and appeals, particularly concerning medical care.  It outlines four distinct situations: medical care problems, Medicare Part D prescription drug coverage, continued coverage for specific services (home health, skilled nursing, and CORF), and questions regarding coverage decisions.  The key is to understand that coverage decisions require a representation, whether from a lawyer, member services, or SHIP.  Refer to Section F for medical care, Section G for Medicare Part D, and Section H for continuing coverage.  It’s crucial to consult with Member Services for assistance and the Appointment of Representative form to initiate the appeals process and act as your representative.
