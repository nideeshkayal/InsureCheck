Medicare Part B Prescription Drug Coverage – Policy Summary

This page outlines the process for appealing coverage decisions regarding Medicare Part B prescription drug costs. It emphasizes the “fast” appeal option, which allows for expedited review (24 hours) if not already initiated.

The key is that requests for coverage decisions are subject to a level 1 appeal process, requiring contact with Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711).

Requests are initially processed through a standard appeal, or a fast appeal can be initiated if it is not already.  An incomplete request, a request made by someone else, or a request withdrawn by the requester will result in dismissal.

Dismissal is followed by a notice explaining the dismissal and a right to request a review.  A 44-day period allows for review. Late requests may receive additional time but can be dismissed. It's important to note that a fast appeal is a means to expedite a review and can be crucial for resolving coverage issues.
