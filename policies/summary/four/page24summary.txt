Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. The policy offers specialized care support through Care Manager and SHIP counseling. Key features include medication dosage limits, impacting prescription drug costs. Coverage extends to behavioral health and mental health services, with limited dosage/usage. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and DME.

Call is free via 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) and through AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.  Important resources are available on the Aetna website and Medicare.gov regarding cost reduction strategies.

The policy’s coverage includes transportation services, emergency care, urgency care, and disaster care.  It also covers health care services in a clinical research study, including payments when in a clinical research study and coverage for durable medical equipment (DME).
