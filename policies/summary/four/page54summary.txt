**Summary**

This comprehensive insurance plan provides coverage for routine medical services, including annual physicals, bone mass measurement, breast cancer screening, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screening, chiropractic, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.  It includes an Extra Benefits Card offering $305/month transportation, utilities, personal care, and rent/mortgage assistance.

Key coverage includes Fall prevention, family planning services, and the provider’s choice of network or out-of-network option.  Coverage for fertility services (family planning exams, lab tests, methods, supplies, counseling, and STI testing) is contingent on the provider’s network status.  The plan covers medical treatment related to infertility and HIV/AIDS, genetic testing, and some family planning services.

Important details include the annual home and bathroom safety device benefit ($150), continued depression screening and diabetes screenings. Provider network requirements apply to all services.  Coverage does not extend to lost or stolen cards, late utility payments, or rent payments; this benefit continues on the next page.
