Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements significantly expand coverage for individuals with diabetes. Key changes include enhanced physician-driven prescriptions, specialized pharmacy access, and updated hospital stay requirements. The MDPP now covers chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease.

New provisions cover emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures. The plan expands coverage to include drug lists and physician-driven prescriptions, limiting access to these resources.
