Medicare Part D offers coverage for specialized medical services, including emergency treatment, with restrictions on direct billing. Members must follow instructions, request coverage decisions, utilize advance directives, and document healthcare. Network providers cannot bill for these services, and balance billing or unapproved care is prohibited.  The Ombudsperson Program and NJ FamilyCare provide assistance.  Members are responsible for all costs, including unapproved services.  It’s crucial to update membership records and communicate with Member Services regarding questions and coverage.  The plan emphasizes respecting other members’ rights and expectations.
