**Summary**

This document outlines Medicare Part D member responsibilities, emphasizing proactive care management. Members are obligated to cover all health services, including unapproved treatments, and must accurately document all health condition, medication, and support system information for effective care planning.

Key policy considerations include facility-specific resident rights, which require timely notification of non-payment and communication with the facility.  Transfer and discharge decisions necessitate timely notification of non-payment and appropriate communication with the facility.  The document also addresses service changes, disagreements with plan decisions, and concerns about long-term services and supports.

Crucially, the chapter provides guidance on navigating potential problems and complaints, including the Office of the Insurance Ombudsman Program (1-800-446-7467) and the importance of requesting a coverage decision.  It details specific steps for asking for a coverage decision, including appealing a denial, making a Level 1 Appeal, and alternative appeals.  The document also addresses Medicare Part D prescription drug requests, including key limitations and requirements.
