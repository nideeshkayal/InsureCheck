**Coverage Summary for Medicare & NJ FamilyCare Medicaid**

This policy offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care with transfer approval required for urgent situations. Coverage extends to behavioral health and prescription drugs, with limits and dates outlined in the full policy.

Key considerations include MLTSS (Medical, Dental, and Surgical Expenses), PCA (Personal Care Assistant Services), and transportation (MAVs & ambulance for non-emergency).  The policy covers eligible individuals in Medicare and NJ FamilyCare Medicaid.

Important details include needing medical records from emergency providers for claims, a requirement for coverage when receiving emergency care, and rules for needing urgent care.  Coverage for out-of-network care is permitted if a network provider is used or the care is deemed “urgently needed”. Outside the plan’s service area, coverage is available for urgently needed care, emergency medical services, and ambulance transportation.
