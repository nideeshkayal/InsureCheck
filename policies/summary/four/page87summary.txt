Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. The plan now covers emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures.

The Drug List is updated regularly, including brand-name drugs, generic drugs, and biosimilars. To determine drug inclusion, visit the plan’s website, Member Services, or the “Real Time Benefit Tool.” Drugs not on the list are excluded, requiring separate coverage and potential appeals.

Specific rules govern drug usage, emphasizing that safe, lower-cost drugs should be prescribed for medical conditions.  Prior to prescribing, providers must assess a patient’s diagnosis and provide necessary tests.  The plan encourages providers to prioritize medications that effectively address the patient’s condition.
