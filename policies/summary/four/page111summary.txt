**Summary**

This chapter details member rights and responsibilities concerning coverage and benefits within the Medicare Part D plan. Members have the right to access, correct, and update their medical records. Crucially, they must obtain written consent before sharing PHI with government agencies or providing it to others, adhering to HIPAA regulations.

The plan emphasizes timely access to covered services – including specialized care and emergency services – without prior approval. Members retain the right to leave the plan if desired, and are entitled to receive most healthcare through Medicare or other MA plans. Network providers cannot bill directly for covered services; balance billing or unapproved services are prohibited.

Coverage limitations exist, including exclusions for specific services and research trials. The chapter also clarifies decision-making rights, including the right to refuse experimental treatments, second opinions, and stopping prescribed drugs. Members can request coverage decisions, utilize advance directives, and ensure their healthcare decisions are documented.
