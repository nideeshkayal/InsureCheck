Aetna Assure Premier Plus HMO D-SNP provides comprehensive coverage for Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements, chiropractic care, orthopedic shoes, and supportive devices, extending up to 90 days for long-term care facility stays. This policy includes coverage for emergency medications, experimental treatments, quantity limits, provider evaluations, temporary supply options (up to 30 days), and EOB details covering drug usage, costs, and price changes.  The plan also addresses NJ FamilyCare Extra Help, provider and pharmacy directories, and payment sources.

A key condition is Exception Requests referencing Chapter 5 of Evidence of Coverage, which impacts deductibles, copays, and potential out-of-network costs.  Vaccinations are covered, with Medicare Part D vaccines being free.  Aetna Assure Premier Plus Member Services is required for any errors identified.
