**Aetna Premier Plus D-SNP Policy Summary**

This D-SNP policy offers comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, encompassing Medicare and Medicaid eligibility.  Dual eligibility is required for both Medicare and Medicaid, with careful billing review to avoid unapproved costs. Individualized Care Plans (ICPs) outline specialized services, including hospice care for low-income individuals, medication management, and access to specific medications, with eligibility restrictions based on condition and quantity.

Network Pharmacy is utilized for prescription fulfillment, prioritizing drug coverage for Medicare Part D. Quality improvement organizations (QIOs) are addressed, focusing on patient care and treatment recommendations.  A referral system is key, requiring a PCP’s approval to utilize providers other than the PCP. Rehabilitation services are addressed, with Chapter 4 detailing treatment pathways.  Service area limitations apply to certain doctors and hospitals, requiring membership within a defined geographic region. Skilled nursing facility (SNF) care is crucial for recovery, and specialized care offers continuous, skilled rehabilitation.  Urgent care, SSI, and Step Therapy are covered, but not the same as Social Security benefits.
