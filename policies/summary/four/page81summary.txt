Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements significantly expand coverage for patients with diabetes. Key updates include enhanced wigs for chemotherapy hair loss, a supervised exercise therapy (SET) program for terminal prognoses, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs) for veterans. Emergency care is now covered, extending Medicare benefits to include chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for the feet, excluding diabetic foot disease.

The MDPP expands coverage to include medication adherence support, including doctor-written prescriptions, utilizing a network pharmacy for “Drug List” medications, and focusing on medically accepted medications. It also introduces new rules regarding non-covered medical benefits, experimental treatments, surgical procedures for morbid obesity, and items not considered “reasonable and medically necessary.”

The MDPP emphasizes physician-driven prescriptions and drug utilization, limiting coverage to drug lists and establishing limitations on certain medications. The program offers specific coverage for hospital stays, skilled nursing facilities, and hospice programs, supporting patients undergoing specialized care.
