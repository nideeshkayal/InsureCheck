Insurance policy coverage details the process for appeals, including Fair Hearing eligibility and payment obligations. If a fair hearing is requested, submission of a completed application form within 60 days is required. Expedited fair hearing allows for a decision within 72 hours, with payment processing dependent on provider billing.  Service stops require written request within 10 days.  The policy outlines coverage limits and exclusions, specifically focusing on covered services (assisted living, caregiver training, chore services, community transition, and home-delivered meals) and exclusions related to New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance. Medicare Part D drug coverage is provided, with a few exceptions, and requires a medically accepted indication. The document emphasizes the importance of using the evidence of coverage to determine coverage eligibility and the potential for additional appeal rights.
