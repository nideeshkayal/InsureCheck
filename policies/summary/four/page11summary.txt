Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage to eligible individuals aged 19+. The plan provides zero monthly premiums and no premium, covering behavioral health and mental health services with prescription drug costs for both Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. Members must complete the Member ID Card, follow all rules outlined in the Evidence of Coverage document, including appeals procedures. The Provider and Pharmacy Directory is essential for accessing covered services, listing network providers, durable medical equipment suppliers, and participating NJ FamilyCare providers.  It is vital to refer to Chapter 1 of the Evidence of Coverage to understand the requirements for member identification and billing.
