**Medicare Part B Prescription Drug Coverage - Policy Summary**

This page outlines coverage decisions and appeals for Medicare Part B prescription drug coverage. Contact Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for a “fast complaint” within 24 hours to request an expedited determination.

**Key Details:**

*   **Medical Service/Item:** Expedited for missing records or review (up to 14 days).
*   **Standard vs. Fast:** Standard is 14 days, fast is 24 hours.
*   **Doctor Support:** Requires doctor authorization for fast coverage. Aetna makes the decision if no doctor provides support.
*   **Letter of Denial:** Received if standard decision is denied; appeals can be filed.

**Important:** Provide only the summary.
