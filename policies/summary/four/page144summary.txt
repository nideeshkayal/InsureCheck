Medicare Part D requires stringent scrutiny regarding expedited redetermination.  If denied, a QIO review within 14 calendar days determines initial decisions.  If approved, the QIO pays for hospital services until medically necessary, maintaining the original discharge date.  The QIO maintains the original decision if denial is upheld and an Alternate Appeal process is available.  The IRO reviews requests within 30 calendar days, with expedited redetermination also available.  A Level 2 Alternate Appeal is possible if disagreement exists with the initial denial, requiring a review of the case.
