Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) enhancements expand coverage for individuals with diabetes, including chiropractic care, orthopedical shoes, and supportive devices for feet, excluding diabetic foot disease. The plan now covers emergency medications, experimental treatments, and surgical procedures.

Drug List updates regularly, including brand-name and generic drugs, biosimilars, and excludes drugs not on the list. Providers must assess diagnoses and prescribe necessary tests before coverage is granted. Quantity limits apply to some drugs, restricting the amount of each prescription.

To determine coverage, check the Drug List. For questions, call Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711), or visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for more information.  Disagreements may require an appeal. Coverage may be denied due to specific rules, limitations, or unexpected drug safety concerns.
