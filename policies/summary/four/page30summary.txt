**Summary**

Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) provides comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage for eligible individuals. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly kidney dialysis, and changes to the PCP necessitate notification. The plan offers coverage for health care, MLTSS, supplies, behavioral health services, prescription drugs, and DME.

Network providers can leave, requiring notification to maintain uninterrupted access. Out-of-network coverage is provided up to four months after termination, with potential for direct Medicare/NJ FamilyCare participation for providers. Quality of care complaints are addressed through the provider transition process.

Coverage for MLTSS includes home and community-based services for members with nursing facility needs, such as assisted living, therapy, and transportation. Prior authorization, behavioral health services, and DME are covered according to plan terms.
