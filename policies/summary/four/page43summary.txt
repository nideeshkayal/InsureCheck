**Summary**

The Aetna Medicare NJDSNP plan provides comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care and behavioral health. Coverage limits apply, and eligibility is crucial.  Prior authorization is required for non-emergency transportation by fixed-wing aircraft.  Members must call Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) Member Services at 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) for assistance and access to the detailed benefits chart at AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.

This page details coverage for Annual Routine Physical benefits, including appearance, head and neck exams, heart and lung exams, abdominal exams, neurological exams, dermatological exams, and extremities exams.  Coverage extends to non-Medicare covered benefits, with limitations on Outpatient diagnostic tests and therapeutic services.  A critical new feature is Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) services, requiring prior authorization for members with a diagnosis.  Bone mass measurement is also covered for individuals at risk of osteoporosis.
