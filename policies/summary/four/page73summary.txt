**Aetna Premier Plus Plus Coverage** covers Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) beneficiaries and individuals/groups undergoing therapy & nursing. Key benefits include physician/provider services (doctor’s office, limited services), prosthetics (breast prostheses, repairs), pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors, medical supplies). Prior authorization is required for surgery, partial hospitalization, and prostate cancer screening. Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) screening & counseling are covered.  Important: Must have a primary care provider session, SNF care (semi-private, private, meals, PT, OT, speech therapy), and drug administration (blood clotting factors). Access through Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) – 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP for more details.
