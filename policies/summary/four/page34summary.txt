**Summary of Medicare & NJ FamilyCare Medicaid Policy**

This policy offers comprehensive medical, dental, and surgical coverage, including emergency care with transfer approval, extending to behavioral health and prescription drugs, with defined limits and dates.  Coverage includes MLTSS (Medical, Dental, and Surgical Expenses), PCA (Personal Care Assistant Services), and transportation (MAVs & ambulance for non-emergency).  The policy covers eligible individuals in Medicare and NJ FamilyCare Medicaid.

Key considerations include eligibility requirements for both programs and mandatory medical records from emergency providers before claims can be processed.  Coverage for out-of-network care is permitted under specific conditions—a network provider used or deemed “urgently needed.” Outside the service area, coverage is available for urgent medical services and ambulance transportation.  Important details include needing medical records for claims and the benefits chart for detailed coverage information.  

Be sure to obtain a copy of all your medical records from your emergency or urgent care provider before seeking claims.
