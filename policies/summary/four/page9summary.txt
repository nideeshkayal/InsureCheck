Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) is a Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid plan offering comprehensive coverage for eligible individuals aged 19+ within these designated areas. Coverage includes behavioral health and mental health treatment, with prescription drug costs for both Medicare and NJ FamilyCare. Key eligibility requirements include contract renewal status verification, Member ID card completion, Provider & Pharmacy Directory enrollment, and DME supplier inclusion. 

The plan’s service area is New Jersey’s counties, and eligibility is contingent on Medicare Part A and Part B coverage, US citizenship/legal residency, and current eligibility for full NJ FamilyCare benefits.  Call Aetna Member Services for details. 

The plan offers a Health Risk Assessment (HRA) to develop care plans, which include medical, behavioral health, and functional needs assessments. The Care Team works with you to meet your healthcare goals, ensuring care is tailored to your needs.  The Care Plan outlines services required, gaps in care, preferences, health goals, and a timeline for receiving those services.  Monthly costs are zero, and the plan has no premium.
