**Summary**

This Aetna Premier Plus D-SNP policy offers comprehensive coverage for end-of-service management, including patient care, care manager, care plan, and care team roles. Key coverage includes Medicare and Medicaid eligibility, with a dual eligibility structure. Medicare Part D covers outpatient prescription drugs, vaccines, and supplies, subject to exclusions for specific drug types.

The policy mandates careful review of billing practices to avoid unapproved costs. Individualized Care Plans (ICPs) outline services encompassing medical, behavioral health, and long-term support. Eligible patients participate in hospice care and receive directed care. Low-income subsidies (LIS) support long-term care needs.

Network Pharmacy is utilized for prescription fulfillment, with Network providers (doctors, nurses, etc.) essential for covered services. Member Services assists members with benefits and inquiries.

Out-of-network pharmacy coordination is restricted; most drugs from outside the network require approval. Out-of-network providers or facilities are not under contract to provide covered services.

The policy utilizes Network Pharmacy for prescription fulfillment.  Careful consideration is given to Medicare Part D drug coverage.
