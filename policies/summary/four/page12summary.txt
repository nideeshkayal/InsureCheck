Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) offers comprehensive Medicare and New Jersey FamilyCare Medicaid coverage to eligible individuals aged 19+. The plan provides zero monthly premiums and no premium, covering behavioral health and mental health services with prescription drug costs. Network providers include doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals specializing in Medicare and Medicaid services, such as clinics, hospitals, and DME suppliers. Network pharmacies are required to accept full payment for covered prescriptions.

The Drug List details which prescription drugs are covered, including potential limitations on dosage or usage.  The Explanation of Benefits (EOB) tracks payments for Medicare Part D prescription drugs, providing detailed information about usage and cost. Members must complete the Member ID Card and follow the Evidence of Coverage document to ensure proper eligibility and billing.  Refer to Chapter 1 for identification requirements.
