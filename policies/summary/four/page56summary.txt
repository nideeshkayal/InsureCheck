**Summary**

Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive coverage for routine medical services, including annual physicals, bone mass measurements, breast cancer screenings, cardiac rehabilitation, colorectal cancer screenings, chiropractic care, genetic testing, dental services, diagnostic imaging, fluoride treatments, and orthodontic services.  The plan includes an Extra Benefits Card providing $305/month transportation, utilities, personal care, and rent/mortgage assistance.

Crucially, the plan covers up to three HIV screening tests per pregnancy for women at risk, and includes home health services requiring prior authorization.  Coverage exclusions include lost/stolen cards, late utility payments, and rent payments.  The plan also covers hospice care, with eligibility dependent on a terminal prognosis.  Hospice services are covered by Original Medicare, not Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP).  This plan provides ongoing coverage for supplemental services like drug therapy and spiritual counseling, continuing the benefits on the next page.
