**Summary:**

This document outlines the termination process for Medicare membership within the plan, emphasizing critical details.  Members are required to request a disenclosure within 60 days of denial, initiating the process through written request to Member Services (1-800-MEDICARE (TTY: 711)) or by enrolling in a new Medicare health plan. The options include enrolling in a new plan or switching to Original Medicare.  

Specifically, the document details three pathways: a new plan enrollment, a dual-eligible special needs plan (D-SNP) enrollment, or voluntary plan selection. Automatic disenclosure occurs when Original Medicare coverage begins. The SHIP program is available for assistance. The document also provides instructions on how to contact SHIP and NJ FamilyCare for additional support.
