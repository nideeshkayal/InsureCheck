**Medicare Diabetes Prevention Program (MDPP) Beneficiaries & Therapy/Nursing Individuals**

This coverage provides Medicare benefits for individuals participating in the MDPP, specifically for those receiving physician services, prosthetics, pulmonary rehab, and drug administration (blood clotting factors). Key conditions include a primary care provider session, SNF care, and drug administration.

Crucially, the policy requires qualifying sessions through Aetna Assure Premier Plus (HMO D-SNP) – 1-844-362-0934 (TTY: 711) or AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP. Two quit attempts are permitted within a 12-month period. OTC smoking cessation products and SET for PAD are covered.

The plan offers supervised exercise therapy (SET) trained in both basic and advanced life support techniques, transportation for these services, and mobile assistance vehicles (MAVs). Modivcare arranges transportation through the state’s vendor. Urgent care is covered.

For more information, visit AetnaMedicare.com/NJDSNP.
