Aetna Premier Plus Plus offers comprehensive medical coverage, including routine medical services, cardiac rehabilitation, and dental care, for members aged 50-77 with psychiatric hospital stays.  Coverage includes inpatient hospital care, outpatient services (including mental health), and dialysis treatments. Prior authorization is required for specialist services, particularly mental health care, and managed by the provider.

Crucially, the plan covers 28 meals over 14 days after discharge from an inpatient acute hospital, psychiatric hospital, or skilled nursing facility, contingent on provider confirmation.  Medical day care is available for individuals with physical and/or cognitive impairments, requiring preventive, diagnostic, therapeutic, and rehabilitative services under ambulatory care. Prior authorization is mandated by the provider.

Medical nutrition therapy is a key benefit, supporting recovery and health conditions.  Observation and outpatient stays do not qualify for this benefit.
