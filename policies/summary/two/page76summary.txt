**Summary:**

This policy governs First Choice Next’s reimbursement of out-of-network provider claims, governed by strict requirements.  Prior authorization is mandatory for most services, unless specified otherwise.  Failure to file a claim within 180 days results in claim invalidation, with potential for further communication.

The IRO’s decision is binding, with a five-day timeframe for final determination.  Expedited reviews are permitted for retrospective claims, subject to conditions.  Coverage is contingent on proper medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status as defined in the Act.

Specifically, reimbursement is conditional on timely submission of claim forms, proof of loss, and a written statement of the loss.  The policy specifies uniform claim forms, with deadlines for written proof and a one-year retention period for losses.  Payments are made monthly, with a 40-business-day cash payment for initial receipt and a 20-business-day electronic transfer for subsequent claims.
