SHSC Ind SC PY25 – EOC 20240618 v2 – 2025 policy provides broad coverage for outpatient care, surgical procedures, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Coverage is voluntary for members and authorized reps, with a 180-day appeal window. Expedited appeals are available for serious risk, jeopardizing life or health. The policy includes limitations: learning disabilities, behavioral problems, dental services, experimental treatments, transplant, animal tissue, high-dose chemo, donor tissue, jaw problems, and surrogate parenting. The provider can file a verbal request for expedited appeal. Documentation of medical justification is required for expedited appeals.  The policy mandates a clinical peer assignment for requests, with evidence required.  Denial results are communicated within 72 hours of request or 2 business days of receipt of information, with a written notice to the covered member or provider.  The policy includes rights to review medical records and maintain physician specialty expertise.
