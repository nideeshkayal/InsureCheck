South Carolina law mandates independent review of Adverse Benefit determinations made by First Choice Next, under the Public Health Service Act. This review focuses on compliance with the Act’s requirements, including medical necessity, suitability, effectiveness, and experimental/investigational status. Expedited external reviews are permitted for retrospective adverse benefit determinations, subject to specific conditions like urgent medical needs.

The IRO (Independent Review Officer) will assign a decision within five business days after receipt of a request, and the decision is binding.  If the IRO reverses the determination, further communication with the requestor may be required. The IRO will communicate final determination within 45 calendar days for standard review requests.

The DOI’s contact information – Consumer Services Division, P.O. Box 100105, Columbia, SC 29202 – is provided.  Consumers can file a complaint at consumers@doi.sc.gov and utilize the online complaint form: https://doi.sc.gov/consumers.
