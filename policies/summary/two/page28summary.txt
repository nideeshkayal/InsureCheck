SHSC Ind SC PY25 policy offers medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. The policy includes incapacitation, continuation, and dependent support. Coverage expires upon payment or non-compliance, with a grace period of 31 days for federal premium credits.

The renewal premium is paid before the grace period to reinstate coverage.  Renewal is contingent upon acceptance by First Choice Next or its agent. If not, a conditional receipt is issued, and reinstatement occurs on the 45th day after the receipt.

Crucially, the policy covers losses resulting from injury after reinstatement or sickness starting more than 10 days after reinstatement.  Other rights remain unchanged, and premiums applied to a period before reinstatement are capped at 60 days.  A certificate of creditable coverage is provided for the end of the policy, and renewal is possible for up to 24 months.
