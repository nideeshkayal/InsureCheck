SHSC Ind SC PY25 – EOC 20240618 v2 – 2025 Evidence of Coverage for First Choice Next – 67

Coverage includes: Outpatient care, surgical procedures, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening for South Carolina residents.

Key terms: Standby Medical Provider Availability, Sterilization/Vasectomy Reversal, Organized Sports Participation, Service Duration Restrictions, Eligibility & Termination.

Exclusions: Learning disabilities, behavioral problems, accidental dental services related to injury, experimental treatments, transplant services, animal tissue, high-dose chemotherapy, donor tissue procurement beyond intended recipients, jaw joint problems, and surrogate parenting.
