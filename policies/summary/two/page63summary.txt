SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Key exclusions include learning disabilities, behavioral problems, and accidental dental services related to injury.  The policy explicitly prohibits experimental treatments and excludes transplant services, animal tissue, high-dose chemotherapy, and donor tissue procurement beyond intended recipients.  It also excludes jaw joint problems, including crowns, bridges, dental implants, orthodontic braces, periodontal disease treatment, and te mporomandibular joint disorders.  Furthermore, it does not cover imaging services, inhalation therapy, laboratory tests, prescription drugs outside of office settings, or volunteer care.  The document emphasizes that care not prescribed in the approved treatment plan, financial, legal, or estate planning, and home healthcare services are not covered.
