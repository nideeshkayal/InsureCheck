SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. The policy covers health services with defined coverage limits and exclusions, contingent upon eligibility for the plan. Key features include accident/injury, adverse benefit determination (denial, reduction, or termination), allowed amounts, and an appeal process.  The policy mandates annual approval and communicates 31 days in advance of premium increases.

Crucially, coverage is contingent upon eligibility for the plan, with Center of Excellence evaluations for transplant programs focused on strict criteria and annually re-evaluated programs.  The policy includes a specific dollar amount for co-payments, which represents a set amount you may need to pay for covered health services or prescription drugs. It also details cost-sharing, including coinsurance, deductibles, and fixed co-payments, outlining how these payments impact coverage.
