**Summary**

First Choice Next offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, with a $40/month premium and $20 initial, with $0 deductible. Coverage is $0 and does not coordinate benefits with other coverage. HIPAA Privacy Notice is available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx.

**Key Policy Details:**

*   **Coverage:** Standard coverage for qualified health plans.
*   **Payment:** Premiums are deducted, impacting payment.
*   **HIPAA:** Privacy protection is mandated.
*   **Benefit Coordination:** First Choice Next does not coordinate benefits with other plans.
*   **Subrogation:** Subrogation applies when a health insurer has liability for medical assistance.
*   **Exclusions:** Congenital defects and pre-existing conditions are excluded.
*   **Lifetime & Annual Limits:** Coverage includes lifetime and annual dollar limits.
*   **Preventative Services:** Covered preventative services are included.
