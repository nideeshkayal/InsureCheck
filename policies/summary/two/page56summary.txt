**SHSC Ind SC PY25** offers South Carolina medical insurance. Coverage includes outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings (35-39), mammograms/OBGYN (baseline), newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.

**Coverage:** Radiation oncology, facility charges, and supplies.

**Formulary:** Closed with exclusions for medications not covered, 72-hour expedited review.

**Cost-sharing:** No cost-sharing for in-network services.

**Rehab:** Speech, language, occupational, and physical therapy for neuromuscular disorders & cardiac rehab.

**Chiropractic:** With physiologic treatment for neuromusculoskeletal conditions, excluding office visits.

**Foot Care:** Routine foot care is covered.

**Specialist Visits:** Dermatologist/Podiatrist visits for specialty care.

**Transplant:** Covered when ordered by a physician, approved through prior authorization, and when the transplant meets the definition of a covered health service.

**Urgent Care:** Network provider services for medically necessary, approved facility costs and supplies.
