SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. Key coverage includes medical services for serious conditions like cancer, myocardial infarction, and pregnancy, and coverage for prosthetics and orthotics. This policy offers qualified health plans certified by the Health Insurance Marketplace, defining “Qualified Health Plan” as essential benefits, cost-sharing limits, and compliance requirements.

The policy details significant coverage for serious condition/illness including death, decline in physical/mental/psychosocial functioning, loss of limb, and excruciating pain.  It includes a Schedule of Benefits outlining covered services, copays, coinsurance, deductibles, and maximums. Importantly, the Schedule of Benefits replaces the previous one upon effective date, and the policy establishes a new Schedule of Benefits for subsequent coverage.  The policy also covers sexual dysfunction, including female and male disorders related to sexual distress. It specifies SNF facilities, including requirements for care, and the term “skilled nursing facility” excludes home care.
