Select Health of South Carolina, Inc. provides a health insurance policy with a network of participating providers, offering coverage for essential health benefits. Premiums are refunded less claims paid, with renewal required. The policy includes cancellation procedures outlined on page 22, effective January 1, 2025, unless otherwise declared.

The policy’s key conditions and exclusions encompass eligibility for loss due to relocation, failure to pay premiums, dependent child age, fraud, and discontinuation of the health benefit plan.  The policy is a regulated insurance product subject to annual approval by the South Carolina Department of Insurance.

Important notices include annual approval requirements and notifications about potential premium increases at least 31 days in advance.  Alternative formats like Braille and audio are available for those needing assistance.

Questions can be directed to the Member Services team at 1-833-983-7272, 8 a.m. – 6 p.m., 5 days a week.
