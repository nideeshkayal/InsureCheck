SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Virtual care services are available at no cost when received through the First Choice Next network provider, with certain specialty services including pediatrics being excluded. Coverage includes radiation oncology, facility charges, and supplies, subject to cost-sharing and out-of-network limits. Speciality visits, urgent care, and transplant services are covered when ordered by a physician.  The policy defines exclusions for medications not covered and has a 72-hour expedited review process.  

The First Choice Next program introduces a case management team focused on member wellness, offering Disease Management and Wellness programs.  The Healthy Rewards program provides incentives for completing activities, but it does not offer rebates or discounts. The Optum Obstetrical Homecare program is available for qualifying members, providing ongoing education and assessments for pregnancy and high-risk conditions.
