SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Key new information includes prior authorization requirements for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care, impacting the member's out-of-pocket maximum.  Surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) necessitate prior authorization, and behavioral health requires prior authorization including out-of-network services except for emergency care.  Retrospective reviews are conducted in writing by the provider. Cost-sharing includes deductibles, coinsurance, and copays. A copayment is a dollar amount shared for covered services or prescription drugs, with a deductible and cost-sharing based on the discounted rate. The out-of-pocket maximum is the maximum amount paid during the year, excluding premiums.
