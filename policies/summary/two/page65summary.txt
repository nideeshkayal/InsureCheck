SHSC Ind SC PY25 – EOC 20240618 v2 – 2025 Evidence of Coverage for First Choice Next – 65

This policy offers comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.

**Key New Details:**

*   **Standby Availability:** Provides standby medical provider availability when no treatment is provided.
*   **Sterilization/Vasectomy Reversal:**  Covers reversal of sterilization or vasectomies.
*   **Organized Sports Participation:**  Covers payments for participation in organized sports, including injuries sustained while participating.
*   **Service Duration Restrictions:**  Service restrictions apply after termination date.
*   **Eligibility and Termination:**  Refer to the Eligibility and Termination section for further details.

The policy excludes learning disabilities, behavioral problems, accidental dental services related to injury, experimental treatments, transplant services, animal tissue, high-dose chemotherapy, donor tissue procurement beyond intended recipients, jaw joint problems, and surrogate parenting.
