SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. The policy covers health services with defined coverage limits and exclusions. Crucially, eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations for transplant programs focused on strict criteria and annually re-evaluated programs. Coverage is mandated annual approval and communicates 31 days in advance of premium increases.

Key features include accident/injury, adverse benefit determination (denial, reduction, or termination), allowed amounts, and a cost-sharing process, impacting coverage through coinsurance, deductibles, and fixed co-payments.  The policy mandates a specific dollar amount for co-payments, representing a set cost for covered health services or prescription drugs.  Emergency medical care is defined as healthcare services provided in a hospital emergency facility to evaluate and treat an emergency medical condition.
