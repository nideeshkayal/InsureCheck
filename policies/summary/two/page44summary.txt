SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Prior authorization is required for contact lens services, pain management, and personal care, impacting out-of-pocket maximums. Surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) necessitate prior authorization, and behavioral health requires prior authorization, including out-of-network services except for emergency care. Retrospective reviews are conducted by the provider. Cost-sharing includes deductibles, coinsurance, and copays. A copayment is a dollar amount shared for covered services or prescription drugs. The out-of-pocket maximum is the maximum amount paid during the year, excluding premiums. Prior authorization is required for out-of-network air ambulance transports, limiting billing to in-network costs.
