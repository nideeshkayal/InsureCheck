First Choice Next offers comprehensive coverage under the Act for out-of-network providers, starting at $40/month with a $0 deductible. This policy provides standard health plans and includes lifetime and annual dollar limits. Crucially, the coverage is deducted from premium payments, and First Choice Next does not coordinate benefits with other plans.

The policy mandates HIPAA Privacy Notice.  It’s important to note that this coverage is *not* covered by other insurance plans. The insured can change beneficiary at any time via written notice, with consent not required unless the beneficiary designation is irrevocable.  This policy excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions.  First Choice Next does not cover medical assistance related to liability claims.
