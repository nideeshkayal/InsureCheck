SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Prior authorization from First Choice Next is required for medically necessary services, including specialist selection, CPT approval, and continuity/transition management. Coverage includes benefits up to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days.

Critical policy details highlight the need for prior authorization for various services, such as chemotherapy, chiropractic, cochlear implantation, dental anesthesia, DME (unlisted items exceeding $750), electively air ambulance, and surgical procedures including joint replacements, discectomies, and laparoscopic surgeries.  Eligibility criteria include life-threatening pregnancy risks and incest/rape-related pregnancy termination.  Exclusions include emergency services and services not medically necessary.
