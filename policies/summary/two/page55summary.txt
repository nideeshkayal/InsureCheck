SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides South Carolina medical coverage for outpatient care, including surgery and supplies. It offers coverage for Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings (50+), mammograms/OBGYN (baseline for females 35-39, every two years thereafter, and 50+), newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.

Key coverage includes radiation oncology treatment, facility charges, and related supplies. The policy utilizes a closed formulary with exclusions for medications not covered, and expedited review is available for 72 hours. Cost-sharing adjustments are not required for in-network services.

The policy covers medically necessary rehabilitation services, including speech, language, occupational, and physical therapy, crucial for patients with neuromuscular disorders and cardiac rehabilitation, and includes chiropractic manipulative treatment with or without ancillary physiologic treatment for neuromusculoskeletal conditions, excluding care not in an office setting, infusion therapy/chelation therapy, and maintenance/preventative treatments that are not medically necessary. Routine foot care is also covered.
