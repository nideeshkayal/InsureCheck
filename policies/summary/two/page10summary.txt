SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age.  The policy covers health services, focusing on emergency medical care, and mandates annual approval and 31-day notice of premium increases. Key coverage includes accident/injury, adverse benefit determination (denial, reduction, or termination), allowed amounts, and a cost-sharing process impacting coinsurance, deductibles, and fixed co-payments.

Crucially, eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations for transplant programs, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria.  The policy includes emergency medical care as defined by hospital licensing, physician licensure, and oral surgeon/dentist provision, addressing critical medical conditions.  The EOC document details the policy’s scope, rights, and responsibilities of members.  The Exchange/Marketplace is operated by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), and experimental/investigational services are subject to rigorous clinical trial requirements.
