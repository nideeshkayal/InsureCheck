SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Coverage includes radiation oncology, facility charges, and supplies, subject to cost-sharing and out-of-network limits.

Crucially, the policy excludes care extending beyond traditional medical management for conditions like learning disabilities, behavioral problems, or intellectual disabilities, such as educational services or testing. It also prohibits accidental dental services related to injury, ambulance services for ride-to-physician visits, or convenience services like air conditioning.  Furthermore, it doesn't cover experimental or investigational treatments.  The policy mandates exclusion of cannabis use, prescription vitamins not in the formulary, and any services not covered by the network.
