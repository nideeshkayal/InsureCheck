SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap.  Coverage extends to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days, with enhanced prior authorization requirements.  

The policy includes six home health care service visits required before coverage extends to additional services, expanding to include home infusion services, hospice inpatient services, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services covering acute care, rehabilitation, and transfers. Prior authorization is mandatory for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care services.

Crucially, emergency services and services not medically necessary remain excluded. The policy incorporates surgical services like blepharoplasty, breast reconstruction, mastectomy, and mastopexy, recognizing the potential for cosmetic procedures. Radiologic services, including CT scans, may require prior authorization.  

Behavioral health services necessitate prior authorization, including all out-of-network services, except for emergency care, ambulatory detoxification, crisis intervention, ECT, mobile crisis management, intensive outpatient treatment, and partial hospitalization.
