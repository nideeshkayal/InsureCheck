SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical insurance coverage for South Carolina residents. It offers coverage for primary and specialty care office visits with qualified providers, including physician assistants and nurse practitioners. Outpatient facility services, such as surgery and supplies, are covered, including anesthesia for surgical procedures like blepharoplasty and mastectomy, but is limited for out-of-network care. Mental health and substance use services are covered in inpatient or intermediate care settings. Pediatric vision services are offered through the last day of the month of a child’s birthday.

Significant exclusions include air ambulance service and billing limitations apply to in-network costs.  Physician services for sickness and injury are covered, with prior authorization required for surgical procedures and anesthesia. Pregnancy services include prenatal care, delivery, postpartum care, and services for related complications. Prior authorization is needed after the minimum inpatient stay duration expires, with discharge prior to that date.
