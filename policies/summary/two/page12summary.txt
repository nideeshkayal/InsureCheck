SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, encompassing relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. The policy offers emergency medical care, including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgeon/dentist provision, and cost-sharing for coinsurance, deductibles, and fixed co-payments. Crucially, eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria.

The policy includes a facility definition – institutions providing health services or settings including hospitals, inpatient centers, and rehabilitation centers. A Final Internal Adverse Determination (Final Determination) signifies an upheld adverse benefit determination, completing the internal appeal process. First Choice Next Virtual Care is a contracted provider offering 24/7 care. Formulary drugs are listed with lower costs, generic drugs are recognized as having the same active ingredient as brand names, and grievances are addressed regarding insurer decisions related to availability, delivery, or quality of services.

The policy defines Health Care Professional/Provider, Health Care Services, Health Plan/Issuer, and Home Health Aide, all essential components of the coverage offering.
