**SHSC Ind SC PY25 Policy**

**Coverage:** Medical coverage for South Carolina residents. Dependent coverage extends to children incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap – eligibility requires enrollment within 31 days of birth. Coverage includes incapacitation, continuation, and dependent support.

**Key Terms:** Enrollment deadline is 31 days of birth. Policy ends upon payment, notice, or non-compliance. Coverage expires upon month of payment.

**Exclusions:** Specific medical conditions and coverage duration limits. Discontinuations result in written notice, premium non-payment, age limits, or non-compliance leading to termination. Prior to termination, coverage extends for a child in a postsecondary educational institution during a leave of absence.

**Grace Period:** 31-day grace period for federal premium tax credits.  3 consecutive months for premium subsidy. Payments due in advance.  Subscribers receive subsidies when premiums are not paid; subsidies returned to the U.S. Treasury.
