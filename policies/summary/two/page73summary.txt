South Carolina law mandates independent review of Adverse Benefit determinations made by First Choice Next, under the Public Health Service Act. This review focuses on compliance with the Act’s requirements, including medical necessity, suitability, effectiveness, and experimental/investigational status. Expedited external reviews are permitted for retrospective adverse benefit determinations, subject to specific conditions including a serious medical condition requiring expedited appeals or standard external reviews, or emergencies.

A key requirement is that the internal appeal process must be completed before external review can commence. If an ineligible request is made, the requestor is notified within one business day. If accepted, the South Carolina Department of Insurance (SC DOI) will assign an IRO to initiate the review. The IRO will provide all relevant documents and information to the IRO within five business days of receipt.  The IRO’s decision to reverse the determination could trigger further communication with the requestor.  The IRO will communicate final determination within 45 calendar days for standard review requests.
