**Summary:**

This First Choice Next policy provides comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers, starting at $40/month with a $0 deductible.  The policy covers standard health plans and includes lifetime and annual dollar limits. Crucially, the coverage is deducted from premium payments, and First Choice Next does not coordinate benefits with other plans.

Key details include HIPAA Privacy Notice, which is *not* covered by other insurance plans. The insured can change beneficiary at any time via written notice, with consent not required unless the beneficiary designation is irrevocable.  The policy explicitly excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions. Medical assistance related to liability claims is not covered.

The policy mandates IRS tax identification.
