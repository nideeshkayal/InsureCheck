SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Coverage includes six home health visits, including infusion, hospice, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services.

Key new information: Prior authorization is required for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care. Surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) are permitted with prior authorization. Behavioral health requires prior authorization, including out-of-network services except for emergency care, ambulatory detoxification, crisis intervention, ECT, mobile crisis management, intensive outpatient treatment, and partial hospitalization.

Utilization Management reviews provider certifications and effectiveness. Decisions are consistent with South Carolina law and company policies, with potential denial based on timely information release. Provider must notify the member of a benefit determination within 24 hours for concurrent requests and 72 hours for prospective requests.  A provider can extend treatment time, but delays could jeopardize member health.
