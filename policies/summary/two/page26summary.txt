SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, significantly altering the policy structure. Key changes include eligibility for dependents, specifically children incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap.  Eligibility extends beyond age 26, encompassing incapacitation, coverage continuation, and a dependent’s support.

The policy mandates enrollment within 31 days of birth for dependent coverage, and ends for reasons including premium non-payment, termination of coverage, or incorrect dependent age.  Coverage expires upon the month of payment, with notifications preceding termination.  

Crucially, the policy clarifies exclusions related to specific medical conditions and limits on coverage duration.  Discontinuations for reasons such as premium non-payment, age limits, or non-compliance result in written notice.  Furthermore, it extends coverage for a child enrolled in a postsecondary educational institution during a medically necessary leave of absence.  First Choice Next provides a plan for continuation, with a refund if the age of the dependent is incorrectly stated.
