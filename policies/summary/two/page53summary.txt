SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, focusing on primary and specialty care. The policy offers outpatient facility services and, subject to limitations, in-network medication costs.

Key coverage includes outpatient services, including surgery and supplies, utilizing qualified providers, including physicians and nurses. Mental and substance use services are covered in inpatient or intermediate settings, with prior authorization for surgical procedures and anesthesia.

The formulary includes both brand and generic medications, subject to change. An IRO is available for expedited reviews, with decisions within 72 hours.

The policy utilizes a closed formulary, meaning products not listed are non-covered. Non-formulary drugs are treated as non-covered, with a prescription drug request possible. Exigent circumstances trigger expedited reviews; decisions within 24 hours are made.  It is crucial to follow the IRO’s decision. Cost-sharing adjustments for direct support services received from in-network providers are not required if these services are from a network provider for diagnostic or treatment purposes.
