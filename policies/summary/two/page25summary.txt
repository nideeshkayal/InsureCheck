SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, significantly altering the policy structure. The updated policy introduces eligibility criteria for dependents, specifically children incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap.  A child’s eligibility extends beyond age 26 if all conditions are met, including incapacitation, coverage continuation, and a dependent’s support.

A retrospective review of medically necessary services is underway, focusing on the prudent layperson standard. Coverage begins upon enrollment, with a premium payment due within the past plan year prior to renewal. Dependent enrollment is permitted outside the open enrollment period, subject to a special enrollment period of 60 days.

New dependents require enrollment within 31 days of birth. The policy clarifies exclusions related to specific medical conditions and limitations on coverage duration. Changes in eligibility may affect coverage, requiring notification to the insurer within 60 days.  Furthermore, the policy will extend coverage for a child enrolled in a postsecondary educational institution during a medically necessary leave of absence.
