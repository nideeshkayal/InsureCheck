**Summary**

This First Choice Next reimbursement policy establishes stringent requirements for out-of-network provider claims. Mandatory prior authorization is required for most services, with a five-day deadline for invalidation if not met. Expedited reviews are permitted for retrospective claims, subject to defined conditions. Coverage is contingent upon proper medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status, as outlined in the Act.

The policy emphasizes timely claim submission, requiring complete claim forms, proof of loss, and a written statement of loss. Uniform claim forms apply, with deadlines for written proof and a one-year retention period for losses. Payments are monthly, with a $40 initial payment and a $20 subsequent payment. Premium payments are deducted and may be deducted from the payment. The policy allows for indemnities to providers, subject to written instructions from the insured.

The policy’s primary focus is on ensuring comprehensive coverage and providing flexibility to the insured. It’s crucial to understand the requirements for providing accurate and complete information to facilitate timely claims processing and adherence to legal guidelines.
