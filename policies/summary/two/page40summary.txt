SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Key new information includes prior authorization requirements for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care; surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) require prior authorization; and behavioral health necessitates prior authorization including out-of-network services except for emergency care.

The policy mandates notification of benefit determinations within 24 hours for concurrent requests and 72 hours for prospective requests. Providers must notify the member of a benefit determination, and delays can jeopardize member health.  Decertification does not occur until notice is given to the covered person.  Retrospective reviews are conducted in writing by the provider.  Cost-sharing includes deductibles, coinsurance, and copays. A copayment is a dollar amount shared for covered services or prescription drugs, with a deductible and cost-sharing based on the discounted rate.  The out-of-pocket maximum is the maximum amount paid out-of-pocket during the year, excluding premiums.
