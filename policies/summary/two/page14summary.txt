SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. The policy offers emergency medical care, covering hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing. Crucially, eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria.

The policy defines Health Care Professional/Provider, Health Care Services, Health Plan/Issuer, and Home Health Aide. It mandates a Facility Definition – institutions providing health services. A Final Internal Adverse Determination (Final Determination) signifies an upheld adverse benefit determination, completing the internal appeal process. First Choice Next Virtual Care is a contracted provider offering 24/7 care. Formulary drugs are listed with lower costs, generic drugs are recognized as equivalent, and grievances are addressed regarding insurer decisions related to availability, delivery, or quality of services.
