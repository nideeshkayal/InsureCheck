SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Coverage extends to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days, with significant changes including enhanced prior authorization requirements.

Specifically, six home health care service visits are now required before coverage extends to additional home health services. Coverage expands to include home infusion services, hospice inpatient services, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services covering acute care, rehabilitation, and transfers.  Prior authorization is mandatory for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care services.

Crucially, emergency services and services not medically necessary remain excluded.  The policy also incorporates surgical services like blepharoplasty, breast reconstruction, mastectomy, and mastopexy, recognizing the potential for cosmetic procedures.  Radiology services, including CT scans, may require prior authorization.
