SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical insurance coverage for South Carolina residents, with prior authorization required for certain services like contact lens, pain management, and personal care.  Coverage includes anesthesia for surgical procedures like blepharoplasty and mastectomy, but is limited for out-of-network care.  Deductibles, coinsurance, and copays apply to most medical and surgical benefits.  Significant exclusions include air ambulance service, and billing limitations apply to in-network costs.  Mental health and substance use services are covered in inpatient or intermediate care settings, including detoxification and medication management.  Mental health services are excluded from the plan when related to court-ordered services or marital counseling.
