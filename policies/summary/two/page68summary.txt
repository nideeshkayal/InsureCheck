**Policy Summary:**

This SHSC Ind SC PY25 – EOC 20240618 v2 – 2025 policy covers South Carolina residents for outpatient care, surgical procedures, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.

**Key Details:**

*   **Coverage:** Broad range of services including outpatient care, surgical procedures, and screening tests.
*   **Eligibility:** South Carolina residents are eligible.
*   **Appeal:** Voluntary for members, authorized reps can initiate.
*   **Appeal Process:** Verbal appeals count, research starts with receipt.
*   **Member Services:** Assistance with appeals provided.
*   **Record Access:** Medical records free of charge for appeals.
*   **Restrictions:** Limited to specific timeframes & conditions.
*   **Exclusions:** Learning disabilities, behavioral problems, dental services, experimental treatments, transplant, animal tissue, high-dose chemo, donor tissue, jaw problems, and surrogate parenting.
