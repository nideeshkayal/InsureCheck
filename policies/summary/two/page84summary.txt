**Summary**

First Choice Next offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, focusing on mandatory prior authorization and expedited review. Coverage is $40/month and $20 initial, with $0 deductible. Premiums are deducted, impacting payment.

Crucially, health information is protected by HIPAA, with a Privacy Notice available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx. The policy does not coordinate benefits with other coverage.

Subrogation applies when a health insurer has liability for medical assistance.  A health insurer can subrogate to the extent benefits are provided under this policy, seeking recovery from the state's medical assistance program.

First Choice Next does not exclude coverage for congenital defects or anomalies, and it does not exclude coverage based on pre-existing conditions.  Lifetime and annual dollar limits apply, and preventative services are covered.
