SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical insurance coverage for South Carolina residents, offering coverage for primary and specialty care appointments with qualified providers, including physician assistants and nurse practitioners. Coverage includes outpatient facility services like surgery and supplies, such as anesthesia for surgical procedures. 

Key exclusions include air ambulance service, and billing limitations apply to in-network costs. Mental health and substance use services are covered in inpatient or intermediate care settings, with prior authorization required for surgical procedures and anesthesia. Pediatric vision services are offered through the last day of the month of a child’s birthday.

The formulary covers both brand (preferred) and generic medications, with a potential change occurring periodically. A searchable formulary is available at https://client.formularynavigator.com/Search.aspx?siteCode=7356865853.  The policy ensures coverage for off-label uses of cancer drugs, adhering to state law and utilizing recognized reference compendia for diagnosis and treatment.
