**First Choice Next Reimbursement Policy – Summary**

This policy offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, emphasizing mandatory prior authorization and expedited review. Coverage is contingent upon medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status, consistent with federal and state law.

Key details include monthly payments, a $40 initial payment, and a $20 subsequent payment. Premiums are deducted, potentially impacting the payment. The policy provides indemnities to providers, subject to written instructions.

Crucially, health information is personal and protected, adhering to HIPAA regulations. A comprehensive Privacy Notice is available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx.  Network providers are independent and not responsible for healthcare services.
