SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive insurance coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, dependent child age, and fraud. The policy covers health services, with defined coverage limits and exclusions. Crucially, coverage is contingent on eligibility for the plan.

Key features include accident or accidental injury, adverse benefit determination (denial, reduction, or termination), allowed amounts, and appeal processes.  The policy emphasizes annual approval requirements and potential premium increases communicated at least 31 days in advance.

The policy defines authorized representatives – individuals with explicit consent to represent the covered person in appeals or external reviews. It also clarifies Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) and behavioral health diagnoses.  The document emphasizes the policy’s requirement that coverage is contingent upon eligibility for the plan.
