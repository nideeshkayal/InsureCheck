**First Choice Next** offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, starting at $40/month with $20 initial, with a $0 deductible.  The policy covers standard health plans and includes lifetime and annual dollar limits.

Crucially, this coverage is deducted from premium payments, and First Choice Next does not coordinate benefits with other plans.  The policy mandates HIPAA Privacy Notice.

Subrogation applies when a health insurer has liability for medical assistance.  Congenital defects and pre-existing conditions are exclusions.

The insured can change beneficiary at any time via written notice, with consent not required unless the beneficiary designation is irrevocable.
