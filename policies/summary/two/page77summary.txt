**Summary:**

First Choice Next’s reimbursement policy governs out-of-network provider claims, governed by strict requirements.  This policy mandates mandatory prior authorization for most services, with a five-day deadline for invalidation if not met.  Expedited reviews are permitted for retrospective claims, subject to defined conditions.  Coverage is contingent on proper medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status as outlined in the Act.

The policy defines reimbursement conditional on timely claim submission – including claim forms, proof of loss, and a written statement of loss. Uniform claim forms apply, with deadlines for written proof and a one-year retention period for losses. Payments are monthly, with a $40 initial payment and a $20 subsequent payment. Premium payments are deducted from claims, and premium unpaid may be deducted from the payment.  The policy allows for indemnities to providers, subject to direct written instructions from the insured.  First Choice Next cannot require a particular provider’s services, offering flexibility to the insured.
