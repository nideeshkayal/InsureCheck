SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. It offers medical services including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing, subject to Center of Excellence evaluations and annual reassessment.  Prior authorization is required for certain services and drugs, often involving formulary restrictions. Prosthetics and orthotics are covered items, including braces, artificial limbs, and ostomy supplies.

The policy defines “Qualified Health Plan” as certified by the Health Insurance Marketplace, ensuring essential health benefits, cost-sharing limits, and compliance with application requirements.  Quantity limits are in place to manage drug utilization and may impact prescription costs.  Rescission of coverage occurs when a policy is canceled, retroactively affecting the individual's enrollment.
