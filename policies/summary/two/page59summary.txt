SHSC Ind SC PY25 offers comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Coverage includes radiation oncology, facility charges, and supplies, subject to cost-sharing and out-of-network limits. Speciality visits, urgent care, and transplant services are covered when ordered by a physician.

The First Choice Next program introduces a case management team focused on member wellness, offering Disease Management and Wellness programs, and the Healthy Rewards program providing incentives through rewards, but not rebates.  A tobacco cessation program is available to qualifying members at no cost, utilizing personalized information, support, and tools. Weight Watchers vouchers are available for members aged 15-64 for up to 28 weeks at no cost.
