SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Coverage includes six home health care service visits, including home infusion services, hospice inpatient services, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services covering acute care, rehabilitation, and transfers.

The policy mandates prior authorization for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care services, excluding emergency services and services not medically necessary. Surgical procedures like blepharoplasty, breast reconstruction, mastectomy, and mastopexy are permitted, subject to prior authorization. Behavioral health services require prior authorization, including all out-of-network services except for emergency care, ambulatory detoxification, crisis intervention, ECT, mobile crisis management, intensive outpatient treatment, and partial hospitalization.

Utilization Management oversees the program’s efficacy through prospective, concurrent, and retrospective reviews of clinical evidence, ensuring adherence to prudent layperson legal standards.  The department provides access to review staff via a toll-free number and regularly assesses program effectiveness, coordination with medical management, and provider satisfaction, addressing risk management and credentialing.
