SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Crucially, the policy explicitly excludes care extending beyond traditional medical management for conditions like learning disabilities, behavioral problems, or intellectual disabilities, and prohibits accidental dental services related to injury. It also doesn’t cover experimental or investigational treatments.

The policy also addresses specific exclusions, including cosmetic improvements like hair follicle implants, dental appliances not for sleep apnea, and lenses for keratoconus. It distinguishes between covered and uncovered services: prosthetic and orthotic device benefits are restricted to cosmetic improvements and dental appliances unless specifically covered, and durable medical equipment exclusions include accessories, replacement equipment due to desire for new equipment, or abuse. Rehabilitation services, including activity or recreational therapy, are not covered.
