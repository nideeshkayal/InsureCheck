**Summary**

This policy, SHSC Ind SC PY25, provides coverage for essential health benefits to South Carolina residents. It’s a regulated insurance product subject to annual approval by the South Carolina Department of Insurance.

Key features include eligibility for loss due to relocation, failure to pay premiums, dependent child age, and fraud. The policy covers specific health services, but excludes discontinuation of the plan.  Important notice regarding annual approval requirements and potential premium increases is communicated at least 31 days in advance.

The policy defines coverage limits, exclusions, and appeals processes.  Member rights and responsibilities are outlined, including contact information for the Member Services team.
