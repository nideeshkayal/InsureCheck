**Independent External Review Procedure**

South Carolina law mandates an independent external review of Adverse Benefit determinations made by First Choice Next. This review is conducted by an IRO, a third-party organization, and is expedited for serious risk.

**Right to Request External Review:** Covered members and providers can request an external review at any point – receiving a denial, an appeal, or notification of a final determination.

**Authorization Requirement:** To initiate the review, you must provide written authorization for the release of medical records.

**Exhaustion of Internal Appeals:** An external review request is not permitted until the covered person has exhausted internal appeal processes. Exiting the internal review means completing the appeal and receiving a written determination, or agreeing to a delay.

**Eligibility for Review:** Coverage must be in effect when the Adverse Benefit Determination is issued.
