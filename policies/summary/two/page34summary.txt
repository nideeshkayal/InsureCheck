SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Prior authorization is required for medically necessary services, including specialist consultations, CPT approval, and continuity/transition management. Key coverage includes benefits up to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days.

Significant changes include enhanced requirements for prior authorization.  Specifically, six home health care service visits are required before coverage extends to additional home health services.  Additionally, coverage expands to include home infusion services, hospice inpatient services, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services covering acute care, rehabilitation, and transfers between acute care facilities.  Coverage for medically necessary contact lenses, pain management (including epidural steroid injections and nerve blocks), and personal care services are also included.  Crucially, exclusions remain emergency services and services not medically necessary.
