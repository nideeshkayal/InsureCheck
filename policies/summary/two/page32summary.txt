SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Renewal requires prior authorization from First Choice Next or its agent. Coverage includes benefits up to 90 days after birth, capped at 60 days.

Critical policy details include:

*   **Specialist Selection:** Requires expertise and responsibility for management.
*   **CPT Approval:** Required prior authorization before coverage begins.
*   **Continuity & Transition:** First Choice Next manages continuity and care transitions.
*   **Dependent Coverage:** Children under 18 receive continuous coverage.
*   **Out-of-Network:** Providers exiting the network are out-of-network.
*   **Premium Limits:** Coverage is capped at 60 days; out-of-network providers are not required to pay.

Prior authorization is needed for medically necessary services and may be taken back after emergency services.  Coverage is dependent on medical necessity, cost limits, eligibility, and deductible amounts.
