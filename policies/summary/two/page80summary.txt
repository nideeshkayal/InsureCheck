This First Choice Next reimbursement policy offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers. Mandatory prior authorization is required for most services, with a five-day deadline for invalidation if not met. Expedited reviews are permitted for retrospective claims, subject to defined conditions. Coverage is contingent upon proper medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status, as outlined in the Act.  The policy emphasizes timely claim submission, requiring complete forms, proof of loss, and a written statement of loss. Uniform claim forms apply, with deadlines for written proof and a one-year retention period for losses. Payments are monthly, with a $40 initial payment and a $20 subsequent payment. Premiums are deducted and may be deducted from the payment. The policy allows for indemnities to providers, subject to written instructions.
