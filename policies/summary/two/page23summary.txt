SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, with a significant shift in coverage structure. The updated policy incorporates Second Opinion reviews for resource allocation and reimbursement monitoring. A new retrospective review of medically necessary services, focusing on the prudent layperson standard, is being conducted.

Key changes include the introduction of eligibility criteria for dependents – specifically, the child must be incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap, and the child must remain eligible for dependent benefits beyond age 26 if all of the following conditions are met: the child is incapable of self-sustaining employment, the child remains eligible for coverage after age 26, and the child depends on you for most or all of their support.

The policy now extends coverage for a child enrolled in a postsecondary educational institution during a medically necessary leave of absence. Coverage begins upon enrollment and the first month's premium payment, with no coverage received prior to the effective date. Payments must be up to date for the past plan year prior to renewal.
