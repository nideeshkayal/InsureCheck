SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. The policy covers medical costs, including incapacitation, continuation, and dependent support. Renewal is contingent upon acceptance by First Choice Next or its agent.

Crucially, the renewal premium is paid before the grace period to reinstate coverage. Renewal is conditional on the authorization of the provider. If not, a conditional receipt is issued, and reinstatement occurs after 45 days.

The policy covers losses resulting from injury after reinstatement or sickness starting more than 10 days after reinstatement.  Other rights remain unchanged, with premiums capped at 60 days. A certificate of creditable coverage is provided, and renewal is possible for up to 24 months. Network providers are not employees of the insurer.
