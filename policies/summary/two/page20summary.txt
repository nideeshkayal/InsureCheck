SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. Key coverage includes medical services for serious conditions like cancer, myocardial infarction, and pregnancy, and prosthetics & orthotics. The policy defines “Qualified Health Plan” as essential benefits, cost-sharing limits, and compliance requirements.

Notably, the schedule of benefits now replaces the previous one, establishing a new schedule for subsequent coverage. It offers SNF care (skilled nursing facility) continuously and daily, with specialized care provided by registered nurses and doctors. Step therapy is a tool for managing medical conditions, and the policy includes urgent care for non-emergency situations. Utilization review monitors resource allocation for patient care, ensuring efficient service delivery. Subscribers are defined as those properly enrolled, excluding dependents, and providing coverage for significant conditions.
