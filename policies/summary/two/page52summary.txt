SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, focusing on primary and specialty medical care. The policy offers coverage for outpatient services, including surgery and supplies, utilizing qualified providers, including physicians and nurses.

Key coverage includes outpatient facility services and, subject to limitations, in-network medication costs. Mental and substance use services are covered in inpatient or intermediate settings, with prior authorization for surgical procedures and anesthesia.

The formulary includes both brand and generic medications, subject to change. A searchable formulary is available at https://client.formularynavigator.com/Search.aspx?siteCode=7356865853.

The policy utilizes a closed formulary, meaning products not listed are non-covered. Non-formulary drugs are treated as non-covered, and a prescription drug request may be required. Exigent circumstances, such as a health crisis, trigger expedited reviews, with decisions within 72 hours.
