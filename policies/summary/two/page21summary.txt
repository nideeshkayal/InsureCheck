SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. The new schedule of benefits replaces the previous one, offering SNF care (skilled nursing facility) continuously and daily, with registered nurses and doctors. Utilization review monitors resource allocation for patient care, ensuring efficient service delivery.  A key new aspect is the introduction of Second Opinion reviews – clinical evaluations by a provider other than the initial recommendation. The policy includes retrospective review of medically necessary services, focusing on the prudent layperson standard.  This review includes evaluation of reimbursement levels, veracity of documentation, and accuracy of coding, ensuring compliance with South Carolina law.  Utilization Review Organizations (UROs) conduct utilization review under a managed care plan, distinguishing them from insurers performing their own review.  Virtual care services, including behavioral health and non-emergency medical issues via audio/video, are also covered.
