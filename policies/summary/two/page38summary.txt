SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. It covers six home health visits, including infusion, hospice, hyperbaric oxygen, hysterectomy consent, and inpatient hospital services.

Prior authorization is required for contact lenses, pain management, and personal care. Surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) are permitted with prior authorization. Behavioral health requires prior authorization, including out-of-network services except for emergency care, ambulatory detoxification, crisis intervention, ECT, mobile crisis management, intensive outpatient treatment, and partial hospitalization.

Utilization Management oversees program efficacy through reviews. They provide review staff via phone and assess effectiveness, coordination with medical management, and provider satisfaction. Utilization decisions are consistent with South Carolina law and company policies.
