**SHSC Ind SC PY25** provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Renewal is contingent upon acceptance by First Choice Next or its agent.

The renewal premium is paid before grace period to reinstate coverage. Renewal is conditional on provider authorization; failure to authorize results in a conditional receipt and 45-day delay for reinstatement.

Coverage includes medical costs, including incapacitation, continuation, and dependent support. Losses resulting from injury after reinstatement or sickness starting more than 10 days after reinstatement are covered.  Other rights remain unchanged with premiums capped at 60 days. A certificate of creditable coverage is provided, allowing for up to 24 months of coverage. Network providers are not employees of the insurer.

First Choice Next complies with the No Surprises Act and any relevant CMS regulations.  Member expenses may exceed coinsurance due to potential provider charges.  In case of provider departure, First Choice Next continues coverage at the in-network cost share for up to 90 days.
