South Carolina law mandates independent review of Adverse Benefit determinations made by First Choice Next. This review focuses on compliance with the Act’s requirements, including medical necessity, suitability, effectiveness, and experimental/investigational status. Expedited external reviews are permitted for retrospective adverse benefit determinations, subject to specific conditions.

The IRO will assign a decision within five business days, and the decision is binding. If the IRO reverses the determination, further communication with the requestor may be required. The IRO will communicate final determination within 45 calendar days for standard review requests.

Out-of-network provider claims are covered if prior authorization is obtained before the service being given, unless otherwise specified. Failure to file a claim within 180 days of service results in claim invalidation, provided the claim is submitted soon after. Written notice of claim must be provided within 20 days of a covered loss starting or as soon as reasonably possible. A disability for which benefits may be payable must be notified to First Choice Next within six months of filing proof, unless legally incapacitated.
