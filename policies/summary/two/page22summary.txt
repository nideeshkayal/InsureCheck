SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive medical coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. It replaces the previous schedule of benefits, offering continuous and daily SNF care (skilled nursing facility) with registered nurses and doctors.

Key changes include the introduction of Second Opinion reviews – clinical evaluations by a provider other than the initial recommendation – for resource allocation and reimbursement monitoring. The policy now incorporates retrospective review of medically necessary services, focusing on the prudent layperson standard. Utilization Review Organizations (UROs) conduct utilization review under a managed care plan, distinguishing them from insurers performing their own review. Virtual care services, including behavioral health and non-emergency medical issues via audio/video, are covered.

The policy defines eligibility for dependents – spouses/domestic partners, children, stepchildren, foster children, and children awarded coverage through administrative or court orders – with specific exclusions regarding birth in wedlock and non-claim status.  A probationary period will be met to the extent coverage was in force under the prior policy.
