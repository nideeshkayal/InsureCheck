SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age.  The policy encompasses medical services, covering hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing.  Eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria.

The policy defines Health Care Professional/Provider, Health Care Services, Health Plan/Issuer, and Home Health Aide. A Facility Definition – institutions providing health services – is mandated, with a Final Internal Adverse Determination (Final Determination) signifying an upheld adverse benefit determination.  Member eligibility is determined by confirmation of enrollment and premium payment, including the subscriber and dependents. Network or in-network coverage is achieved through agreements with healthcare providers, including hospitals, networks, and pharmacies. Network providers must accept full payment for services, while out-of-network providers are excluded.
