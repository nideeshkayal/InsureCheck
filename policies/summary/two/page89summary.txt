**Summary:**

This First Choice Next policy offers comprehensive out-of-network coverage starting at $40/month with a $0 deductible. It covers standard health plans and includes lifetime and annual dollar limits. Crucially, coverage is deducted from premium payments, and the policy does not coordinate benefits with other plans. The policy mandates IRS tax identification. It explicitly excludes congenital defects and pre-existing conditions, and medical assistance for liability claims is not covered. This policy focuses on providing robust coverage, while excluding certain conditions.
