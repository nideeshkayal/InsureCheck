SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides South Carolina medical coverage. It offers outpatient care, including surgery and supplies, using qualified providers. Key coverage includes Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings (50+ risk), mammograms/OBGYN (baseline for females 35-39, every two years thereafter, and 50+), newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.

Coverage is subject to limitations, including formulary exclusions. The policy uses a closed formulary, meaning some medications aren’t covered. Expedited review is available for 72 hours. Cost-sharing adjustments are not required for in-network services.

It emphasizes current guidelines from the American Cancer Society and HRSA for preventive care & screenings. Refer to the Health Insurance Marketplace for complete preventive service requirements: https://www.healthcare.gov/coverage/preventive-care-benefits.

**Important:** This policy covers medical needs and must adhere to current guidelines.
