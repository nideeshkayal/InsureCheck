SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening. Key exclusions include care extending beyond traditional medical management for learning disabilities, behavioral problems, or intellectual disabilities, and prohibits accidental dental services related to injury. The policy also excludes experimental or investigational treatments.

Specifically, transplant services are not covered, including animal or artificial tissue s or organs transplant services, experimental or investigational transplants, high-dose chemotherapy, and the procurement of donor tissues/bone marrow cells for recipients not covered, or organ sales rather than donations.  The document also excludes jaw joint problems, including crowns, bridges, dental implants, extractions, orthodontic braces, occlusal adjustments, periodontal disease treatment, and treatment of te mporomandibular joint disorders. Hearing aids, incontinence supplies, maternity benefits for dependent children, laboratory tests not ordered by a physician, and refractive laser eye surgery (LASIK) are also not covered.
