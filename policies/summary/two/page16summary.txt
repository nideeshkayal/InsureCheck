SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age.  It encompasses medical services – including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing – and eligibility is contingent upon Center of Excellence evaluations, annually re-evaluated, and centered on strict criteria.  Out-of-pocket costs are defined as the member’s cost-sharing requirement, including deductibles and co-insurance payments. The policy defines Health Care Professional/Provider, Health Care Services, Health Plan/Issuer, and Home Health Aide.  The Pap test assesses cervical cancer detection, and partial hospitalization services provide ongoing care.  Physicians bill for services, and the policy includes a provider agreement with insurance contracts to ensure payment. The plan year covers calendar year and initial effective date less than 12 months.
