**First Choice Next – Coverage Summary**

This policy offers comprehensive coverage for out-of-network providers under the Act, emphasizing mandatory prior authorization and expedited review. Coverage is contingent on medical necessity, suitability, and experimental/investigational status, aligning with federal and state law.

Key details include monthly payments of $40 and $20, a $20 initial payment, and a $0 deductible. Premiums are deducted, potentially impacting the payment.  The policy provides indemnities to providers, subject to written instructions.

Crucially, health information is protected by HIPAA, with a Privacy Notice available at https://www.firstchoicenext.com/privacy-notice.aspx. Network providers are independent and not responsible for healthcare services.

**Important Note:** This policy includes provisions regarding potential legal action for recovery within 60 days of loss proof, and a 6-year limit after loss proof.  Other insurance coverage is evaluated based on the amount of other valid coverage, adhering to state statutes.
