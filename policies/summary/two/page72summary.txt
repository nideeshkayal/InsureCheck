South Carolina law mandates an independent review of Adverse Benefit determinations made by First Choice Next. This review focuses on compliance with the Public Health Service Act, specifically regarding medical necessity, suitability, effectiveness, or experimental/investigational status. The review team assesses whether the service is appropriately covered under the policy.

Expedited external reviews are now permitted for retrospective adverse benefit determinations, subject to specific conditions: a serious medical condition requiring expedited internal appeals or standard external reviews, or a request concerning emergency care or continued stay. There is no time limit for expedited external review requests. A review is completed within five business days of receipt, and eligibility is determined based on meeting the criteria. If not eligible, the requestor is notified within one business day.
