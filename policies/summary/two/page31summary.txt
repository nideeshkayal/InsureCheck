SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides medical coverage for South Carolina residents, extending to dependent coverage for individuals incapable of self-sustaining employment due to intellectual disability or physical handicap. Renewal is contingent upon acceptance by First Choice Next or its agent.

Key policy details include:

*   **Specialist Selection:**  A specialist must have expertise in treating the covered person's disease or condition and be responsible for management.
*   **CPT Approval:**  Prior authorization and medical review are required before coverage can begin.
*   **Continuity & Transition:** First Choice Next will determine continuity and manage care transitions.
*   **Dependent Coverage:**  Children under 18 receive continuous coverage.
*   **Out-of-Network:**  If an in-network provider stops participating, they become out-of-network.
*   **Premium Limits:** Coverage is capped at 60 days; out-of-network providers are not required to pay a premium.

The policy’s continued coverage includes benefits for serious medical conditions up to 90 days after birth.
