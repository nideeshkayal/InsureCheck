Select Health of South Carolina, Inc. HMO policy establishes a Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) contract for Individual Member Health Maintenance Organization (HMO) insurance. Coverage includes essential health benefits, adhering to the documented provisions within the policy.

Key details include cancellation procedures outlined on page 22, the effective date being January 1, 2025, unless otherwise declared. The policy’s essential features are:

*   **Network:** Select Health of South Carolina, Inc. operates as First Choice Next, a network of participating providers.
*   **Coverage:** Benefits are covered without cost-sharing, ensuring access to essential healthcare services.
*   **Premiums:** Premiums will be refunded less claims paid, with the policy nullified upon renewal.

The policy uses a network of providers for services; out-of-network services are limited and subject to change. Renewal is guaranteed renewable if premiums are paid, and cancellation is possible if either party chooses to terminate it.
