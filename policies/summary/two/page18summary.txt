SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including relocation, failure to pay premiums, and dependent child age. This policy offers medical services including hospital licensing, physician licensure, oral surgery, and cost-sharing, subject to Center of Excellence evaluations and annual reassessment.  

Key coverage includes qualified health plans, which are certified by the Health Insurance Marketplace. The policy defines “Qualified Health Plan” as essential health benefits, cost-sharing limits, and compliance requirements.  Quantity limits exist for drug utilization and may impact prescription costs.

The policy also covers prosthetics and orthotics, including braces, artificial limbs, and ostomy supplies.  Rescission occurs upon cancellation, retroactively affecting enrollment.

The Schedule of Benefits details covered health services, copays, coinsurance, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums. A new Schedule of Benefits replaces the previous one upon effective date.  The page details serious condition/illness coverage including death, significant decline in physical/mental/psychosocial functioning, loss of limb, and excruciating pain.
