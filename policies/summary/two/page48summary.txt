**SHSC Ind SC PY25 Medical Insurance**

**Coverage:** South Carolina residents. Dependent coverage for individuals with intellectual disability or physical handicap. Prior authorization needed for: contact lens, pain management, personal care. Surgical procedures (blepharoplasty, mastectomy, mastopexy) require prior authorization. Behavioral health & out-of-network services are limited.

**Key Terms:** Deductibles, coinsurance, copays, out-of-pocket maximum (excluding premiums), inpatient hospital services, physician & surgical services, anesthesia, Lymphedema services, breast reconstruction benefits (stages).

**Conditions/Exclusions:** Prior authorization for air ambulance, billing limited to in-network costs.

**Policy Details:**
*   **Coverage:** Illness & injury care, including anesthesia.
*   **Prior Authorization:** Required for out-of-network.
*   **Cost Sharing:** Deductibles, coinsurance, copays.
*   **Out-of-Pocket Maximum:** $3,000 annually, excluding premiums.
