SHSC Ind SC PY25 provides comprehensive coverage for South Carolina residents, including outpatient care, surgery, Pap tests, colorectal cancer screenings, mammograms/OBGYN, newborn hearing screening, nutritional counseling, and ovarian cancer screening.  The policy excludes learning disabilities, behavioral problems, accidental dental services related to injury, experimental treatments, transplant services, animal tissue, high-dose chemotherapy, donor tissue procurement beyond intended recipients, jaw joint problems, and non-traditional alternative medicine.  Specifically, abortion, court-ordered parole services, marital and relationship counseling, aptitude/intelligence testing, and cosmetic procedures are not covered.  Coverage for fetal reduction surgery, immunization services for foreign travel, and television/phone expenses is not included.  The policy emphasizes that care outside the U.S., except under emergency services, is not covered and restricts services related to surrogate parenting.
