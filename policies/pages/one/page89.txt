Section 3.2        If we tell you that we will not pay for all or part of the medical care or drug, you can make an appeal
If you think we have made a mistake in turning down your request for payment or you don’t agree with the amount we are paying, 
you can make an appeal. If you make an appeal, it means you are asking us to change the decision we made when we turned 
down your request for payment. The appeals process is a formal process with detailed procedures and important deadlines.  For 
the details on how to make this appeal, go to Chapter 9 of this document.
