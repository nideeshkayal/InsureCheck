How to contact us when you are making a complaint about your medical care or Part D prescription drugs
You can make a complaint about us or one of our network providers or pharmacies, including a complaint about the quality of your 
care. This type of complaint does not involve coverage or payment disputes. For more information on making a complaint about 
your medical care, see Chapter 9 ( What to do if you have a problem or complaint (coverage decisions, appeals, complaints) ).
