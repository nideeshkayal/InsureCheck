  Doula Services  
 
We pay for the services of a doula. A doula is a trained 
professional who provides continuous physical, emotional, 
and informational support to the birthing parent throughout 
the perinatal period. A doula can also provide informational 
support for community ‑based resources. A doula does not 
replace a licensed medical professional and cannot perform 
clinical tasks.
