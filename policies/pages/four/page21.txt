J. Social Security
 
Social Security determines eligibility and handles enrollment for Medicare. U.S Citizens and lawful 
permanent residents who are 65 and over, or who have a disability or End ‑Stage Renal Disease (ESRD) 
and meet certain conditions, are eligible for Medicare. If you are already getting Social Security checks, 
enrollment into Medicare is automatic. If you are not getting Social Security checks, you have to enroll in 
Medicare. To apply for Medicare, you can call Social Security or visit your local Social Security office.  
 
If you move or change your mailing address, it is important that you contact Social Security to let them
