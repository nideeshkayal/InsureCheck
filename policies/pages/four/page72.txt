  Pulmonary rehabilitation services   
 
We pay for pulmonary rehabilitation programs for members 
who have moderate to very severe chronic obstructive 
pulmonary disease (COPD). You must have an order for 
pulmonary rehabilitation from the doctor or provider treating 
the COPD.

these tests once every 12 months or at certain times during 
pregnancy.
