Chapter  10 Ending your membership in our plan
 
G. Your right to make a complaint if we end your membership in our plan
 
If we end your membership in our plan, we must tell you our reasons in writing for ending your 
membership. We must also explain how you can file a grievance or make a complaint about our decision 
to end your membership. You can also refer to Chapter 9 of your Evidence of Coverage  for information 
about how to make a complaint.
