If your Member ID Card is damaged, lost, or stolen, call Member Services at the number at the bottom of 
the page right away. We will send you a new card.  
 
As long as you are a member of our plan, you do not need to use your red, white, and blue Medicare card 
or your NJ FamilyCare card to get services. Keep those cards in a safe place, in case you need them later.

you get a bill from a provider.
