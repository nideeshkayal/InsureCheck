QIO  
 
When your complaint is about quality of care, you have two choices:  
You can make your complaint about the quality of care directly to the QIO.•
You can make your complaint to the QIO and to our plan. If you make a complaint to the QIO, we 
work with them to resolve your complaint.•
The QIO is a group of practicing doctors and other health care experts paid by the federal government to 
check and improve the care given to Medicare patients. To learn more about the QIO, refer to Section H2  
or refer to Chapter 2  of your Evidence of Coverage .
