Member  responsibilities  
A member  has the responsibility  to: 
• Let the plan and network providers  know , to the extent possible, information that 
they  need to care for the member. 
• Follow the plans and instructions for care that they  have agreed on with their  
provider s. This responsi bility includes considering  the possible results of failure to 
follow the  recommended treatment . 
• Understand their  health problems and participate in creating  mutually agreed -on 
treatment goals as much as  possible . 
• Review all benefits  and membership materials carefully and follow health plan rules . 
• Ask questions for their  understanding of any  explanations and instructions . 
• Treat others with the same respect and courtesy they expect to receive.  
• Keep scheduled provider  visits  or give adequate notice of delay or cancellation.
